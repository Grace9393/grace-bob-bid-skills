## Offering Description

IBM AIOps Services is the application of Generative AI, Machine Learning and Data Science to the end-to-end Value Streams observing IT processes and solutions to detect anomalies, predict outages, analyze their impact on business and prescribe solutions to proactively avoid them. The north-star of AIOps solutions are progressively achieving an autonomous, zero-touch enterprise IT landscape.  

IBM Consulting uses an asset-based approach to infuse AI across the Hybrid Business Solution Stack (hosted on hybrid-multi cloud environments). This offering is driven by our signature asset – IBM Consulting AIOps. This asset features a modular architecture with three core modules—Instrumentation Hub, Insights Hub, and Operations Hub. It consists of capabilities built on top of a framework consisting of harvested assets & accelerators, that helps reuse more than 80% of its functionalities and provides a modular architecture enabling a lean consumption model.

## Value Proposition

- IBM Consulting AIOps performs the job of integrating the data from multiple data sources providing insights that aggregates data from a multitude of sources across the IT stack. This provides the top-down visibility and aids the detection and resolution of the problem much faster.  
- Enables customers to prioritize outage alerts for business-critical applications, enhance application development and operations efficiency by reducing mean time to resolve tickets (MTR) by 50%, and cut root cause analysis cycles by 20–40%. Additionally, it helps customers lower their total cost of ownership (TCO) by 25 – 40%, depending on their enterprise's maturity.  
- By incorporating AI-driven smart operations, enterprises can optimize Day 2 operations, in turn optimize their Opex and maximize their Capex investments.
