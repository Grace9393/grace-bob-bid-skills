## Offering Description  
**SAP Managed Services** provides comprehensive business value across every stage of a customer’s modernization journey. Our pillars offer end-to-end outcomes aligned with SAP’s and IBM’s vision for growth and seamless end-user experiences. The blend of digital and human labor enables customers to accelerate their business and IT modernization.  

- **SAP Manage for S/4HANA, ECC and SaaS Solutions** is a scalable solution for clients to modernize across processes, applications, technology, and hybrid landscapes. Utilizing digital labor, automation, AI/GenAI, and service assistants, it enhances efficiency, maintains consistent performance, modernizes end-user experiences, and supports continuous improvement.  
- **SAP RISE and ManagePlus** offers a modern, cloud-based, SLA-driven architecture with a predictable SaaS billing model. It’s fully scalable, supports SAP around non-SAP applications, and reduces total cost of ownership, helping customers manage workloads with more predictability. SAP Operations as-a-service offers customers a seamless end to end operating model to manage business and IT.  
- **Move to SAP on Cloud** is IBM’s streamlined approach to migrating SAP to the cloud. It simplifies and accelerates transitions by combining version upgrades, RISE migration, and cloud moves into one project. Templates and automation ensure efficient, high-quality outcomes with minimal manual intervention.

## Client Pain Points  
- Clients face uncertainty in managing SAP’s continuous evolution, with SAP’s new BTP driven composable architecture and ECC (older pkg) to be sunset by 2027. Clients need solutions which help them modernize/replace their legacy solutions while safeguarding prior investments.  
- Managing business applications across multiple clouds leads to inefficiencies, increased costs, and slower response times, affecting business agility and scalability along with resilience.  
- Accumulated technical debt due to heavy customizations and fragmented cloud strategies hinder innovation and agility, making it challenging to adapt quickly to business needs.  
- Maintaining IT resilience and business continuity in a complex, evolving cloud environment under external pressures to lower costs and higher evolving business expectations.  
- SAP’s push to drive SaaS products (RISE with SaaS that pushes customers to adopt standard architectures, clean core, new processes, and the cloud which they are not ready to adopt).
