## Offering Description  
**IBM is Reimagining S2P: AI-Powered Source to Pay BPO for Incremental Savings and Breakthrough Efficiency**  
The new S2P offering addresses four key points:  
- **One**: Drive insights, and efficiencies to Procurement teams to build more strategic, innovative relationships with suppliers that drives more savings.  
- **Two**: Ensure that the supplier agreements and contracts that are in place are leveraged and optimized; and reduce leakage.  
- **Three**: Streamlines, digitizes and automates the end-to-end process for a near 100% touchless process downstream.  
- **Four**: Leverage technology and AI capabilities to enhance the user experiences across the value chain.  

---

## Client Pain Points  
- Inadequate capacity to drive sourcing across tail spend and tactical spend  
- Inability to consolidate the data across multiple systems and geographies and drive meaningful insights for supplier engagement and savings  
- Ineffective Ariba/Coupa/Vala/SAP user experience  
- Supplier discovery – time consuming and not effective  
- High volume of low value transactional procurement  
- Time consuming contract drafting process  
- Limited visibility over existing contracts and terms  
- Limited guidance for business users to buy on catalogue and on contract  
- Contract Leakage – invoices not aligned to contract terms/pricing  
- Manual routing of invoices to approvers for exception resolution (via email)  
- Increased invoice receipt-to-posting timelines due to the manual nature of the process  
- Manual processing of non-PO invoices  
- Lack of OCR engine, requiring manual data input  
- Increasing the risk of data inaccuracies  
- Delaying in payments and lack of automated controls  
- No visibility over invoices received and under processing  
- High Cost per Invoice and processing cost
