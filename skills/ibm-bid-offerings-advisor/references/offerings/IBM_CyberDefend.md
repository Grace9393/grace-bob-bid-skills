## Offering Description  
Use Zero Trust principles, underpinned with AI and assets to advise, design, build, and manage the security of our clients’ digital ecosystems and hybrid multi-cloud environments, including the security of:  

1. Sensitive and personal data across geographic boundaries  
2. Enterprises for a Quantum future  
3. AI and AI Ops pipelines  
4. Modern applications by building threat models, and shifting security left  
5. Enterprise application posture, data, roles & access (i.e., SAP app & infra)  
6. User and privilege roles, access, and governance  
7. Hybrid cloud platforms across network, workloads, containers, and cloud providers
