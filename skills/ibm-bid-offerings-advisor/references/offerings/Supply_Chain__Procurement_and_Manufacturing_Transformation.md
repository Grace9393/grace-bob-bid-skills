## Client Pain Points  
- **Slow Response to Disruptions & Market Changes:** Inability to adapt quickly to changes in demand, leading to missed opportunities or overproduction.  
- **Inefficient Supply Chain Operations:** Disconnected processes and lack of real-time insights hinder effective decision-making.  
- **Asset Underutilization:** Inadequate asset management leads to higher maintenance costs and shortened asset lifecycles.  
- **High Operational Costs:** Inefficiencies in manufacturing processes drive up costs, reducing profitability.  
- **Lack of Technological Integration:** Slowed systems and outdated technology limit the adoption of Industry 4.0 innovations.  
- **Sustainability Challenges:** Difficulty in implementing sustainable practices that reduce environmental impact while maintaining operational efficiency.
