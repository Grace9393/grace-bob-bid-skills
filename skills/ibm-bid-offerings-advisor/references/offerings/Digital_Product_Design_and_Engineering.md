## Offering Description

This offering helps clients transform ideas into digital applications and platforms using IBM’s methodology, combining design, engineering, and product management. We develop standalone and edge applications, business platforms, rebuild existing ones, and integrate advanced technologies including GenAI, to address evolving business needs.

The offering delivers compelling e2e value propositions to create and scale innovative digital products and platforms by leveraging unique new commercial models with pre-defined high performing squads and Generative AI assets. This accelerates product delivery across analysis, design, development, testing, and release phases, enhancing adaptability, reducing costs, and driving impactful digital transformation outcomes aligned with strategic goals.

## Client Pain Points

- **Competitive Disadvantage and waste**: 40% of enterprises do not have the capability to pivot based on market response with inability to integrate customer feedback.  
- **Slow Time-to-Market**: Traditional development methods lead to delays, impacting agility and increasing time-to-market.  
- **Business Misalignment**: Lack of product-centric alignment between business prioritization, requirements development and software development sites, leading to mismatch in functionality delivered, churn and delays  
- **Declining Developer Productivity**: Developer Productivity reduced to 50% or less because of legacy SDLC processes with multiple dependencies and "unproductive" hand offs across teams, limited automation / development pipelines.  
- **Underutilized GenAI**: Struggling to unlock the full potential of GenAI, missing opportunities to automate software development processes to end.  
- **Data Utilization Challenges**: Difficulty in harnessing data hindering speed of decision making.  
- **Security Gaps**: Security is often overlooked in the development process, creating vulnerabilities.
