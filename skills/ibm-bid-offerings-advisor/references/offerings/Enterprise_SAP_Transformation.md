### Offering Description  
SAP advisory and implementation services that deliver quantifiable business outcomes for our clients powered by a proven methodology and IBM proprietary and 3rd party assets and accelerators.  

The offering encompasses transformation and implementation of core SAP ERP platforms including SAP S/4HANA on premise, RISE or GROW editions and SaaS line of business solutions such as Aruba and IBP.  

SAP S/4HANA serves as the digital core for mission critical business systems and is a foundation for business innovation and serves. It empowers users with contextual, real-time information for faster, better decision making, supports business model improvements and enables an innovation with simpler, more efficient and secure IT operations. Offering pillars include:  

- **Rapid Discovery**: A comprehensive approach for initiating an S/4HANA enabled transformation. It defines the business capabilities and benefits needed while providing clarity to the implementation roadmap.  
- **Business Transformation on SAP S/4HANA**: A greenfield transformation and implementation approach leveraging IBM and SAP best practices, industry innovations and technical solutions to unlock business value for our clients.  
- **Rapid Move**: A unique migration approach accelerating the move from SAP ECC to SAP S/4HANA through automation, and selective data migration. This differentiated method allows clients to leverage their existing SAP investments, while also enabling transformations where it matters most for them.

### Client Pain Points  
- Antiquated ERP system design and complex landscape not meeting today’s business process requirements and hampering digital transformation.  
- Disparate processes across the enterprise that impede effective decision making and limit the ability to measure business performance and scale automation for efficiency.  
- End of life for ERP Central Component or Suite on HANA adds challenges to migrate and secure ERP to the cloud.  
- Complex integration between ERP and other applications adds challenges to migrate and secure ERP to the cloud.  
- Customers with a large SAP footprint are challenged to migrate to S/4HANA or to the cloud with a balanced (risk vs. speed) approach.
