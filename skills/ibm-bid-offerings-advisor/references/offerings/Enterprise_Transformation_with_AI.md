## Offering Description  
We empower clients to align and drive their business strategies leveraging the transformative potential of AI powered by enterprise data. Our approach unlocks new opportunities, drives innovation and speed, and enhances decision-making. We orchestrate value realization and ensure responsible governance through a tailored AI implementation roadmap built on four offering pillars:  

1. **AI Strategy & Value**: Identifying and prioritizing AI use cases, developing transformation roadmaps and business cases, and orchestrating value realization.  
2. **Responsible AI Governance**: Ensuring ethical, transparent, and compliant AI practices through comprehensive governance frameworks.  
3. **M&A & Divestitures**: Accelerating transformation through strategic transactions, with a focus on technology integration and separation management.  
4. **Risk & Compliance**: Navigating regulatory requirements and establishing robust risk management for AI scale.  

This comprehensive framework **accelerates and de-risks** the AI transformation journey and drives sustainable growth and competitive advantage.

## Committed Outcomes | Client Value  
1. **Enterprise AI Strategy Development**: Define vision and objectives, develop a business case and ROI framework, and create an AI capability model that builds or strengthens the client’s own AI capabilities (including COE or other models).  
2. **AI Portfolio Optimization**: Systematically discover, assess, and prioritize AI use cases across BUs considering value, feasibility, and risk; create portfolio roadmap; and establish AI program governance and funding mechanism.  
3. **Data & AI Maturity and Risk Assessment**: Evaluate technical, organizational, and process maturity and benchmark against industry peers and leaders. Develop a maturity improvement roadmap with clear milestones. Assess risk exposure across regulatory, operational, and reputational dimensions.  
4. **Data & AI Governance Framework Implementation**: Design and implement a comprehensive governance structure operationalizing AI principles and risk management controls, roles and responsibilities, and a secure model lifecycle—underpinned by tools that efficiently scale AI across the organization.  
5. **Organizational Enablement**: Orchestrate change management and commensurate strategy, assess AI capabilities & skills gaps, develop AI training and upskilling program, establish COE and communities of practice, define talent acquisition & retention strategy.  
6. **M&A and Divestiture Execution**: Guide technology due diligence, integration/separation planning, synergy identification, day 1 readiness, and post-transaction value realization.  
7. **Risk & Compliance Management**: Drive regulatory impact assessments, compliance framework design, model risk management, audit readiness, and ongoing monitoring systems

## Client Pain Points  
- Misalignment between AI initiatives and overall business strategy  
- Difficulty in identifying and realizing tangible value from AI investments, e.g.:  
  - Limited AI literacy across the organization  
  - Insufficient data and AI engineering and operational capabilities  
  - Weak governance structures  
  - Ineffective change management processes  
- Inadequate data and AI governance to:  
  - Address traditional, amplified, and new AI risks due to GenAI  
  - Ensure compliance with new AI regulations (e.g., EU AI Act)  
- Insufficient compliance & risk management frameworks for enterprise-scale AI deployment  
- Lack of integrated approach to managing technology during M&A and divestitures
