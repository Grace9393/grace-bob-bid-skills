## Offering Description

### AI Powered Application Modernization for AI Enabled Enterprise
Legacy applications slow down innovation and increase operational costs. Our Application Migration & Modernization Services enable you to transform your IT landscape, ensuring scalability, security, and cost efficiency while leveraging the full power of cloud, AI-Ready Infrastructure and AI-driven automation.

---

## Client Pain Points

- **Competitive Disadvantage and waste**: 40% of enterprises do not have the capability to pivot based on market response with inability to integrate customer feedback.
- **Slow Time-to-Market**: Traditional modernization methods lead to delays, impacting agility and increasing time-to-market.
- **Business Misalignment**: Lack of technology and business prioritization, requirements development and software development sprints, leading to mismatch in functionality delivered, churn and delays ($274B wasted on failed projects annually).
- **Declining Developer Productivity**: Developer Productivity reduced by 50% or less because of legacy SDLC processes with multiple dependencies and “unproductive” hand-offs across teams, limited automation of legacy processes.
- **Underutilized GenAI**: Struggling to unlock the full potential of GenAI, missing opportunities to automate software development processes to end.
- **Data Utilization Challenges**: Difficulty in harnessing data hindering speed of decision making.
- **Lack of Application Innovation**: Tool sprawl, lack of reuse and automation workflows.
- **Risk of Data Misuse Due To Sprawl**: Fragmented management of sensitive data across cloud/on-prem environments creates risk of data misuse due to significant data sprawl.
