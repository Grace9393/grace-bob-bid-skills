## Value Proposition  
While 95% of CEOs pushing for digital and cloud adoption, only 25% of enterprises have seen positive ROI from their cloud and technology programs. Our Cloud & Technology Strategy offering, powered by the Hybrid by Design framework and a Gen-AI-driven, asset-first model, bridges this gap by delivering  

- **Avoid Vendor Lock-in:** Develop a coherent cloud & technology strategy with a plug-and-play architecture and minimizes risks.  
- **Accelerate Innovation:** Embraces open innovation and enables enterprises to keep up with cloud advancements and technology evolution supported by robust value-adoption frameworks.  
- **Optimize Costs and Enhance Efficiency:** Achieve pricing flexibility by selecting the right cloud destination for workloads while boosting IT operational efficiency with granular cost allocation and an "IT as a Service" model.  
- **People and Operating Model Outcomes:** Enable seamless cloud adoption with a future-ready operating model, fostering talent transformation, streamlined processes, and enhanced collaboration across teams.  
- **Strengthen Security:** Integrated "security as code" best practices, as well as threat monitoring and testing services.

## Client Pain Points  
- **Uncertainty in Driving ROI:** Struggling to identify the right starting point and strategic priorities for cloud adoption, leading to delays in realizing tangible ROI and business value.  
- **Suboptimal Cloud ROI:** Clients are failing to realize the full potential of cloud investments, resulting in lower-than-expected return on investment.  
- **Uncontrolled Cloud Costs:** Clients are struggling to manage and optimize cloud spending, leading to budget overruns and financial uncertainty.  
- **Legacy System Burden:** Legacy applications hinder cloud adoption and inhibit the ability to release new products and functionality quickly to changing market needs.  
- **GenAI Security Concerns:** Clients are hesitant to embrace generative AI due to concerns about data privacy, security risks, and potential misuse of the technology.
