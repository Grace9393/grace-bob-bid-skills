## Offering Description  
As Finance Operations we are about 9K strong process practitioners, servicing 80 clients in 25+ locations managing simple to complex transactions be it from 100m journals processed, managing 70bn of accounts receivable. The focus has been in equally delivering on process efficiencies 40-60% and process value improvement of cash flow and spend savings to the tune of 10-15%.  

The business functions we service includes:  
- **Record To Analyze** (managing controllership activities E.g. Reconciliations, journals, financial reporting)  
- **Bill To Cash** (managing receivable management activities. E.g. Collections, dispute, cash application)  

These services to client have resulted in a high NPS of 67 and enables operational diversity globally.
