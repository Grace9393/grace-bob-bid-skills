### Offering Description

Quality Engineering Service (QES) offers a comprehensive set of innovative quality and test services providing scalable solutions for clients including consulting and transformation, discrete test solutions and integrated enterprise-wide solutions in small & multi-year engagements.  

QES leverages a standardized methodology to deliver end-to-end QE processes reinforced by GenAI-infused assets that accelerate clients' implementation and transformation journeys.  

Our extensive cross-industry experience leveraging multiple technologies, enables the creation of repeatable, scalable solutions delivered efficiently and consistently. Our global network of Client Innovation Centres (CICs) enable us to deliver high-quality solutions with local insights and global reach.  

Leveraging IBM’s unique cognitive and test optimization technology, QES helps clients reduce risks by leveraging predictive and cognitive capabilities, tools, cloud-enabled methods, industry test plans, and advanced test automation techniques.
