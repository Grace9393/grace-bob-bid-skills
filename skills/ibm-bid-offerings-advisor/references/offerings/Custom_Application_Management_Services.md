## Offering Description
IBM’s Custom Application Management Services (Custom AMS) maintains and enhances clients’ custom-built applications and data to help them significantly lower costs and extract value.  

We manage systems whether they’re on mainframes, in the cloud, on-premise, or in hybrid environments.  

By tapping into AI-driven assets, agents and assistants, a well-integrated service management system, we manage processes, and a global talent pool at competitive rates, Custom AMS helps clients make the most of their IT spend.  

Custom AMS applies an asset-first AMS approach to shift work from humans to gen-AI infused assets and automation in key AMS processes. This results in increased productivity, yielding reduced operational costs, enhanced visibility, security & resilience, and drives continuous cloud modernization. Incidents get resolved faster, enhancements are deployed quicker, applications become more resilient, and IT services align more closely with business goals.  

Custom AMS establishes a base for ongoing innovation and modernization, reducing technical debt and total costs, while improving the resilience and overall performance of applications. Use Custom AMS as a catalyst to pull in adjacent offerings like AI Ops, Modernisation, Quality Engineering and Cloud Platform Engineering Services.

## Client Pain Points
- The CIO is grappling with the need for increased IT funding to support rapid digitization. They are under pressure to optimize operational spend and reduce costs, but also need to maintain or increase IT operational efficiencies to drive transformation programs for their business.  
- The IT Operations Manager is struggling with the complexity of managing applications on a hybrid multi-cloud environment. Complex SLA commitments and management processes are leading to poor business outcomes, high costs, slow response times, long resolution times, and inconsistent performance. This is affecting the overall performance and productivity of the IT team.  
- The high technical debt due to a misaligned cloud strategy and the proliferation of applications from M&A activities and overlapping development efforts is causing the Technical Architect significant difficulty. They are finding it challenging to maintain the existing IT environment.  
- The lack of IT resilience and business continuity is a major concern for the Business Continuity Manager. They are worried about the potential impact of IT failures on the business operations and are lacking solutions to ensure business continuity.  
- The lack of expertise with the right mix of business-technology skills, AI-led AMS operation, and transformation is a challenge for Delivery and Talent Managers. They are finding it difficult to find and retain talent with the required skills to support the organization’s digital transformation journey.

## Value Proposition
In today’s rapidly evolving digital landscape, CIOs grapple with escalating costs, burgeoning application complexity, and technical debt in hybrid multi-cloud environments, hindering their capacity to innovate and drive growth. Traditional methods necessitate significant resources, both human and financial, yet they often fall short in delivering optimal results and value.  

How can CIOs manage these challenges without compromising on business objectives? IBM Custom AMS is a transformative IT management solution. It helps CIOs manage costs, complexity, and technical debt in multi-cloud environments, using AI-driven tools and a global team of experts. With industry-specific solutions, outcome-based pricing, and seamless integration with transformation objectives, it aligns with business outcomes and reduces overhead. Leveraging IBM’s ecosystem, Custom AMS offers a comprehensive approach to IT management, enabling CIOs to drive growth without operational complexities.  

Unlike any other solution, Custom AMS offers a zero-touch model for continuous modernization, reducing application management costs by up to 40%, and providing a holistic view of IT, application, and business process health, all while enhancing IT resilience.
