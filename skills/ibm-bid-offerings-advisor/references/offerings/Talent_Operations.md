## Offering Description

We help clients to plan, acquire, and upskill candidates, to drive business growth and success. This innovative offering combines the power of AI-powered technologies with the expertise of experienced Talent professionals to streamline talent acquisition, reduce time-to-hire, and improve overall candidate experience. The solution also includes expert HR consulting services to ensure tailored guidance and support throughout the talent lifecycle. Services include workforce planning, talent acquisition (RPO) and talent development.

> *The offering is still anchored on Talent Acquisition / Recruitment Process Outsourcing, with extended services to include workforce planning and talent development – working with clients to plan for their talent needs to helping to skill new joiners.*

## Value Proposition

In partnership with our clients, we enable business strategies by ensuring the right skills are available at the right time at the right price point – reinventing ways of working with data-rich, AI-powered workflows and a new talent operating model. Services are enabled by:  

- **Transformed experiences** for candidates, hiring managers and your HR professionals with real-time, personalized digital and human interactions.  
- **Industry-relevant workflows** that guide teams to optimize impact through greater transparency for faster time to fill, greater quality of hire and reduced costs of operations.  
- **New Insights** through unprecedented access to market and operational data with the ability to dynamically adjust your sourcing and talent strategies

## Client Pain Points

- **Difficulty achieving basic KPIs for hiring:**  
  - Long time to fill  
  - Poor retention record  
  - High cost  
  - High cost per hire  

- **Misaligned business strategy:**  
  - Inefficient data and insights makes forecasting difficult  
  - Unable to align talent needs with overall business goals, leading to unrealistic financial planning  

- **Disconnected or outdated data and technology:**  
  - Workforce planning hamstrung by insufficient data  
  - Lack of talent analytics and market research hinders sourcing strategy development  
  - Technology today doesn't allow for inclusive hiring processes  

- **Inconsistent service delivery approach:**  
  - Difficulty in hiring for niche skills  
  - The tools are not in place to mitigate human bias  
  - Increasing attrition rate drives the negative impact  
  - Poor candidate experiences impact the scale and quality of pipeline, increasing cost per hire
