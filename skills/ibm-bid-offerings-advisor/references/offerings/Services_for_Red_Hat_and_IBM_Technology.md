## Client Pain Points  
Challenges are holding back AI implementations:  

- **AI stuck in pilots, not platforms**: Many organizations are experimenting with AI, but lack the platform, operating model, and governance to scale it across the business, leading to isolated use cases with limited strategic impact.  
- **Too much use of assistants, not enough reinvention**: Task-level tools like copilots may improve productivity, but they don’t address end-to-end processes or business model innovation, leaving enterprise transformation potential unmet.  
- **Disconnected Tech, Inconsistent Execution**: Fragmented AI environments across cloud, on-prem, and business units create AI agent sprawl, inefficiencies, and risk, making it challenging to scale responsibly or deliver consistent outcomes.  
- **No clear enterprise AI strategy or operating model**: Without a clear enterprise AI strategy, supported by the proper data foundation, governance, and talent, AI investments remain tactical and fail to generate long-term business value.
