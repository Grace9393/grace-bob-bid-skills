### Offering Description
- Cyber Strategy and Risk ("CSR") provides a proactive approach to help organizations manage increased cyber risk demands.
- Our people, skills, ecosystem partners, and delivery strengths power us to boost Cyber Risk Oversight, Continuous Compliance and Cyber Resilience Management through Automation, Machine Learning (ML), Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Generative AI ("GenAI") intelligent capabilities.
- Our structured approach is based on the IBM Active Governance Framework ("AGF"), which is organized in five Domains - Cyber Strategy, Cyber Risk Management, Cyber Resilience Management, Cyber Policy and Compliance Management, and Cyber Metrics Dashboards and Reporting.

### Client Pain Points
- **Cyber Risk Oversight Program Measurement and Reporting**: Organizations lack qualitative, quantitative, financial measurement and single view of cyber risks and insights
- **Regulatory Landscape**: Continued expansion of global and local regulatory changes which require additional reporting about cyber risk governance, risks and material cybersecurity incidents
- **Nth Party Risk Management**: The ecosystem of digital supply chains and interconnected solutions require enhanced cybersecurity integration across ecosystems and unified view of cyber risks
- **GRC Programs**: Complex, inconsistent and fragmented practices trigger the need for organization automation, intelligence capabilities and a cyber risk insights driven organization
- **Technology Park Aging and Complexity**: Tracking the large number of technology applications and key data points that should be used for the effective cyber risk management program

### Value Proposition
Although 86% of organizations have a cyber strategy, only 35% have started executing on that strategy, leaving organizations vulnerable to cyber risk. AGF helps clients address this problem by:
- Offering a transformative approach to cyber programs by leveraging automation, AI, and ML technologies to address regulatory complexities, reduce cyber risk, ensure compliance, and operationalize cyber resilience.
- Delivering a real-time view of cyber risks across the digital ecosystem, transforming organizations into cyber-risk intelligent entities capable of navigating the complex cybersecurity landscape with confidence and agility.
- Providing a comprehensive, five-domain strategy that integrates qualitative and quantitative metrics, offering unparalleled visibility into cyber risk exposure. This enables data-driven decision-making and fosters an organizational resilient cyber ecosystem.

IBM is recognized as a leader by multiple analysts in cyber and risk, offering expertise created for some of the largest global enterprises and critical government entities.
