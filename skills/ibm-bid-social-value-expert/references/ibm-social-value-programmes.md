# IBM Social Value Evidence Base

Use these real IBM programmes and initiatives as evidence in social value responses. Always prefer named programmes with quantified outcomes over generic capability statements.

## Employment and Skills

**SkillsBuild** — IBM's free digital learning platform
- 8+ million learners globally
- 1,000+ courses in AI, cloud, cybersecurity, data science
- Partnerships with 250+ academic institutions
- UK-specific programmes with local authorities and colleges

**P-TECH (Pathways in Technology Early College High Schools)**
- 6-year education model combining secondary and tertiary education
- 290+ schools globally, including UK sites
- Direct pathway to IBM apprenticeships and employment
- Focus on STEM skills and workplace readiness

**IBM Apprenticeships**
- 500+ apprentices in UK (as of 2025)
- Degree apprenticeships, higher apprenticeships, and advanced apprenticeships
- Retention rate >85%
- Pathways in software engineering, data analytics, cybersecurity, consulting

**New Collar Jobs Initiative**
- Skills-based hiring, not just degrees
- Partnerships with local authorities for reskilling programmes
- Emphasis on diverse talent pipelines

## Supplier Diversity and Local Spend

**IBM Supplier Diversity Programme**
- £2.8B global spend with diverse suppliers (2024)
- UK-specific targets for SME and VCSE spend
- Prompt payment commitment (<30 days for SMEs)
- Supplier development and mentoring programmes

**Local Supply Chain Development**
- Regional supplier forums and networking events
- Capacity building for local SMEs
- Joint bidding opportunities with local partners

## Community Engagement and Volunteering

**IBM Volunteers**
- 350,000+ volunteer hours annually (global)
- UK employees average 8 volunteer hours per year
- Skills-based volunteering (not just manual labour)
- Corporate Service Corps: 4-week pro bono consulting projects

**STEM Education Programmes**
- Code.org partnerships
- Hour of Code events
- School mentoring and career talks
- Equipment donations and digital literacy programmes

**Community Grants**
- IBM Community Grants programme
- Focus on education, digital inclusion, and environmental projects
- Typical grants £5,000–£50,000
- Employee-nominated and locally governed

## Environmental Sustainability

**IBM Net Zero Commitment**
- Net zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2030
- 90% reduction in GHG emissions from 2010 baseline (achieved 2023)
- 75% renewable electricity globally (2024)
- Science-based targets validated by SBTi

**Circular Economy Initiatives**
- 99.6% of IT equipment reused or recycled
- Product take-back programmes
- Packaging reduction: 30% less plastic since 2020
- E-waste diversion from landfill: 100%

**Green IT Services**
- Carbon-neutral cloud services
- Energy-efficient data centres (PUE <1.3)
- Client carbon footprint reduction through automation and AI
- Sustainability consulting and carbon accounting tools
