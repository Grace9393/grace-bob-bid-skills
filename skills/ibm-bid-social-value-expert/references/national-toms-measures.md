# National TOMs Framework Measures

This document provides a comprehensive reference for the National TOMs (Themes, Outcomes, Measures) Framework used in UK public sector procurement for social value measurement.

**Last Updated**: January 2026  
**Framework Version**: National TOMs 2024/25

## Overview

The National TOMs Framework provides a standardised approach to measuring and reporting social value in public procurement. It consists of 5 themes, multiple outcomes per theme, and specific measures with defined units and proxy values.

**Proxy values** are monetary values assigned to each measure to enable comparison and aggregation. These are updated annually by the Social Value Portal.

## Theme 1: Jobs - Promoting Local Skills and Employment

### NT1: Number of local people (FTE) employed on contract for one year or the whole duration of the contract, whichever is shorter

**Unit**: FTE  
**Proxy Value**: £18,500 per FTE  
**Description**: Direct employment of local residents (within the contracting authority's area) on the contract.

**IBM Application**: Local hires for service delivery roles, project teams, support functions.

### NT2: Number of employees (FTE) taken on who are long-term unemployed (unemployed for a year or longer)

**Unit**: FTE  
**Proxy Value**: £25,000 per FTE  
**Description**: Employment of individuals who have been unemployed for 12+ months.

**IBM Application**: Targeted recruitment through Jobcentre Plus partnerships, New Collar Jobs initiative.

### NT3: Number of employees (FTE) taken on who are not in employment, education, or training (NEETs)

**Unit**: FTE  
**Proxy Value**: £20,000 per FTE  
**Description**: Employment of young people (typically 16-24) not in education, employment, or training.

**IBM Application**: Apprenticeships, graduate schemes, P-TECH pathways.

### NT4: Number of 16-25 y.o. care leavers employed for 26 weeks or more

**Unit**: Number  
**Proxy Value**: £15,000 per person  
**Description**: Employment of care leavers (young people who have been in local authority care).

**IBM Application**: Targeted recruitment with local authority care leaver services.

### NT5: Number of disabled people employed for 26 weeks or more

**Unit**: Number  
**Proxy Value**: £15,000 per person  
**Description**: Employment of individuals with disabilities.

**IBM Application**: Inclusive recruitment, workplace adjustments, disability confidence employer status.

### NT6: Number of hours of support into work provided to unemployed people through career mentoring, including mock interviews, CV advice, and careers guidance

**Unit**: Hours  
**Proxy Value**: £10 per hour  
**Description**: Pre-employment support for unemployed individuals.

**IBM Application**: SkillsBuild career coaching, IBM volunteer mentoring, employability workshops.

### NT7: Number of staff hours spent on local school and college visits e.g. delivering careers talks, curriculum support, literacy support, safety talks

**Unit**: Hours  
**Proxy Value**: £10 per hour  
**Description**: Employee volunteering in education settings.

**IBM Application**: STEM talks, career guidance, Hour of Code events, P-TECH engagement.

### NT8: Number of training opportunities on the contract (BTEC, City & Guilds, NVQ, HNC, HND) that have either been completed during the year or that will be supported by the organisation until completion in the following years

**Unit**: Number  
**Proxy Value**: £3,000 per qualification  
**Description**: Vocational qualifications supported.

**IBM Application**: Apprenticeship qualifications, professional certifications, vendor qualifications.

### NT9: Number of apprenticeships on the contract that have either been completed during the year or that will be supported by the organisation until completion in the following years

**Unit**: Number  
**Proxy Value**: £25,000 per apprenticeship  
**Description**: Apprenticeships created and supported to completion.

**IBM Application**: IBM Apprenticeship Programme (degree, higher, and advanced apprenticeships).

### NT10: Number of weeks of training opportunities on the contract (BTEC, City & Guilds, NVQ, HNC, HND) that have either been completed during the year or that will be supported by the organisation until completion in the following years

**Unit**: Weeks  
**Proxy Value**: £100 per week  
**Description**: Duration of training provided.

**IBM Application**: SkillsBuild courses, technical training, professional development.

### NT11: Number of hours of support into work provided to over 24 y.o. unemployed people through career mentoring, including mock interviews, CV advice, and careers guidance

**Unit**: Hours  
**Proxy Value**: £10 per hour  
**Description**: Pre-employment support for adults over 24.

**IBM Application**: Career transition support, reskilling programmes, IBM volunteer mentoring.

## Theme 2: Growth - Supporting Growth of Responsible Regional Businesses

### NT12: Total amount (£) spent in LOCAL supply chain through the contract

**Unit**: £  
**Proxy Value**: £0.12 per £1 spent  
**Description**: Spend with suppliers based in the local area.

**IBM Application**: Local SME subcontracting, local procurement for services and goods.

### NT13: Total amount (£) spent through contract with LOCAL micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs)

**Unit**: £  
**Proxy Value**: £0.20 per £1 spent  
**Description**: Spend with local SMEs (fewer than 250 employees).

**IBM Application**: SME partnerships, subcontracting, professional services procurement.

### NT14: Total amount (£) spent through contract with LOCAL voluntary, community and social enterprises (VCSEs)

**Unit**: £  
**Proxy Value**: £0.30 per £1 spent  
**Description**: Spend with local third sector organisations.

**IBM Application**: Charity partnerships, social enterprise suppliers, community organisation contracts.

### NT15: Percentage of LOCAL MSMEs and VCSEs in your supply chain that you pay within 30 days

**Unit**: %  
**Proxy Value**: £0.50 per % per £1 spent  
**Description**: Prompt payment to local SMEs and VCSEs.

**IBM Application**: IBM prompt payment policy (<30 days for SMEs).

### NT16: Number of employees (FTE) hired on the contract who are rehabilitating or ex-offenders

**Unit**: FTE  
**Proxy Value**: £15,000 per FTE  
**Description**: Employment of individuals with criminal records.

**IBM Application**: Ban the Box policy, partnerships with rehabilitation services.

### NT17: Number of hours of support provided to help local voluntary organisations or social enterprises through donations, mentoring or consultancy

**Unit**: Hours  
**Proxy Value**: £50 per hour  
**Description**: Pro bono support for third sector.

**IBM Application**: IBM Corporate Service Corps, skills-based volunteering, pro bono consulting.

## Theme 4: Environment - Decarbonising and Safeguarding the World Around Us

### NT28: Tonnes of CO2e carbon emissions reduced on contract

**Unit**: tCO2e  
**Proxy Value**: £70 per tCO2e  
**Description**: Carbon emissions reduction from contract delivery.

**IBM Application**: Remote working, cloud migration, energy-efficient operations, renewable energy.

### NT29: Plastic recycled or reused on contract

**Unit**: kg  
**Proxy Value**: £0.50 per kg  
**Description**: Plastic waste diverted from landfill.

**IBM Application**: IT equipment recycling, packaging reduction, circular economy initiatives.

### NT30: Single-use plastic packaging eliminated on contract

**Unit**: kg  
**Proxy Value**: £1.00 per kg  
**Description**: Single-use plastic eliminated.

**IBM Application**: Sustainable packaging, reusable alternatives, supplier requirements.

### NT31: Renewable energy generated on contract

**Unit**: kWh  
**Proxy Value**: £0.10 per kWh  
**Description**: Renewable energy produced.

**IBM Application**: Solar installations, renewable energy procurement, green tariffs.

### NT32: Car miles driven using low or no emission vehicles on contract

**Unit**: Miles  
**Proxy Value**: £0.05 per mile  
**Description**: Low-emission vehicle miles.

**IBM Application**: Electric vehicle fleet, hybrid vehicles, EV charging infrastructure.

### NT33: Car miles driven on contract using diesel or petrol vehicles

**Unit**: Miles  
**Proxy Value**: -£0.05 per mile (negative value)  
**Description**: High-emission vehicle miles (negative impact).

**IBM Application**: Minimise through remote working, public transport, EV transition.

### NT34: Initiatives taken or supported to engage people in protecting and improving the natural environment

**Unit**: Number  
**Proxy Value**: £1,000 per initiative  
**Description**: Environmental engagement activities.

**IBM Application**: Conservation volunteering, biodiversity projects, environmental education.

### NT35: Donations or investments towards initiatives aimed at protecting and improving the natural environment

**Unit**: £  
**Proxy Value**: £1.00 per £1 donated  
**Description**: Financial support for environmental causes.

**IBM Application**: Environmental charity partnerships, conservation funding, green technology investment.

## Theme 5: Innovation - Promoting Social Innovation

### NT52: Innovative measures to promote and embed social value

**Unit**: Number  
**Proxy Value**: £5,000 per initiative  
**Description**: Novel approaches to social value delivery.

**IBM Application**: AI for social good, digital inclusion innovations, new partnership models.

### NT53: Innovative measures to promote and embed environmental sustainability

**Unit**: Number  
**Proxy Value**: £5,000 per initiative  
**Description**: Novel approaches to environmental sustainability.

**IBM Application**: Green IT innovations, circular economy pilots, carbon reduction technologies.

### NT54: Innovative measures to promote and embed economic growth

**Unit**: Number  
**Proxy Value**: £5,000 per initiative  
**Description**: Novel approaches to economic development.

**IBM Application**: SME digital transformation, innovation hubs, technology transfer.

## Using TOMs Measures in Bids

### Selection Strategy

1. **Prioritise buyer-weighted themes**: If the buyer weights Jobs at 40%, focus commitments there
2. **Choose measures IBM can deliver**: Only commit to measures with proven capability
3. **Balance high-value and high-volume**: Mix high proxy value measures (e.g., NT9 apprenticeships) with high-volume measures (e.g., NT19 volunteering hours)
4. **Align with contract**: Larger contracts support larger commitments
5. **Differentiate**: Choose measures where IBM has strong evidence and can outperform competitors

### Typical IBM TOMs Portfolio

For a £5M, 3-year contract, a typical IBM commitment might include:

**Jobs (40% weighting):**
- NT1: 15 local FTE employed (£277,500)
- NT9: 5 apprenticeships (£125,000)
- NT10: 500 training weeks (£50,000)

**Growth (20% weighting):**
- NT13: £500K spend with local SMEs (£100,000)
- NT15: 100% prompt payment to SMEs (£250,000)

**Social (20% weighting):**
- NT19: 2,000 volunteering hours (£30,000)
- NT20: £50K donations (£50,000)

**Environment (15% weighting):**
- NT28: 100 tCO2e reduction (£7,000)
- NT32: 50,000 EV miles (£2,500)

**Innovation (5% weighting):**
- NT52: 3 social innovation initiatives (£15,000)

**Total Social Value**: £907,000 (18% of contract value)

### Reporting Requirements

Most buyers require:
- **Quarterly reporting** via Social Value Portal or equivalent
- **Evidence of delivery**: Timesheets, invoices, certificates, photos
- **Variance explanations**: If targets are missed or exceeded
- **Annual audit**: Independent verification of outcomes

### Common Mistakes

1. **Over-committing**: Promising measures IBM can't deliver or track
2. **Ignoring weighting**: Focusing on low-weighted themes
3. **Generic commitments**: Not tailoring to local context
4. **Poor evidence**: Claiming measures without proof
5. **Weak governance**: No clear accountability or reporting mechanism

## Updates and Changes

The National TOMs Framework is updated annually. Key changes in 2024/25:

- Updated proxy values (inflation adjustment)
- New measures for digital inclusion (NT54-NT56)
- Enhanced environmental measures (NT36-NT38)
- Strengthened reporting requirements

Always check the Social Value Portal for the latest framework version and proxy values before submitting bids.
