### Infosys

- Among IPPs, Infosys is the provider which often brings in and positions domain expertise as a differentiator even in ADM opportunities
- Has the tendency to go aggressive on transition timelines in large bids to excite the prospective client, which at times is seen as a risk by many conservative clients
- Infosys is found to be most matured IPP when it comes to articulating the solution and offerings, however, still lags from global peers
- FTE rates for North America region are found to be competitive when compared against market, however, their rates for Europe region tend to be relatively higher than other Indian peers
- Observed to be one of the most competitive providers in the ANZ and Singapore market
- In the ADM space, throughout 2023 Infosys had implemented cost rationalization measures and tried to retain clients by refraining from requesting high price increases
- The level-on-level jump of ADM rates tend to taper down at higher experience levels thereby making it more competitive at upper levels
- Has been observed to be costlier than other IPPs in the LATAM region
- Despite proposing competitive and well-articulated solution, enterprises raise concerns around Infosys’ ability to deliver transformation at scale
- In a few transformation deals, Infosys found to have jointly submitted the bids along with an MBB firm, where MBB is expected to do change and overall program management while Infosys responsible for remaining portion of execution. While MBBs involvement increases the fee drastically, the solution is positioned as “best-of-the-breed”
- Tends to offer a part of consulting service as investment in mega implementation deals
- Infosys has been observed to use Peru as a nearshore location, instead of the frequently proposed Mexico by its peers. Despite Peru being a more cost-effective option, it appears to be offered at a similar price point as Mexico, implying a strategy to achieve higher margins through low-cost nearshore LATAM locations
- Tends to go with higher offshoring and maintains a lean onshore presence which is sometimes considered a risk by clients
- Leverages mostly landed resources for Onshore locations to keep price competitive
