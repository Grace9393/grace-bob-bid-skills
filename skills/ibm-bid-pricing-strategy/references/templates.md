# Pricing Templates

Ready-to-use templates for commercial proposals and pricing approvals.

## Commercial Proposal Section Template

Use this template when writing the pricing/commercial section of a proposal (typically Section 5 or 6).

```markdown
## 5. Commercial Proposal

### 5.1 Pricing Summary

IBM's total price for delivering [Solution Name] is **£[X] million** over [Y] years.

| Year | Cost | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| Year 1 | £[A] | Setup, discovery, build, go-live |
| Year 2 | £[B] | Steady-state support and evolution |
| Year 3 | £[B] | Steady-state support and evolution |
| Year 4 | £[B] | Steady-state support and evolution |
| Year 5 | £[B] | Steady-state support and evolution |
| **Total** | **£[X]** | [Y]-year total contract value |

### 5.2 Pricing Model

We propose a **[Fixed Price / Time & Materials / Hybrid]** commercial model.

**Why this model:**
- [Reason 1: e.g., Provides certainty for well-defined scope]
- [Reason 2: e.g., Flexibility for evolving requirements]
- [Reason 3: e.g., Risk-sharing aligned to delivery approach]

### 5.3 Rate Justification

Our rates are competitive and represent excellent value:
- Benchmarked against [Gartner / IDC / TechMarketView]
- Blended onshore/offshore model reduces costs by [X]%
- Deep [domain/technology] expertise
- Industrialized delivery accelerators

### 5.4 Payment Terms

- **Invoicing:** [Monthly / Milestone-based]
- **Payment due:** [30 / 14] days from invoice
- **Indexation:** Fixed for [Year 1-2], CPI thereafter
- **Change control:** Per Section 6.3

### 5.5 Total Cost of Ownership

| Option | 5-Year TCO | Savings |
|--------|------------|----------|
| IBM Solution | £[X]m | Baseline |
| Status Quo | £[Y]m | £[(Y-X)]m |

### 5.6 Commercial Assumptions

**Client responsibilities:**
- [Assumption 1]
- [Assumption 2]

**Exclusions:**
- [Out of scope item 1]
- [Out of scope item 2]
```

## Pricing Approval Memo Template

```markdown
# Pricing Approval: [Opportunity Name]

**TCV:** £[X]m over [Y] years
**Margin:** [Z]%
**Recommendation:** Approve at £[X]m

## Scenarios

| Scenario | Price | Margin | Win Prob | Expected Value |
|----------|-------|--------|----------|----------------|
| Aggressive | £[X]m | 25% | 75% | £[E]m |
| Competitive | £[Y]m | 35% | 50% | £[F]m |
| Premium | £[Z]m | 45% | 25% | £[G]m |

## Rationale

[Why this pricing is appropriate]

## Competitive Intel

- Expected bidders: [List]
- Our position: [Ranking]
- Client budget: £[X]m
```
