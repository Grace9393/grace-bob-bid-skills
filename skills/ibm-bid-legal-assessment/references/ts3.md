# Technology Services 3 Framework Assessment Criteria

TS3 framework-specific legal risk assessment questions.

## Framework Overview

Technology Services 3 (TS3) is a Crown Commercial Service framework for technology products and services including hardware, software, cloud services, and associated professional services.

## Key Differences from DOS6

- **Product sales focus**: Higher proportion of product vs pure services
- **Reseller relationships**: More complex IP licensing chains
- **Hardware liability**: Physical goods warranty obligations
- **Volume/value discounts**: Complex pricing structures
- **Support/maintenance**: Post-delivery obligations extending years
- **Software licensing**: Third-party vendor relationships and escrow

**TS3 Context**: Product liability provisions in addition to service liability. Hardware typically carries higher caps due to replacement costs. Third-party software licensing indemnities flow through from vendors.

**IBM Position**: Product sales warrant separate liability treatment from services. Hardware defects capped at replacement cost. Software licensing indemnities limited to IBM's rights from licensor.

## Risk Classification - TS3 Specific

**RED FLAGS for TS3**
- Unlimited hardware warranty periods
- Software indemnity exceeding vendor coverage
- UK manufacture requirements for hardware
- Export control conflicts with IBM global supply chain
- Sub-licensing restrictions preventing resale

**AMBER for TS3**
- Hardware warranty >3 years without maintenance revenue
- Software support extending beyond vendor lifecycle
- Price protection clauses beyond 12 months
- Stock holding requirements
- Exclusive supply arrangements
