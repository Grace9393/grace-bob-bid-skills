How we will share thought leadership to improve your leadership and capabilities 
We’ll develop your leadership and capabilities with our behavioural science expertise. To start with, we will work with your leaders to establish regular monthly governance meetings and agree priorities. If helpful, our leadership development coaches will work with you on an ongoing basis, to provide coaching in the behaviours and capabilities needed for establishing and maintaining strong governance, driving a culture of continuous learning and embedding agile ways of working. This will include development in capabilities such as decision-making, collaborating with stakeholders, risk management and other key areas to improve and sustain governance capabilities at scale. 
Our work with your leaders will also ensure knowledge transfer from our team, so you can be confident of maintaining these capabilities in the long- term. Some of our thought leadership appropriate to improving your leadership and capabilities is outlined below. These will serve as our source for market trends and industry insights that we will continually feed into our learning approach. 
Our ThinkXChange event is where we showcase our latest insights on ways of working and the best technology for developing capabilities. These insights shape the development of our digital learning solutions. Your leaders can also participate in our global event to connect with other leaders and industry experts from all over the world. 
IBM’s Institute for Business Value (IBV), part of our IBM Ecosystem, provides expert analysis on the latest insights and best practices in Government and Education. This has helped us understand the growing expectation of a strong user experience, increased customer satisfaction standards and stronger technological capabilities. We can use these insights to enhance the learning content we’ll include in the SLC Digital Academy. 
Our leading HR in the age of AI programme, built in partnership with the Josh Bersin Academy, is how we use technology to build the best user experience. This will inform the development of the SLC Digital Academy and provide our talent acquisition expertise, to help you attract top talent, if you need that support.
