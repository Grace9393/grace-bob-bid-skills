IBM's Enterprise Design Thinking framework demonstrates that prototypes are fundamental to how IBM solves problems. IBM worked with the Department of Work and Pension (DWP) to develop a service allowing 90,000 government users to quickly and easily query over 100 million national insurance records. This successful project had high user satisfaction ratings of 77%, better access to key data, resulting in the DWP transitioning 90,000+ staff to the new service with 0 hours of training needed, achieved through extensive qualitative and quantitative research and usability testing throughout. The design and technical teams built many prototypes of varying fidelity to evaluate hypotheses and establish how to deliver better user, business and technical outcomes. This significantly de-risked the delivery of highly technical features, aligning the team on the problem to be solved and amassing stakeholder buy-in before a single line of code was written.
