IBM has worked safely in a secure engineering environment with the Home Office for >16 years. We provide a UK secure delivery centre with 300 staff and digital skills covering design, build, test, operate and govern. Within a secure engineering environment, we deliver IABS and Semaphore, which are Internet connected and are mission critical to border, visa and passport operations. We also work with MOD and other agencies at DV. With NCSC we delivered security architectures and procedures (including vetting, On/Off-boarding, cyber training, laptop encryption) to enhance safe working in secure environments. We have >1250 UK based staff with SC/DV.
