Using a User-Centred approach at DWP pensions the team conducted qualitative user research, ran IBM Design Thinking workshops and quantitative surveys to uncover the complex needs of different user groups in the design of services to support policy change. Using this understanding of needs, IBM designed end-to-end journeys, prototyped new services, creating iterations which were subject to regular usability feedback. The Digital Service standard was followed from Discovery through to Live, with accessible content n-line with the Style Guide (including assisted digital user standards) using Plain English and existing pattern libraries. The project successfully passed all Service Standard assessments.
