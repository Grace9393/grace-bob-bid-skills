Our recommendation for ease-of-use operation and maintenance -  We recommend that NHS Digital continues to mature its‘ product based’ operating model, as in our experience, this model best aligns business and IT strategies. A product has clearly defined interfaces, is valued in terms of its ability to enable goals to be achieved and is managed by a core,empowered team.Product teams are dedicated, cross-functionalteams of business, design, engineering and operations, with clearownership and accountability. As an example, we have recently used our product-centric ways of working to enable Social Security Scotland to deliver their first benefit to launch 6 months early, have a digital uptake of 90%,average application completion times less than 5 minutes, and a 97% positive user satisfaction score for applications.Further products have been delivered onto the platform at 6 weekly releases, directed and prioritised by Scottish Government Product Owner.
