IBM re-designed a portal for DWP's Customer Information System underpinned by a large and complex database. During discovery we uncovered needs associated with this data, to map and understand the complexities of the APIs and database before beginning design. Our Garage approach encompasses an iterative design and development of solutions approach and we worked closely with DWP's users to understand how the data is used, testing prototypes early ensuring their feasibility and value. The service was adopted successfully by over 60,000 DWP users and resulted in a 12% increase in trust in the data despite the data source not changing.
