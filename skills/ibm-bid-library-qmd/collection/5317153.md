IBM has been at the forefront of S/4HANA adoption and implementation for a large number of clients since SAP released the new business suite back in early 2015. We have already worked with a number of clients on their S/4HANA journeys.
To help businesses keep up with increasingly rapid change, and accelerate the pace of their digital transformations, IBM and SAP are collaborating to expand SAP Model Company offerings with IBM’s Industry Impact solutions. With IBM as the first SAP Partner to leverage the SAP Model Company framework, we are working together to offer the market a complementary set of capabilities helping to simplify and speed outcomes for clients that want to become AI-enabled enterprises.
The IBM/SAP partnership has been in place for more than 40 years. IBM is SAP’s largest global partner; the two organizations have a long history of joint development efforts, ranging from the development of industry and cross industry solutions, to advisory support for beta software testing.
