A key component of IBM’s value proposition for application services is delivery of improved services with contractual service levels, providing delivery excellence in key areas like quality and responsiveness. This approach injects an appropriate level of rigour into the delivery processes, enabling ongoing visibility into delivery performance against a set of agreed to service level targets, and the supporting activities for analysis and reporting to enable continuous improvement against these targets.
 
The IBM team will ensure that SZC’s AMS services are focused towards bringing in stability in the operational chain delivering across Availability, Capacity and Operational Management with a proactive and prescriptive approach. IBM’s approach is a comprehensive set of measures that can assess its performance in conjunction with SCZ's business and IT strategies. This forms the basis for a set of Key Performance Indicators (KPI) - quantitative and qualitative to measure overall performance.
 
IBM understands that Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) form the basis for continuous improvement. We will support implementation of a Service Request management process to enable continuous improvement of service quality. We will work with SZC to implement a governance model for end-to-end service level agreements and service availability.
 
Standard set of Service Level Agreements and Key Performance Indicators used to provide effective monitoring of service provision.
 
IBM will establish the framework for on-going monitoring, controlling and reporting to effectively govern the services delivered through the length of the contract. We propose to measure the service quality on industry adopted standards, measuring the degree of achievement with the pre-quantified minimum Performance level relative to each service level and KPI (Key Performance Indicators).
