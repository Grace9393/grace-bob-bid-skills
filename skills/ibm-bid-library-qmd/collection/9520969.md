IBM and Plexal, bring you access to a world leading innovation capability delivered with a 'one team’ mindset.
 
IBM is a world-leading innovator working at the forefront of many of today’s most innovative technologies (AI, Quantum). Plexal brings unique tech scouting and recruitment capabilities, and has been successfully delivering across Government for >5 years. 
We are a mature partnership, working together for >2 years. Our management and delivery processes meet all aspects of your requirement.
 
Rapid Agile Delivery
Our agile delivery method is “IBM Garage.” It complies fully with DWP’s 6-8 weeks rapid agile delivery cycle; bringing access to 100+ tools tested with 4,500+ clients. Garage enables teams to deliver value at pace, co-developing with users, effectively managing risk and leveraging our knowledge of DWP. We collaboratively identify elements of Garage that best meet your need.
 
We have 10,000+ resources to draw on to deliver a resource plan that meets your requirements. We will collaboratively scope projects, identifying value drivers and key outcomes for each innovation project, agreeing team make-up and unique skills/experience blend. Our approach ensures you’ll have the multi-disciplinary resources needed to deliver your outcomes.
 
Resources
We will assign suitably qualified and experienced resources, many of whom have previously worked on DWP programmes. We’ll ramp up/down as required to meet project demands, ensuring value for money.
 
At the outset of a project, we will construct a resource profile with role descriptions (with pre-requisite industry accreditations) that meet your requirements, interviewing jointly. We will be relentless in building joint teams that exceed expectations.
 
Use Case: DWP Innovation Lab commissioned us to explore the user need and technical feasibility of Personal Data Stores (PDS) based on a new technology (“SOLID”). We rapidly identified and engaged a multi-disciplinary team of 10 (Senior Engineer, 1 DevOps engineer, 1 data scientist, 2 Developers, 2 User Researchers and a Scrum Master, supported by IBM experts). We provided appropriate management and blend of user-research, technical/agile delivery and Technical Architecture/Engineering skills to undertake 20 user-research interviews, shape the solution, assess the technology and deliver a PoC in 8 weeks.
 
We used Jira, Confluence, Mural, MS-Teams and MS-SharePoint to collaborate with 30+ stakeholders to design, manage the delivery, share findings and engage stakeholders.
 
Managing Risks
We manage risk through co-creation and collaborative design (leveraging DWP and IBM Garage tools), tailored to DWP’s risk management policy. Risks will be documented in DWP’s RAID log for transparency and escalation.
 
Our approach goes beyond static risk-logs, creating real-time mitigations and action as risks/issues materialise. Empowering the team to remove delivery impediments complements a clear escalation route to the Innovation Lead and, if required, Steering Group. This agile approach ensures that we deliver to budget heading off resource/budget impacts.
 
Use Case: On DWP’s PDS project, while user research indicated we could prove the value of using SOLID as a PDS, there was a risk that we could not scale or drive more effective services delivery without understanding the compatibility/integration issues with DWP systems like DTH. We mitigated this risk through technical deep dives with DTH, SOLID, and IBM engineers to understand the underlying technical architecture (libraries, code, patterns). We identified the iterations required for SOLID to function effectively as an access and authorisation component. The outcome was a clearer understanding of how to implement and scale across DWP (CIS, RTI/E) and wider HMG.
 
Standards Adherence
Working with DWP for over 15+ years, we fully understand how to embed DWP quality standards (architecture/accessibility/OWASP/code quality/Data Security Standards) across projects in diverse areas, e.g. integration, CI, health and the labs.
 
Use Case: The ‘Apply for NiNo’ project, a Covid-19-related digital service delivered in 8 weeks. To meet Service Manual Standards, we held fortnightly health-checks with DWP’s digital assessment team, ran specific GDS preparation sessions every sprint, engaging user-research and design-assessors to share materials, gaining their confidence ahead of every assessment.  Since go-live, there have been 90,000+ applications, a 57% improvement in application processing, 81% user satisfaction and an anticipated £108m operational savings.
 
Value For Money
Use Case: The PDS project demonstrated that whilst there was uncertainty and concerns around security/privacy, there was evidence that PDS could unlock value for the citizen by providing personalised experiences, reduction in time to complete digital forms, operational savings and greater control over personal data.
 
IBM Garage embeds a focus on speed to value realisation. An independent Forrester report (Total Economic Impact of IBM Garage) found that clients generated 10x more innovation ideas, reduced time-to-market by 67%, and released 6x more projects into production — without increasing delivery team headcount.
