IBM – Accelerated Mobile Application Design and Development

Addressing Business Needs

Take your idea or need through the Mobile Prototype workshop as the initial step —now enhanced with generative AI brainstorming and rapid ideation tools, to transform the way your users work and interact.

A Workshop to begin your Mobile App Journey

This service jumpstarts your enterprise mobile transformation by providing an initial Mobile Prototype workshop to take your idea or need through an accelerated, AI‑augmented ideation and solutioning process.

Output from the prototype workshop will be the use cases based on the way your users work and interact. AI‑generated concepts and variations will broaden solution possibilities. These use cases are then used as the foundation to build the To-Be Scenario Journey and the initial application solution design, (using the Scenario Journey to validate the fit of the Mobile solution within your enterprise and users)

When the Journey is complete and the designs have been validated, High-Fidelity Prototypes supported by AI-assisted wireframing and layout generation can be built – these will be mock-ups of possible digital interfaces to validate design concepts and interface flows prior to actual development.

Finally the concepts and flows are used to define the MVP (Minimal Viable Product) which breaks down the long term mobile experience into the most essential near-term outcomes vital to your users and then delivering them as mobile applications using an agile development methodology at scale if required via the IBM Mobile Garage.
