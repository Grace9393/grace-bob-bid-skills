Scaled agile: SAFe is the world’s leading framework for scaling agile to the enterprise level and is used by hundreds of the largest organisations worldwide. It empowers complex organisations, like SLC, to achieve the benefits of Lean-Agile software and systems development at scale, and aligns organisational objectives by focusing on collaboration, transparency and trust among the teams and the management. For complex projects and programmes, SAFe will be the method for scaling larger projects within Garage’s co-operate phase. Our response to 6.7 (Refer to link for more information) describes how SAFe techniques will be applied to effectively manage large-scale agile deliveries. 
 
Waterfall: where SLC’s requirements are fixed, fully defined and not subject to change, waterfall offers a simplicity which results from fixed scope and clearly defined transition between project phases. Roles, phases and tasks can be defined with greater clarity and the intended outcome can be known from the outset. Whilst we see your transforming landscape better suited to agility, for certain well-defined policy or regulatory requirements, we will adopt robust waterfall methodology, tools and governance within the overarching Garage framework. 
 
Hybrid: The hybrid approach includes the best principles and elements identified in both agile and traditional methods. When greater planning accuracy is required, a defined goal is known and rigour in documentation is needed, but evolving needs and external factors must still be accommodated, a hybrid model can be used. Converging a structured planning approach whilst allowing flexibility in the content of production delivers against both the needs for a defined plan and known outcome with the flexibility of reprioritising scope during delivery.
