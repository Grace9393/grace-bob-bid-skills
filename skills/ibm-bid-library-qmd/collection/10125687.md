We have worked with the Home Office delivering secure projects for over 20 years.  The technical challenge of building consolidated datasets from disparate sources to provide coverage for risk mitigation is something we have helped many clients with across multiple sectors. This includes our work on SEMAPHORE & e-Borders where we worked with you and across airlines/airports, bringing parties together in technical conversations to build a consolidated view of real-time passenger data. We have built a unique understanding of working with over 327 carriers and UK airports authorities.  In addition, our product focussed, Enterprise Services rainbow supplier environment teams, have supported MBTP services at L2/L3 for many years.
