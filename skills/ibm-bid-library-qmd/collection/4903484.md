Previous experience
Our transition approach follows our proven method used for many transitions every year. Recently we transitioned NHS Test & Trace’s data function and ongoing development backlog to IBM. Critical to success was establishing close working relationships with existing client and incumbent provider teams. 
 
Key lessons learnt:
- Joint planning, creating shared understanding of vision, priorities and ongoing work;
- Early all-party engagement and regular communications to ensure full commitment;
- Method alignment and education before starting transition;
- Careful planning and agreement on incumbent team time commitments;
- Early connectivity testing and system access provision;
- Integrating our staff into existing agile teams for rapid immersion, ‘one-team’ culture and safe transition of ongoing work;
- Identifying key resources that present potential ‘flight risks’ and target them for early KT.
