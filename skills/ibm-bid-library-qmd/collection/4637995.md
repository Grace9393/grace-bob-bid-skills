BP, PayPal and IBM collaborated as a multi-supplier team to create a seamless experience for customers in a highly complex operational and technical environment to deliver their Retail’s global digital payment platform. We provided E2E services, covering the full product lifecycle: high-level business requirements, design, UX, UI, development, testing, support.
The BPme application is changing the way customers engage with BP through personalisation of offers and flexibility on how customers choose to pay.
Benefits/Impacts include:
•	Lean back-office operations
•	Enhanced loyalty program
•	Supply-chain optimised using QR code
•	Business analytics for BP dealers to make better decisions
