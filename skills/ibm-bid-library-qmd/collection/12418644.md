T1.1 - Understanding the Requirement 
- Our understanding of the Buyer requirements as detailed in the Call-Off Schedule 20
The Digital Prevention Services Portfolio (DPSP) and Vaccination Digital Services (VDS) are integral to the NHS’ Long Term Plan’s drive for greater prevention and self-management. To deliver and support these services, it’s crucial you have suppliers that intimately understand the context and requirements for DPSP and VDS. Our partnership of BJSS, IBM, Axiologik and Akeso (we/us henceforth) has a deep understanding of your strategic and technical requirements, and is uniquely positioned to support VDS. Our combined experience illustrates this:
- We know you and your technical requirements through delivering and supporting critical national services for DPSP (e.g. DToS, CSMS, vaccinations strategy) and wider NHSE (e.g. Spine2, NHS App, NHS.UK, Notify, CaaS, PDS), 
- We’ve delivered national digital vaccination services in Spain and for Ireland’s Health Service Executive, as well as strategic thought leadership across vaccination services, digital prevention services and healthcare data.
- In contrast to our competitors, our partnership is already proven; BJSS, IBM and Axiologik have worked together seamlessly in prevention before
- We’re also bringing forward Akeso’s deep understanding of Population Health Management data and vaccination programme delivery.

C) Risks, mitigations and/or issues associated with the technical architecture.
Drawing on our experience delivering large-scale technology programmes in the NHS, including key adjacent services for VDS (NHS.UK, NHS App, CaaS, NHS Notify, & PDS), and successfully managing transition and other risks in highly-pressurised environments, we can provide additional mitigation against risk. We have key relationships with stakeholders across the technical environment in P&P and NHSE, ensuring smooth governance and architectural sign-off. We’ve amassed lessons learnt across the technical environment that we’ll apply to VDS, minimising duplication and maximising reuse. We also bring scale/depth across the required technical skillsets, reducing the risk of skills gaps.
We have identified key risks below associated with some aspects of the architecture: 1) components built for legacy purposes, in some cases at pace, to support pandemic needs; 2) different technologies & patterns used within VDS are split across two clouds; & 3) the diverse nature of the data sources & the complexity of data flows that this causes.

D) Our understanding of your strategic direction and insights for the products
BJSS, IBM and Axiologik bring a unique understanding of the strategic and delivery implications for the products from: supporting DPSP on vaccinations strategy; delivering COVID-19 Vaccine programmes across Europe (Ireland and Spain); rainbow-working in key DPSP delivery areas for the Prevention agenda (Screening), that pioneered the model of work now scaled into Vaccines; and lastly, our broader P&P delivery (e.g. NHS App, NHS.UK, e-RS, NHS Notify et al.). We’ve critically analysed the 2023 Vaccination Strategy and the DPSP bid pack through the lens of our NHS delivery experience, thoroughly assessing both the big picture and the finer detail.   
In addition, our leadership team consists of Health and Technology leaders with deep strategic understanding of vaccines and strong relationships with key stakeholders/decision-makers across NHS England, in particular with the COO, Vaccination and Screening Directorates and the FDP team. It includes leaders like Lauren Bevan (BJSS), Angela Spatharou (IBM) and Adrian Stanbury (Axiologik), alongside experienced colleagues like Emily Cross (IBM/NHS App), Chris Sewart and Mike Dicks (BJSS/CSMS), and Simon Barcessat (IBM Screening/App), who will coach our rainbow teams, putting experience into practical delivery for Lot 1. 
Our delivery for Lot 1 needs to reflect the ‘3 NHS shifts’, from Hospital to Community, from Analogue to Digital and from Sickness to Prevention, which for DPSP translate into supporting the 2023 Vaccination Strategy priorities to: (a) improve access, expanding online services, with a ‘front door’ to vaccines easy for people to understand and use; (b) help make vaccines available in convenient locations locally (e.g. pharmacies) with targeted outreach into underserved populations; and (c) support a joined-up prevention and vaccination offer, including multiple vaccinations to families and/or prevention interventions like blood pressure checks. We need to seamlessly integrate with NHS App and NHS login to deliver a unified and accessible digital experience for all users.
We understand these issues well, as our joint team is helping lead the DPSP thinking on Personalisation, starting with Cervical Screening, and on Neighbourhood Health - both key enablers of the integrated Prevention and Vaccines strategy. We have the crucial understanding of key products and their strategies that will underpin this work. Our team coordinated with NHSE the App integration strategy, which is influencing the wider integration approach for P&P including secondary via Wayfinder and primary via GPIT. We’re also: leading the personalisation work being used in cervical screening; engaged in the Personal Health Record work, which will be key to all data transactions for P&P, and; we’re working across P&P and the FDP to move data analytics work over. All these elements will support better P&P interlock and make execution faster for DPSP. 
Ultimately, your strategy focuses on establishing scalable digital vaccination services that provide rapid access, while offering high security and seamless user experience.
