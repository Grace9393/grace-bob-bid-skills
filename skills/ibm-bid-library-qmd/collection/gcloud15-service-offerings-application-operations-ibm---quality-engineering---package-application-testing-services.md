IBM - Quality Engineering - Package Application Testing Services

Addressing Business Needs

By combining our IBM Quality Engineers with these techniques and tools, we can leverage AI algorithms to help identify the most critical tests and streamline your testing processes, ultimately resulting in higher-quality outcomes that meet exacting standards.

Testing packaged applications (SAP, Salesforce, Oracle, Workday) requires specialised knowledge and expertise to overcome significant challenges, including:

Business Transformation - Customers are revisiting their end-to-end business process landscape. This is driving the need for a solid understanding of their existing landscape, domain, SAP, peripheral technologies, cloud, and advanced features such as AI/ML, chatbots, & Analytics.

Complex Integration - Due to Clean or Lean Core: Customers are implementing S/4HANA as a lean core application with complex integrations with other specialised systems, such as Salesforce, which can create increasing test-related challenges as integration levels rise. An informed end-to- end quality viewpoint is needed.

Increased focus on speed to market -  A requirement for faster deployments, with a need for high levels of end-to-end lifecycle test automation. The legacy approach to automation, which is pure regression automation, does not serve the purpose here.

IBM can help you address your requirements for testing packaged applications with rigour and depth:

Data - Packaged applications typically handle large volumes of data, making it challenging to test them thoroughly. As a result, coverage is often inadequate, and field-mapping failures occur during data import/export. SAP and S/4HANA applications are data-intensive, requiring effective data management during testing.

Customisation - Packaged applications are highly customisable, making it challenging to create a comprehensive test plan. Combine this with the need to test a multi-cloud solution with complex business processes.

Integration - Packaged applications often integrate with other systems, such as marketing automation, financial systems, or third-party applications.

Security - Testing the security of a packaged application is essential to ensure that the data is protected from unauthorised access.

Regulatory Compliance - Many organisations use packaged applications for regulated activities and, as a result, must comply with regulations such as HIPAA or PCI DSS. Testing compliance can be complex.

Automation: Automated testing for advanced features such as LWC and Visualforce pages is challenging. In addition, automated tests must work across all test environments.

Performance testing - These applications are often cloud-based or hybrid and therefore, require thorough performance and Operational testing.

Quarterly Cycle Demands - Oracle Cloud quarterly release cycle requires frequent cycles heavily reliant on regression testing, which, if not automated, can become tedious, prone to mistakes and costly.
