1.	IBM has extensive experience collaborating with diverse stakeholders, including suppliers, clinicians, patients, and carers across NHS programs like the NHS App, Test and Trace, DTOS and others, including Trusts and ICSs. We establish a shared vision, expert governance frameworks, and crucial success metrics. On the NHS App, we operate as a rainbow team with NHSE and BJSS, prioritising role clarity and accountability while fostering inclusive communication through activities like lunch & learns, retrospectives, playbacks, and all-hands calls. Our practise disciplines empower craft leads to support their teams through knowledge shar-ing and mentorship. IBM shares expertise with NHSE leadership to create a com-prehensive governance system.
