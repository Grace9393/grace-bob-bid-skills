We will use agile delivery to manage DDaT resources, prioritising multiple demands and planning for future resource requirement. We will collaborate with DDaT stakeholders to prioritise across backlogs, assessing relative value of tasks, and will work with you to understand resources required to deliver outcomes at the pace required alongside our existing obligations. Working with you, our SME partners, and using our own flexible capacity of SC-cleared resources, we balance our collective resources across projects to deploy the right skills based on demand, scaling teams up or down to support product development needs and ensure no delays to delivery timelines.
