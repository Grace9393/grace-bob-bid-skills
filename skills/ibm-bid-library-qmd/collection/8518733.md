We’ll foster effective collaboration by: 
•	Building and sustaining effective working relationships with the ERCoE, IaaS provider, Users, and broader stakeholders/external partners through  behaviour to build honesty, transparency, and trust
•	Working as one team with the ERCoE and industry partners to keep collaboration and sharing of new ideas evergreen
•	Jointly create a Partnering Agreement enshrining collaboration culture, keeping it up-to-date and accurately reflecting necessary activities and ways of working
•	Earn the respect of the ERCoE community through our technical expertise and delivering on what we say
•	Instil a culture of initiative, innovation, flexibility, and agility in our staff through leading by example
•	Agree on shared incentives with ERCoE and IaaS providers, aligned to shared outcomes and objectives
•	Creating and executing an effective customer and partner stakeholder engagement with regular, simple feedback mechanisms (e.g., online surveys, F2F meetings, workshops) to help us identify areas we can improve as early as possible. 
•	Provide a clear escalation route through our management team and into the more comprehensive IBM if you should need it.
The above approach has been successfully implemented in our Prime Contract Role on the UK Government MSDA Contract over the last seven years.
We’ll maintain firm ERCoE and user contact to grow relationships and understand your business drivers, vision, and desired outcomes. We’ll maintain agility and responsiveness to ERCoE‘s needs through proven agile methods to deliver innovation and agility. We’ll align user teams and innovation tasking through a defined HMT prioritised backlog.
In September 2020, IBM partnered with NHS Digital to work in collaboration to build a replacement COVID-19 Test and Trace solution. Within one week, IBM mobilised 50 experienced agile professionals, further expanding the team as required. Within a month, a real-time link to test results were successfully done
