IBM's ecommerce team is dedicated to developing commerce-based solutions, we have worked with AbCam, Cardinal Health, Amerisource Bergen and Ascena on transforming their digital commerce solutions. At Amerisource Bergen we enabled digital buying across a complex commerce portfolio. IBM led a team that implemented a new commerce portal, based on SAP C/4HANA Commerce, offering a world-class experience, at scale, with the flexibility to grow over time. The customer experience significantly improved, and the new platform improved the benefits that their customers can pass on to their patients, improving their ability to find products, visibility into availability, and updates on pricing.
