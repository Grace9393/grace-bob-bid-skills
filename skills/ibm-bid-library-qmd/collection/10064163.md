Our Approach to Aligning with Existing Teams
 
Our five years of working with you means we have unrivalled experience of working alongside, and as part of, existing teams and service providers in the Programme, Agency and CDO. This experience, understanding of the delivery ecosystem and existing relationships with these teams makes us an invaluable partner to deliver your Enhancements, Notifications and Live Running (ENLR) requirements.
Our success centres on a transparent, inclusive, and collaborative approach to working with multiple teams and service providers across the Programme and Agency. Our alignment with existing teams is summarised by four categories – Core Team, Embedded, Collaborative and Informed - to manage dependencies, resolve risks and issues, and deliver quality services to the Agency.

 
During the mobilisation period we will work with you to refine these categories to develop a stakeholder map.

Embedded 
Partnership with the Agency is key; to understand their pain-points, co-design solutions and deliver genuine value. Establishing a regular cadence for feedback from Agency Operations and CDO, allows us to prioritise enhancements which best improve the services. We will review scope iteratively and forecast the benefit to the Agency of each iteration, ensuring we deliver with speed, scale and security right from the start. Our Live Running model enables a smooth handover of services to Agency, including them within our teams to gain the skills needed to independently maintain the services.
We know that policy and legislation may change throughout delivery, our approach accommodates that by including or closely aligning policy colleagues in Alpha and Notifications teams. Our Beta teams’ partner with Release Test and SIT, including them in all Agile ceremonies to ensure feature development is transparent. 
 

 
Collaborative 
Every service fits into the wider ecosystem. Collaboration with existing delivery teams enables the management of programme-wide dependencies and the early identification, and resolution of risks and issues.
 
Our team will support collaboration across SSP, Agency and CDO through the following channels and practices:

Our architects create technical designs and make key architectural decisions in collaboration with the SDT and support reviews. 

Utilising PI Planning sessions enables ELNR teams to align with the goals and deliveries across the programme and manage cross-team risks and dependencies. Pre-release workshops with Security, Release Group and DevOps will ensure all parties can plan effectively for ITHC, D1 assessments and go-live.
We will continue leveraging our relationship with IBM teams working with DWP, allowing for quick resolution of issues to CIS APIs.
 
Informed 
We will provide regular touchpoints with Agency, CDO and Live Running stakeholders through showcases and documentation to inform of new features and issues being resolved in upcoming releases. We will bring ideas and suggestions on how to improve services, as we draw on our network of Social Security systems worldwide, and knowledge of similar industries and relationships with leading experts in Social Security. 
Additionally, these channels will provide other product areas, RSDG and Delivery Train the opportunity to stay informed of upcoming solution work and feature development. This will build on the existing library of documentation and videos we’ve delivered previously. 

 
Managing Programme-wide Dependencies
 
We will use Programme Increment (PI) Planning to plan-ahead, in-line with programme objectives. To support this, we will work with the POs and Agency to determine priorities aligned to product goals and Agency needs. We will identify internal and external dependencies, managing them with the appropriate teams and tracking them through Jira, ensuring clear traceability of dependencies. This aligns with the programme and delivery train process for dependencies.
We maintain a central dependencies log on Confluence, reviewed with the PO and key stakeholders weekly. We will also support the identification of dependencies with scope items raised through Product Sync.
Our Delivery Manager (DM) will work closely with other DMs and suppliers across the programme to ensure awareness of our dependencies. They have first-hand experience of complex systems delivery and understand the importance of working jointly to deliver an integrated solution.
Our DM will escalate dependencies at risk to the Service Manager to ensure a timely resolution. 
We know from experience that sometimes, missed dependencies are unavoidable. Our Alpha teams will mitigate this by working with the PO to maintain a healthy product backlog. That way, if a dependency is missed, we can identify alternative items to deliver. 
 
Resolving Risks and Issues
 
Scrum Masters will maintain a RAID-log on Confluence, reviewed with the Product Owner and other key stakeholders every sprint. Where relevant, we will escalate risks and issues through Scrum of Scrums.
We will provide weekly progress updates, raise risks, issues and programme-wide dependencies during the Service-Management meeting, alongside mitigating actions and potential solutions.
When necessary, we will work with you to identify which risks and issues need to be raised at a Programme-level to ensure they are treated with the appropriate priority and severity. 
We will produce a monthly MI report highlighting the critical risks and issues and will work collaboratively to resolve these as a team. 

 
Over the last 5 years we have consistently demonstrated our commitment to working collaboratively with you, your partners, and suppliers. Together, we have established common ways of working with our programme, agency and CDO colleagues and, in doing so, we are fully engaged with product and service delivery teams. We look forward to our continuing partnership.
