At HMRC, IBM is providing Business Analysts to the new Customs Declaration Programme. That programme is delivering a new solution for management of all of the UK imports and exports post-Brexit. The delivery is following the SAFe framework, with stories designed and planned into Increments. IBM Business Analysts work through complex policy and user needs, to create implementable business rules to manage the flow and validation of trade. The BA's work with many stakeholders, internal and external, to feed the Agile delivery with granular acceptance criteria. IBM have capacity to supply BA's experienced in Agile delivery immediately.
