Our delivery model is designed to meet the unique requirements of public sector contracts and frameworks. We have built a successful ecosystem of sourcing channels to use best-in-class skills and capabilities that we will bring to this framework, utilising our pool of 4000+ security cleared resources, and a diverse supply chain of >200 SME partnerships which we can draw on if required.
 
At the heart of this model lies a dedication to cultivating talent across every level of expertise and geographical location. By using our breadth and depth of experience, we can source and deploy the right resources at the right time, ensuring successful execution of projects within this Framework.
 
Our model encompasses a proven approach to forecasting and resource allocation, centred around collaboration and transparency. We will appoint a dedicated BlueLight Contract Manager to work with BlueLight and interested Contracting Authorities to forecast expected Statement of Work (SoW) requirements, accounting for skill types, experience levels, and quantities. We are using this model as part of Home Office (HO) Central Contracts: 
 
In 2022 IBM was appointed as the prime supplier to provide highly skilled resources
across three frameworks: Security Architecture, Data Architecture and Multi-Disciplinary. The dedicated IBM Contract Director works closely with senior HO stakeholders to understand, plan and forecast capacity and demand. Once demand has been identified, reviewing HO’s forecast at regular interlocks, they engage with our HO dedicated resource management team, internal Capacity and Workforce Management teams, Practice Leads and Recruitment teams to ensure requirements are fully understood and met. Since 2022, we have successfully delivered 80+ SOWs and deployed 185 resources. 
 
Our Contract Manager, in collaboration with our dedicated HO and Policing Resource Management team, will input demand into our internal "Professional Marketplace" platform, matching demand with supply. Resources in the pool will be pre-cleared to facilitate rapid deployment. If forecasted demand exceeds the size of that pool, we will expand by bringing new resources from our wider ecosystem of suppliers or take other corrective actions such as upskilling and recruitment. We have proven the success with this approach for UK HO Shared-Application-Services, where, after the contract was signed, we mobilised a 30 strong SC cleared team to meet project needs within 10 working days.
 
Throughout the contract, we will maintain transparent lines of communication with ecosystem resources, providing regular updates on project status, constructive feedback on performance, and opportunities for career progression.
 
In each engagement, we create a culture of continuous learning and development that extends beyond our internal teams. We recognise the importance of integrating and upskilling police officers and staff into service delivery and have structured our delivery model with this ambition in mind. From day one, we will collaborate with Contracting Authorities to train and integrate the right blend of resources, thereby contributing to the broader objective of enhancing the Authority’s digital transformation capabilities should they wish to develop their own in-house RPA Centres of Excellence.
 
Challenges, Risks and Mitigations
 
Talent shortages and fluctuating market demand for senior skill levels (SFIA 5 to 7)
 
Solution: We have developed an innovative approach through our UK Client Innovation Centres (CICs), who serve as development hub for early career professionals, equipping them with the necessary skills and knowledge through targeted training and mentoring. By investing in the cultivation of early professional talent, we aim to bridge the gap between supply and demand for senior resources, ensuring a steady stream of skilled professionals to meet contract requirements.  We have used our CIC’s extensively over the past 5 years to train resources in RPA development, obtain NPPV3 clearance and deploy them to Police RPA projects and we will expand this approach to support the demands of the Bluelight RPA framework. 
 
In addition to our internal resources, we can leverage our extensive ecosystem of subcontractors and suppliers such as Grayce, the Software Institute, Sandersons and Experis.  As our work on this framework increases, these relationships will become important to our success, and we have developed robust subcontractor management processes. Upon contract commencement, we select subcontractors, aligning them with our governance framework under the contract. Regular performance reviews ensure that our suppliers meet our high standards of social responsibility, privacy, and security.
 
 
Difficulties in sourcing resources across a wide geographical area
 
Solution: IBM employs a flexible workforce model, enabling us to assign resources from multiple UK locations including our List X accredited sites in Hursley and a secure UK HO dedicated area at our Leicester office. Furthermore, our CICs contribute significantly to this effort by recruiting and developing local talent in deprived regions. This dual approach not only broadens our talent pool but also supports regional economic development by introducing higher-paid job opportunities.
 
Addressing potential supply chain issues
 
A priority in our robust risk management framework, pertains to delays in securing security clearance for consultants, which could impede our capacity to mobilise teams swiftly. To mitigate this, we have implemented an incubator model. The Incubator is our investment in you, bringing additional value to our proposition. We identify candidates awaiting deployment and align them to the incubator whilst they progress through security clearance. They undergo focussed training in the technologies needed for future projects as well as detailed knowledge transfer and immersion in your way of working. Operating as an agile scrum team, embracing modern delivery techniques (API Development, DevOps, Cloud Native) our team will work on business problems that you may have struggled to tackle alongside the pressures of delivering critical services.
 
To further enhance supply chain resilience, we adhere to stringent IBM Global Procurement guidelines. These guidelines ensure that our suppliers comply with the Responsible Business Alliance Code of Conduct, maintain business continuity plans, and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures for data protection and cybersecurity. Furthermore, as a signatory to the UK Government Prompt Payment code, IBM demonstrates its commitment to maintaining healthy relationships with suppliers
 
Ensuring we have sufficient candidates across geographical locations
Our model utilises multiple talent pools, across multiple locations. Many of our practitioners work remotely but we are flexible on UK travel and work with government Departments in many geographically diverse locations of the UK. Some examples include Southend, London, Glasgow, Edinburgh, Newcastle, Manchester, Leeds, Somerset, Norwich, Croydon and Swansea.
- Utilising Communities of Practice: Our Data, Technology, and Transformation Service Line represent a rich pool of experienced UK-based RPA specialists. We actively engage with this community to identify and consider internal candidates for these specialist roles, ensuring growth to meet the needs and demand of Forces.
- CICs: Focussed on creating around 100 new STEM-based jobs each year, from apprenticeships to senior roles, stimulating workforce opportunities in deprived areas and bringing higher paid jobs to the regions. Our CICs provide a steady supply of current and emerging RPA talent. Through targeted training and mentoring programs, we can develop capability to ensure we have coverage across multiple roles and technologies for current and future demand.
- Ecosystem of Partners: Our ecosystem of partners, including over 200 SMEs, encompasses a wide range of specialised RPA skills. By leveraging these partnerships, we can augment our internal capabilities, accessing the exact expertise needed to fulfil the specialised roles as per the contract's Statement of Requirements.
- External Recruitment: Recognising that on occasion specialist roles may not be fully met by our internal or partner resources. We will complement our internal and partner resources, through targeted external recruitment campaigns to source the necessary specialists.
 
Contingency and Risk Management
 
We will be transparent with you; if there are risks or barriers to delivery, we will identify the risk and find alternative approaches. When a SOW is requested, we will share the same understanding and expectations and be confident it can be delivered. 
 
We have a strong track record of mobilising resources quickly, and if we commit a plan, we will achieve it. We will be operating as part of a wider team of public sector security cleared individuals and a dedicated resource management team. We will not wait for the SoW to be signed to start identifying resources, both internally and through our supply chain, and we will request Contracting Authority equipment and access as soon as we know that they are required. 
 
In the unlikely event we are unable to meet a required request (such as capacity constraints):
- We will immediately raise this with the Contracting Authority 
- We will document and share proposed mitigations along with the impact of these (e.g. time to source).
- We will propose mitigation plans where possible (as we have on other Police RPA projects, such as accelerating Discovery work whilst waiting for Developer resource to become available.
 
IBM's delivery model for resource sourcing is characterised by its adaptability, resilience, and commitment to nurturing talent across the entire spectrum of skill levels and geographical locations. By leveraging our extensive partnerships, fostering innovation through our CICs, mitigating supply chain risks, and maintaining transparent communication with candidates, we are well-positioned to address the unique requirements of the Blue Light RPA framework.
