Answer:

We understand the workplace can significantly impact a person's health and wellbeing, both positively and negatively, depending on various factors such as work environment, job demands, work-life balance, and social support. A positive work environment that promotes well-being, respect, and job satisfaction can lead to improved physical and mental health, increased productivity, and better overall quality of life. On the other hand, a negative work environment characterised by stress, long working hours, lack of autonomy, and poor management can lead to increased absenteeism, burnout, and a decline in wellbeing. Therefore, it is crucial for organisations, such as ours, to prioritise the wellbeing of all employees and create a supportive work environment that fosters health, happiness, and productivity. Promoting the wellbeing and development of staff is one of our long-standing commitments, supported by a number of programmes/measures in which we will encourage Home Office participation throughout the life of this contract. 
 
Method Statement: 
We tailor our programmes to reflect the latest guidelines in this area. The following table demonstrates examples of the actions we will roll out, based on our existing work with the Home Office, supporting the Mental Health at Work Core and Enhanced Standards to improve health and wellbeing on this contract: 
 
Mental Health at Work Standards IBM’s Commitment/Actions                       Prioritise mental health in the workplace by developing and delivering a systematic programme of activity. 
(Enhanced Standard  
Demonstrate accountability by nominating a health and wellbeing lead at Board or Senior Leadership level, with clear reporting duties and responsibilities) (MAC 7.1) 
We continue to demonstrate accountability in this important area by appointing Health and Wellbeing representatives at our Senior Leadership Level within our organisation. These representatives drive a programme of positive change within our organisation. 
At a contract level, we will: 
appoint a ‘People Champion’ whose responsibility is to lead and develop a programme of activities to encourage a focus on mental health and wellbeing for the duration of this contract. These activities will be determined by the team to ensure they have a positive impact and active participation. Activity suggestions will be taken quarterly by the people champion. 
provide access to and signpost existing resources and activities for IBM and Home Office staff. For example, we have an extremely active UKI Maternity/Paternity community who provide webinars and ongoing mentoring and support for those about to depart for/currently on leave/returners (voted in the Top 10 Best Companies for 34th year by Working Mothers). 
Proactively ensure work design and organisational culture drive positive mental health outcomes (MAC 7.1) Our People champion will: 
continuously create opportunities for employees to feedback through all phases of the engagement to prevent poor work culture and conditions.  
foster an environment to support personal and professional growth, offering mentoring opportunities for all team members across both IBM and Home Office employees. 
encourage our team to find a healthy work-life balance through embracing flexible working (part time, job share, hybrid) to improve job-satisfaction. 
roll out our annual Public-Sector wide Inclusivity and Wellbeing Survey, which we use to engage with our workforce and identify the most important issues to address. 
Create a project charter as part of transition, highlighting the team’s culture, values and agreed ways of working, which will be reviewed as the programme evolves.   
Promote an open culture around mental health 
(Enhanced Standard  
Improve the disclosure process to encourage openness during recruitment, ensuring employees are aware of why information is required).(MAC 7.1 and 7.2) 
We will promote a culture where our team feel empowered to speak openly about their mental health through: 
access to our Mental Health First Aiders (MHFAs)  Network. We have 7 dedicated MHFAs on the Home Office account alongside a wider group of 139 UKI IBM Consultants now trained as MHFAs, providing colleagues with a broad network of support.  
an established training programme for all employees to become Mental Health First Aiders (MHFAs).  
encouraging self-identification of any mental health and or wellbeing issues during the recruitment process and throughout the contract to enable the right support and adjustments to be provided.   
providing specific Home Office account Mental Health training to our delivery teams.  
access to our specific Home Office microsite that signposts team members to resources supporting mental health and wellbeing. 
providing areas of quiet space for reflection, available across our 3 key offices for this contract (Leicester, London & Hursley). 
Increase organisational confidence and capability (MAC 7.2) We will ensure all employees working on this contract are provided with access to up-to-date mental health and wellbeing training e.g. 
our Be Equal Campaign (https://www.ibm.com/impact/be-equal/) launched in 2019 and has tens of thousands of employees undertaking an extensive training to be recognised as Be Equal Ambassadors, including personal pledges. 
our Mental Health Ally Badge program, designed to help employees become mental health allies. Covering basic information about mental health and teaching how to recognise and address stigma, and how to connect peers in need of support with an appropriate service or resource 
Provide mental health tools and support 
(Enhanced Standard  
Ensure provision of tailored in-house mental health support and signposting to clinical help) (MAC 7.1) 
We will: 
provide access to occupational health and mental health support through professional services and trained volunteer MHFA’s e.g. via our 24x7 Employee Assistance Programme (EAP), offering confidential telephone or face to face counselling and access to comprehensive online resources. The list of MHFA’s is readily available and you have the option to speak confidentially to individuals you know and work with or to someone who doesn’t know you day to day.  
provide access to Health and Wellbeing awareness webinars with Specialist Consultants on topics such as Resilience, Menopause, Cancer, Nutrition, Male and Female Health, and we will also run voluntary initiatives to support physical fitness in ILEAP delivery team (e.g. Virgin Pulse GO Challenge, which aims to build resilience and improve health and fitness). 
Increase transparency and accountability through internal and external reporting 
(Enhanced Standard 
To Include a leadership commitment and outline of the organisation’s progress on mental health). (MAC 7.1) 
Through contract delivery we will monitor wellbeing through: 
fortnightly wellbeing temperature checks within our delivery team, providing the opportunity for identification of wellbeing concerns that can be immediately addressed within the programme.  
we will jointly develop and publish a Project Charter to agree commitments the team will make with regards to behaviours and events/learning activities surrounding wellbeing.  
as an organisation we encourage self-identification of diverse abilities or disabilities, so we can offer adaptations for colleagues with visible/ non-visible/ neuro disabilities. We publicly publish our commitments and progress in our annual Environmental, Social and Governance report (IBM 2022 ESG Report).   
our People Champion will be responsible for collating and reporting feedback/insights to the Home Office team and IBM to further strengthen our strategy and approach. 
 
In addition to the 6 Standards, we will: 
Commit to inclusive and accessible recruitment practices - we will utilise IBM’s open and accessible recruitment processes making it clear to candidates that adjustments can be requested and made. We will leverage IBM’s focus on skill-based hiring over academic degrees – opening pathways for all regardless of background, disability, or education level; creating an inclusive and accessible recruitment practice; bringing an increasingly diverse workforce to I-LEAP. 
Engage in retention activities - we will implement strategies and incentives to encourage retention; role rotation within I-LEAP to offer growth opportunities, promotion, review of compensation such as retention package, and tailored training and development. 
Continuously look at ways in which we can improve conditions and reduce societal harm within our organisations. We are committed to regularly reviewing our workforce policies and guidance. A recent example of this is our introduction of new guidance to managers and employees on Domestic Abuse in the workplace – a policy area we know is of high focus within the Home Office. To support this we have: 
launched a new dedicated space on our internal microsite to raise awareness and signpost domestic abuse support and resources for all employees, 
produced guidance and scheduled educational webinars for Managers on how to spot the signs of domestic abuse, actions Managers should take, and highlight the help and support available within IBM,  
Publicised our new support offerings and resources through our recent World Mental Health Day Communications,  
updated our HR chatbot with the guidance, often the first port of call for managers and employees when an issue arises.  
Measure team engagement - we will run regular quarterly surveys and events to promote engagement and identify actions to help us improve overall satisfaction of the team; and 
Introduce a process to implement improvements – we will assign an accountable owner for all improvements within the team ensuring they are completed and address any previous issues. Improvements will be clearly articulated to the wider I-LEAP team and appropriate actions/timelines will be approved by all parties.
