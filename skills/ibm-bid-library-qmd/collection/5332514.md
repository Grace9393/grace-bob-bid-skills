IBM have identified the following commercial risks associated with this proposal and offer the following mitigations to reduce / eliminate the impact should any of these risks be realised. We can confirm that our pricing offer is protected against these anticipated risks. 
Risk: The impending EU Exit may result in currency fluctuations and/ or increased tariffs for incoming goods and services.
Mitigation: Our offering is based primarily on UK based solutions and services and as such will not be subject to the impact of UK’s EU Exit. Core personnel servicing the solution will be UK based as will the core hosting solutions. 
Risk: Novation of licences to NHSD may result in unexpected legal and licensing costs Mitigation: We propose that NHSD contract directly with both Salesforce and AWS for the relevant licences detailed in our proposal. This will allow for NHSD to realise no pass- through charges and also to retain the underlying platform with no legal transactions in the event of an exit event or the completion of the call off agreement. On discussion with our partners we also believe this approach will offer the largest opportunity for discounts to NHSD 
Risk: The uncertainty associated with COVID-19 results in the volumes for the proposed system being under / over-estimated resulting in ‘regret’ charges for NHSD.
Mitigation: We are proposing a solution with a minimal floor regarding fixed charges and a flexible per user / per licence model to allow for a true consumption-based charging regime. This model affords NHSD the flexibility to rapidly scale up or down the service with a consistent view of the cost. Salesforce will offer NHSD a custom licensing model that has been built to support contact tracing requirements with the ability to flex demand through the first year of the contract and in line with the demand profiles guided by the NHS. Should additional user or product licenses be required these can be added during the lifetime of the contract. Any reductions in demand can be addressed at the contract end point (i.e. point of contract renewal) and may be subject to re-pricing based upon the volumes committed going forward from that point. We understand through our partnership with AWS that NHSD would prefer to contract directly for the AWS element through existing commercial models. AWS have confirmed that a direct procurement approach will offer the best price and terms. IBM therefore suggest that route is used and have assumed this will be the case. We have used in our commercial model pricing that is available to all (and published at https://aws.amazon.com/connect/pricing/) and are happy to look at alternative arrangements with IBM if preferred. 
Risk: Unexpected charges enforced by the incumbent provider of the legacy system as part of the transition to the new system (e.g. application changes, data cleansing, knowledge transfer).
Mitigation: We are proposing a fresh start / greenfield implementation of the new system. This will eliminate the need for existing providers to perform any activities to ready the legacy system for data migration or transition activities. This will also allow for a clean data set to be created from service commencement of the new system.
