On the large Home Office (HO) Law Enforcement (NLEDP) Agile Programme, IBM leads value streams comprising blended teams of 5 other major delivery partners alongside HO to deliver all aspects of the solution. Each stream is multi-discipline and multi-supplier, comprising technical and business architecture, design, build and testing. IBM runs the quarterly Programme Increment (PI) planning event using the Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe), where cross-stream dependencies and joint testing plans are identified and agreed. Throughout the subsequent PI, IBM then coordinate the planned activities from all partners and the HO to ensure alignment into testing and a successful, joined-up delivery.
