As a second example, the HMRC ‘securing our technical future’ (SoTF) programme is a mission critical initiative to transform the technology which underpins tax payments. We were appointed as Delivery Lead taking ownership and accountability for the programme, working across multiple suppliers. We led the re-planning, acceleration and on-time delivery of 85 business services from SAP to Azure. We introduced a one-team approach across the programme, centred on deep collaboration across all stakeholders through multi-supplier planning workshops, joint RAID reviews, a single integrated plan and collaborative Agile ceremonies, including daily stand ups. This successful migration stabilised and improved services, saving HMRC c.£18m in extended support/project costs and ensured all suppliers successfully met their contractual commitments.
