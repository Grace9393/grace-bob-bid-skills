We also coach our client teams, for example at Department of Work and Pensions (DWP) we coached staff on the Customer Information System (CIS) programme to build skills in agile techniques by joint agile teams and pairing our resources with DWP counterparts, so that there was continual knowledge sharing and upskilling. This was very  successful and we were delighted to receive feedback that "IBM have shown genuine care about leaving a legacy with regards to a digital delivery capability within CIS". We are working in this way now with Scottish Government (refer our Case Study) and also run a similar agile capability development programme for BT.
