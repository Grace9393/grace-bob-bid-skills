Case Study: NCSC - Working as a strategic delivery partner for the NCSC IBM have adopted an agile and iterative DevOps approach to capability development establishing a 2 weekly rhythm of delivery of capability ready for deployment to end users and enabling a fail fast and rapid response culture to respond to user needs quickly.  This approach requires the development team to remain closely connected with the end users and provides a level of inimacy which allows us to truly understand our client smission and the pressures involved with delivering it.  By gradually maturing a capability in this way we ensure that not only do we deliver business value, we incrementally address the security, policy and supportability requirements to ensure that each are addressed in proportion to the maturity of the capability and are built up alongside the functional capability and not left to the end.
