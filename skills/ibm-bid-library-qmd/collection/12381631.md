We take pride in delivering high-quality solutions that meet our clients' expectations. Our quality assurance (QA) & quality management (QM) processes are designed to ensure that our deliverables meet the highest standards of quality, reliability, and performance. They are built on industry-recognised best practice. 
Our QM system aligns and is certified to the ISO 9001:2015 standard, and one of the first global IT organisations to be appraised at CMMI v2.0 Level 5, ensuring we follow structured processes in managing quality. It is used to drive excellence for our clients globally and is based around seven principles providing a consistent and robust approach to QM.  
Deliverables - Documentation:  Our deliverables are rigorous, where outputs, findings and recommendations are thoroughly documented to ensure clarity, traceability, and future reference. 
Deliverables - Reviews: our regular reviews, many with our clients, ensure that our deliverables are of high quality, are maintainable, and follow industry practices. Our final reviews ensure all objectives have been met and deliverables are complete and accurate.  
IBM Quality Partners, independent of the team, ensure we adhere to our client’s conditions of satisfaction, whose focus is on Quality and Risk, and provide guidance and support to the team 
Monitoring Quality Throughout the Project 
At the beginning, we define and agree with you, the quality measures we are going to use for the project, and establish them in our QM system, along with a detailed project plan with the objectives, scope, methodology, timeline, and resources: This roadmap provides the benchmark for quality assessment. 
 
During the project, we monitor the quality of our work using various metrics and tools, including: 
Milestones and Checkpoints: We divide projects into manageable phases and at each checkpoint, the work is reviewed to ensure alignment to the project plan, requirements, and quality standards. 
Risk Management: we identify potential risks early in the project and develop mitigation strategies. Regular monitoring ensures risks are effectively managed, preventing impact to project quality. 
Project metrics: We track project metrics (in tools like Jira), such as velocity, burn-down rates, and cumulative flow diagrams, to ensure that projects are on track and that we are delivering value. 
Customer satisfaction: We conduct regular surveys through the Qualtrics toolset and hold feedback sessions with you to ensure that we are meeting your expectations. 
At the end of the project, we undertake a formal Post-Project Review (with you where you support this) and share the feedback with you to assess what went well and identify areas for improvement. We use this feedback loop (via tools like Confluence) to enhance the quality of our future engagements.  
 
Our quality control measures have led to superior outcomes in similar Operational Support projects:  
 
IBM demonstrated quality-assured Discovery phases at Scottish Government - Change and Transformation for the first Scottish Social Security benefit products. We engaged 1400+ citizens in user-research through 483 sessions across 40 locations. We iterated 8 new high-quality benefit services (from Carer’s Allowance through to Young Carer's Grant), changing the benefit experience for 100,000 households and averaging over 97% customer satisfaction. Quality measures for each activity gave assurance of successful outcomes. We defined the overall objectives, broken down to sprint level criteria, to allow assessment of outputs in each sprint. A checkpoint at every sprint assessed progress against the Discovery plan, measuring against original criteria. We also assessed Discovery outputs with an external panel from Gartner, and this project was UK MCA Finalist in 2022 (having won in 2020). 
 
With our Agile approach to deliver a project for NESO to improve their dispatch optimisation (sub-lots 2.3, 2.4), we used daily stand-ups to closely monitor progress, regular reviews of deliverables by the integrated IBM / NESO team. This ensured the deliverables met quality expectations. Retrospectives at the end of each sprint, brought learnings from previous sprints, improved working practices, ensuring the level of quality in our deliverables was so good that the client was prepared to publish them - https://smarter.energynetworks.org/projects/nia2_ngeso044/.
