We understand the criticality of BESS and the importance of providing a resilient, robust service that mitigates any potential risk of failure. Consequently, our approach to co-delivering the service is based on templated approaches with a track record of success across our work adopting, enhancing and managing mission-critical services within public sector clients, including the Home Office. Our goal is to provide reliable, high quality services whilst empowering your stakeholders to drive direction of enhancements through our multi-disciplinary team of specialists. To provide a robust service we will also leverage our existing teams/experts within the HO to eliminate single points of failure, centrally manage knowledge/ high-quality documentation, and take advantage of our cross-organisation experience.
 
Resource structure
We propose to form a flexible scrum-team to manage maintenance/enhancement of BESS, working closely with Stakeholders, Product-Owners and delivery teams to balance strategic objectives in-line with the dynamic needs of your operating environment. This model allows for adaptive, cost-effective management of your estate, which can be pivoted in response to changing priorities. Additionally, our approach integrates this team into our wider HO L2/L3 delivery capability that we provide to you, ensuring a frictionless transition and unified approach.
 

 
Your sprint team will be led by an experienced Service Manager, aligning your Product Manager and BESS stakeholders/teams to a unified product backlog, with prioritised features and technical debt driving enhancements/maintenance activities. We previously developed this approach in collaboration with the Home Office and will seamlessly adapt for BESS. Our service manager will coordinate work and report progress per-sprint. This approach provides a focussed, agile delivery aligned to your strategic objectives, whilst empowering you to be flexible and pivot priorities in response to evolving objectives. 
 
Our pricing template reflects this approach, including the following assumptions:
- We include the integration of a junior civil-service role within team, whom we will mentor/develop. We suggest this is a HO SFAI3 Developer
- Effort estimates include 20 L3 Support-tickets, 240 hours of enhancement work and one out-of-hours callout a month.
 
Delivery approach
Our delivery methodology has seen success across agile service-delivery engagements within the UK Public sector, including our work providing L2/3 support to the HO Enterprise Services on a number of platforms. Carefully managed CI/CD pipelines and toolchains provide predictability for product releases and drives a stable, continuously-improving service. The 'definition of done' for new work will incorporate adherence with DevSecOps/SRE best practice, including automated testing, deployment and DR activities to mitigate release risks.
 
IBM provide support to the HO Enterprise Services across a range of applications, services and technology. This breadth/complexity necessitates a comprehensive continuous-delivery approach to assure service continuity. We developed a number of CI/CD pipelines to enable previously periodically-deployed applications to now being deployed multiple times a day, without impacting the end users or service.
 
Our Talent Pool
 

 
IBM maintains a pool of 4300 SC-Cleared resources highly-qualified across the skills required for BESS and plan to align the BESS delivery team within our wider Service Delivery capability already within the HO. By taking this approach, we offer a leading service with deep industry/technical expertise, without points of failure benefiting from economies-of-scale through our existing partnership with you.  We understand that the quality and reliability of the service is dependent on having the right skills at your disposal, and this skill profile may change over BESS's lifecycle. Therefore our staff undertake continuous learning/training to stay up-to-date with the skills important to you. Through our work with the HO we are aware and supportive of the aspiration to integrate and up-skill more civil servants into Service Delivery. We are committed to this ambition and have built our delivery model assuming that from day 1 we will work with civil servants to deliver this requirement, training/integrating the right blend to help you achieve this ambition.
 
Engagement Model
Engaging across the portfolio with Product delivery teams and stakeholders will be key to delivering the BESS Service. Our approach is to build a culture of frequent, open and transparent communications, with equal representation across governance forums and service delivery checkpoints. The frequency and format of communications will be continuously reviewed with you to ensure relevance/appropriateness. This framework is critical to mitigating cross-team risks, managing stakeholder expectations and collaboratively delivering a best-in-class service.
 
At DWP we developed a strong partnership with senior stakeholders across departments, influencing and co-creating strategy and management of their long-term vision. We led and delivered the initiative, achieving key outcomes of Major Incidents down by 75%, lost user hours down by 550k, and winning 'Highly-Commended' at the 2020 MCA Awards.
