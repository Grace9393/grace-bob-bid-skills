Introduction
We will work with you to make the testing assurance and management function for Matcher a success.   We recognise the critical role that test has in the overall Matcher programme, with many of the critical path activities dependent upon the testing being carried out, assured, and managed in a professional and reliable way.  
 
Proactive actions, in line with your stated requirements, are key to driving improvements and assuring that testing activities are successful.  These include undertaking smoke testing ahead of a test stage, Live Proving preparation ahead of actual Live Proving and importantly meeting the deadlines you have set for receiving information.  Professional administration and governance are key enablers to a successful test function.   We confirm that we will meet all your requirements relating to Test Assurance and Management (Section 7.2 of Schedule 2.1)
 
We want to work as partners, this includes giving you the access and information when you need it.   We welcome you to engage with us during testing activities, we will share outputs (such as test results / completion reports within 24 hours) and include your input and guidance into test planning and assurance.   All the testing artefacts will be kept current, and available to you, and will be completed in line with the Test Strategy, Assurance Plan and your schedules.   
 
Environments will be managed, audited and available for testing activities in line with the Testing Plans for each stage.   Where you are responsible for a testing activity, we will be there to support you, ensuring environments are available at the right baseline.  We will work with you to make the scheduling process simple and effective for attending Witness testing.
 
By giving you access to Jira and Bookstack, both securely hosted by IBM, you will be able to see the progress of defects and testing activities, plus the testing reports and dashboards when you need them, this is on top of the formal artefacts we will deliver to you.
 
We have worked this way with you before during our partnership for IABS and will bring the same approach and processes throughout the System Development Life Cycle.   Our Test Manager, will be accountable for Test Assurance and Management, ensuring that all artefacts meet the quality gates required.  You will also have a clear escalation process.

Measuring effectiveness
In our response to 7.1 Test Governance, we have identified a number of KPIs across 7 areas of test that we propose to use to measure the service.   Once agreed with you, as part of the Test Strategy, we will begin to measure and track these KPIs on a regular basis.  Each metric will be measured, and a suitable RAG (red, amber, green) threshold agreed for each so that a simple dashboard can be provided along with the formal measurements.   Over time we will measure the trends of each metric and report findings to you to work out if there are areas of concern or worthy of bespoke action.   These measurements will be across us, 3rd parties and testing activities that you are responsible for – giving us clear information to measure the effectiveness across the Matcher programme.
 
We will create an improvements roadmap and agree with you which items should be prioritised and when best to implement them – this will be closely managed and integrated with the overall programme plan.  We will measure the overall improvements of the testing KPIs and map this against the changes being implemented to validate their success.   We propose to formally assess our testing maturity on a regular basis (yearly) using TMMI or IBM MAT tool which will also be tracked, across the Matcher Programme (including 3rd parties) to demonstrate improvements.
 
Implementing testing processes
Our testing method, will be used to manage all aspects of testing, including that of 3rd parties and the authority test assurance activities.    We will manage and carry out activities you would expect from a testing team to improve effectiveness and improvements, which include:
- Static testing (peer, spot / desk checks, walkthroughs, 1-2-1 reviews 
- Joint planning (including Live Proving, Witness Testing, MES and MSB integration)
- Testing early (including smoke testing and pretesting preparation)
- Risk based testing, test design and shift left approaches
- Quality Gates
- Introduction of automation and re-use – especially relevant given the repetition of activities for the later stages of Matcher.
- Professional documentation – including test reports, requirements traceability, plans, standards and testing artefacts.
