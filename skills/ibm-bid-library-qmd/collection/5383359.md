How the OC will be enhanced to resolve other SLC challenges 
The SLC Operations Centre won’t just replace the reach and focus of your existing NOC, it will be designed as a strategic capability to manage your end-to-end technology estate. It will evolve from a reactive service that responds to outages and incidents, to a proactive one that actively manages the health and performance of systems and technology, driving down unplanned outages and creating a greatly improved user and customer experience. 
 
The operations team will play an important role in the engineering and proving of new and modernised systems by setting the quality expectations for service standards eg error handling, alerting, fail-over, backup and restore, capacity management, patch management and intelligent workflows. They will drive best practice around not just the operability, but equally as important, the sustainability of applications and systems. The Operations Centre will work with the applications, platform and network teams to validate that systems are designed to be resilient and that proper consideration and testing has been given to fail-over, load balancing and data integrity. They will do this by forming an ‘expert review board’ that will assess major changes, drawing in input from key SMEs and third parties to verify and understand the risk to service prior to deployment and to check that lessons learned have been incorporated effectively. 
 
We establish and run ‘Black Friday’ command centres for several retail UK clients, enhancing tooling and processes and successfully navigating them through the complexity of scaling up their networks and platforms to handle exceptional volumes over a 5-10 day period. 
 
In the event of an incident that impacts service, the Operations Centre will drive the triage and resolution process across the impacted technology teams. If an engineer is required to provide hands-on support in a data centre, the OC will co-ordinate and manage this as part of this process. Automation, as described in our response to 2.2 (Refer to Link for More Information), will gather diagnostic data automatically which will be shared with the engineer to accelerate fix time. They will be fully accountable for co-ordinating incident management, drawing in SMEs from across SLC and partners as required to fix the issue and identify preventative measures to stop it happening again. In the event of a major service outage, or complex issue that cannot be resolved, a ‘red team’ will establish and run command and control techniques to prioritise all activity required to restore service and distribute communications to the business and senior leadership. 
 
The Operations Centre will also take the lead on important business as usual activities such as disaster recovery and business continuity testing. The Operations Centre will own the plan for these activities and will co-ordinate and manage the plan across SLC, but also third party teams where required. The OC will have the authority and expertise to make critical decisions about when and whether to proceed and also to evaluate the success of the testing once complete. The OC will also manage routine and on-demand data centre visits by engineers conducting routine inspections and audits but also responding to actual and predicted failures. This will be automated as far as possible.
