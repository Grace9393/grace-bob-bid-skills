IBM are a world leader in Security Architecture and Technology Thought Leadership; defining future technologies and the technical architecture that supports them, consistently doing this over many decades through both our service offerings and our products.  IBM are ranked among the top contributors of Thought Leadership worldwide by Source Global Research, who publish a yearly study across the Industry.  Our Thought Leadership is open and all employees are encouraged to share and be eminent to grow the skills of others and influence the global Architecture community.  We will bring this approach to our work within the Profession, learning from best practice, creating the patterns and methods of the future together and sharing appropriate success to the benefit of others – establishing the DDaT Security Architecture Profession as a Thought Leading organisation, enriching the Security Architect ecosystem that we build with you, to help you and your professionals to grow.
 
Web Content and Industry Engagement
IBM has an extensive and thoughtful web content – prompting engagement with people from all over the industry.   Our website (www.ibm.com) is a wealth of content as well as the following examples:
- We have a website dedicated to Security Thought Leadership (https://securityintelligence.com/) which contains articles from leading IBMers as well as guest writers;
- The Cloud Architecture Centre which provides reference security architectures and architecture diagram templates (multi tool and for all the cloud hyper-scalers) which are available to all and allow modern, standard and consistent diagrams – something we could evolve into the current approaches at the Home Office (https://www.ibm.com/cloud/architecture/architectures/securityArchitecture);
- IBM also creates content such as the Institute of Business Value (IBV) which is an extensive and marketplace recognised resource providing industry insight along with technical and architecture papers. (www.ibm.com/thought-leadership/institute-business-value/);
- Specifically to Public Sector – IBM produces the IBM Centre for The Business of Government which also includes papers, points of view and extensive blog content (www.businessofgovernment.org); and
- RedHat (an IBM Company) produces extensive security architecture content on both its website (www.redhat.com) and its Open-Source blog (https://www.redhat.com/en/blog).
 
As well as the above formal content, which has millions of hits and comments, IBM, and its ecosystem of companies and partners produce extensive social media content through multiple channels – predominately LinkedIn and Twitter which also prompts huge volumes of engagement and discussion.   We also provide podcasts, dedicated to cyber security, via https://securityintelligence.com/media/
 
IBM actively support security teams around the world by making some of our advanced Threat Intelligence available for free. At https://exchange.xforce.ibmcloud.com/ you can see the latest Threat Intelligence information, download “Indicators of Compromise” (IOC), review in-depth technical articles on the latest attack techniques and even perform automated malware analysis. We provide this service as part of our mission to make the online world safer.
