T1 Legacy Technology
Should ARE require legacy support services, IBM is fully equipped to assist.
With 180,000 consultants, 43 practices, and over 800 skills, your legacy requirements are
covered. Our Custom and Exponential Tech Practice is the largest and most diverse practice,
with over 20,000 professionals covering the broadest range of technologies and skills. We have
over 10,000 Oracle consultants, including APEX Cloud Developer Certified Professionals, and
have completed over 6,000 Oracle projects, which led to us being awarded Oracle’s Strategic
Partner of the Year in 2023.
Not all of our clients use the latest technologies and platforms, and we continue to manage and
maintain applications that have gone out of support from the technology vendors. Our industry-
leading, purpose-built function specialises in providing multivendor enterprise hardware and
software support.
Whether the technologies are legacy or not, we have extensive experience successfully
supporting and maintaining environments in large-scale enterprises, ensuring smooth
operations, even for systems that have been unsupported for years. This service aims to extend
the economic life of technology, giving clients greater ability to make investment decisions in
line with their strategy and roadmap rather than based on the availability of manufacturer
support.

We have proven success of providing Oracle support that integrates into our client’s specific
processes and aligns with their business requirements. We provide a personalised, expert, and
responsive service so our clients can be confident in our ability to support their Oracle systems.
Our support delivery entails break/fix support for standard and custom code, global tax and
regulatory software updates, security and vulnerability features, and interoperability support.
Our dedicated team of Oracle support engineers works 24/7 to solve all technical issues. We
keep our clients’ Oracle environments secure and functional by proactively monitoring Oracle’s
CVE reports for newly identified security vulnerabilities. Once our engineers evaluate the
reports, we provide our clients with a remediation plan within days, adhering to our standard
processes of a very high-touch support function.
When a critical vulnerability is identified, we will provide a mitigation plan and virtual patching to
eliminate any exposures. The fixes, mitigations, compensating controls, advice and
recommendations for published security vulnerabilities are individualised to secure each of our
client's enterprise applications; we do this following an integrated, multi-layered and multi-

Supporting the SPARC T4-2 servers will come under our standard multivendor hardware

We have a UK policy only to use genuine and certified OEM parts and to have them close to our
