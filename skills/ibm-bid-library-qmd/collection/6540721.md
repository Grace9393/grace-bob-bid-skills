IBM has a well-defined approach to providing timely, actionable feedback within and across teams based on a system of Career Coaches, Performance Goals and Project Assessments. This is supported by an open culture based on trust which encourages both formal and informal 360o feedback. Prior to programme kick-off we will align our approach with the NHS Digital internal approach to performance management, as part of a broader alignment on operational processes, so that there is agreement and transparency regarding a shared pathway for raising, resolving or escalating any issues regarding individual team members. 
This will set the context for collaboratively addressing any questions or concerns regarding an individual’s performance, whether that team member is from IBM, one of our sub-contractors or the NHS. We will jointly review the context, situation and determine the best actions including additional coaching, mentoring or ultimately replacement within the team.  Where the individual is from IBM or one of our sub-contractors, we will replace them as swiftly as possible with a suitably qualified alternative colleague. There will be a handover process, outside of the team context if required, to minimise loss of knowledge and maintain pace. Where necessary, more senior members of the team will step in to support the continued integrity of the work plan while a new team member is onboarded.
