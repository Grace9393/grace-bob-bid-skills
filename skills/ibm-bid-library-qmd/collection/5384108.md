Our security services capabilities
IBM is the largest enterprise cybersecurity provider in the world, with technology experts, consultants and architects based across the world. We will use this world class expertise to work with you to build your capability and secure your systems. 
 
Using IBM QRadar security information and event management (SIEM) software, with help from IBM architects, The Weather Company architected a defence-in- depth cloud-based solution. 
 
We are Cyber Essentials Plus Certified and have over 200 consultants in the UK specialising in: 
- security strategy, risk and compliance (including Information Assurance) 
- Identity and Access Management (IAM) 
- infrastructure and end point security (including cloud) 
- data and application security (including DevSecOps) 
- security intelligence, operational consulting and cyber incident response, and 
- penetration testing and IT Health Checks (including Cyber Essentials assessments). 
 
We also offer ISO27001 Certified Managed Security Services including: 
- managed SOC services (including List-X based SOC facilities) 
- managed container security, Endpoint Detect and Response (EDR) services 
- vulnerability scanning and management services, and 
- threat Intelligence and threat hunting. 
 
Our specialist Information Assurance (IA) cell provides services including: 
- Risk Management Accreditation Document Sets (RMADS) 
- development of Security Management Plans (SMP) 
- development of Information Security Management Systems (ISMS), and 
- Security Operating Procedures (SyOps). 
 
We are ranked as a leader in security across 13 different market segments by industry analysts including Gartner, Forrester, Everest and IDC. 
 
As the only security vendor that is also a cloud company, we are uniquely positioned to be your trusted security advisor. We’ve been delivering security services for UK Government departments for over 20 years and we have consultants cleared to work on the most secure projects, and dedicated List-X facilities. Our specialists in the IA cell are also highly experienced in the use of NCSC guidance.
