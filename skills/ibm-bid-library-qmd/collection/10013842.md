In addition to sharing learning from client engagements and wider IBM research, initiatives that support our teams in keeping pace with advancements in AI include:
- mandated GenerativeAI training for all employees globally by Q4 2024
- in August 2023, IBM ran a Global watsonx challenge with over 143,000 colleagues participating in a 2-week challenge to gain experience using GenerativeAI tooling on specific business use cases
- in November 2023, IBM implemented a global internal GenerativeAI tool (named ‘Sidekick’: a private, conversational, multi-model ChatGPT alternative) for daily activities
- IBM internally published the IBV library knowledge corpus and the IBM Blue Pile dataset (documents, product code, publications) within our Sidekick tool to assist summarising best practice method, market trends and benchmarking to complement our AI Framework assessment activities with clients
- use of Slack communities globally on AI advancements and encouraging ownership and participation by linking this to learning pathways and annual performance metrics
- using Sidekick, IBM teams are investigating safely the feasibility of code modernisation use cases to improve enterprise architectural robustness, and
- ‘IBM as client zero’: IBM practices what it preaches, for example; Internal HR Implementation of the IBM AskHR Virtual Agent, a GenerativeAI infused tool that enables IBM colleagues to automate 94% of all HR-related queries via the Virtual Agent, such as change of address, bank details or holiday allowance requests.
