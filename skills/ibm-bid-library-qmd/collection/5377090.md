How development and learning activities align to SLC career pathway initiatives 
We will work with your team to identify skill maturity and build capability. Using IBM Watson Talent Development Frameworks, UK government Success Profiles and IBM research into best practice CoE operations, we will identify skills gaps. Watson brings a ready to use skills taxonomy which aligns to government standards and provides job profiles and skills required. Through proven approaches, including structured interviewing, we’ll identify what this means for roles and tailor our Watson frameworks to align with the DDaT framework being adopted by SLC. From this we will undertake, through managers, an assessment of individuals against different roles in the CoE. Individual learning needs can then be identified, avoiding unnecessary training and cost. We have relevant content available which can easily be repurposed for SLC.
