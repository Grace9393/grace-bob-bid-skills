NICE Nexidia Interaction Analytics Integrated
Nexidia Analytics uses big data to help call centre focused organizations transform into cost effective and efficient producers of revenue while also enabling agents, managers, and citizens to have better experiences and improved retention rates. Nexidia's inherently agnostic solution provides minimal disruption by overlaying existing recorders and data gathering methods and serves to enhance the communication of information to downstream data lakes or management programs.
NICE Nexidia Customer Journey Analytics
NICE Nexidia Customer Journey Analytics connects and transforms disparate customer interactions into a single, consolidated journey. With a holistic view of the end-to-end customer journey, organisations can uncover cross-channel inefficiencies and optimise business processes from the customer’s perspective to understand and improve the customer experience. 
 
CXone Performance Management (PM) 
CXone Performance Management enables employees to own performance and align with organisational goals by providing transparency and continuous feedback in a social environment. It enables you to report “one view of the truth” via customisable KPIs and dashboards aggregated from disparate sources to drive continuous improvement and a high-performance culture.  Elevate your agent performance with real-time, personalised insights, provide a creative outlet through gamification and wallboards, and motivate success with prizes in a customisable virtual storefront. CXone PM also increases supervisor efficiency and enables them to focus on high-value activities by automatically creating and sharing performance scorecards and automating managerial tasks. CXone Performance Management improves the customer experience by uniting the organisation behind transparent CX goals and creating the culture of continuous development essential to reaching business objectives.
