Process for mobilising resources and cascading the Call-off Terms and Conditions:For mobilising resources for this Call-off contract, we have proposed a best-fit approach, leveraging IBM’s Nordcloud Centre of Competency for cloud skills. For future work, we could mobilise resources from both our onshore offices in Leeds, Leicester,and London, combined with our Globally Integrated Capabilities (GIC) in our nearshore and offshore locations. We have mapped the specific capabilities of our subcontractor ecosystemsupply-chainto the Call-off requirements, so that there is clear alignment of requirements with their skills. In terms of cascading call-off terms and conditions, allour subcontractors have provided written acceptance of the core termsandthese have been incorporated into a Master Agreementwith IBM. Individual call-off contracts refer to this Master Agreement for consistency, throughout the 4-year duration of the Framework contract. 
 
We have a large team who work across Public Sector and Healthcare engagements, such as NHS Digital, Manchester Genomics, Cleveland Clinic, Defra,Scottish Government and Home Office. IBM recognises the value of rotating domain (Healthcare) and technology (cloud) specialists across our client engagements, as this brings domain specialism to new engagements.For NHS Digital, we have mobilised our team, utilising this model and we will continue to leverage our IBM Nordcloud CoE for continued mobilisation. As an example, Scottish Governmentand IBMco-created a scalable, integrated, cloud-based benefits solution enabling citizens to apply via a portal forLow-Income Benefits. The teams needed to rapidly scale hence IBM mobilised 7 agile development teams in 6 weeks supporting the full delivery life-cycle from architecture, service design and analysis to solution development. IBM has also partnered with NHS Digitalto build a replacement COVID Test and Trace solution. Within the first week IBM ramped-up an experienced Agile team of 50, further expanding the team as required.
