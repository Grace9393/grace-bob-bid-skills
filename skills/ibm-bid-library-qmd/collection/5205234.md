More locally, IBM hosts regular technology events at our London Office for clients and business partners to learn from, or experience Emerging Technology demonstrations in our Hursley lab or partner sites such as Daresbury Sci-Tech.  We would like to invite Home Office professionals to attend any of the events of interest (physical events when appropriate and safe to do so).  We also bring together clients who are working on similar data categories that align to the management of the Home Office “Trade” journey such as HMRC and Trade Lens (Our joint venture with Maersk).
