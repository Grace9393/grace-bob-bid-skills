IBM has completed major migrations for many of the country’s largest organisations, including UK banks, global manufacturing companies, publishing houses and more, that are comparable with SoTF in size, complexity and in terms of collation of structured (e.g. Device42/other discovery tooling outputs) and unstructured content (e.g. design documents). 
At the Co-operative Bank, IBM completed a complex discovery and migration programme delivering multi-million-pound savings. This encompassed 6 datacentres, with over 10,000 assets. 
At Co-Op we consolidated large volumes of data from many sources, including: 
- Asset auto-discovery and dependency analysis tools (using a primary asset discovery tool equivalent to Device42 and utilising similar data volumes to SoTF). 
- Multiple historic asset registers - several having multiple versions. 
- Software licencing databases. 
- Web questionnaires to the business departments and key users. 
- Structured workshops. 
- Technical complexity analysis, including direct investigation of applications.

All this data was consolidated, maintained and managed in SCOPE+, a specialised, multi-source discovery data management tool. 
As described in our 6-week plan, IBM will deploy SCOPE+ securely within the HMRC estate, with integration to Device42 and other sources of target data. All data will be mastered within SCOPE+ as the single-version-of-truth.
