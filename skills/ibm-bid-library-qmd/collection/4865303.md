IBM worked with HMRC to lead the creation of programme governance across rainbow teams to increase process and governance maturity. Working with HMRC’s senior leadership we established terms of reference and a process framework to drive improvements that were handed to delivery teams to improve their planning. We ran technical forums to assess solution viability and build progress. We built Design Review processes to optimise solutions and capture metrics that led to continuous improvement initiatives. This increased process maturity, ongoing governance throughout delivery, and improvement of architectural methods has led to an architectural artefact defect rate of < 1%.
