IBM’s ‘1-3-9’ values align strongly with the four NHS Digital Behaviours. Whilst enacting these values, your IBM team have defined five principles for our partnership - ‘NHS C.A.R.E.S.’. These principles will drive the behaviours that ensure our success. 
Clear – Delivering citizen outcomes and improving public health is our #1 priority, we have a clear purpose. We will use the IBM Garage method, driven by users needs, our operating model to ensure transparency as multiple squads deliver. All are clear on their role and responsibility to deliver target outcomes. 
Empowering – We believe that empowering citizens, contact tracers and the NHS with digital capability and insights will deliver a successful test & trace service. We will build simple human- centred CRM experiences from sign-on to reporting, delivered rapidly to scale. 
Collaborative – We work best when uniting cross-sector expertise and partners in our Garage method. Your IBM team bring diverse perspectives, we will not have all the answers but we have the open culture to ask for help. 
Constructive – We are your intelligent partner, establishing a Garage model of continuous learning built on agile delivery, user feedback and measurement of outcomes. We will courageously, constructively and respectfully challenge others and embrace citizens, agents and team members doing the same. 
Diversity, inclusion, well-being and support are the cornerstone of IBM team culture. The quality of solutions hinges on the inclusivity of perspectives in the team. Our diverse, multi-talented group work as one, in a social contract upheld by trust and personal responsibility in all relationships.
