Keeping our team up to date with technical, delivery and team working skills 
Process, tools and techniques: IBM implements CBKI (Client Business Knowledge Index) model/technique for our teams, balancing technical and functional expertise with Client knowledge and soft skills, so that the staff are operating with up to date skills and knowledge. The CBKI maps skill and knowledge on three dimensions: 
Domain / Functional / Technical / Design Expertise: This includes training on IBM and Third Party tools for skills development, certifications (internal IBM and external) and IBM Badges (Technical & Functional). Teams will have Data management Tools and Automation boot-camp sessions on latest developments and an audit to confirm that skills are maintained. 
Soft skills development: This includes problem solving techniques, communication skills, organisation and leadership skills. 
NHS and Healthcare domain expertise: This maps the staff’s knowledge of the NHS domain / functional area, applications / systems, organisation structure, on-boarding / off-boarding framework, SDLC / ITSM steps, owners, deliverables, tools and regulatory compliance. 
While working with Demographics data transform team, this model will be implemented transparently, with regular reporting on skills development and use. We use this approach with a large hospital chain in the US where multiple squads work towards a common objective through adoption of Agile principles and relevant collaboration tools.
