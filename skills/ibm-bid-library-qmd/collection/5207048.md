For deployment the operations team took the lead working with business users to identify a low impact deployment window.  The patch was deployed to production and the issue resolved. Lessons learnt, continuous improvement and knowledge management processes were initiated to improve early resolution times should a similar issue arise in the future. Given our experience in secure incident resolution, we propose bringing a similar approach to the delivery of your service.
 
Bringing our experience to HO
We recognise that to deliver a quality service end to end management is key to maintaining availability, ensuring visibility to stakeholders and to prevent issues being impacted by hand-offs between suppliers and delivery teams. 
We want to give you a high level of confidence that we can meet your requirement, backed by comprehensive evidence. For that reason, we will mobilise, resource and manage the BX S* service using a dedicated team that is an extension of IBM’s UK based established Shared Services Delivery (SSD) Centre which already provides application and system support and security incident management for Semaphore, Enterprise Services Level 2 and other critical UK Government applications.
