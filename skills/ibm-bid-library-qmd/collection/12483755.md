A1 Capacity Planning
ARE requires a Service Provider to deliver the capacity and capabilities needed to support
sustainable growth in agriculture, the food industry, and rural areas. This includes adapting
support schemes, introducing new ones, and using innovations to enhance customer-centred
services and ensure successful outcomes. We understand and comply with paragraphs 39-43.
Adapting to Support Sustainable Economic Growth
We will evaluate ARE’s Enterprise Architecture using our standard Enterprise Architecture
Maturity Model, focusing on crucial capability areas and specific KPIs to assess maturity. Led
by our Chief Architect and conducted in Mobilisation, we aim to identify areas for improvement
in strategy, technology, and governance. Our track record of success across various industries
showcases the effectiveness of this approach in driving strategic alignment and operational
efficiency. This assessment aims to position ARE well to adapt to sustainable growth or identify
where enhanced capabilities are possible.
Changing Existing Support Schemes and Adopting New Schemes
We will collaborate with ARE to adopt a data-driven approach for transforming support
schemes. Using digital platforms, we will provide insights on agriculture, environmental impact,
and biodiversity, streamlining the delivery process and facilitating the adoption of new schemes
through integrated data pipelines. A shared services model may also offer flexibility to meet
varying demands and create adaptable support schemes. Our Platform Engineering Services
support hybrid cloud solutions as a shared service, enhancing scalability and optimising
resources. Shared services may be a cost-effective future consideration for ARE.
Application of Digital Thinking and Innovation to Exploit Technologies
To promote digital thinking and establish data as a core asset, we will assist ARE in
consolidating your data within a unified platform. This will involve creating pipelines to
ingest various sources, such as weather and GIS data accessible through APIs. The
consolidated data will support AI models, providing actionable insights and enabling new
services like environmental impact assessments and improved rural land-use decisions.
Modernisation and Improvement of the Technical Architecture
To modernise ARE's technical architecture, we will use our in-house Hybrid by Design
framework, combining IBM’s Application Modernisation and Mosaic methods. This approach
employs prebuilt architecture patterns for microservices, integration, and security. We have
experience with similar As-Is estates, where we have successfully designed target architectures
for clients like Delta Air Lines and Philip Morris International. This shows that this method can
boost productivity by 30% and cut ownership costs by 40%, minimising development effort and
risks while establishing a future-ready infrastructure for ARE.
Helping ARE to Deliver Secure and Stable Services
To support ARE's demand planning, our support analysts will analyse data from IT service
management, monitoring systems, and intrusion detection systems. This data will be integrated
into our Hybrid by Design framework. We will identify the capacity and resources necessary for
providing stable services by aligning these insights with business needs. Additionally, we will
enhance threat detection and encryption to secure your services further. This proven approach
optimises resource allocation and strengthens cybersecurity, enabling ARE to deliver secure
and stable services aligned with organisational goals and industry best practices.
1A2 Horizon Planning
ARE wishes to leverage advancing digital technologies while managing costs and risks. A
partnership that delivers across all four horizons and assesses the support implications of new
technology is critical to success. We understand and comply with requirements R44-R46.
IBM is uniquely positioned to help ARE benefit from advancing digital technologies while
managing costs and risks effectively. Our methodologies and digital transformation experience
enable us to deliver measurable value across all horizons. At Defra and NHS, we migrated
legacy applications to cloud platforms, increasing efficiency and reducing operational costs.
Taking Advantage of Technology Advancement
Our team will provide expert guidance throughout the contract on the latest technologies and
leading practices in Enterprise Architecture, solution design, and delivery. We will provide
ongoing support, advice, and recommendations to ARE so the best decisions on adopting
technological advancements can be made.
Supporting the Delivery of the Horizon Planning Strategy
At no cost, we will provide a Chief Architect for three days a week during Mobilisation to
collaborate with the DISD Architecture Office to build on the existing work of Horizons 1 and 2
and assist in developing Horizons 3 and 4, sharing learnings and incorporating IBM's
experience from similar work with other clients. By the end of the Mobilisation phase, we will
have helped you to develop the horizon roadmap.
Horizon 1 (Current State): We are committed to maintaining the stability and security of
existing systems while proactively preparing for future transitions. We will use our knowledge
transfer process to understand existing systems and implement an operational strategy that
prioritises critical functions from legacy applications to target cloud platforms. By employing
industry-leading practices, risk assessments, and advanced tools, we can minimise service
disruptions and accelerate the adoption of new technologies, delivering enhanced value.
Horizon 2 (Decommission SCOTS): We will work with the DISD Architecture Office, utilising
our Application Modernisation Methodology to transition legacy systems to modern platforms.
We will analyse the legacy estate for business impact and dependencies and evaluate the risks
of maintenance vs transformation. Applications are decommissioned, and critical services are
migrated to the cloud, mitigating risks and enabling the continuity of payment processes.
Horizon 3 (Steady State): Our experience from other engagements is establishing a process to
continuously identify and prioritise opportunities (eg automation through integrating monitoring,
event management and ITSM tooling), enables operational value. This will help build a cohesive
service management framework. A robust platform engineering capability is essential for
provisioning and storage management services. We offer comprehensive service management
and platform engineering solutions, utilising industry standards such as ITIL.
Horizon 4 (Future Needs): We will collaborate with the ARE Architecture Office to identify
emerging technologies, design a future-state architecture, and create a comprehensive
execution plan using our in-house Hybrid by Design methodology and prebuilt accelerators.
Considering Support Implications of New Technology Choices
Our architects, Service Managers, and Contract Manager will advise ARE on the support
implications of new technologies through our delivery of impact and options assessments and
solution designs. ARE stakeholders will be informed of advantages and disadvantages and
supportability considerations enabling informed decisions based on the best value for money.
