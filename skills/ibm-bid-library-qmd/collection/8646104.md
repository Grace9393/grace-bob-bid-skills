IBM understand that DWP is reviewing the market to make decisions on the future platform strategy for the DWP Contact Centre. The service DWP provides to citizens is mission critical, it supports the most vulnerable in our society and ensures they can understand and access their entitlements. IBM is proud in our long-term relationship with DWP and value our partnership together in the vital services you provide.
IBM recognise that the RFIs in this process leverage a common set of capabilities (namely, CCaaS WFM, single desktop, quality management to name but a few). These capabilities are shared amongst the RFI requirements; however, we feel it is important to review all requirements together, to ensure any vendor selection decisions are fully informed. Our experience has shown that where organisations have made product selections for core customer contact technologies in isolation from each other (e.g. contact centre as a service, digital channels, WFM etc) this can lead to siloed, disparate solutions that are often expensive and do not allow for a seamless citizen or colleague experience.
IBM uses a robust Cognitive Care Methodology to support clients in making the Contact Centre technology ecosystem and operating model decisions that support their business outcomes. This has been implemented for many large enterprise clients, across multiple sectors, for the likes of Lloyds Banking Group, TSB, e.on, and Vodafone.
IBM has an established global AI & Customer Care Centre of Excellence with an experienced team of over 4000 business, strategy and technology consultants that design, build, and run foundational systems and services for Customer Care and AI solutions based on our own and market leading Contact Centre and AI solutions.
This Cognitive Care Transformation approach covers the full end to end process to ensure that technology decisions support your overall business and operating model.
Figure 1: IBM Cognitive Care Transformation Approach 

Our technology assessment and future state definition stages use our Cognitive Care Capability template, based on 6 Core Anchor Platforms, that must be considered together to ensure a holistic view of your technology ecosystem and to drive the correct decisions as there is no ‘one size fits all’ approach. 
 
Figure 2: IBM Core Anchor Platforms and Capabilities

The selection of the appropriate platforms is dependent upon: 
- DWP Objectives & design principles – Short, Medium and long term (e.g. data security, simplified architecture, future proofing and Voice First)
- Capability requirements – to deliver the DWP objectives
- Current Technology Landscape – those enterprise resources which are existing strategic platform choices for DWP
- Target Operating Model for the long term ownership and development of the platform 
IBM Consulting is a partner with all leading Omni Channel Contact Centre vendors and as an existing partner of DWP we feel we are uniquely positioned to respond to these requirements with a holistic view.
