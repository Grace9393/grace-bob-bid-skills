Implementation Plan: Based on the requirements specified in the ITT documents, our tentative implementation plan is detailed in the picture below. This will be discussed and agreed at SOW contract award stage. We propose a 2-week resource ramp-up period from the date of award, followed by a 12-week discovery phase which will run concurrently with implementation of the existing backlog. The outputs from the discovery phase will then be used to shape the plan and profile for the implementation phase. The discovery phase will use the “IBM Garage” method for insight gathering from stakeholders and users, as well as data analytics to generate and prioritise a backlog. We also propose that a “Continuous Data Quality Improvement” framework be embedded into the DevOps processes to ensure ongoing maintenance of data quality.
