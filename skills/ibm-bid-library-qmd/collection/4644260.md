IBM has a history of successful long-term multi-team / vendor partnerships across Government and proven design and delivery experience, including at HMRC, Defra, Home Office, MOD and DWP. At DWP, we have been successfully providing services for complex solutions across multiple workstreams under the ADEP framework since 2011. In the latest contract year of the ADEP framework, we completed 302 contracts (over 50,000 Application Development days) of varying size and complexity, using an agile delivery approach and covering both digital design and delivery. These workstreams each had different delivery plan duration depending on the required outcome (e.g. 1/3/6 months).
