For the Home Office, we have been delivering the Semaphore programme for over 10 years.  During this time we have created many ROMs and IAs and taken them through agreed Change Control processes.  We bring different approaches, including bespoke to meet requirements or ‘t-shirt’ size estimating principles against pre-agreed criteria.  We also pro-actively create when we identify a need rather than just responding to requests. To help the approval process we deliver a narrative alongside or support presentations to the approvals board.  For Semaphore we initially proposed a ROM to support the business case for their successful AWS migration project.
