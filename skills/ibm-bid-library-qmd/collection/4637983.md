The Food Standards Agency engaged IBM to develop a food business registration service replacing processes in 387 Local Councils. Research was conducted with food business operators, inspectors, policy makers and large enterprises and, upon analysing the findings, identified opportunities for at least 3 separate services to address user needs. The team used roadmaps, storytelling and personas to communicate insights and available options to stakeholders and at board meetings. The board used this to take action and set a clear direction for the project, resulting in a highly successful Alpha and Beta phase to build and deploy the service live.
