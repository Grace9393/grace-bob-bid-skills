We will deliver this SOW in line with overall demand and will flex this accordingly, based on joint demand forecasting and also external conditions e.g. new COVID waves.  Further details are provided in Q7.1. For patients, the ability to easily find and understand NHS services, and supporting self-referral, has never been more important. This SOW will improve user experience, reduce fear and confusion, and relieve pressure on frontline services. Within the wider-call off, the service taxonomy should be aligned to the new information architecture and content modularisation agenda, so that a more integrated experience can be delivered to patients, e.g. displaying relevant local mental health services on landing pages. This could support personalised experiences aligned to the proposed transactions platform, displaying relevant local services, content and decision support aligned to the patient’s own health data, problems or diagnoses. We are very excited about the potential for a modern NHS.UK publishing platform to support this agenda more efficiently, incorporating the best multi-channel personalised experience technology, automation and governance workflows to enable greater publishing velocity without overloading NHS.UK staff. This platform must also enable a more modular ‘developer-friendly’ infrastructure to speed up service delivery.
