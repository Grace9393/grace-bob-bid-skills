N1 Community Benefits
The Scottish Government is committed to contributing to Scotland's people’s social, economic,
and environmental well-being and delivering against its National Outcomes. We are proud of
our record of delivering Community Benefits in Scotland for the last seven years. We will
support your Community Benefits goals, which align with our mission to be a catalyst that
makes the world work better by working with you to help create opportunities that enable
Scotland to flourish through sustainable economic growth.
Economic Well-Being
We will create employment opportunities focused on individuals facing barriers, supporting the
‘Young People’s Participation’ National Indicator. We have already hired 12 Scottish
Apprentices through our existing partnerships with Uptree and Napier University. We will offer
up to nine Scottish Degree Apprenticeships in Developer and QAT roles for this contract
(five in the initial term), targeting diverse, disadvantaged school leavers. Apprentices will earn
a BSc Honours in Software Development or IT Management over four years, will be embedded
into the ARE delivery team and gain hands-on experience with support from managers and
communities of practice networks.

Social Well-Being
IBM SkillsBuild is a free digital learning programme that helps students in Scotland develop skills and access career opportunities. They will earn industry-recognised digital credentials on topics such as AI, data and cloud computing. We will deploy this by: 
 
- Hosting seminars tackling inequality: eg women in STEM, to inspire and empower students to break down the barriers
- Partnering with four schools/colleges: working with you to target underprivileged areas and agricultural courses
- Curating tailored learning pathways: continue our collaboration with Education Scotland and Developing the Young Workforce to address local skills gaps 
- Delivering employability skills workshops: on topics such as Agile methodology to enhance student development.
- Providing IBM mentorship and coaching: (eg chatbot design) for students aged 14-18 to apply their learning, and
 
These initiatives will help future-proof students’ careers and address the Skill Profile and Skills Shortage National Indicators.
 
Environmental Well-Being
Through our IBM Community Engagement Portal and partnership with Goodera, we will participate in volunteering initiatives and support local non-profits. Our teams, including ARE staff, will be offered opportunities to support environmental initiatives, such as creating pollinator gardens and cleaning up green/blue spaces through litter picking. IBM donates the equivalent of $10 per volunteer hour logged, which our team will donate to Scottish climate-focused charities. We commit to delivering four tailored volunteering events per year.
 
Additionally, we will organise an annual charity event involving all contract teams. This will combine in-person and virtual options to raise funds for a chosen non-profit organisation.
 
Collaborative Approach
For this contract, we will appoint the following roles:
 
- Social Value Lead in the first three months to oversee our Social Value commitments across all Scottish Government contracts, and
- Community Benefits Champion in the first month, dedicated to this contract.
 
These resource will integrate with our dedicated Social Value Public Sector group, consisting of SMEs and global experts who meet bi-weekly to exchange best practices. In the first three months, we will work with you to establish a Community Benefits working group of representatives from ARE and other contract suppliers. We will host quarterly checkpoints to set clear objectives and drive initiatives. This collaboration means we will maximise the impact and support of relevant charities, such as Scottish Young Farmers, throughout the contract.
 
Measuring and Reporting Our Impact
We will use a dedicated social value platform, eg Thrive, to measure and report our progress and impact. With over 150 social value metrics available, we will tailor these to the national indicators and our specific commitments, detailed below. The Social Value Lead will provide a customised monthly report for ARE, featuring quantitative and qualitative data to be reviewed in our contract management meetings, highlighting progress on our commitments.
