Change Management 
A key element of our approach is to support changes needed to enhance your cloud platform and extend benefit services. We will provide technical expertise to evaluate and assess the impact of change and apply process rigour and lessons learned to prevent change failures. We will work in compliance with the CDO change management process and support changes, including out-of-hours.
