We have a long and extensive working knowledge of many of the federated organisations in public sector, both those within Cabinet Office and those outside. For many of these we remain embedded in their teams today. For example, we are working with MoD, DWP, DEFRA, Home Office, NHS England, and others to help them with their use of artificial intelligence.
 
Through our work designing and scaling citizen-facing services like the NHS App, the NHS payroll, DWP’s Money and Pension Service, HMRC’s Single Trade Window and the Scottish Government benefit’s system, we have unique insight into citizen needs and how to undertake digital change at scale and address the digital needs of a business.
