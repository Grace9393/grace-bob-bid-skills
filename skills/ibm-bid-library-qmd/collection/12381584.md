IBM employs approximately 280,000 staff globally, and 10,000 staff in the UK, with many holding UK Security Clearance, enabling them to work on a wide range of projects. We have extensive experience identifying the best people for each role, and rapidly building and deploying teams to wherever they are required. Our comprehensive Workforce Management function and processes, emphasise skill sourcing, balance, and optimisation, prioritising prompt responses to client needs, ensuring appropriate resource allocation. Our Professional Marketplace (PMP) tool allows for real-time staffing decisions, accessing professionals' profiles globally, and managing staffing fluctuations. Our systems are set up with the sole purpose of ensuring that we provide you the best resources possible.  
 
Each job role / skill set raised, signals demand which feeds into our advanced analytics business forecasting and mapping, which commits IBM resources from the relevant Practice with the right skills and availability. Skill gaps will drive upskilling / cross-skilling plans, as well as hiring decisions. 
 
We employ various resource models, including on-site, offshore, and nearshore teams, to adapt to varying levels of demand and project requirements.  Our UK Delivery Centres provide cost-effective resources with technical expertise. Third-party vendors are also engaged when specific skills are scarce or rapid scaling is needed This flexibility enables seamless scaling and cost optimisation. We have experience in working with the DCC to be clear on which resources can access which data types from which location/shore. 
 
Adapting to varying levels of demand and handling changes in project scope - we support as follows:  
Where additional resources are required on a project, we will take your demand request and source resources from the resource pool in the same way as the start of the engagement. 
Where staff are released from the project, they will be placed back into the resource pool from where they can be allocated to other projects. We agree a minimum notice period (typically 4 weeks, sometimes longer) for rolling resources off the project. 
 
We engage regularly with you to plan for future skill gaps and address changing demands.  We have proven success with this approach.  To respond to a change in demand, for the Home Office Shared-Application-Services, we mobilised a 30-person SC-cleared team to meet project needs within 10 working days. 
 
Ensuring continuity of service through knowledge sharing and handover practices 
Accurate forecasting and planning of requirements and demand is key for service continuity – this requires close collaboration between us, and a robust process to evaluate resource adjustments based on impact, feasibility, and aligned to project objectives. This minimises disruptions and maintains resource efficiency. 
 
If change is needed, continuity of service is enabled through effective IBM handover practices including: 
Cross-Functional Collaboration: Encouraging collaboration among team members, promoting knowledge exchange and collective problem-solving – this team-first approach builds resilience by embedding knowledge, experience and skills in the wider team. 
Documentation: Thorough documentation of project artifacts, processes, and lessons learned. 
Training and Onboarding: Comprehensive training for incoming team members, ensuring they quickly ramp up and contribute effectively. 
Depending on the size, scale and nature of our projects, we mitigate risks in retaining knowledge and skills by providing: a skills matrix showing transparency of skills across the team and where necessary secondary cover for each skill area; a training plan providing a clear path for personal development; rotation of work within teams to build knowledge, provide variation and promote innovation.  We remain connected to our workforce professionals to identify cover in case needing to back-fill a role 
 
By implementing these strategies, we continuously demonstrate a robust approach to resource planning and management, enabling adaptability to varying demand levels and changes in project scope, whilst ensuring continuity of service through effective knowledge sharing and handover practices to team members. This approach applies to all our projects, regardless of type, area of focus, skillsets required and thus is the same principle stated across all Lots within our framework tender response
