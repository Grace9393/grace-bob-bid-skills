Proposed solution and meeting programme outcomes

- Our extensive experience delivering HMG and global customs and border transformation programmes (CDS, STW/XXXXX, XXXXX)
- Delivering critical national services elsewhere, e.g., XXXXX, NHS, XXXXX

These tell us that:  
- To build an integrated, rules-based digital solution in this complex domain and meet specific ministerial commitments by November 2023 requires a significant head start.
- Adopting STW solutions from other jurisdictions will not translate to the UK. The solution needs to be designed for the complexities of the UK border operating model and address the needs of infrequent and frequent traders. 
- Future proofing and maximising platform adoption requires extensive user research and a fully digital service with guided journeys, enabling users to satisfy their border obligations.

- Seamless integration with UK Trade Tariff – Throughout the digital user journey, providing context-specific guidance and improved data quality. 
- Additional value – Such as the opportunity to bring full export declarations into SR1 - and driving adoption through more comprehensive intuitive, self-serve digital experience.

Secure by design, meeting programme/GDS standards
We follow ‘Security and Privacy by Design1 ’ practices, aligned with the NIST Secure Software Development Framework and NCSC Secure Design Principles. This means:
- Security specialists within the Core Management team and delivery squads. 
- Team members will complete security and privacy by design training before onboarding, and cybersecurity/data privacy education annually.
- Automated security testing, including static/dynamic analysis and vulnerability scanning within CI/CD pipelines, using SonarQube, Snyk/Tenable.
- DevSecOps practices throughout engineering, including secure development and threat assessments.
Early vulnerability/penetration testing during delivery - mitigating risk of late discovery of issues.DevSecOps practices span the development lifecycle, including security event monitoring integrated with cybersecurity incident response, with platform services publishing real-time events into SIEM (Splunk), and feeding into HMRC SOC.
As part of each major release, we’ll also support independent vulnerability/penetration testing of the system, providing access/documentation to HMRC’s appointed independent tester.
Optimised for agility, change, portability, scalability
Our experience delivering critical national services for HMG demonstrates: 
- Frequent change is inevitable, adopting the right tools and optimising the change/release process is key to delivering world-class services and outcomes.
- Predicting demand is inherently difficult, affected by domestic/international and political events. It is critical for STW to be responsive, auto-scaling on demand, specifically when mandated for transactional data submissions.
Addressing these from the outset ensures the platform foundation supports both SR1 and future needs of the service.
Agility and Change:
- At XXXXX, we delivered c.430 functional releases p/a on the XXXXX service, enabling its roadmap to flex rapidly to deliver new policies (e.g., XXXXX built/deployed within 10 days).Optimising the release process to support variable change, enables frequent releases into Production. Techniques such as ‘feature toggles’ will promote release agility.
- Using low-code/no-code technologies enables selective changes to be delivered by operations teams, reducing time-to-value and increasing delivery flexibility e.g., business rules, reference data.
Portability:
- Our platforms are industry-leaders (XXXXX) in their segments, with skills across the market.
- Use of ‘as a Service’ platforms facilitates account transfer to HMG/supplier at service transition.
Use of open-standards allows products/platforms to be replaced without impacting users, e.g., SOAP/WSDL, OAuth2/OpenID Connect and WCO Data Model.Scalability:
- For Test&Trace, we delivered 100+ releases within 9-months and the Real-Time Testing Service (RTTS), on MuleSoft, processed over 1 million tests per day (>300 per second).Salesforce, MuleSoft, and AWS are designed to handle millions of users and transactions, auto-scaling as required.
- Solution components, such as the Guidance Service will be hosted on AWS EKS with container auto-scaling.
 
 
Accelerate delivery and manage technical risks
- UK Tariff and completion rules
We provided the UK Tariff business rules within CDS and have a proven digital service, XXXXX, which interprets these. We will reuse analysis/design and components from XXXXX to integrate the UK Trade Tariff within STW.
- Our specialists have delivered these APIs for HMRC via IBM’s DMS product, and we have real-world experience of consuming them in XXXXX. Complex CDS Integration
Successful integration with CDS requires detailed knowledge/experience of the WCO Data Model and CDS APIs. Declaration amendments and error responses involve interpreting the complex WCO pointer mechanism, this presents a risk for any supplier.
- Providing an intuitive digital UI
Previous attempts by HMG to develop digital UIs to support Customs Import Declarations have not succeeded.
We will de-risk the delivery of an intuitive, digital UI by reusing trader research, requirements/analysis and UX journeys/wireframes from XXXXX. This included the same trader segments as STW, infrequent, non-expert traders. 
- SR1 delivery timelines
In addition to accelerators, we are already building the ‘STW Foundation’ which will provide 40% of SR1 functionality at contract commencement. Built using our technology platforms to support journeys for user registration, S&S/Import Declarations, with integrations to CDS/S&SGB.

 

Continuous updates/patching with minimal disruption
Salesforce and MuleSoft provide continuous availability, with patching/upgrades applied without service disruption: 
- Each platform is distributed across multiple availability zones within the UK, supporting rolling updates without service interruption.
- Application updates will use the HA-deployment pattern, allowing seamless replacement of old applications with new.
For those STW components deployed on the AWS public cloud platform:
- Serverless cloud-native components, technologies are patched/updated without requiring outage.
- Containerised components, e.g., the Guidance Service, will be hosted on AWS EKS, which is patched/upgraded by Amazon.
- Application updates will be performed using blue/green deployment patterns, avoiding interruptions while workloads are migrated.
We automatically raise alerts raised through automated operational monitoring and vulnerability within the ServiceNow tool for triaging and action by the L2 DevOps and SRE teams.
Partnering with industry to embrace innovative and transformative technologies 
We will partner with industry to embrace transformative technologies that allow the STW to:
- Connect to commercial supply-chain platforms underpinned by blockchain, accessing commercial data directly, using frameworks like TradeTrust for interoperability, to move towards a ‘tell-us-never' model.
- We recently supported the set-up of the Centre for Digital Trade and Innovation, developed their Innovation Playbook, and designed pilots using blockchain based platforms to share eBLs in the UK/Singapore trade corridor. Adopt AI and Machine Learning to tailor guidance, compliance and risking based on trader profile, history and supply-chain, facilitating efficiencies across the border system.
- Use big-data to support ‘smarter-risking’, with open standards to exchange data with other STWs, going beyond ‘tell-us-once' to a global trust model: ‘my import is your export’.  
We’ll use this experience to tailor our STW Connected Border, partnering with industry to innovate:

The STW Connected Border will:
- Ecosystem Advisory Board – Provide insight, advice, and global best practice to challenge the roadmap, identify opportunities, and help scope experiments and pilots (related to SR2-5 ).

It allows (trusted) third parties to test ideas within our sandbox environment, and could become our interface to the Ecosystem Of Trust.
