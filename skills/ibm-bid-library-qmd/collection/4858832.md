IBM has experience of providing oversight and advice to teams working with complex data.   At a multinational bank, we helped them design a new data platform including data governance management and multiple data architecture artefacts.
 
We worked closely with the organisation professional's to resolve the lack of standard definition of terms (conflict between local and global terminologies) and inconsistencies between the different metadata repositories across the organisation.  Adopting Egeria, the Open Source Metadata Standard for which we are a key supporter, as a mechanism for federating metadata across systems, the bank was able to drive consistent metadata across their enterprise.
