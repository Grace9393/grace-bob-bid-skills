Finnair were undertaking a significant digital transformation journey and needed support to scale their mobile application development function. Using our Mobile@Scale framework we established integrated global capability across Finland, Canada and India. Establishing practices for mobile application design, delivery, governance and DevSecOps built on top of our extensive iOS accelerators (Prebuilt UI Design Patterns, Pre-set styles, UI components, guidelines for iOS, Prebuilt Technical Components, entire prebuilt starter apps and Mobile DevOps framework). This enabled global deployment of 7 transformative apps with 60% reduction in cost per app, 76% decrease in maintenance costs and 35% faster to integrate into core systems.
