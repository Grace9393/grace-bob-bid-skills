Our resource teams will work holistically across all channels, both in house and through our partner base to establish the right supplier against demand. Partner management is supported and contracted via our commercial contracts team.  They ensure that the necessary commercial arrangements are in place for the eco system to meet and uphold our quality standards.  Contractual arrangements are agreed on a contract-by-contract basis ensuring that all supplier participating in the delivery of the contact are fully conversant with their commercial obligations – such as IR35 compliance and clearance.   Once on boarded our commercial team, in association with the contract manager will continue to monitor performance of the eco system to ensure that all quality thresholds are being met.  We will collaborate with our partner network throughout the engagement, sharing our planning inputs and strategy to ensure we are best able to rapidly field the most effective teams for your requirements. We have carried out similar activities across the public sector, including recently with the NHS, supplying a wealth of specialist resources.  We will use this experience to bring the same successful partnering approach to DDaT.
 
We have long-standing partnerships with a wide array of industry SMEs that we will engage with to find the best fit for your requirements.
