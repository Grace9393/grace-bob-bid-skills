We are proud of the work we do with government, and in particular the Home Office, including PPPT. Over many years we have worked with you to deliver and support critical complex programmes, evolving over time and operating in a number of delivery models to best support your needs. We are passionate about the PPPT landscape and the work to support policing, victims and the emergency services – bringing ‘technology for good’. 
 
As you, legislation and policies have evolved, we have supported you to ensure that delivery requirements reflect the needs of your customers and this ever-changing landscape.  This work has included:
- adopting new technologies and paradigms such as cloud computing, for example recently migrating the Semaphore borders system to AWS
- responding to legislation, both routine and wide-ranging such as Brexit which impacted many borders and trade systems which we manage,
- or reacting to unexpected major events such as the COVID pandemic and the work we have done with the NHS.
 
These examples demonstrate that we both experienced, but also prepared for and able to react to changing needs and that we care about helping you to deliver the services which are vital to running the country. They have also taught us that we operate best when we partner with you and a wider ecosystem, which we have invested in and continue to grow. It is these factors that we believe make us uniquely placed to be your delivery partner – focussed together on the provision of world class user and product centric services.
 
Current technology supported and the level of development and support 
Our teams have strong experience across an extensive range of technologies, and are confident that this, supplemented with our extensive eco-system of partners will provide coverage across all your technology demands. We have a depth of expertise in strategic technologies used by PPPT, for example:
- application development including Java, SpringBoot, React, Node.js, Javascript, Python
- DevOps technologies including AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform, Jenkins
- data & messaging technologies including PostGres, DynamoDb, ElasticSearch, Kafka, SQS.
- cloud – through our own teams who have record numbers of cloud certifications plus recognised partnerships with all the leading Hyperscalers
 
In addition to currently strategic technologies, we are experienced in supporting systems in a wide range of technologies, as part of ongoing enhancement, support, and ongoing modernisation. For example, with Defra we manage a portfolio of 100+ applications using Microsoft .NET, SQL Server, Java and Oracle, and are currently delivering a programme of migration to AWS and modernisation.
 
We bring experience at all levels of the operating model from L1 – L3 support, enhancements, large-scale development, agile delivery, DevOps, and testing services. Our support services incorporate tooling such as ServiceNow, and next-generation application management techniques including AIOps and FinOps. Our work at Enterprise Services on the EBSA platform is a great example of the range of cutting-edge technologies we apply, and the development support we provide, ranging from L1.5 to L3, spanning very diverse stacks, architectures and suppliers with whom we work closely.
 
New joiners to our CIC delivery centre who will be working with you undertake a training bootcamp encompassing core technologies, as well as AWS certification, and we continue to support their ongoing training alongside delivery work to grow and stretch their skills in-line with market and client demands.
 
How emerging technologies are identified and built into operating / delivery model 
Embedding emerging technology and bringing innovation is vital to keep your portfolio current over the lifetime of our engagement. We recognise that this needs to be achieved in a controlled way, interlocking with the PPPT TDA and the DDaT tech radar.
 
Emerging technologies of interest may be identified from innovation from external sources, or from within our joint delivery teams. For technologies that delivery teams will bring immediate value, we will seek to perform a short investigation to explore the potential value and make the PPPT TDA aware of this work via our technical leadership.  We will present the findings and our recommendations to help inform other teams and projects on benefits/pitfalls. 
 
For more potentially disruptive technologies, we will use technology incubator team to help take concepts from idea to MVP stage, to prove the concept and value, which can then flow into and inform projects. We have also recruited a number of technology partners into our EcoSystem marketplace - they are both large and SME, and all with relevant offerings to PPPT. 
 
To bring external thought leadership around emerging technologies and capabilities we’ll help develop your own capability to make use of emerging innovation and trends by:
- building a strong, diverse and thriving ecosystem of SMEs that brings together niche and emerging skills for PPPT
- introductions to our law enforcement clients across the world to share and learn, such as the work we have done recently with the NYPD on predictive analytics
- talking to other clients from other industries who are facing similar challenges – enabling diverse approaches to solving problems – such as lessons from the banking industry
 
How these emerging technologies and capabilities are developed to meet both initial demand and future scale
In the previous section we outline how emerging technologies are identified and brought to MVP or initial project exploration. Skills training and education across our joint teams are key to creating organisational capability to moving forward successfully and scaling to meet initial and future demand. 
 
During initial adoption team-members including your key individuals will undertake training to be the pioneers, using either formal or recognised online training. To move beyond this into building production strength systems we will engage vendors from our technology EcoSystem marketplace (where appropriate) or engage with community best practices to help you to build it right first time. We will seek to bring you best practices and learning from our other engagements from UK public sector and globally to inform this, as well as engaging across the wider DDaT technology landscape.
 
After first implementation, if the technology is successful and you are seeking to roll it out further, the challenge is then to scale out to future projects and organisational learning. The implementing project will capture lessons learnt and best practices into a common knowledge base in Confluence, outside of the project-specific area to provide visibility to wider teams. Your key team members will be encouraged to share across the wider community including lunch and learn sessions, and with guilds / communities of practice in PPPT and beyond, building their eminence. They will then be seeded into future projects as the experts to help lead teams on subsequent use of this now maturing technology – now moving from being pioneers to settlers.
 
Manage and retain legacy skills and capabilities for sunset and open source 
We invest in our people including those with skills that are rare yet important to our clients. As with our future skills, we track and manage the skills of our team through data and accurate records of all our employees. We supplement these skills with the use of contractors and specialist SMEs to provide deeper coverage where needed. An example of our commitment is shown with an MoD programme where we support a custom developed logistics application and have done so since building it over 25 years ago. Each role on that programme has a succession plan and over time we have cross trained the team to remove single points of failure as natural attrition occurs. We also look to keep our teams motivated by identifying opportunities to grow and apply new skills alongside their core legacy skills and find this is a good way to retain and complement their legacy skills. Through the size and breadth of IBM we can reach far and wide to supply the legacy skills PPPT will need across the complex portfolio.
 
IBM is unmatched in the breadth of our open-source involvement. From quantum and blockchain to containers, AI, and operating systems, we are actively leading in today’s most influential projects and creating new projects to push technology forward for tomorrow. We train our employees in best practices for engaging with open-source and the importance of open governance – we will bring these approaches to PPPT. 
 
We recognise that enterprise IT is increasingly built upon open-source technology, indeed this at the heart of the PPPT and DDaT strategy and we have helped many public sector organisations to deliver open-source solutions. For example with DBS we helped them to transform from traditional out-source IT to in-house agile delivery. We co-delivered the first digital service using similar technologies to those in PPPT: JavaScript, Node.js, Java, PostGres, Selenium test tooling and integration to existing legacy systems. With our support, DBS delivered an award-winning digital service within 12 months, and grew in house agile delivery skills, with DBS’ product owner noting: "What’s really notable is that nobody on the project thinks about whether they are IBM or DBS. We have a true one-team mindset". Building on this we continue to deliver additional digital services with DBS following GDS discovery-alpha-beta methods.
 
Operating Effectively with an Extended Value Chain
Working with an extended value chain across clients, portfolios, sectors, and locations is a very common operating model for us and in particular our work across Home Office. We are the Home Office SAS Delivery Partner. In that role we have been helping DDaT define the new IT operating model including ways of working for this very approach and the establishment of a Collaboration Agreement across the supplier ecosystem. For the NHS, to support the pandemic, we worked in a multi supplier team, using modern technologies such as Salesforce, Mulesoft, AWS and many others, leveraging best practice from not only other sectors but across the world to quickly deliver and support a secure critical government service. Other relevant experience, that we will build upon to help PPPT, includes our work across the portfolios such as MBTP, DBS, and other government departments including HMRC and Scottish Government.
 
As a company we are aligned with the ethos of this question – how to meet an evolving future – we will continue to invest in developing ourselves and bringing new relationships to you. IBM have made over 10 major acquisitions alone in the last 2 years as well as announcing major partnerships with the world’s leading technology companies. We will bring our investments as a company to help navigate the evolving world with you, as your partner.
