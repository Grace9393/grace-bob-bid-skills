On this T&M contract we will provide monthly financial metrics detailing mandays worked for each role, individual day rates, total price per individual and the total invoice amount. This will accompany project status reports for the same time period.
 On NLEDP our reporting ensured that this project had 100% accuracy of spend verses budget, aligned to project delivery status. we provided a monthly invoice alongside our sprint reports for the same period, detailing the deliverables and value add covered by the invoice. We also provided the burn rate against the total contract value, and financial forecasts on a regular basis.
