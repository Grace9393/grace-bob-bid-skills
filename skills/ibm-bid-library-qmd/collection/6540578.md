The wider call off envisages two scenarios; one where IBM continue to work in a rainbow team and another where IBM takes responsibility for migrating some or all of the remaining datasets.  
The simple view of the relationship between the SOW and the wider Call Off could be that the SOW is just the first task of many, and if NHSD prefer it to remain so, we would be happy to oblige. In this case, the IBM teams would grow in knowledge and experience of the task and would get more proficient. There may be some efficiencies of experience to be gained from that approach. 
However, we can also imagine a scenario where IBM would like to have the opportunity to suggest improvements to the process or to the products. If this were the case, the SOW is then the opportunity for IBM to understand the current processes and products to consider how they could be evolved and improved. In our response to questions 5.1 and 5.2 we have suggested some of the challenges that might be overcome, and opportunities created if this approach was taken.
