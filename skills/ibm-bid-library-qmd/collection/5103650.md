In our experience we have found that through robust demand management we are able to enable the initiation of the on-boarding process at the earliest opportunity.  We recognise that security clearance is often the longest lead time item and would recommend  establishing a pool of pre-cleared (or close to clearance) resources as part of any demand management.  We are familiar with a variety of on-boarding processes used across Home Office DDaT including the request and allocation of security passes and user access devices e.g. POISE laptops, Chromebooks or BYOD, as well as access to project data shares and other Home Office on-boarding orientation to enable individuals to quickly get established within your teams.  
 
IBM is the Home Office’s SAS Delivery Partner. In that role we have been helping DDaT define the new IT operating model including ways of working and the establishment of a Collaboration Agreement across the supplier ecosystem.  As a major supplier of Level 2 support services, we are very familiar with working in a multi-supplier rainbow model, collaborating closely with other suppliers to deliver an end-to-end integrated service to the Home Office. In support of this we have an induction process for people who are new to working in the Home Office to become familiarised with the work we do and how we are expected to behave.
