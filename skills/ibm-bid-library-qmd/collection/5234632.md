We have delivered data services in a number of Government departments including the Department of Work and Pensions (DWP) and the Department for the Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (DEFRA). We have a consistent method for establishing requirements, monitoring and delivery of new data services, that will enable operation support for MI services over the next 12-24 months: 
Data Service Request – Using Design Thinking, which is our global method for putting users at the heart of everything we’ll do, we’ll run sessions for you, business users and us to jointly explore, ideate and establish new data service requirements. This way, we ensure that MI is considered upfront. We will examine requirements to strip the ask back to a minimum viable product (MVP) design, which allows faster delivery of value, whilst still meeting product functions aligned with the previous dataset. For requirements that are less well understood, production of rapid prototypes or Proof-Of-Concept will be considered;
Impact Analysis of Request – Using the ITIL change management process we will evaluate the data service requirement impact across all key stakeholders, including development, test, architecture (including cloud re-platforming), support services and business users;
Agile delivery – Using Agile delivery methodology we will work together to manage the service priority against the existing backlog, sprint plans and release schedule, to determine a release date; 
Monitor Service Delivery – We will help you use VOTE Dashboards and Sprint playbacks and retrospectives for real-time insights into the delivery status of the data service;
DevOps Deployment – DevOps automated deployment of your data service pipeline will be enabled to deliver a known tested configuration of the new data service baseline; 
Delivery of the Service – Home Office compliant ITIL delivery processes will be used to manage the delivery and support of the data services in your operational environment; and 
Monitoring the Service - Ongoing monitoring of the data service including data volumes, performance, availability, capacity and usage, will be configured to report against the data service SLAs and KPIs. 
Department of Work and Pensions (DWP): 
We delivered, maintain and operate the batch file transfer platform for the Customer Information System, which is a store of 100 million citizen records, and used by 80,000 users. The solution integrates across cloud, legacy, and external platforms to securely transfer 100s of files daily using multiple protocols to transport data on legacy systems, across multiple consumers with complex transformations and transient storage. The deployment of each new service integration requires robust processes, as described above, as well as experienced delivery teams to communicate with and engage stakeholders across the portfolio to maintain high service availability throughout the deployment.
Department for the Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (DEFRA):
IBM provide Data Warehouse (DW), and Data Management services to DEFRA including managing Business Intelligence (BI) applications. DWs utilise Datastage technology with spatial layering and physical repositories for managing GIS and spatial data. We support SAP Business Objects, which is used for management reporting from several Applications and maintain over one hundred universes for business communities. The level of service provided enables the client to design their own reporting, but also request additional, more complex reports to be created and supported by IBM. 
We are excited for the opportunity to tailor this approach to the DSA’s requirements and ways-of-working and for our organisations to learn from each other across this engagement.
