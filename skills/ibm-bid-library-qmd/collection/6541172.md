Our succession planning approach is built into our resourcing model, we will work with NHS D to tightly integrate resource forecasting, on-boarding, roll-off, succession planning and knowledge management. The risk of dependence on individual resources will be mitigated through proper knowledge management using the CBKI model described above. In the event of key staff exits or replacement, succession planning including education, shadowing and knowledge transfer is organised by IBM to reduce the risk of disruption in service delivery. 
As part of our resource forecasting model, we will work with NHS D to initially understand any required skills and develop a corresponding resource plan. We will increase these resource pools through recruitment, resource and succession planning, skill horizon scanning and, if required, partnering with our ecosystem of subcontractor organisations. We will work with NHS D to resource internally within the Vaccination Programme or NHS D if possible. Where niche skills are not available in IBM, our central procurement function will be utilised to respond rapidly to demand where needed, recruiting high value resources rather than increasing the quantity of resources. 
Our professional development and knowledge transfer approaches, as described in T3.1, minimise the impact of resource shortages by reducing reliance on individuals through T-shaped skill development and flexible resource pools that can be accessed at short notice. If there is an unplanned shortage, with your agreement, we will use other resource sources if required (e.g., industry and offshore teams, Partner Ecosystem). 
Our succession planning approach adds value through encouraging a high performing team, enabling individuals the opportunity to take on more challenging and/or senior roles as they gain more experience and knowledge.
