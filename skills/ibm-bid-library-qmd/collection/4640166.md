In rapid response to the pandemic, 200+ IBMers alongside civil servants/3rd parties, at a large central government department, are delivering one of the UK’s largest data transformation programmes. We developed the operating model, supporting data governance & privacy, and the Azure architecture where timeliness of data and insights, to drive policy, are paramount. At Social Security Scotland we built a new benefits system in 12 months, deploying new benefit types every 6 weeks. With a digital uptake of 90% and high satisfaction rating, the joint team won an MCA award for Best Change and Transformation Project in Public Sector 2020.
