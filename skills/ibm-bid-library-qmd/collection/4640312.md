Within 7 days IBM responded to the COVID-19 pandemic, worked with Gold Command to rapidly scale up our standing delivery and management capacity delivering a multi-disciplinary team experienced in NHS systems architecture, security and service management to provide a technical blueprint for the Nightingale hospitals. 
 
This enabled the central IT organisation to swiftly assess and validate technical readiness of the 10 hospitals ensuring a robust solution had been delivered. In parallel, IBM extended their Security Operations service for NHS Digital to provide 24/7 monitoring and alerting services; standing up additional resource to support extended hours without impacting existing critical services.
