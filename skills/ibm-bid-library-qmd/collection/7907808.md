Delivery Locations
We have resourced our team from our practices in the UK and India. We have proposed key leadership roles located with the UK and they will have the ability to be working with you in your Canada Square offices. Throughout our engagement on Cancer DSS with GEL we have regularly attended the GEL Offices for one or two days per week to hold working sessions and to build that all important rapport that allows a cohesive working environment.

Specifically to support the Onboarding Support engagement our technical team members covering, for example, Microsoft Dynamics, AWS Platforms and QA/Test engineers will be based out of India.  They will be working standard UK office hours and available for meetings from 09.00 through to 18.00 UK Time.  They will primarily be working out of IBM office locations however there maybe occasions where they will be working from home and they will be able to support effectively from either location.

Ways of Working
We will establish a Ways of Working Team Charter that all our team members will participate in the creation of and this will outline how the team will operate and specifically: why the team exists; its purpose, context, roles, procedures, workflows, and norms; and how the team members will accomplish their objectives.  This will include the daily and weekly interactions that each of our team members will be expected to attend and participate within – this will sit within the Agile Framework Ceremonies and will be customised to meet the requirement of delivering the enhancements to the Onboarding solution.

Working Remotely
Our Team is skilled in remote working and utilising the tools and technologies to able communication and collaboration to meet your goals. Whilst working on Cancer Decision Support System we delivered both a global team that sourced team members from UK, Romania, United Arab Emirates and India and for AWS Platform Engineers and Test Capability these were wholly sourced from offshore from India.  Our team understand the communications challenges and are adept in engaging with colleagues via Teams, Slack, Miro, Product Boards to both prepare appropriately for meetings, conduct them and allow an environment where all team members have the space and confidence to provide their input and feedback. 

We are mindful of the following factors when working remotely and our team are empathetic to these challenges:
- Video calls require more focus than face-to-face conversations – we have to work harder to process non-verbal cues
- Silence is unnatural in Video calls – there is a skill and art to facilitating online and embracing silence while thinking is normal in a face to face meeting but may need to be called out in an online meeting
- Workshop and meeting durations – always best to slice these into shorter more focused sessions to keep the energy levels high and design them to include collaboration activities where all attendees have a role to play.
 
Our team will consist of practitioners who are highly experienced in global delivery and therefore will ensure that these ways of working are enabled successfully within our joint team.
