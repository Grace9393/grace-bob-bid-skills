IBM – Quality Engineering – Quality Engineering

Our offering Framework

The Quality Engineering service is part of the IBM QE (IBM Quality Engineering) suite of offerings.

IBM QE offerings cover the full breadth of testing needs.

The purpose of these services is to help you deliver quality government services at scale and to get increasingly complicated applications to market faster, with fewer defects and at a lower cost.

Guided by our unique QE advisory offering, we will help our clients to build a tailored QE journey map, transforming their organisation to deliver meaningful quality change in 3 areas:

A shift in the Quality Culture: Driving cultural change and coaching best practice within programs and across your leadership team.

Across everyday activities: Enabling teams to embed quality and validation into their day-to-day delivery on their own technology stack.

Leveraging today’s technology, now: Embedded alongside development, IBM will use technology and automation to spread the quality load to a point where quality becomes everyone’s responsibility.

With IBM Quality Engineering, your enterprise can quickly and efficiently deliver high-quality products by leveraging unique optimisation, automation, and cognitive capabilities, along with a client's existing knowledge base and reusable IBM industry assets. IBM will also accelerate the modernisation journey by conducting practical cloud testing and leveraging AI to test AI-infused applications.

Addressing Business Needs

IBM’s proven approach to Quality Engineering will help your enterprise avoid common mistakes in transformation. IBM will focus on coaching anybody engaging across your application development life cycle, to adopt a 'Quality First' mindset aimed at ensuring Quality is embedded from the outset & is a constant throughout the process.

IBM QE Advisory Services offer an experience-based value proposition that includes access to IBM Research capabilities, extensive assets, effective processes, learning from thousands of QE engagements, and, most importantly, access to a highly skilled talent pool.

Our teams can help you address your requirements for testing essential government services with rigour and depth.

What is blocking quality today?

Enterprises that deliver new programs and solutions are experiencing similar quality-related issues:

Incumbent vendors not delivering quality outcomes. Approaches are manual, inflexible, effort-focused, with no innovation and a headcount which is too high and expensive

The “Burden” of Test Automation – an inability to automate, a lack of automation skills in your enterprise or experiencing high associated costs of automation

Not having enough time to test while still having demands to deliver at speed with quality and to align to an Agile/DevOps multiverse

Technology continues to evolve, which highlights an organisation’s inability to easily test API, digital solutions, SAP, Salesforce, AI or access skilled Quality Engineers in the marketplace.
