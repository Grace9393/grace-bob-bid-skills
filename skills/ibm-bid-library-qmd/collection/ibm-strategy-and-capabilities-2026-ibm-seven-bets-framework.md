# IBM Seven Bets Framework - Strategic Market Trends and Business Recommendations

Based on thousands of client interactions, deep-dive interviews, and survey results from thousands of executives, IBM has identified seven business trends that will shape the world in the next three years—and seven strategic bets companies should make to benefit from them.

## Overview: Why These Bets Matter

When US President John F. Kennedy gave his famous "moon speech" in September 1962, he described "an hour of change and challenge, in a decade of hope and fear, in an age of both knowledge and ignorance." Those words resonate today as climate change, economic turmoil, geopolitical conflict, and generative AI converge in the aftermath of a pandemic.

**IBM's Core Belief**: We bet on human ingenuity. Working together, we can slow global warming, prevent the next pandemic, manage systemic financial risks, cure cancer, and create a more sustainable future for humankind. The bets we make today redefine what's possible tomorrow.

## Implementation Approach: Making Your Bets

### Assessment Phase
**Evaluate Current State**:
1. Rate your organization's readiness for each bet (1-5 scale)
2. Identify gaps between current capabilities and required capabilities
3. Prioritize bets based on industry dynamics and competitive position
4. Assess resource requirements and potential ROI for each bet

### Strategy Phase
**Develop Integrated Roadmap**:
1. Select 2-3 primary bets aligning with business strategy
2. Design complementary initiatives across selected bets
3. Establish metrics and KPIs for measuring success
4. Identify quick wins to build momentum and demonstrate value

### Execution Phase
**Implement with Agility**:
1. Launch pilot programs with clear success criteria
2. Apply IBM Garage methodology for rapid experimentation
3. Scale successful pilots across the enterprise
4. Continuously iterate based on outcomes and learning

### Optimization Phase
**Scale and Sustain**:
1. Institutionalize successful practices into operating model
2. Expand to additional use cases and business units
3. Build internal capabilities through training and development
4. Establish centers of excellence for ongoing innovation
