Proposed delivery model for the Customer Portal SOW 
Our proposal for the Customer Portal migration is to adopt an on-site and off-shore delivery model, applying our Convergent (hybrid) delivery methodology. Our jointly managed SLC Customer Garage will work as a virtual delivery engine across on-site (Glasgow) and off-shore (India) Client Innovation Centers, led by the Customer Garage Board and coordinated by the Customer Garage Interface Team. 
The benefits of our approach include the delivery of a committed outcome, delivery schedule, outputs (as per Policy to Production) and ability to commit to a Fixed Price (aligning with your systems of measurement). 
We will establish the SLC Customer Garage and governance model early, with a bias to on-site resourcing up-front, transitioning to off-shore throughout the delivery and development cycle. Detailed planning and dependency mapping will be completed in collaboration with you and the other Strategic Partners and agreed through the Garage Board. 
At the start of this project we will create a Benefits Realisation Plan to monitor and manage the desired outcomes as we proceed through the project. In addition, we will measure and report against the Earned Value metrics stated in your SOW. This is the first project we will deliver with you, so we will also aim to deliver immediate improvements to the project related measures within your Balanced Scorecard such as ‘Increase in % of project milestones met’ and ‘Decrease in average turnaround time of CRs’.
