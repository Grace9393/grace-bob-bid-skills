We have tooling, that can be set-up at a project/ service level, for managing planned absence which also includes identifying appropriate cover. Team members add their planned absence keeping it up-to-date and this overseen by the engagement/ delivery lead. 
We have initiatives to minimise unplanned absence from setting expectations on role, location, and a professional working day through to how we invest in mental health and well-being, provide flexible working, provide infrastructure for secure remote working and provide autonomy to our teams, not micro-managing them. 
We adopt the following steps to maintain team knowledge and capacity to achieve continued service delivery. 
- Establish clear responsibilities across the whole team to avoid single points of failure, ensuring there are peers with appropriate skills who can cross cover as needed whether this be due to planned or unplanned absence - the IBM team structure includes a great cross section of experience, with more junior resources in cross team roles to provide support across projects when required
- Reduce the dependency on an individuals’ knowledge through our T-shaped skills team composition squad ways of working (agile ceremonies); IBM Strategic Sponsor oversight; pair programmes; succession planning to guard against single points of failure; escalation processes; and transparent tooling (eg. JIRA)
- Foster a culture of continual development and responsibility to others, including cross education of roles across both senior and junior employees to develop and enhance skills and provide cover where needed
- Use automation and DevSecOps to minimise reliance on manual tasks and improve stability and availability of service delivery, and
- Where services require call-out provisions, we adopt “hunt groups” which if an automated support call does not reach a team member within a configured time limit it will automatically dial the next support contact in the list until the call is answered.
