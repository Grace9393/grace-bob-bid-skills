Our external peer review process will ensure alignment of t across HMT partners, including requirements, design, and outputs review.
For DaaS, we anticipate the external reviewers predominantly being the ERCoE, the IaaS Provider, and Defence Digital (for compliance).
We’ll achieve the above reviews in conjunction with governance structures such as the joint design authority for key decisions e.g. architecture, and standards. The physical process will vary depending on the artefacts under review and will build trust between the partners.
We’ll tailor our process for each engagement and will do so for HMT considering aspects such as frequency of review, artefacts (data structures, APIs, non-functional requirements, access) and review cycles.
We recognise that effective ways of working between ERCoE, DaaS and IaaS Providers is critical. The external review process is key to supporting successful solution definition, design, and delivery.
Our technical design, solutions, quality assurance, governance, and delivery management are accredited to BS EN ISO/9001 and BS EN ISO/IEC 27001 27002.
On our MOD GUARDIAN project, we undertake an external peer review of our supplier’s architecture, designs, and documentation. Likewise, we provide our designs and plans to our suppliers for their review. Activities across our suppliers are externally reviewed by our safety team against SIL1 in IEC 61508. The safety management system is externally reviewed by independent safety auditors acting on behalf of the Military Airworthiness Authority.
At the Home Office, we are SAS Delivery Partner, helping define a new IT operating model including ways of working and a Collaboration Agreement across the supplier ecosystem – including the Peer Review Process.  We are responsible for the Data & Design Authority across new and existing projects and services. This includes undertaking external peer reviews of architectures, designs and documentation across the ecosystem of suppliers.
