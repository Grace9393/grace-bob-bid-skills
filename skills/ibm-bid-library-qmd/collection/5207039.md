We will develop a clear SIM RACI covering our teams, third parties and wider stakeholders. During service transition we will agree those responsibilities with you; then review regularly to ensure they are up to date. 
 
The roles and responsibilities are incorporated into our playbooks and operational procedures which all our team use to quickly identify who is required during any situation. Shift leaders are responsible for ensuring that the playbooks are used, and correct escalation procedures are followed. All team members will review the RACI and are given regular briefings should the RACI change or as a refresher as part of CSIP.
