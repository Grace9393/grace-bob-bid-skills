All of our Cúram consultants have trained, assisted and mentored less experienced resources - both junior resources and senior resources. For example on the PIP project, Cúram technical consultants mentored and assisted client technical staff ranging from junior test team and support members, through to senior architect resources who had 20+ years industry experience but who needed to understand the Cúram application in more depth. The client team recognised the IBM team's professionalism and dedication in spending time to impart their valuable knowledge.
