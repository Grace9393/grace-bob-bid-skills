We have existing relationships which will be leveraged to benefit UKHSA: research facilities, academic institutions, and technology partners, etc. As a large and influential part of your team are scientists and academics, you may want to draw on this capability more broadly to support the Big Rocks transformation.
Academia and Research: We have deep experience in healthcare and life science innovations, and we have our own academic engine, IBM Research (IBMR) to develop and bring new innovations to our clients. IBMR is a group of 3000+ global researchers, scientists, technologists, engineers, and designers who invest in ‘What's Next in Computing’. We have 12 labs and have led the ranking in informatics patents for the last 28 years in the US, with 6 Nobel and 6 Turing winners. In healthcare and life science, IBMR has made major research and development breakthroughs using the latest advancements in AI, hybrid cloud and quantum, such as enabling genomic bio pipeline workflows in hybrid cloud and designing personalised care and healthcare products. We routinely work with many universities and academic health science networks (AHSNs) to introduce innovation into healthcare:
- Oxford University Innovation – the consulting arm that supplies academics to consulting work, who EY are currently partnering with to support UKHSA’s COVID Mass Testing Programme

- Saïd Business School – conducted research on effective ways to drive organisational change. This created EY’s framework to increase success when implementing change

- Oxford Pandemic Sciences Institute and the Big Data Institute – supporting the development of new diagnostics, treatments vaccines and digital disease control 

- Pankhurst Institute – strategic partner for a health innovation accelerator

- UK Research and Innovation – partnered with IBM to enable organisations to adopt innovations, such as AI and quantum, by launching an AI and quantum computing centre

- Cleveland Clinic – have a 10-year partnership mission with IBM of advancing the pace of discovery in healthcare and life sciences using the hybrid cloud, AI and quantum computing technologies

- HPC Consortium – during COVID, IBM was a core contributor to the private-public HPC consortium, making available 603 petaflops of capacity for discovery in the fight against the virus

Our Strategic Partners(and at times, Suppliers): Recognising your needs may change as requirements evolve, we will be responsive, agile, and flexible to this. When needing broader expertise or the ability to ramp-up more significantly, we will leverage our strong alliances with AWS and Microsoft. As a Premier AWS Partner Network member, some of our people are AWS Heroes and Ambassadors, providing early access to AWS product roadmaps and previews of new features. Our partnership with Microsoft provides access to multiple Azure assets, accelerators, and offerings to drive efficiencies at scale. We have attained 34k certifications and 15 advanced specialisms (such as application integration and development, cloud, data platform and analytics) exemplified by many successful implementations and capabilities.
We will establish a partner ecosystem quarterly governance board to discuss latest product roadmaps, academic level research, thought leadership, opportunities for joint innovation and partner investment, keeping UKHSA and the Client-Side Partner ahead of the market.
