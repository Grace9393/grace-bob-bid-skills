IBM have delivered some of the largest AAD/M365 rationalisation programmes in the world, including the migration of 120 tenants into a single tenant. We know that rationalisation programmes of this kind are hard, but are foundational to drive future technology modernisation, and in a secure manner.
IBM have created a dedicated approach to M365 rationalisation, which combines repeatable architectural patterns with a user and business change approach that enhances user adoption whilst reducing migration timelines and issues relating to the consolidation process. We have found that the impact on the users and their experience has a significant influence on the success of the programme, which is why our fundamental approach for the Big Rocks programme is to execute a robust business change strategy to underpin the migration.
We would ensure the Big Rock outcomes are delivered by applying our wealth of experience in tenant rationalisation, combined with our tooling and processes to enable a streamlined rationalisation and migration programme for UKHSA, mitigating common risks early whilst reducing user friction and helpdesk calls. 
Our experience is demonstrated by our work for a large American Media and Marketing Organisation, where we migrated and consolidated over 120 separate M365 tenants into a single M365 tenant that enabled seamless collaboration and communication across 1,500+ organisations within the parent organisation.  In this programme, we also delivered a single standard identity and directory service to drive a consistent approach to federated SSO, authentication and authorisation across the organisation’s portfolio of companies and applications.
