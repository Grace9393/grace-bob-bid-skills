We will bring to you our extensive experience working in rainbow teams delivering secure, robust, platforms for business-critical applications using agile methodologies. Key expertise encompasses: 
- Developing, evolving and supporting your cloud platforms; 
- Supporting your development teams to successfully adopt, use and maximise benefit from the platforms;
- Providing DevOps and SRE (site reliability engineering) expertise;
- Supporting and preparing for AWS Well Architected Framework reviews, bringing our AWS Premier Partnership and close working relationship to support HMPO.
 
Our team will be selected on their experience with platform and DevOps solutions, where they have used modern cloud technologies, and with a clear demonstration of collaborative behaviours for improved outcomes.
 
As a similar capability and context, the Home Office Law Enforcement Data Service (LEDS) had no suitable cloud hosting platform to store sensitive policing data. We provided support and design advice to create an AWS-based cloud platform with high automation that met the stringent security requirements. This enabled Home Office to successfully transfer data from the Police National Computer and Police National Database to public cloud for the first time.
We have provided design guidance to Home Office on the creation of the Law Enforcement Cloud Platform (LECP) which builds on LEDS design concepts. Similar to HMPO working with the EBSA platform and team, we work as the ‘bridge’ between the LEDS development teams and the LECP platform. We provide support, guidance and best practice on how to use the platform to meet project development goals, and feedback enhancement proposals to the LECP platform team to better meet tenants needs.
