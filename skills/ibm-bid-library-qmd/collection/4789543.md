We have been working with UK Government for 20+ years, delivering many solutions based around the Risk Management and Accreditation Document Set (RMADS), based on these principles of assessing risk, developing security architectures, policies and processes to reduce  risks and then auditing  the BAU delivery to ensure those policies and processes are being followed. Working with the Home Office/NCSC on the IABS programme we developed the security architecture, which significantly reduced security risk at the start of the programme.  Our teams worked to up-skill and han- over to in house teams to evolve the programme for the following 10 years.
