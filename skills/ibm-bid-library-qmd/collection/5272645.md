IBM is ISO27001 & HIPAA certified & our sub-contractor, SCW CSU, is certified to IG Toolkit Level2. SCW staff who process sensitive personal data are seconded to NHS D & comply with related IG Governance including Data Protection Act and GDPR. Personal Identifiable Data(PID) is landed into a secure/ring fenced environment where access is managed with Role Based Access Control(RBAC). Data processed within this environment is tightly controlled/managed/disseminated to authorised recipients. PID is processed within NHS D approved Regional Processing Centre where it is logged/scanned/validated prior to dissemination. PID is separated and stored away from main dataset and maintained with an elevated level of security. Pseudonymised data is released in accordance with asset registers with NHS Opt Outs redacted. Identifiable fields (NHS Number) are pseudonymised using non-reversible hashing. SCW manage processing environment on behalf of NHS D with a population coverage of 12.4 million.
