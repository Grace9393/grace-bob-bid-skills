Working with Charité Berlin, Europe's largest university hospital, we co-developed the HealthAdvisor machine learning-based application to determine a patient’s individual stroke risk. We provided in-depth market research interviewing and involving representatives of the risk group, as well as physicians and nurses to create a marketable product that shows people with an increased risk of stroke their personal risk profile, and also visualizes which factors influence it. This helps those affected to change their behaviour in a self-motivated manner to avoid a future stroke. Through these insights we built out the service concept, tested and successfully tested in a pilot market.
