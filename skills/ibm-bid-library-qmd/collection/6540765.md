To enshrine your philosophy and ways of working into the delivery functions, we will use agile techniques such as social contracts, retrospectives, collaboration tools, architectural principles and guardrails, and embed this way of work as part of the culture. We will also foster engineering culture and ways of working from the start, such as pair programming, mob programming, Test Driven Development (TDD), blameless post-mortems, chaos engineering, etc. as well as Engineering Guilds to drive consistency and best practice across the teams. 
 
We believe very deeply in the “you build it, you run it” philosophy and this approach features in all IBM delivery methods which put agile and DevOps at the centre. This model has consistently shown that when a team is responsible for running their application there is marked improvement in how applications/services are logged, monitored, tested, and deployed. This sense of ownership improves the quality of the solution and improves speed of incident resolution. Fewer handoffs, less time communicating, and more time fixing means production incidents are solved faster. We have extensive experience with this model with clients such as TK App (our case study), HSE (where we are delivering a vaccine management service), BP, Audi, Lloyds, Scottish Govt, HMRC, DWP, Virgin Money and TSB Bank. We have seen an average productivity gain of 10% per year, when clients adopt this approach. 
 
To maintain a healthy separation of duties, when applying these principles to the production environment, our teams use the principles of SRE (Site Reliability engineering). SREs are engineers that sit across the lifecycle, working with the development teams to develop code whilst also maintaining the production system. These SREs have differing access to the average team member, meaning that a deployment into production requires both SRE and team members, a separation of duties, to be achieved without separating knowledge between people. 
 
The DevOps function is constructed so that the DevOps/SRE engineers will be responsible for the running of the core application and platform in-production. For the teams to consider non-functional/operational elements an SRE model will be embedded (SLO’s, SLI’s, Error budgets). This allows each feature team to own their functionality and run it end-to-end, so platform standards, consistency and quality are upheld.
