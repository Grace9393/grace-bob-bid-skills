User research is a key tenet of our Design Thinking led delivery approach, including a dedicated User Researcher community. We have a wide range of research experience, including the design of A-B usability testing, and management of panels and interviews. In our AI chat delivery for Vodafone, with customers engaging with a bot to answer queries and make account changes, we designed and ran a series of user engagement studies to shape the solution to retain high customer satisfaction. This included follow-up interviews with key user groups to continually refine and improve. IBM have capacity to supply this experience immediately.
