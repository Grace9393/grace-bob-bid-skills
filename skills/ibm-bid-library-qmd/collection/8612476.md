- Market-leading SaaS low-code/no-code platforms from our global alliance partners, Salesforce, AWS, and Quantexa
- Socially responsible SME delivery partners who bring specific digital delivery skills, who we have a track-record of working with in UK Government. 
- An Innovation partnership with the XXXXX and XXXXX, and recruitment of small business users through Enterprise Nation.
Working together:
- A joint STW Leadership fostering collaboration between STW Programme, Deloitte, IBM, and an SME representative 
- Deloitte and IBM Steering Group, specifically to govern our ways-of-working, ensuring a joint approach to managing risk and bringing the best of our global organisations, chaired by James Taylor, Deloitte GPS Lead and Rhodri Arrowsmith, IBM GPS Lead.
- Blended delivery teams, using a mix of Deloitte and IBM experts across all workstreams, and supplemented by specialist skills from our SME suppliers.

- An ethos of collaboration, one-team, with badges left at the door (as described in 5.10), to achieve overall outcomes.
