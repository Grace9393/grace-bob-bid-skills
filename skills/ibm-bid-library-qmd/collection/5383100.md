How we will understand your skills gaps 
We’ll use our Component Business Model for to agree the functions you expect to provide, particularly in your ICF, and the roles needed to support those functions. We’ll then use our Watson Talent Development Framework to understand your current skills and the gaps in the skills you need. This framework is our ready-to- use skills taxonomy that provides industry-specific job profiles and the associated skills needed. We’ll use it to understand the capabilities you need in your ICF (and more widely if appropriate) and the skills you have today. Mapping those two views together will tell us your current skills gaps. We used this framework internally to identify our own skills gaps and successfully reskill over 10,000 roles, to better align with our business strategy and priorities for the future. 
Once we’ve understood your skills gaps, we’ll use our Workforce Strategy model to define how we’ll close those gaps over time. We anticipate that the ICF will evolve through several iterations, so we expect to agree an evolving, prioritised ‘to be’ skills target in your workforce strategy. We have recently implemented this model at a leading UK broadcaster to assess, enhance and maintain their digital capabilities. After demonstrating its value, the model has now been scaled for their long term workforce plan to over 27,000 employees. We’ll use our model and industry best practice insights to help set your direction, whilst continually adapting in line with current and future needs. Our model will also help you identify the key skills you need to establish the effective governance necessary to manage your partners. 
We see the process of understanding your skills gaps as a perfect opportunity to engage your people in your future and the opportunities it offers for them to develop their own skills. We can help you paint a picture of their role in realising your future that engages them in learning, encourages them to put learning into practice, and creates advocacy for the transformation you’re undertaking. We developed the IBM Garage framework to do exactly that for IBMers, and it worked so well we made it core to our delivery approach used successfully with clients including Nationwide, Scottish Government, The Rugby Football Union, American Airlines and Frito-Lay.
