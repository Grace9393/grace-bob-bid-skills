We have implemented a number of innovative commercial models designed to deliver mutual benefits to our clients and to IBM. These include traditional commercial constructs based on FTE, Per Log-in Hour, Per Transaction through to fixed price with committed outcomes, and full Outcome Based ‘pay as you go’ models.
Typically, we tailor the pricing model to the client preferences and characteristics of the service or project. In our experience Time & Materials (variable based in rate card) is mostly suitable when the client is leading and IBM is providing skills or capacity, and Fixed Price when the scope and timeframe is fixed.
We can enhance these models to tailor the pricing to the needs of the client and the nature of the program. For example, fees at risk / reward basis based on:
•Risk sharing
•Efficiency improvement
•Project certainty, budget and quality
•Realisation of business objectives (including product focussed ones)
•
A fixed capacity model can also be considered for significant-sized project with many tasks, or a series of projects and continued for an extended period of time. Particularly if all the requirements are not clear from the beginning and the project's scope is constantly changing, a fixed capacity team can help with quality. This model agrees a dedicated team’s skills and size and a fixed period of time for the team’s effort.
