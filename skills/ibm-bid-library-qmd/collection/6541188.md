The initial engagement from IBM will involve mobilisation of our teams and transition from the incumbent resources to IBM as the new supplier. It will be crucial that any ongoing delivery continues and is not impacted whilst transition is in progress. Our approach is sprint-based, focusing on incoming team-members assimilating quickly into existing teams through gaining hands on experience, learning through doing and avoiding a ‘big bang’ transition which increases business risk. We have used this approach at many clients, including at NHS Test and Trace in November 2021. Figure 3. shows the high-level plan on a page for transition and mobilisation for the Vaccination Programme. The plan shows the steady ramp up of resources starting from the 22nd of April with all resources being in place by 17th June. The transition plan is split out into sprints which include the following comprehensive activities: 
Sprint 0 - Plan 
Starts with a kick-off on 22nd April which will be attended by our lead and senior team members as indicated on the mobilisation plan.  
Detailed planning of the transition, mobilisation and knowledge transfer takes place. This will include mapping of key capabilities and areas of responsibility to establish a 1:1 handoff to the receiving resources. This establishes a culture of positive collaboration to ensure smooth transition and collective purpose, while also checking the responsibilities between parties to ensure nothing is missed. It will also provide a solid base for the IBM team to begin analysis on topics and focal areas to drive initial continuous improvement activities which can then be built upon. 
On-boarding of the IBM team will commence to include access to systems, tooling, and provision of laptops (if applicable). 
Sprint 1 – Learn/Plan 
Initial knowledge transfer. IBM will run a combination of self-study, interactive knowledge transfer sessions with the existing teams. These sessions will be recorded to enable playback and to produce a resource that can be used for training new team-members in the future. 
Sprint 2 – Learn/Perform/Plan 
The incoming team members will shadow the existing team while they perform their roles. These observations will provide practical knowledge to build on the knowledge transfer from Sprint 1. 
The incoming team will play back to the existing team to demonstrate their understanding of the knowledge gained through the learn activities. 
Sprint 3 – Learn/Perform/Plan 
The incoming team members will carry out reverse-shadowing where they will lead activities under the guidance of the existing teams. 
Sprint 4 – Perform/Plan 
The incoming team members will now be fully assimilated into their teams, performing activities independently. 
Sprint 5 – Perform 
Teams will perform as ‘Business as Usual’. 
The transition acceptance criteria will be reviewed, and the transition closure report formally closed off. 
We will work with NHS D throughout the transition to raise and resolve any issues or concerns that arise during the transition phase. Feedback from each sprint will be used to inform any amendments needed to the transition and mobilisation plan or to highlight any issues to the programme team.
