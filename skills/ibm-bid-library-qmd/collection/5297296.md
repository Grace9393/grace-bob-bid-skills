- We will use Agile methodology and appropriate tools such as JIRA to manage a backlog of work, tracking individual stories against Epics on a four-week sprint basis; and capture changing requirements, scope, risks and dependencies as part of Sprint management. Delivery plans will also interlock with the Radars we produce to align with overall strategy. We have successfully taken this approach with the Department on multiple engagements, including 13 four-week sprints of activity with DS from 2019-2020. 
- We understand the importance of transparent delivery for DS and its stakeholders. We have produced and presented key project progress updates each week and provided a summary of the activities completed/to be done, including key risks, issues and dependencies.
