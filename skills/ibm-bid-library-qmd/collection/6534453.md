We understand there's a significant demand for NHS login to keep evolving to satisfy the demand of digital healthcare applications in a cost-effective manner whilst adding value for your end users. A key approach to achieving this is agreeing a 'Value Orchestration Framework' as part of the mobilisation phase which sets out the ways in which we will measure the value. By focussing on UCD principles, we’ll iteratively improve and avoid expensive large scale rework that could impact your organisational goals. 
Our designers will balance the often conflicting needs of users with business requirements and technical constraints successfully because we are equipped with tools from 'IBM's 'Enterprise Design Thinking’ framework, to bring user-centred thinking to the forefront of even the most technology-centred stakeholders minds. This will be needed when integrating the NHS login with HMPO's passport solution for instance, by reimagining the end-to-end experience with the end user in mind; or when redesigning the ID Checker App to ensure verification is done in the most effective manner from the outset.
