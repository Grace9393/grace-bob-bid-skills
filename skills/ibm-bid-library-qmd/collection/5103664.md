As a strategic supplier to the Home Office we actively support the use of SMEs in our supply-chain and have a well-established supplier ecosystem that share our key business ethics and focus on delivery excellence.  Whilst bringing an SME into that ecosystem we align their skills and domain knowledge to your need and balance the risk profile appropriately, assessing what commercial terms are required and how we can remove overburdening liabilities for our SMEs. We commit to providing value for money by aligning to your rate cards e.g. Optimus,  and work closely with SMEs to ensure we can both commit to meeting your delivery outcomes using those target rates.
 
We note that some target rates within the Optimus approach for specific in-demand skills maybe difficult for SME's to support, given higher costs in the market.  In order to build an effective supplier ecosystem it is essential that whatever rate card targets are used  e.g. Optimus or CCS, these reflect the current dynamics of the market and specific areas of demand. We note the approach to classify all subcontractors as  'key'  and as such go through the full CCS on-boarding process, this will result in on-boarding time implications and drive a less dynamic ecosystem as CCS have to approve each SME. This attracts a cost and potentially could preclude some SME's if they do not meet all criteria. We would suggest a tiered system for larger 'key' subcontractors and a more dynamic light-touch process for smaller SME's offering niche services.
