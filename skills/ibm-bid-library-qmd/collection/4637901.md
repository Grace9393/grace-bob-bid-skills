Mentoring junior staff is embedded in IBM's culture. We attract design professionals who work with their design schools to run events and mentor the next generation, many of whom join IBM’s award winning graduate programme where they receive our externally recognised design training. We mentor junior staff for our clients. At DWP (2018-9) we established a 1:1 mentoring model between IBM and DWP team members. We ran weekly knowledge sharing sessions covering User Research, Agile, Design Thinking and UX Design. This collaborative upskilling approach was extremely successful, enabling us to transition responsibilities to DWP teams, achieving an award-winning service.
