IBM will work with you to develop the approach, scope, priorities and constraints of the effort as well as the statement of work for the successful exit of services for the Test and Trace Service Contract in the form of an Exit Plan. 
If you decide to bring the support totally back under the support of yourself or a new vendor, then IBM will provide “Exit Assistance” by adopting the IBM Reverse Transition (Exit) approach. This process includes a set of methods and tools to transition the services. A joint NHSD and/or new vendor and IBM reverse transition team will be formed that will coordinate and manage the associated activities to integrate transition processes, procedures and methodology across impacted groups, enabling a seamless transfer of the services including the novation of any contracts as required. 
The IBM Reverse Transition (Exit) approach is a proven transition approach which will minimise risk to the live service with the aim of providing a seamless change in support. The approach will use the Guided, Assisted, Parallel Perform methodology; a proven methodology IBM uses to transition services to a different supplier. The Guided Perform phase sees the incoming support teamwork alongside the existing team and watch them complete their day-to-day work. This can be either be co-located or using technology to facilitate, i.e. webex/teams etc. The Assisted Perform phase is where the incoming team take over completing the work with the existing team available to provide advice, guidance and instruction where necessary. Finally, the Parallel Perform phase is where the incoming team is fully responsible for the work and can work independently of the existing team. The existing support team is available to support if needed or to input into any gaps in the knowledge base. 
Knowledge transfer will be fundamental to the success of the reverse transition and as such, IBM will develop the plan and execute it in such a manner as to align with your requirements and expectations up to the final cutover of services from IBM to you and/or new vendor. 
The overall Reverse Transition project will be managed by an experienced IBM (Exit) Project Manager who will work to realize your and IBM’s objectives, including: 
- Flexibility to minimise service impacts and risks 
- Innovation, quality service to achieve your satisfaction 
- Meeting of price/cost targets and speed to respond 
IBM recommends an objective-based Reverse Transition to obtain maximum results. Following are the objectives that will be achieved: 
- Transfer of operational processes, knowledge transfer, redeployment of assets and human resources 
- Closing of the organisational services 
- Controlling, monitoring and closing activities that runs through the project, including Control and Close phases of the project 
 
We fully appreciate that with any exit or transition event there are concerns and risks about the quality of the knowledge transfer and the ability of the incoming supplier (be this NHSD or another provider) to manage the service from the point of transition. To mitigate this we have included specialist exit management resourcing in our overall proposal to provide assistance to you and your partners regardless of when you decide to exit this contract. This specialist resource will manage the exit plan on your behalf. This role combined with the key personnel you have requested will form the core governance for the smooth transition of services to yourselves or your preferred supplier. 
As part of our Reverse Transition (Exit) approach we have previously utilised a system of scoring to provide increased assurance related to the readiness of the new party to take on the smooth running of service. Throughout the transition phase both the incoming provider and ourselves score the readiness of the incoming supplier to take on the operational service. We propose working with you to identify a scoring approach as part of reverse transition to provide you and any future provider with an increased level of confidence that your service levels will be maintained and that the handover of service is complete. 
Our proposed solution with you assumes contracting licence agreements directly with our partners which minimises the challenges of the novation of licences and assets in the event of an exit from the contract. The direct relationship you will have with the licence providers minimises the disruption of exiting a services contract. Our delivery, outside of the AWS and Salesforce licences, is delivered using Open Source tooling and as such there are no limitations to the novation of either the proposed toolset or the procurement by yourselves of a new suite of publicly available licences with any assets stored being migrated as part of our managed exit.
