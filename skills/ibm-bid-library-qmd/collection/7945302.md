- From our current engagement with SGN, we are aware that the optimal date for the Sentinel based MDR services to be production ready would be End of April 2023.  
- From our experience on contracting for these services, we also make a safe assumption that the services will kick off end of December 2022, giving us a period of 4 months for Transition and Transformation
IBM Definition of Transition & Transformation
At IBM, we typically define transition as the change of service provider teams from incumbent provider to IBM, without changing the service provision platform.  It follows that Transformation is the change of the service provision platform.  
After analysing the proposed Transition approach in the RFP document – “ITT_549 for MMS-Management of SGN SOC & SIEM – Instructions & Overview v1”, we have aligned with the transition program as described in that document.  However, we think the timelines suggested in the document are generous, and we will be able to deliver both transition and transformation in a shorter period than suggested.  For now, our plan assumes that SGN can meet their dependencies at the same pace as we suggest.  We can further fine-tune this timeline after the due-diligence process as part of the down-select.
We will execute a combined Transition and Transformation phase to provide SGN a leap into the Next Gen SOC on the Sentinel platform, while retaining the previous functionality and a smooth transition.
Proposed Transition Approach
IBM will perform transition of services from existing provider using a parallel delivery approach.   There are two Tracks to parallel delivery –
Track 1:
- The IBM team of analysts will work with the incumbent team to understand current mode of delivery, processes, interfaces, etc., They will also review existing documentation for runbooks, playbooks, reports etc.,
- After 4-5 weeks of working with the incumbent team, the IBM team will start to develop and rewrite the runbooks for the new SOC platform, develop and rewrite the playbooks aligned to the requirements specified in the RFP, and in general develop processes that are built on current but are aligned to requirements and reflect IBMs experience in best practice.
Track 2:
- The new Sentinel platform will be built in this track, and all logs will be sent parallelly to this platform also, apart from the current SIEM platform. Majority of the log sources are expected to support parallel destinations from our review.
Given the two tracks, at the end of 3 months, we expect to have a parallel working SOC service on our end and can be tested for effectiveness with minimum risk.  The primary advantage of this risk is that for the most part, it keeps the involvement of the current incumbent provider at a minimum.  IBM can fill the gaps where the provider is unwilling or unable to provide information.  
The timelines will be:
Track 1
- Jan 9th 2023 to Feb 17th 2023 – Review of current documentation and rewrite
- Feb 17th to Mar 31st – Production of SOC service design, Runbook, documentation of playbooks, and completion of all documentation
Track 2
- Jan 9th 2023 to Mar 31st 2023 – Build of Sentinel platform, configuration of log sources, parallel ingestion into the platform, configuration of basic threat intelligence, basic MDR integration, integration of vulnerability management, basic SOAR capability (orchestration).
Parallel Run
- Apr 3rd 2023 to Apr 28th 2023 – Parallel run of SOC services on the Sentinel platform
- May 1st 2023 – Go Live on the new platform.
