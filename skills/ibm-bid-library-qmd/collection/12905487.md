Quality Assurance of Resource Output
To assure the quality of output from our teams, we build in assurance steps at every stage of delivery: 
- Assigning the right resource: We’ll find the best match for the role, through a thorough assessment of the resource skills, experience, and expertise aligned with the specific technical requirements of the role. 
- Clear Roles and Responsibilities:  We’ll clearly define roles within the team, to ensure each member understands their responsibilities and conduct daily stand-up meetings to keep everyone updated on progress and issues. Regular sprint reviews and retrospectives will provide opportunities for feedback and continuous improvement. 
- Deliverable assurance: We’ll firstly agree on deliverable format, structure and relevant standards with you to ensure expectations are aligned at the outset. We’ll have transparent tracking of progress so you are assured of timely delivery and we’ll validate the quality of work products by performing work product peer reviews and assigning dedicated QA team members or rotating the responsibility within the team. 
- Measurable Metrics: We’ll establish quality metrics, such as defect rates, customer satisfaction scores, adherence to coding standards, etc and maintain up-to-date documentation to encourage knowledge sharing across the team, which helps drive quality and ensures continuity even if team members change. Monthly reviews will verify the relevance of reported metrics, ensuring they align with your objectives, inform data-driven decisions, and enable proactive performance management of the service.
To provide added value, our Chief Architect will conduct regular delivery and technical governance reviews to ensure delivery health. We have successfully applied this model across DWP Digital for the last 20 years.
