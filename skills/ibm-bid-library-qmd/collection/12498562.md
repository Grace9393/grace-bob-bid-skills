R1 Climate Emergency
We share your commitment and goals to reduce emissions through the delivery of this
contract, as declared in the Climate Change (Scotland) Act. As a responsible business and one
of Forbes Top 100 Net Zero Leaders, we will achieve net zero by 2030 to counter the climate
emergency and will support you to do the same.
On this contract, our teams will be working on your sites and comply with your policies, using
your laptops and systems. As well as our company wide targets and initiatives, we will work with
you on the following contract specific reductions. We will contribute to the Scottish Ministers
objectives of creating a more sustainable Scotland for current and future generations, through
influencing our staff, suppliers, customers and communities to support environmental
improvement and protection.
Working with ARE to Meet Targets to Reduce Energy Consumption
We are on target to achieve net zero by 2030 through a programme of initiatives including:
• Migration to renewable energy
• Implementing a minimum of 3,000 energy conservation projects to avoid the
consumption of 275,000 megawatt-hours (MWh) of energy from 2021 to 2025
• Procuring 75% of the electricity we consume worldwide from renewable sources by
2025, and 90% by 2030 – we are currently at 70.6%
• Embedding Green IT into our own operations, and
• Designing and procuring energy efficient hardware.
Although these initiatives are not specifically for this contract, this will directly contribute to your
targets to be net zero by 2045 whilst we are part of your supply chain.
Reducing Carbon Footprint in the Delivery of This Contract
As a company we have 21 environment sustainability goals which we continue to exceed. We
report upon our progress against them each year, including:
• Continuing to reduce IBM’s GHG emissions following on from our successful reduction
of GHG emissions to 68.5% in 2023
• Exceeding our goal to plant 50 pollinator gardens at company locations globally by
year-end 2023 to support biodiversity by planting 70, and
• Diverting 94.2% of our non-hazardous waste from landfill and incineration to recycling
in 2023.
We have long-standing environmental policies of providing a safe and healthy workplace,
protecting the environment, and conserving energy and natural resources since 1971. These
policies are governed and reviewed by our board annually and we maintain full compliance with
ISO14001 (certified by Bureau Veritas, UK). We have eight programs dedicated to these
policies including energy and climate, water conservation, and product reuse, recycling and
disposal.
At the start of this contract, we will apply our CO2 Project Estimating tool to assess how Scope
1, 2 and 3 emissions will be reduced over the lifetime of this contract based on staffing
levels. Our estimation method is based on published corporate commitments which are applied
pro rata per employee. We will share this data with you in our contract management meetings
and with our teams to improve awareness of our own actions, to baseline and set CO2
reduction targets, and to drive behavioural change across this contract.
This assessment will exclude additional contributions we will make through local reductions in
our logistics operations, waste management and energy efficiency practices outlined
below, specifically for this contract.

• Healthier lifestyle choices such as walking or cycling, to reduce our impact on the
environment and support the wellbeing of our teams.
Waste Management
The Circular Economy Bill was passed in June 2024 requiring Ministers in Scotland to prepare
and publish a ‘Circular Economy Strategy’ to set circular economy targets and address waste
reduction, recycling, and management. In addition, the Scottish Government led the way by
being the first UK nation to legislate to removal of single-use plastic. We commit to helping you
achieve your waste reductions and recycling targets.
IBM partners with accredited and licensed waste management companies, selected for their
‘zero waste to landfill’ policies, innovative solutions and net zero ambition.
Our teams on this contract will comply with your waste management policies. We will mandate
training on recycling, legislation in Scotland and our policies through our onboarding process.
This will educate on industry best practices that maximise recycling and minimise waste

Raise Environmental Awareness Through Education
Alongside supporting sustainable projects, we have started a pilot programme in Dundee with Education Scotland and SCQF to educate students and teachers on green and technology skills to better shape students for a more sustainable future.
Using our free online learning programme SkillsBuild, we offer self-paced learning pathways to help tackle sustainability issues and an opportunity to achieve industry-recognised digital credentials. We will work with you to target schools and charitable organisations in underserved communities to get them using SkillsBuild and inspire future generations to adopt sustainable practices. You too can use SkillsBuild to educate your workforce and other suppliers and inspire creative thinking to implement sustainable solutions.
 
Drive Sustainable Behaviour Change Through Volunteering
We will provide our teams with a range of opportunities to support community initiatives through our IBM Community Engagement Portal and in partnership with Goodera. The contract team, including other suppliers, will be invited to participate in sustainability initiatives such as creating pollinator gardens and building young planter's kits to inspire future environmental stewards. IBM donates $10 for every hour of volunteering logged on the portal, which we can donate to support local non-profits fighting climate change in Scotland.
 
For example, like this year, we will participate in the annual Scottish Government Earth Day Event to showcase our support to Scottish communities and you on your sustainable journey.
 
Embed Sustainability Initiatives in Our Ways of Working Through Collaboration
We believe in building sustainability into our projects which is why we will embed Sustainability Champions into our project teams on this contract.
 
Our appointed Sustainability Champions will be integrated with a wider IBM Sustainability Public Sector group with SMEs and access to global resources, meeting bi-weekly to share ideas and best practices. We will use this forum to reflect on current initiatives and implement continuous improvements. 
 
Our Champions will work with your dedicated sustainability leads and other suppliers on this programme, to set up a sustainability working group and implement quarterly checkpoints. They will work collaboratively to:
 
- Set and communicate clear objectives and a climate emergency strategy for this contract
- Drive and deliver climate emergency initiatives 
- Understand and share policies and processes across all contract suppliers to ensure alignment, and
- Agree account wide comms to ensure consistency in messaging.
We will communicate any changes to policies with our teams regularly via our Sustainability Champions, through our internal dedicated Slack channels and during our All-Hands meetings to ensure circular economy principles are embedded in this contract. We too believe in co-designing and co-creating a fairer, greener future for all like the Just Transition Commission.
