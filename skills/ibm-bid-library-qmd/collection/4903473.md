One of our core objectives is to increase the number of Civil Servants in digital roles, ultimately enabling self-sufficiency. Over the years we have worked with many government clients bringing our extensive delivery experiences into the team from day-1 and ensuring effective knowledge and skills transfer throughout our partnership. 
 
Our approach starts with joint planning sessions to get an initial skills baseline across the data family teams.  As part of service transition, we will run joint bootcamps creating an environment for shared learning, understanding and alignment of vision. Whilst we recognise your intent is for us to transfer knowledge to you, we equally look forward to learning from you, gaining rich knowledge from your existing teams to support the platform evolution over time. In terms of culture and ways of working, we welcome the opportunity to become part of your team working together collaboratively to deliver the vision. We will agree a skills transition over the contract duration embedding people across the teams to work together in gaining knowledge and understanding of the solution.  This will help HMPO staff to gain self-sufficiency over the contract duration.

At the Disclosure and Barring Service, we delivered the Basic Check Service, using joint teams with DBS, adopting a ‘one-team’ mentality, and gradually increasing DBS staff.  DBS’s Digital Service Manger commented “What’s really notable is nobody on the project thinks about whether they are IBM or DBS, we’ve a true one-team mindset. Everyone just focuses on what they can contribute to get us to the next milestone”.
 
IBM has partnered with the Danish Government's Tax Data Authority since 2012. Our data solution is a central repository for many internal/external systems with critical data, an integration layer and applications written in Java. We worked collaboratively as one-team with client resources embedded into teams, so supportability was designed into the programme.  End-users were engaged throughout via activities including large-scale playbacks and testing, ensuring ease of use.
