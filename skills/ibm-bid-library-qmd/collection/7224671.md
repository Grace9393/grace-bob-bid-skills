Approach to Training
The IBM training approach is a structured and tailored methodology that enables a blended learning approach. It establishes the optimal way WPD employees, partners and contractors will learn their work management system (WMS) roles requirements – both what to do and how to do it optimally. At the outset of the WMS project, we (WPD and IBM) will assess critical training needs and leverage Maximo’s existing training materials to create process-based learning programmes using practical examples. Training Strategy
We aim to hold a co-creation training workshop to outline the overall approach to the WMS training effort and document an agreed training strategy. This will outline the overall principles of training and agree on the primary tools and methods that are most suitable for the users associated with each capability and phase of the WMS deployment.
We will conduct a high-level Training Needs Analysis (TNA), which will identify primary groups of users, the components of WMS that WPD will be required to learn, and the learning methods/channels (self-paced or instructor-led) that will be most appropriate. As the majority of the requirements are met with Maximo out of the box capabilities, we assume Maximo’s existing training materials will cover most training needs. IBM will leverage these training materials to develop the user-based training curriculums for each key user group.
The training strategy will also help you define learning success measures and KPIs.
Analysis and Design
We will work together to perform a thorough review of all user’s requirement in the MVP phase as an input to the training workshops and to help identify the specific training aspects of WMS that they will require. Our aim will be to group users by need so that training can be effectively delivered and measured.
Training development objectives and requirements will be assessed and evaluated for the MVP phase. We will design the training curriculum so that it can be delivered in line with release dates. If needed, we will work with your training function to understand the learning management system and agree on a process by which the system is used to support the training effort. We will also utilise an appropriate technical test or pre-production environment for delivering training.
Develop and Prepare
Our aim is to develop a detailed training plan and curricula from the agreed objectives. We aim to develop a WMS course curriculum, which recommends training modules for each key user group and can be maintained after go-live so that new users may be onboarded even after IBM leaves. We recognise the difficulty in making adequate time available for employees who are working to tight deadlines, and also in the commissioning area who will be working a 24-hour shift basis. 
Training requirements will be discussed and agreed with your training function and the wider business to secure buy-in and commitment. We will advocate training windows for the completion of modules and will suggest locations for self-tuition that maximise learning effectiveness. 
Training will be delivered via two primary channels: 
- We will use digital self-learning (materials available in an agreed location) as the primary learning channel, which will enable users to access it via various devices. This allows flexibility in training consumption, as well as the ability to revisit and refresh regularly. We are also mindful of the potential ongoing COVID-19 restrictions that will affect the ability to hold any group/classroom learning sessions.
- A Train the Trainer programme will be designed that will ensure effective transfer of teaching skills and WMS module knowledge to the in-house super users. The programme will potentially include trainer simulations, scenarios, evaluation criteria and support. Our aim will be to design the Train the Trainer approach that can leverage the existing instructor-led courses. The aim is to enable effective trainers with relevant material for rapid deployment.  IBM will support the super user trainers during the training sessions
- We will conduct three classroom training sessions so the super user trains the trainers can see first-hand how the training is conducted and also ask questions or update materials to be more affective during these sessions.
We will also advise your training function on how to build and prepare all the training support and logistics required to deploy an effective training programme. The WPD training function will be responsible for ensuring that materials and trainers are available on prearranged dates, that users receive training requests, and coordinate training logistics, registrations, evaluations and other KPIs are tracked and monitored. Our aim will be to use existing processes and templates where possible.
The WPD training function will also be responsible for advising suitable spaces for users to consume training, at times and places that do not interfere with the work environment or safety considerations. Curriculums will be designed with timing windows to give users flexibility in how they access materials.
The training plan will be developed to link with the communication plan and messaging. This will ensure that all users understand the upcoming need and rationale for training, and expectations are set at all levels.  We will include within our communications messaging advice for leaders and senior managers on sponsoring and endorsing the need for training and its importance. For the bulk of functional users, we will centre communications on the use of WMS as an essential tool of their trade, and the benefits it brings.
Deliver
Our understanding for actual training delivery is that this will be performed by IBM trainers and WPD super users (see limits above) and in English. In practice, this means that where there is instructor-led learning, these will be delivered by our IBM or WPD colleagues. Standard End User familiarity training is provided to all users which include basic access, features and navigation. Separate additional training is provided for nominated super users who are taught access controls, configuration tools, role assignment etc.  The number of super users tends to be about 5%-10% of the user population and so will decide how many instructor-led courses are needed when the final user numbers are agreed. 
Training is normally aligned to the testing of the solution during the implementation of each phase and so uses a test or pre-production site set-up on the customer’s system so that they can use the actual solution and any customer provided test data.
We have delivered similar training strategies/programmes at Northumbrian Water, Irish Water, Southern Water, Scotia Gas Networks and many more. IBM will be reusing collateral from these past projects as accelerators during the project.
