As part of WPP's Data Centre Exit and Migration programme, IBM provided delivery management across 12 workstreams, encompassing both migrations and technical enablement. Delivery leads for each workstream managed a workstream RAID, updating on a daily basis, based on input from their stand-ups. Items would be escalated and integrated to the programme level RAID subject to agreed likelihood/impact thresholds (calibrated monthly) or if support was required from the wider programme. A full programme level RAID review was carried out weekly, with critical items highlighted and actions agreed as part of the daily Leadership calls, addressing impact to delivery rapidly.
