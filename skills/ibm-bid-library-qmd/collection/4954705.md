IBM, along with the acquisition of RedHat, are one of the world’s leading Cloud Services and Cloud Environment providers.  IBM’s “Cloud Application Practice” specialises in Cloud-Native Development and has over 8,000 personnel engaged in Build-for-Cloud activities worldwide.  Our people have extensive experience with industry standard products with deep skills across architecture, many application development platforms, components and software products.  Our security specialists work in partnership with our clients to deliver these developments securely and have done so for many Home Office engagements already, along with other secure Government clients. We use our cloud-first (native) thinking and secure delivery to co-create value with our clients, and we provide below a few of the many examples that we have:  

- At the Disclosure and Barring Service, IBM works with the HO and other supplier multi-disciplinary teams to deliver their secure online digital services. IBM developed this service, using agile sprints in parallel streams with other DBS activities before integrating at completion.  Using Design Thinking, User Personas, and Journey Maps as unifying artefacts, the service was evolved in a product-centric, cloud first manner.  IBM co-developed GDS-based coding standards, carrying out manual and automated standards checks in delivery.  The value delivered by this service meant that DBS recently won the ‘Best Use of Customer Insight’ award at the 2020 UK Customer Satisfaction Awards.
- For the Semaphore project, we have recently successfully migrated the messaging zone to AWS, including use of the ACP Platform.  This migration, saw part of the secure Critical National Infrastructure system, moved to the public cloud which connects to all of the airline carries is also securely integrated with the on-premise back end system.   We worked closely with the Home Office security team, presenting at multiple Security Working Groups alongside your team, and successfully passed multiple rounds of Penetration testing as part of the overall testing phases.
- To support citizens applying for low-income benefits with the Scottish Government, we successfully co-created a scalable cloud-based PaaS solution using an Agile implementation approach. This included the full delivery life cycle from architecture and analysis to solution development and package configuration. Our DevSecOps approach enabled the automated build of proof-of-concept, development, test and production environments and services delivering significant benefits. The results included 7 new benefits systems delivered using an agile approach in 2 years, with 85% of claims made using the new digital mobile platform and £190m payments completed.
- As part of our work in the Defence & Intelligence, we have been able to utilise cloud-native technologies within AWS (including serverless workflows, Elastic Container Service, Security / vulnerability scanning) to rapidly deploy, secure and scale new applications to the highest standards of governance and security, with a small TCO benefitting from the economies of scale that cloud provides. We worked closely with the client’s infrastructure teams to upskill and equip them with the knowledge to manage the application and drive secure methods of operation. 
- IBM have also developed the world’s first public cloud platform specifically designed to meet the regulatory needs of the financial services sector. By designing the platform to meet the regulations it allows the Banks and other financial institutions, along with ISVs, to provide value through outcomes rather than concerning themselves with meeting the stringent requirements for regulatory compliance, security, and resiliency. As an example, the Bank of America is hosting key applications and workloads on this platform which is comprised of IBM Cloud and RedHat OpenShift for the Kubernetes environment. This innovative thinking and approach further demonstrate the way IBM are working with secure Cloud-Platforms.
 
In recent press announcements, IBM has announced that it will split its legacy infrastructure organisation into a new company.  This choice is made so that what remains of IBM is 100% cloud-first focussed.  It is a significant decision to make given that this shift will entail the split of a company that delivers $30bn of infrastructure services a year but is a clear indicator of our cloud-first focus. 
 
For the Home Office, we will bring this vast cloud first secure architecture experience and thought leadership to support your overall vision and technology roadmap progression.  We can leverage the good and bad lessons from previous projects and introduce clients to share their experience with the DDaT teams.  IBM can also offer fresh thinking as the cloud architecture continues to evolve – bringing hybrid cloud options, the introduction of mainstream AI and data-driven operations through our recent acquisitions, new products and new approaches to solve Home Office security challenges.
