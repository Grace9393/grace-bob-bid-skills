BM is managing the concurrent delivery of over 10 digital solutions for Home Office and Police covering a number of different digital technologies and requirements, including national critical infrastructure; from securing borders to managing a national public portal for background checks to providing critical back office capability to large UK forces.  
By using an extended team of security cleared resources, IBM shares key resources and skills between the different projects, provides 24/7 support where required and makes use of IBM’s delivery centres in India and across the UK for additional capacity and skills for a robust and trusted delivery model.
