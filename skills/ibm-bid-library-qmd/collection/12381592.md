IBM is a world-leading innovator. We align our services with industry advancements/best practices, by:  
Investing heavily in R&D (c$6bn/yr), focusing on areas such as AI, quantum computing, and cloud. 
Working with leading universities, research institutions, and businesses globally for knowledge sharing, co-developing solutions, and gaining insights into emerging trends. 
Actively participating in industry forums/events to understand evolving industry needs & advancements. 
Acquiring and developing top talent: investing in their learning to build the latest skills and knowledge. 
Seeking client feedback to continually improve our services, incorporating insights into our offerings. 
Adhering to global standards and regulations to ensure services’ reliability, security, and innovation. 
Sharing insights through research papers, whitepapers, and blogs to lead industry discussions. 
IBM Garage: Immersive design thinking and agile experience facilitates rapid delivery of digital solutions. 
To enhance service delivery on advisory projects, IBM provides several innovative tools and methods, including a series of AI assistants to help our consultants to accelerate the engagement delivery: 
PowerPoint Generation (to create professional and engaging presentation outlines);  Project Communication Generator (to craft clear and concise stakeholder communications); Market Research and Industry Trends (to provide valuable insight to clients); Notes Analyser (to quickly and accurately analyse and summarise notes); Executive Summary (to create concise and impactful executive summaries); Expert Perspective Builder (Blogs/LinkedIn Posts - to develop thought leadership pieces that showcase your expertise and develop your reputation ); Meeting Transcript Analyser (to analyse and summarise meeting transcripts); Email Generator (to create professional and personalised emails, saving time and improving your communication with clients and stakeholders; Internal IBM Research (accessing a wealth of internal IBM research and insights).  
 
IBM was asked to support a large UK telecoms organisation wanting to tackle complex manual processes across its various divisions. IBM brought its Lean Thinking, Design Thinking and Automation to develop and build a new capability, which was named Design_4Value.  This approach started with customer value and worked backwards (‘Right-to-Left’) to re-engineer ways-of-working to remove waste, tackle user pain-points and simplify processes and deploy cost-effective and reliable automation.  One example of the impact this approach made, was in Corporate Billing (mobile phone accounts), where the client was able to reduce monthly disputes from >2% to <0.3%, and reduce average time to resolve by 60 days.
