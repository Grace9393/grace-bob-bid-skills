We recognise that amongst the 200 datasets in scope for migration through the Data Optimisation programme, some will have very specific nuances that may be most efficiently addressed by supplementing the core delivery teams with specialist skills. When addressing similar requirements for legacy systems such as the Customer Information System (CIS) at DWP or parts of the Borders systems for the Home Office, IBM has worked with specialist resourcing organisations to onboard individuals with these niche skills on a contract basis; where there is a longer-term requirement for niche skills, such as in the Ministry of Defence, IBM has onboarded small specialist organisations as part of our subcontractor ecosystem. 
 
For access to subject matter expertise, IBM can reach out across the global services and research organisations or through industry forums to bring deep experience and leading edge thinking to tackle a specific challenge or shape a roadmap for delivery improvements.
