Roadmap details are captured in the following ways: 
Product Analytics Dashboard: Capturing data that tracks how users interact with a product-set allows an understanding of what is working and where improvements are required with insights such as drop-off rates. 
Visioning & Shaping workshops: The vision and mission of the Vaccination service should be defined and shared. Shaping workshops allow multiple capability groups to triage and prioritise the outcomes needed to achieve the vision.  
Product Management Capability Syncs: The cross-functional teams will constantly be generating feedback. The Product Manager should continuously share this across the service to help inform the future roadmap.  
Alignment with user-centred design: The product management capability needs to work closely and daily with the user-centred design capability to set research directives and to design prompts to identify new roadmap opportunities.  
NHS collaboration platform: IBM is a member of the platform which shares good practices and lessons learned from other similar programmes of work.
