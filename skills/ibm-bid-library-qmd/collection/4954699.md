In line with your Collaboration Agreement we will work with you constructively and with the interest of the service at the centre of our thoughts and actions. This includes constructively highlighting risks and opportunities for improving the service being delivered by yourselves and by our team to you. We will do this at different levels:
Contract / Strategic Level – At the contract level we look across the portfolio of current and potential SoWs and report progress to you. As part of this we will highlight risks and opportunities, either that are significant and specific to an individual SoW or that are common and persistent across multiple SoWs.  At this level we will also report on the ecosystem as a whole, particularly considering our progress in helping you become self-sufficient by the end of our term.
Where we are responsible for a project / service – In addition to the Contract reporting we will report in line with the governance of an individual SoW. Here the reporting of risks and opportunities will have insights from our other work but will be limited to those that are relevant to the SoW. The thinking of risks and opportunities will start as the SoW is being shaped, reviewed and approved and continue through the delivery of the SoW.
Where we provide resources into a Home Office Team – In line with our response to the Cultural Fit Question, we expect that the people we provide as part of a Home Office led team will actively partake in the constructive identification of risks, mitigations and opportunities for improvement.
