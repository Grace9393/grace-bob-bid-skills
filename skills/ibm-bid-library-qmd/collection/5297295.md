Combining industry Agile techniques (Kanban, Scrum and SAFe) enables the integrated IBM/DWP team to jointly agree the overarching objectives of the work including Departmental outcomes and Executive Team Risk 03 items. 
A prioritised backlog of items is agreed covering delivery in each domain outlined in the diagram above. This work will be then divided into sprints, enabling regular adjustment to delivery plans as required by Digital Security, Technology Services and overall Departmental aims. 
Daily, weekly and monthly reviews are undertaken to gauge progress against the backlog. Where there is a change in requirements (regardless of size) this will be discussed, agreed and the backlog will be reprioritised to meet the Department’s needs. Our resources will be adjusted according to the requirements identified in the backlog and hence allow IBM and DS to deliver solutions of varying sizes according to your demands. 
IBM produces Weekly Progress, Sprint Planning and Sprint Completion reports to summarise progress and necessary adjustments for subsequent phases/sprints.
