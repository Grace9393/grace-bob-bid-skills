IBM/Shell created the world's first B2B global marketplace for the mining industry (Oren).  Our agile approach included workshops and interviews with internal/external stakeholders, e.g. functional teams, users and designers, all with differing skillsets/roles, seniorities and in different countries.  A multitude of written material was created in different formats and tailored to specific audiences and languages. This material was also delivered verbally through face to face meetings including presentations at board level. Written and verbal communication often needed to convey different technical and business detail depending on the audience. Oren is accelerating mining industry digitisation and transforming mining workflows and build.
