IBM has extensive experience of developing roadmaps which embrace a client’s preferred target delivery architecture. For example, for Home Office IPT, we designed environments to match requirements against appropriate UK Cloud service levels. We then evolved the solution based on usage and changing service offerings to optimise the overall costs. We then helped with migration to AWS, which became the preferred choice after a review of capability, performance and value. At DWP, we worked with the client to evolve the application maintenance service as we transferred applications onto government provided cloud hosting services.
