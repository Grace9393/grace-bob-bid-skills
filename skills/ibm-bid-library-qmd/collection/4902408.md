We recognise a critical success factor for platform services will be collaboration across your organisation eco-system.  
 
As we did at SG, by positioning platform resources within the heart of product families a detailed user-centric understanding of platform consumers (developers and expert users) is established. When this is collated with security, legislative, architecture and service obligations into a holistic prioritised platform backlog, a clear roadmap is achieved enabling orchestration of platform changes to flexibly meet the needs of each Product Family.  By following this approach, it enables your Product Managers and other stakeholders to evaluate tactical and strategic platform changes against product family commitments.
