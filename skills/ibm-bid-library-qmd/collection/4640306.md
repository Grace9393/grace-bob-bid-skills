At BP, IBM designed, set up and currently support a ‘service incubator’ to drive innovation, alongside our regular delivery commitments. Ideas are rapidly assessed and developed by joint BP / IBM teams. A customer feedback portal was developed through this incubator and is now live across Europe and has strengthened BP’s relationships with its partners. This approach has been highly efficient, with 140+ service concepts evaluated. As part of our commitment to adding value, we support other incubators across BP. We deploy continuous improvement services via an agile support team, who develop a backlog of improvement activities alongside 24/7 maintenance.
