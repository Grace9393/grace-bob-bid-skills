IBM has successfully implemented numerous Government Digital Transformation programmes where we've worked in blended agile teams. For example, the Home Office Immigration Platform Technologies Programme where IBM designed, developed and ran an award-winning platform (UK IT Industry Awards 2015) underpinned by the agile development of new digital services. The platform provides the DevOps tooling for automated infrastructure build and maintenance and drives Agile@scale methodology across multiple projects, multi-disciplinary teams and suppliers on the programmes, in some instances working with in excess of 10 different suppliers. We receive consistently positive feedback for our collaborative behaviours from our customers.
