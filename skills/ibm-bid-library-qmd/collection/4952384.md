Working with Home Office Enterprise Services (ES), itself working with large complex transformation programmes, our L2 organisation works within your rainbow teams with department staff and other suppliers. We work collaboratively with common delivery processes and tooling, whilst supporting continuous improvement to refine Governance models and ways of working for the specific needs of each service.  
Our teams have proven experience working within your delivery organisations and have extensive knowledge of your tooling, policies, processes and services.  In this product delivery environment, we share and collaborate through much of your tooling (e.g. Jira, Confluence) and have a working knowledge of your TDA Forums and Governance Boards.  Our work is widely recognised within DDaT and we regularly receive 10/10 Customer Satisfaction scores. 
Based on this experience, we offer a proven L2 onboarding approach (plan, learn and perform) and ITIL compliant support processes.  This enables rapid mobilisation, incorporates existing service improvements, de-risks service transition and delivers greater value for money.  
Our proven transition methodology aligns with your service onboarding processes, in which our required knowledge level is realised through resource shadowing, documentation review and historic incident and problem management reports, which we align and map to business need and impact.  The figure below details the transition approach:
