On the DWP Apply for a Nino project we launched a new citizen digital service within 8 weeks to support the reduction of footfall into local offices. Rapid progress required clear communication with a wide set of stakeholders across Digital, Operations, Policy and other government departments. We successfully tailored our communication method to the audience; from high level, concise walkthroughs of the service at our show & tells to explain findings to business focused stakeholders through to detailed reviews of our technical processes and user journeys with our digital colleagues to drill down into the requirements and plan the roadmap.
