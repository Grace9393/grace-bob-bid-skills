(2017-2021) We work with Scottish Government to deliver 7 new low-income benefit services, compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA Guidelines, conducting user research throughout the project lifecycle with 1400+ users. For each service, our designers and developers worked to gain WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, obtained accreditation from an external accessibilty auditor and completed a Digital First assessment to evaluate the quality of the service. 
We achieved this by creating accessibility personas around user needs during Discovery, and testing regularly with users with accessibility needs. We incorporated IBM's thought leadership research and Accessibility toolkit to bring best practice approaches to each project.
