Our shared sustainability goals
 
The Scottish Government and IBM share the same commitments and values in their efforts to reduce global emissions. We have committed to net zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2030 which is just one of our 21 environmental sustainability goals.
 
 
We have been recognised for driving innovation and leadership within our industry, and for having a credible roadmap in place towards decarbonisation.
 
 
 
Our approach to reducing emissions during the delivery of the contract
 
Our work with you over the past 18 months has shifted to a fully remote delivery model, which has significantly reduced travel emissions for over 250 employees. We will proactively encourage a hybrid working model to reduce where possible emissions as a result of travel, IBM will encourage and track the following activities in the effort to reduce emissions:
 
- Responsible travel and community involvement for its employees
- Educating employees on the topics of climate change and climate justice through eco-challenges and learning sessions.
- Adopting sustainable use of technology and design practices leveraging IBM’s leadership in this space.
 
More detail in the diagram below which outlines IBM’s approach to a fairer and more sustainable Scotland.
 
 
 
How we will measure and report our progress
 
Our monthly contract management report will include a section fully dedicated to the tracking and reporting of metrics for the following four areas:
 
- Responsible Travel
- Education and Learning
- Community Involvement
- Sustainably Technology and Design
 
We will reference our estimated reduction in CO2 emissions and energy savings as a result of our initiatives implemented under these areas as well as the number of activities / hours dedicated to helping local communities and educating our employees.
