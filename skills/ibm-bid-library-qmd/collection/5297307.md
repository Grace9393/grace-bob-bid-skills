Within the Public Cloud Instance Protection capability, IBM has led and owned the end to end delivery of the Malware Protection tool Trend Micro since the start of 2020. Working closely with Digital Security’s Product Owner, the delivery team devised the solution High and Low-Level Designs, configuration and test; and are in the process of orchestrating its deployment to the Department’s AWS and Azure hosted cloud applications. Throughout all these phases, IBM has managed engagement with DWP teams such as HCS, CRC, and PDU teams (which included demoing, knowledge transfer and engineering support). 
To date, IBM has managed the roll-out of Trend Micro to approximately 8% of the Department’s cloud-based instances; and have raised awareness of Digital Security’s strategic position for Public Cloud Instance Protection by ensuring around 90% of its Lines of Business are engaged in the project.
