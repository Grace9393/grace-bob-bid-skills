From our LIB delivery we have proven experience of: 
- Ensuring effective cross-collaboration and communication between all parties, including your technical teams and other suppliers. 
- Successfully managing parallel teams to implement and release multiple benefits and continued improvements. 
For Operational Readiness and Continuous Improvement (ORCI), we will build on these principles of collaboration, agreed processes and joint team management to support the co-operative working needed; keeping in line with the standards, processes, and governance procedures that we supported you to create through the Design Team, Design Authority and Architecture Review Boards. 
Key will be how the ORCI team collaborates effectively with existing delivery teams across the wider programme. We will achieve this by continuing to follow the methods that we know work from delivery on LIB: 
- Communicating early and often, in a transparent manner (e.g. weekly reports). 
- Attending Scaled Scrum Stand Ups to discuss progress, cross team dependencies and joint approaches. 
- Having regular architectural reviews with project SMEs to ensure any proposed solutions for new features align with existing work. 
- Utilising tools such as Slack, Jira, and Confluence to share information across teams. 
This collaborative approach will be underpinned by an appropriate GIT strategy that supports all ongoing parallel development activities across the different programme strands. We have been key in creating with you the existing strategy and have a draft below of how we can incorporate ORCI features and releases into the current approach. 
We will be agile in our approach to this strategy and adapt to the changing programme demands. For example, we demonstrated flexibility in re-planning existing releases and code branches to accommodate recent re-planning, which resulted in the smooth transition to an additional branch that was created for IR6. 
Alongside the processes used in the strategy it’s important all teams work together on the same practices and we would recommend formalising our existing GIT agreement for everyone to sign up to. 
Our excellent LIB track record and experience in delivering complex multi-team projects will help ensure a smooth process of parallel configuration and development of ORCI without impacting other strands and vice versa.
