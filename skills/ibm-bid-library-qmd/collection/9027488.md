IBM's information and computing assets are managed in line with IBM’s IT Security policy which is supplemented by standards and guidelines to protect IBM's Infrastructure. Anti-virus / malware standards are one of many standards that are used to protect IBM's information and computing assets. IBM utilises a number of tools to continuously check for effectiveness and confirm compliance with IBM Security policies.
 
 
At the enterprise-level, IBM has implemented
multilayered data loss prevention controls to monitor
data and prevent data loss.
The IBM enterprise has a global email security and
routing infrastructure that employs numerous
technologies to provide a layered defense for email
transactions. All external mail to IBM flows to/from a
service with multiple layers of protection and is
scanned. Email is protected via a variety of security
controls including anti-malware, anti-spam, anti-
phishing, and DLP (Data Loss Prevention).
