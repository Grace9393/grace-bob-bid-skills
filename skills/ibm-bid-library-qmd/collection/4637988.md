We worked with The British Psychological Society to redesign career support for Psychologists. We established a design and user research community of practice with the BPS research team, Ravensbourne University and psychologists as practicing researchers, with specialist skills such as: participatory and community research and multi method qualitative techniques. The community was established with a focus on key research objectives for the project, design thinking principles and a ‘Living Lab’ to maintain the community, with regular review and user feedback to continually assure, improve and innovate the research community of practice.
