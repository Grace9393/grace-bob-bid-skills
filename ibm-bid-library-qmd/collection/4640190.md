IBM supports services and infrastructure for several Government Critical National Infrastructure systems including HO’s IABS and Semaphore. We deliver 24/7 Level 3 arrangements with on-call teams and 3rd parties to support on-site Level 2 technicians and Service Managers. Utilising a security-accredited support centre we cover a broad range of technologies whilst delivering cost savings to the HO. 
 
Using DevOps, Automation, and Swarming, we are delivering further cost savings and an improved service by automating many of the standard support tasks; reducing L3 dependencies and enabling IBM and HO staff to focus on higher priority incidents, delivering a more agile service.
