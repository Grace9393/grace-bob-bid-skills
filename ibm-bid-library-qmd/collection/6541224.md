This Call Off contract will be made up of distinct SOWs. The scale of the team requirements will differ by SOW, and we are able to meet the overall proposed team structure and size requirement you are asking for. We anticipate that you may need to flex the type of skills you might need in each role-type. For example, during the life of the contract, you may change the technology used for part of the screening platform. As a large global SI, we are confident we can provide the volume and range of skilled resources you require. Typically, we would agree with you the resources needed as a SOW was being initiated. However, we also acknowledge that external pressures may alter priorities during the delivery of a SOW. For instance, delays to agreeing changes to governance structures as a new screening service is brought onto the platform may reduce (or increase) the need for resources. We will work with you transparently to flex the team size and skills in response to programme needs.
