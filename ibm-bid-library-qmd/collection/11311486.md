Method Statement
Incorporating social value, including diversity and inclusion (D&I) into delivery of the project
MAC 6.1: Identify and tackle inequality in employment, skills and pay
We implement inclusive arrangements and tools to create awareness for inequality in employment, skills and pay, embedding the value/importance of equity into our organisational culture and thus, our delivery team. For example, we:
• have flexible working arrangements (reduced hours, annualised hours, job share and
compressed hours)
• are skilling 30m globally by 2030 through IBM Corporate Social Responsibility initiatives
• have a free education platform, IBM SkillsBuild, which supports underrepresented
communities in tech by providing access to external tech courses and career pathways
• conduct pay equity analyses in the countries we have IBMers, applying our ‘Equal Pay
for Equal Work’ policy (established in 1935), making salary adjustments if needed, and
• utilise IBM Compensation Advisor with watsonx to support managers in mitigating
unconscious bias in salary increases and hiring decisions.
 
 
In addition to these, we’ll make the following commitments during delivery:
1. we’ll train 100% of the delivery team on D&I through our Business Conduct Guidelines (BCG) course before onboarding. Modules include sexual harassment,
discrimination, bullying and retaliation prevention, using typical delivery ethical dilemmas
2. as SFIA 5 is the minimum for resourcing, we will not be contracting apprentices from mobilisation, however, we are open to flexing this throughout the term, supporting social value. In the meantime, we’ll upskill your apprentices by providing them week-long shadowing opportunities with our AI SMEs, and
3. we’ll recruit staff for this contract through our delivery centres (Hursley, Bristol, London) to provide opportunities across the UK, covering deprived areas and supporting the Government’s Levelling Up agenda.
 
 
MAC 6.2: Support in-work progression
We’re committed to creating in-work progression opportunities for disadvantaged
employees. Since 2019, promotions for underrepresented groups have increased from 3.1%
to 27%. Our team will benefit from our tools and processes, including:
• Beehive Model – IBM tool providing tailored career paths (including keynote sessions and support resources for minorities). It contains role descriptions and corresponding success profiles, detailing what ‘good’ looks like, and the required skills and behaviours for progression
• THINK40 – our self-initiated learning programme of at least 40 hours of development through YourLearning platform that every IBMer undergoes annually, and
• coaching/ workshops on CV writing and mock interviewing for apprentices.
 
Our nominated Sustainability and Social Value Lead (SSVL) will promote these tools and initiatives via Slack, tracking promotions and accreditations. In addition, we will commit to:
4. scheduling regular career check-ins, enabling team members to discuss progression with their task manager, and
5. running one AI and data science lunch and learn per month (at a minimum).
 

MAC 6.3: Identify and manage risks of modern slavery, including in the supply chain
We comply with your requirements and the Modern Slavery Act 2015, complete an annual modern slavery assessment, and publish our transparency statement globally (adhering to UK legislation). Exceeding your requirements, IBM partnered with Stop The Traffik (UK NGO) to enhance our training for IBMers, subcontractors, and clients (modules include ‘Introduction to Human Trafficking’ and ‘Disrupting Human Trafficking’).

IBM is a founding member of the Responsible Business Alliance (RBA), which ensures stringent supply chain standards. We undertake robust annual assessments in countries where noncompliance risks are elevated, sharing findings in ESG reports. Additionally, we’ve set out our own Supplier Conduct Principles for our suppliers to abide by, and regularly audit them to assess compliance, including site visits. To maintain high standards, we have a dedicated Supply Chain Social Responsibility team, who focus on development and deployment of policies, practices, and initiatives.
 
IBMers will be equipped with training (eg, BCG has a modern slavery module) and tools so they can better identify and manage modern slavery risks. We also offer services to support our employees, subcontractors, and clients such as TalkItOver@IBM (HR professionals
providing guidance) and the IBM Employee Concern app (a tool to formally andconfidentially submit issues). We’ll make sure we meet our equality standards through:
6. bi-annual anonymous D&I surveys. This will capture team feelings on priorities, fairness, and client/team cohesion, identifying D&I impacts and enabling informed staffing decisions.
 
 

Incorporating sustainability into delivery of the project
MAC 4.1: Deliver additional environmental benefits, including net zero
We’re well on target to achieve our ‘Net Zero by 2030’ goal through our programme of initiatives, including migration to renewable energy, implementing minimum of 3,000 energy conservation projects between 2021 and 2025, embedding Green IT into our own operations, and designing and procuring energy efficient hardware. For this engagement:
7. each team member will be provided one funded volunteering day. Through our IBM Community Engagement platform, we will track this, making a $10 charitable contribution for every hour an IBMer volunteers, and
8. we’ll track improvements on CO2e emissions saved by measuring business miles travelled during delivery; percentage of these miles travelled sustainably (eg, public transport and cycling); and travel avoidance using dynamic delivery (eg, virtual collaboration and practices).
 
MAC 4.2: Influence to support environmental protection and improvement
We understand that, in order to influence individuals and organisations to effectively steward the environment, they need to be aware of the detrimental effect of climate change. To create awareness for this engagement, influencing our joint teams, we’ll:
9. appoint a SSVL during mobilisation who will champion our commitments
10. jointly evolve a roadmap (utilising the action plan below), and
11. establish a quarterly Sustainability and Social Value (SSV) Steering Group, where Cabinet Office and our IBM SSVL and champions will review the roadmap.
 
 
Plan to implement, monitor, measure, and report on our 11 commitments
