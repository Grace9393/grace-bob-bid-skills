Our multi-supplier engagements focus on collaboration, facilitated by a partnership approach, common methods and collaboration platforms.
On the EU Settlement Scheme programme, IBM worked in a multi-supplier environment, with 5 different suppliers and civil servant teams collaborating to achieve shared outcomes of delivery milestones, and acceptance into early life support. IBM worked collaboratively within this multi-supplier environment leading on identifying common operational pain points caused by technical/business-process issues. IBM advised on improvements and prioritisation of tactical/strategic fixes which improved the end-to-end service from an internal case working perspective, and from an external general public perspective. This has resulted in a recent Client Satisfaction survey scoring 10/10
IBM is the Home Office’s SAS Delivery Partner. In that role we have been helping DDaT define the new IT operating model including ways of working and the establishment of a Collaboration Agreement across the supplier ecosystem.  As a major supplier of support services, we are very familiar with working in a multi-supplier rainbow model, collaborating closely with other suppliers to deliver an end-to-end integrated service to the Home Office.
