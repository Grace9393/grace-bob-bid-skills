How we propose to acquire staff with the appropriate skills
 
 
We have secured all the resources required for the Live Service Team (LST). We know
the correct mix of knowledge and skills you need to successfully support Social Security Scotland. Six of the proposed resources are already deployed in the LST and the additional resources required from January 2022 will come from other parts of the programme that have successfully completed delivery.
 
COVID-19 has not impacted our ability to acquire additional resources with the appropriate skills. Throughout the pandemic we have grown our team and mobilised these resources in a virtual world to meet the demands across both the agency and programme
 

 
Our approach to providing staff with Scottish Government clearances to meet the commencement date of the contract 
 
All the proposed resources are BPSS cleared. During the Pandemic we have continued to follow a robust BPSS process, because of local lockdowns we worked with you to establish a process to allow us to continue to comply with all elements of the process including the challenge of F2F identity check.

 

 
How we will ensure all resources are effectively engaged and clear on work and priorities
 
Our delivery approach on SSS is proving successful during COVID-19 remote working. By focussing on the remote working challenges, we can maintain delivery velocity; this is proven by our successful deliveries during COVID. 
 

 
Our Account Manager will keep team members effectively engaged and motivated, contributing to deliveries. 
 

 
In the immediate term resource will take direction from your Scrum Master(SM) and Product Owners(PO) to refine and prioritise the product backlog by actively participating in backlog refinement sessions to ensure they have a clear understanding of your priorities and upcoming work. Our BA’s will work with the Product Owner to define a roadmap of longer-term priorities.
 
In our experience working with you and wider engagements during COVID-19, we’ve identified key factors that ensure successful remote team engagement. Lessons taken forward to enhance our delivery approach, while ensuring the team is productive and contributing to the programme goals, are detailed below. 
 

 
We’ve applied these lessons across all our deliveries, expanding our one-team model across locations while adopting the following: 
 
- Tried and tested mechanisms to embed the culture and programme objectives into our teams.
- Collaboration, task-management and development tooling, crucial to effective engagement; already embedded into our teams with proven processes to enable tooling adoption and maximise collaboration. 
We recognise that ongoing COVID restrictions may require teams to work from remote locations. To tackle this, resources will continue to use our secure IBM managed laptops and access SG's tooling and environments via the same SG managed VPNs that they have been using since April 2021.
 

 
 
Management and reporting arrangements of the impact of any relevant COVID-19 issues on resources
 
The management structure and reporting remains unchanged during COVID-19. We published a Business Continuity document team-wide, communicating reporting structures, monthly MI meetings and daily management stand-ups to maintain velocity. The resource will report to your SM to ensure they are aware of all leave, sick or COVID related absence, to allow them to manage resource appropriately.
 

 
 

 
 
Any potential delays or risks with mobilising and retaining the team you have identified which may impact on delivery and how you propose to mitigate these 
 

For any non UK remote working staff (not based in the UK) proposed, provide confirmation and evidence that you comply with the fair work policies of where employee resides
 
We can confirm that we abide by all local labour laws for non UK based resources and that we pay above the minimum wage as prescribed by our global working policies.
