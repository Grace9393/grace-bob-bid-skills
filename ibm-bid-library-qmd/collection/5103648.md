To be able to meet requirements at speed and have the flexibility to adapt to changing demand we would recommend a multi-channel delivery approach utilising a broad spectrum of internal resource management, to forecast skills availability in the near term, and external SME suppliers to balance the load during peak demand phases. We have used this approach successfully with many clients to ensure we maintain a high utilisation workforce whilst adapting to changing demand through the use of this extensive supplier ecosystem. 
 
This approach was used with the Disclosure and Barring Service where we provided a skilled and security cleared team to develop and support the ‘basic check’ GOV.UK service.  Working with in-house staff in blended teams using agile delivery we maintained a single backlog for development with allocated capacity for support every sprint. The team delivered the support required alongside regularly releasing significant new features.
