ERCoE has first-hand experience of IBM's culture of innovation from its visit to the Yorktown Labs and contribution to the robot-dog hackathon. 6(UK)XX experienced our flexibility when our Indicators & Warnings team responded to a new use case with 3 weeks to go and delivered a mission-impacting capability.
 
IBM has consulted several candidate IaaS and RAS suppliers while constructing our service design to inform our views on the scope, APIs, IPR, and what we need to provide in the DaaS to support integration. This activity allowed us to de-risk aspects critical to a successful partnership.
 
Our people will use their initiative and work collaboratively with the ERCoE, Users, IaaS Provider and RAS suppliers. We will propose and capture ways of embodying trust, openness, honesty, flexibility, and innovation in an HMT Partnering Charter (enshrining HMT’s culture) agreed upon by all partners in the first two months.
 
Our team is UK based and will embed/co-locate at least one member of the PPMT into ERCoE. We’ll do the same with the IaaS Provider to accelerate integration at a site to be determined (e.g. Defence BattleLab).
 
We propose an Objective and Key Results (OKRs) approach to enable all partners to remain focused and goal aligned with shared metrics to gauge progress. We have made provisions to support communications and stakeholder engagement activities with the HMT community. We also included expertise capable of accelerating identification and sourcing potential third-party APIs, data, and innovative new software. We will take tangible risks e.g. hiring SQEP ahead of demand and setting up a RAS integration training course.
 
We offer, at no charge, ½ day/week of independent, directly embedded support to the ERCoE's governance and assurance function from Andy Stanford-Clark (IBM Mayflower Autonomous Ship Chief Architect) and the same level of remote support from IBM Research evidencing our willingness to put ‘skin-in-the-game’, innovate, and add value.
