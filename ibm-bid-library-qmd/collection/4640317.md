IBM is delivering Digital Automation for a large UK police force to tackle backlogs of repetitive work, freeing up staff for higher value activities. IBM uses a bottom up approach with process Subject Matter Experts to brainstorm the target state for their processes and the alternative digital automation options to meet this end state, considering factors including through-life affordability and speed of implementation. Processes are then adapted, improved, and digitally automated where it is most appropriate.  To date, thirteen processes have been delivered through this trade-off process with planned savings of 70FTE to the business and £5M cost savings.
