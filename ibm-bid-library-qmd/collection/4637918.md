Working with Government Gold Command we successfully formed a COVID 19 rapid response team to quickly scale delivery and management capacity. We were tasked to identify and onboard a team experienced in NHS systems architecture, security and service management to provide a technical blueprint for the Nightingale hospitals. Our team worked closely with NHS Digital’s technical and project leadership to effectively assess and validate the technical readiness of 10 hospitals. Additionally, we extended Security Operations to provide 24/7 monitoring and alerting services; standing up additional resource to deliver against rapidly changing demands and short lead times, driven by the pandemic.
