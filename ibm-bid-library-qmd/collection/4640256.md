IBM is the world-leading advanced data analytics and AI services provider with 30,000 client engagements across 20 industries. We are reducing waste/leakage in the UK Water industry using latest advanced techniques to rapidly prototype and deploy ML-models, using unstructured and infrastructure IoT data, enabling identification, assessment and prioritisation of response. Utilising-open source tools (e.g. Python/SQL/R) and leading AI technologies, data teams explore visualisations, refine models and create simplified optimised plans. IBM and UK Government's unique Hartree Centre research partnership offers cutting-edge data-centric computing techniques to boost UK economic development and in 2017 we established global data protection and responsible-usage principles.
