Yes - University Hospitals Leicester is an example where we have achieved certification for ISO27001. A team of Security specialists were responsible for reviewing and documenting the operations and processes at UHL in order to identify risks. The results were internally and externally reviewed against the ISO27001 criteria, ensuring that UHL were compliant and certification was achieved. Additionally, at other clients, such as DWP, we operate an ISO27001 equivalency process enabling us to deliver accredited AD/AM/hosting services. We are capable and have experience in achieving ISO27001 certification, or following equivalent compliancy processes, which ever best suits your programme requirements.
