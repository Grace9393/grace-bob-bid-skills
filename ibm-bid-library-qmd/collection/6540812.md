Our proposed approach to enable analytics operations includes the lifecycle management of notebooks (requirement 6.2.2.4) and the maintenance and monitoring of analytical models.
To maintain and monitor the analytic consumption services available to users, your requirement (6.2.2.3), to collect metrics for analytical models, underpins a Monitor-Maintain cycle for machine learning models. Models are monitored and alerts triggered when thresholds are breached. These initiate corrective maintenance actions.
Our approach will monitor the following metrics:
 Data Quality
 Ensuring completeness of data
 Data consistency
 Data format
 Data veracity.
 Model Performance
 True / False positives and negatives
 Accuracy, Precision, Recall, Specificity
 Specialised metrics dependent on the model, for example KL divergence, and breakdowns per class or feature.
 Drift Detection
 Prediction drift
 Data drift
 Conceptual drift.
 Explainability
Appropriate documentation, version control and archiving of notebooks ensures that analytics-driven insights will be backed up by evidence where required. For notebook management, we will provide users with guidance and training on lifecycle management, documentation, version control and archiving and equip them with the tools necessary to make this an easy part of their day-to-day workflow. In this way, responsibility is passed to service users minimising additional input by the UKHSA team. We have a publicly available methodology on preparing documentation for AI models (AI FactSheets) that could be incorporated into UKHSA practice. A FactSheet is a collection of relevant information (facts) about the creation and deployment of an AI model or service. Facts range from information about the purpose and criticality of the model, measured characteristics of the dataset, model, or service, or actions taken during the creation and deployment process of the model or service. These provide information to model users and act as a record and means of knowledge transfer if the model owner moves to a different role.
Our propsed solution uses AWS-native components. These are proven to work together, minimising risk in delivery for you. The tooling we will use includes:
• Amazon SageMaker for notebooks
• SageMaker Model Monitor for monitoring
• Amazon CloudWatch and CloudWatch Alarms for monitoring and maintenance
• SageMaker Model Registry as a model catalogue
• Amazon Clarify for explainability monitoring
• Amazon CodeCommit for notebook storage and versioning.
We have implemented capabilities like this with Bouygues Telecom in 2020, where we established a platform and technology foundation for them to operationalise and scale AI. Their objectives included providing an open, scalable, cost-efficient, secure infrastructure and platform for AI.
In a four-month timescale the first version of the production ready AI platform was released on AWS allowing the deployment of four different cloud native AI projects.
Enabling Analytics Operations was an important part of our project with Bouygues Telecom. We propose a similar approach and technology for you, allowing us to minimise your delivery risks and maximise benefits. We have already received input to our solution design from their chief architect and will continue to leverage their experience.
