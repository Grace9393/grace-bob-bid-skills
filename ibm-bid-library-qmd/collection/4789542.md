We will work with your security team and  suppliers to build a model of your systems and store them in suitable solutions based on your preferred approach.  For a large UK Retail client we developed a central security website based on the Seismic platform, with an easy to navigate structure where we collated all security documentation into a central location. We worked across all suppliers to ensure the data held was accurate and put processes in place to keep that information up to date.  We have also used common open collaboration tooling such as Git,/Confluence/Jira/etc to provide similar searchable repositories
