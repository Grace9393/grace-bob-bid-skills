IBM’s agile development focuses on keeping code simple, testing often, and delivering as soon as ready. IBM supports the agile manner of development to maintain a clear focus on ease of use, operation and maintenance and recommends the following considerations: 
Real-time Planning to accelerate time to delivery by: 
Providing a single plan for requirements, development, and test for understanding scope  
Integrating planning with execution, so team understands true project status 
Allowing everyone can participate in keeping the plan current and accurate 
Helping teams respond to the unexpected in a timely manner to stay on schedule 
Lifecycle Traceability to improve quality by: 
Helping identify and close artifact gaps, ensuring coverage across disciplines 
Providing visibility across the entire team to the completeness of planned items  
Providing easy access to related artifacts, ensuring everyone shares the same view 
Delivering transparency, to enable fully informed decision-based business priorities 
In-Context Collaboration to improve product value by: 
Making information immediately accessible to all team members in work context 
Empowering teams to collaborate on and review development artifacts for feedback 
Providing single source of truth for team collaboration 
Development Intelligence to improve predictability by: 
Applying business intelligence techniques to software and systems development 
Enabling fact-based decision making (to communicate status, monitor progress, diagnose problems, identify corrective actions) 
Steering projects and programs to deliver on time
