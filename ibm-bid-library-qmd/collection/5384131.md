Our IA team is security cleared and consists of cyber-SMEs specialising in UK Government security. This includes development and maintenance of Risk Management and Accreditation Document Sets (RMADS), and development of Threat Assessments and Risk Treatment plans using methods such as the IRAM2 methodology recommended by NCSC. We also have experience of using IAS1&2, although this is no longer recommended by NCSC. Our IA team will work with your security architects to identify the risks and threats based on our extensive experience and align security controls and help you identify the most suitable treatment to mitigate risks. These might be personnel, procedural or technology based. Our IA team will assist you with architectural and non-functional requirement reviews to ensure the documentation has identified critical security risks and mitigation plans.
