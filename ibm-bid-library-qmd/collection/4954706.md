We understand that in order to successfully foster a security-first mindset and unify approaches to design of applications across various stages of lifecycle delivery, the Security Architecture leadership needs to be a collaborative discussion. The expertise of IBM can be used to provide a framework and guidance to empower the DDaT Delivery & CTO Teams to advance the profession across the business. We look forward to working with your staff to lay the foundations for how IBM can feed into a centralised governance framework through which we can audit the ‘risk landscape’ across the business, allowing us to prioritise our work with you to address gaps within your technical ecosystem. 
 
Our Security Architecture Leadership will not take the form of merely checklists and quality gates, but will be an ongoing community effort to engage, inform and upskill teams through sharing of best practice, development of shared secure assets and collaborative reviews of portfolio items and strategy. This includes wide analysis and adoption of lessons learned to shape future strategies.
 
The team we will provide to support this profession will come with the experience and adaptability of working in modern delivery structures adept at working within teams / squads or across a number of them depending on the exact requirements.  Our teams are trained in agile and user centric methods and appreciate the need to work alongside other roles be that architecture, product owners, DevOps or Engineering to deliver successful and secure outcomes.
