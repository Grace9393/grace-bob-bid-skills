IBM took over management of DESNZ’s live Data Management System, running 25 grant schemes on an Azure plus Databricks data platform post a re-procurement. As part of transition, a scrummaster, architect, platform engineer and data scientist: shadowed the exiting delivery team, attended agile ceremonies, reviewed documents/wikis, reviewed the architecture, governance and took on service management. Data scientist and senior platform engineer undertook collaborative deep-dive sessions with the data and Energy Affordability policy teams, took over management and prioritisation of the deployment backlog, and assumed ownership of an inherited codebase. The result was seamless transition of a live service and including reduction of bugs by 70%.
