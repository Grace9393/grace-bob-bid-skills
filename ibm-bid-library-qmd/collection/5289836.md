The first thing we did to identify the skills we needed and how we were going to scale the team was to work with the immediate DWP stakeholder group to understand and document the project scope as well as creating a skills matrix and team ramp up plan.
 
Within the first week, we had mobilised a core team with the skillset required for the immediate tasks we had jointly identified with DWP. We then scaled the team size and skills mix based on forward planning of work in the form of Epics and Sprints which were agreed with the DWP Product Owner on a weekly basis. This was a continuous process.
 
We worked with the wider IBM resourcing teams to share early views of expected resource and skill requirements for the project as well as working with DWP to complete onboarding activities such as issuing log-ons, processing clearances and issuing badges. Following this process ensured we identified new team members on time, and their onboarding activities were completed ahead of when it was required. Throughout the programme, we mobilised new resources in 5-10 days on average.
