Within the Continuous Vulnerability Management capability, IBM has led and owned the end to end delivery of the security tool Tenable since the start of 2020. Working closely and managing relationships with Digital Security’s Product Owner, CRC, Fabric and Network engineering and TVAT. The delivery team has supported the extension of CVM scanning via the deployment of Nessus Scanners across the Top 52 PDU applications in the On-Premise Hosted Production Estate, whilst also enabling Non-Privileged User Access to generate Vulnerability Scan Reports. The team also supported the design, build and test of Tenable in the On- Premise Hosted Pre-Production estate. 
To date, IBM has managed the deployment and transition of Tenable Nessus Scanners to approximately 97.6% of the Department’s OPH Production estate (increased from approximately 25% on commencement), designed and supported the build of Tenable. SC and four Nessus Scanners in the Department’s Pre-Production estate and enabled Non-Privileged User Access to the tool.
