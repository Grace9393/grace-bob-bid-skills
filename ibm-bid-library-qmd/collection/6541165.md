The Vaccination Programme will mobilise capability and capacity, rapidly and effectively, to commence, continue and stop work to adapt and respond to the nature of the COVID-19 pandemic. Being able to make people with the right skills and experience available at short notice is paramount due to the expected rapid changes in policy and/or business priorities.
