At the Food Standards Agency, IBM was responsible for the design of a new food business registration service to serve 387 Local Councils. The registration service involved complex inputs from members of the public, so interactive prototyping was the best option. Using the gov.uk prototyping kit, we built over 30 iterations of prototypes in code for testing with users and SMEs. UX designers built basic prototypes to test individual interactions, and front-end developers built higher fidelity prototypes to test end-to-end journeys. These prototypes hosted on Heroku, were used to communicate intricate designs to the developers building the live service.
