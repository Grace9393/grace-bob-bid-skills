We believe that our work should have a lasting impact on your business and strive to provide sustained value through a variety of transparent activities. Our approach is to try to build long-term relationships with clients and provide ongoing strategic advice, as opposed to ‘transacting’ on one-off engagements. This allows us to understand your needs more deeply, so we can provide continuous support and improvement. These examples show value delivery beyond project completion, and how they could apply to the DCC: 
Ongoing Support: 
Dedicated Support Teams: We establish dedicated support teams for our clients, providing them with a single point of contact for any questions or issues that may arise after project completion. This ensures that you receive timely and effective support, allowing you to focus on your core business activities. Whether support remains an IBM responsibility, or if we handover the service to a DCC Support Capability, we will continue to provide the other in-life services described in this answer. We did this with you on the Network Traffic Management Discovery project in June 2023. 
Regular Check-Ins: We schedule regular check-ins with you at different levels (e.g. with DCC in-life support teams and the DCC senior leadership team) to review your progress, address concerns, and identify areas for further improvement or innovation. This helps us stay connected with you, providing ongoing value through strategic guidance and recommendations as a trusted advisor. We will maintain these even if we have handed over service to the DCC service teams. 
Additional Insights to be shared during and after the project: 
Knowledge Sharing: We will share our knowledge and expertise through blog posts, webinars, and workshops. We will invite you to industry roundtables to network with peers from/outside of industry, updating you on latest trends and best practices, so you can make informed decisions and drive business performance. DCC’s Senior Leadership came to IBM THINK to understand driving forces that impact business operations and learned about possible optimisation opportunities, such as AI. 
Benchmarking and Analysis: Through IBM’s thought leadership capability IBM’s Institute for Business Value (IBV), we conduct regular benchmarking and analysis to help you understand your position and the position of your service providers and systems relative to industry standards. This provides valuable insights that informs business strategy and drives continuous improvement in the right areas, provided to DCC as part of our trusted relationship. 
Innovation and Research: IBM invests heavily in research and development, constantly seeking new ways to improve existing systems and services and to develop new ones. This commitment to innovation ensures that IBM's offerings remain at the leading edge. IBM will proactively share with DCC this R&D analysis, to help inform your strategic policies and suggest research activities and innovation pilot projects which DCC may wish to be involved with. IBM’s IBV is responsible for 35-50 major studies each year, relevant to DCC and we would share these during and after the project. 
Improvements implemented after the project: 
Continuous Improvement Plans: We develop these to outline key areas for improvement and provide a roadmap for implementation, so you can sustain and build on the momentum generated during a project. With our Enterprise Design Thinking approach, this encourages you to consider future growth and planned evolution beyond just your current needs when developing solutions 
Change Management: Managing change in a highly complex environment, restricted by regulations and codes, is extremely challenging for DCC. Many stakeholders need to be included in the analysis and potential implementation of a requested change. IBM would guide you to optimise your change process, and help implement project recommendations, including planning, supporting the change approval process with Industry, and subsequent training, coaching, and mentoring to ensure that your teams are equipped to drive and sustain the changes. 
 
Many past engagements resulted in sustained value for clients after project completion, for example: 
Barfield (part of Air France KLM Engineering and Maintenance) needed to consolidate three sites into one to reduce their footprint and improve on-time-delivery (OTD). This significant reorganisation needed to avoid alienating staff and disrupting customers, whilst also boosting employee engagement and delivering enduring transformation management capability. They engaged IBM and used our collaborative, learn-by-doing approach, which achieved a 38% reduction in footprint and 15% improvement in OTD.  
"The IBM approach helped us create one team, one company and one process."   
Olivier Pelloux-Prayer, Vice President of Strategy Innovations and Special Projects.
