As IBM-Nordcloud operates a unified entity interacting with an European-wide organisation, all interfaces with the “CLIENT” will feature the same culture and approach used to handle a complex model of requests originating in “CLIENT” entities. Our structure is designed to enable flexible scale, timing, competencies, and connections to stakeholders, ecosystem partners, and SMEs to drive predictability, speed, and consistency.
The Contract Board (CB) is directed by a Contract Manager who manages the relationship with the “CLIENT” and the hyperscalers. The Delivery Project Executive (DPE) leads the workrooms that handle the demand (Customer Demand Workroom [CDW]), supply (Delivery Engine [DE]) and the following supporting teams:
- Business Process Office (BPO) supported by the Business Operations Manager
- Architecture, Improvement and Innovation (AII) Board supported by the Lead Architect
- Quality Management (QM) Board supported by the Quality Partner
- Change Control Board (CCB) supported by the Change Manager
The CB oversees this structure and can steer initiatives to tailor it to the “CLIENT” way of working and the maturity of the organisation.
The BPO manages the contractual interface with the “CLIENT”, admin workflows, (financial) programme dashboards, RAID logs, IP rights, supplier relationships, and HR activities like boarding, security clearance, activity reporting. The DPE consolidates CSI initiatives, while the QM Board intervenes with the CDW and DE.
The CDW is the single interface for all requests for service (RfS) submitted by the “CLIENT”. It is composed of smaller, demand-scalable cells and manages the RfS cycle, guided by the AII Board and supervised by the QM team.
At the DE, the core of European-wide activities is agreed, designed, developed, and delivered on location. It is built around demand management teams with a predefined set of capabilities and delivers pre-sized scopes.
All teams will be scaled and adjusted to the effective demand and are expected to range between 3%-5% of the total FTEs for the required governance and 10%-12% for the overall project and programme management activities.
