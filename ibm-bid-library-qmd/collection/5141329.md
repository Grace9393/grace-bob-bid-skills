At IBM, we understand behavioural and cultural change through our own digital reinvention as well as being instrumental in shaping and delivering digital transformation programmes for our clients.  Our Talent and Engagement practice has worked across government and our IBM iX teams are intimately familiar with the GDS Design Principles and Technology Code of Practice.  At DWP, we recently partnered to transform the Self Enquiry Function for 120,000 CIS users.  We employed a range of user research techniques (qualitative, quantitative, attitudinal and behavioural) and then jointly planned, iteratively developed and user tested changes, transforming the agent experience and increasing efficiency.
