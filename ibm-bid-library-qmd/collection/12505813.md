Community Benefits
We are committed to supporting your Community Benefits goals as it aligns with our core
purpose to make a difference to the world. We were recently named one of the World’s
Most Ethical Companies for our commitment to culture, environmental and social practices,
ethics and compliance activities, and diversity initiatives.

We have outlined the initial commitments we are making below but we recognise that as the
contract progresses, things may change so we will continuously look to expand these and
incorporate new ideas so we can maximise the impact the team make to your goals.

Training and Educational Support
Digital transformation creates global opportunities across a range of technical disciplines. We believe it is crucial to help people develop the skills needed for these new-collar careers. To support this, IBM has committed to skilling 30 million people worldwide by 2030, focussing on vulnerable populations through accessible training via IBM SkillsBuild, a free
digital platform to help narrow the digital skills gap.

We will work with you through this contract to introduce this platform to schools and job seekers in Scottish under-served communities so they can benefit from this opportunity to explore new technologies and earn professional digital badges.
Our team will work with our existing school and non-profit organisation contacts to introduce the platform through tutorials and provide dedicated resources to deliver support for using the platform.

We will support the Scottish community with the help of national and local organisations,
relating to employability, skills and tackling inequalities within under-represented groups.
We plan to work with organisations supporting carers in Scotland through initiatives such
as fundraising and workshops on technology support and careers as well as support carer
groups you are already working with.
We have already partnered with two organisations, Equate Scotland and MCR Pathways,
who we will collaborate with to deliver workshops and events for people in Scotland.

Each organisation will host a Learning Hour event to our team to promote initiatives and
recruit volunteers. Our volunteers will work with these partners to plan and deliver ongoing
monthly activities as well as continuously looking to expand and scale them.
Through these initiatives we will provide a minimum of 2-3 hours per month of training and
educational support.

Modern Apprenticeships
The IBM Apprenticeship Scheme offers training and a path to technology jobs such as
developers or digital designers to people from all backgrounds. We are reducing our focus on
roles requiring University Degree’s where possible, to broaden the pool of diverse talent and
as part of our wider aim to close the economic divide.
We will create six additional Scottish Modern Apprenticeship positions during the
duration of this contract through our Apprenticeship Scheme, with all positions having
permanent employment from the start of their apprenticeship.
We will target students in deprived areas in Scotland, through our partnerships with
suppliers Pathway CTM and Uptree who support our School Leaver Diversity & Inclusion
attraction strategy.

We provide all apprentices with a defined role and real work from day one, alongside
experienced IBMers. They are assigned a buddy from their team and offered daily support
from their manager. During their apprenticeship, we invest in their training designed to
address skills shortages, focused on relevant pathways like DevOps Engineer or
Management Consultant whilst working towards a diploma qualification ranging from
Level 3 – 6.

We will encourage people of all abilities to apply by ensuring our application process is
tailored to the needs of disabled or disadvantaged jobseekers.
We are proud of our commitment to a competitive starting salary above the Scottish Living
Wage.
6Encouraging SME involvement in the contract
When working with you to evaluate resource demand for upcoming periods, we will assess
the requested role types and capacity to understand if opportunities exist to subcontract work
to SME companies.
To aid this, we have partnered with the Accord SME Alliance, an established network of
SME companies based across the UK providing a diverse range of digital skills and
capability. We can get work requests out to these SME companies quickly, giving a greater
opportunity for a suitable match to be found.
