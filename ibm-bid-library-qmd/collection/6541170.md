We have a network of over 200 delivery centres across the globe with over 300,000 employees, over 6000 dedicated to healthcare. Our globally integrated capabilities will meet NHS D demands across technologies, domains, and geographies, enabling us to establish a team with the right mix of Onsite, Onshore, European Nearshore and Offshore practitioners, should we all agree that this is the optimal solution for the Vaccination Programme. 
Our IBM India Global Delivery Centre has over 110,000 resources covering all roles and technologies identified in Attachment 3 Pricing Matrix of this call-off contract. We have an offshore rate card agreed as part of the Framework for our team in India. Our IBM India Global Delivery Centre gives a 48% reduction on maximum day rates and a 36% reduction in average day rates versus our UK rates. This enables us to deliver a cost-effective mix of expertise and experience across a broad spectrum of services.  
If the capability requirements for the programme aligns better with our other off-shore centres, we will work with NHS D to extend our rate-card to include our other off-shore resources. 
We have used this model effectively on the NHS Electronic Staff Record programme and at University Hospital Leicester, where we leverage offshore and landed resources to deliver a fully integrated service via Rainbow teams. This model provides an opportunity to drive greater diversity of ideas and a wide pool of talent to bring further benefits to the Vaccination Programme.  
If required, we can also run security cleared teams completely offshore and already do this for our clients such as DEFRA and DWP to enable cost savings.
