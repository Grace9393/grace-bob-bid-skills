IBM worked with the Food Standards Agency to develop a service to register food businesses in 387 Local Councils. Using UX flows and blueprints to convey service concepts and prototypes to communicate design ideas at regular playbacks and engagement events, representatives from many Local Councils were able to evaluate the different parts of the proposed service and provide vital feedback to the development team. This resulted in a positive reception from the Local Councils, with all 387 engaging with the FSA in at least one activity, and the FSA awarding IBM a net promoter score of 9.5/10.
