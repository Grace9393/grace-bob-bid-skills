We will create a structured Knowledge Repository with all associated programme documentation for any future suppliers, including building a library of Recorded ‘how to’ KT and Question & Answer sessions with the associated outputs and presentations. These recordings will be uploaded to central Confluence pages to allow any future resources who join the programme to be able to watch and review to accelerate and streamline their onboarding and handover. 
Our practices to knowledge sharing during ongoing BAU Delivery: 
As part of Delivery, all materials will be stored on Confluence to provide a central point of access for knowledge for current and future resources. Additional governance will be established to provide forums for audiences to hear about and ask questions on specific topics. These will happen in a number of ways: 
Project Bulletins 
via email 
Short and sharp updates on key progress and challenges 
Celebrating Successes & championing people 
Lunch & Learn sessions 
30 min virtual sessions over lunch 
Covering topics from Delivery Solution, DevOps, Testing, User-centred design, Strategy, and any other areas of interest 
Demo session 
Short presentations  
Short sessions to demonstrate key areas of the solution / plan 
Hackathon Days 
Full day face to face 
Interactive innovation and alternative thinking workshops to explore ‘what if’ using Design Thinking methods 
All Hands Call 
Regular Leadership Presentation 
Status presentation from Leadership on progress and ‘hot topic’ items with a short Q&A at the end for all 
All materials and recordings from these sessions can be stored on Confluence. On NHS Test and Trace, sessions like those above were used across the programme frequently.
