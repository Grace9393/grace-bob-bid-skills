Resource Identification & Selection
We will identify appropriate resources by using our specialist Public Sector Resourcing Team, enterprise-wide resource management capabilities and flexible resource pools to ensure we rapidly mobilise multi-disciplinary teams to meet your timescales.
 
 
Resource Pools
Resources will be chosen from our flexible global resource pools which can provision all roles identified by the Lab.
- IBM Consulting - 10,000 UK-based colleagues with technical and functional specialisms, where our dedicated Public Sector team (~650) are delivering for DWP.
- UK Competency Centres - Six specialist centres providing resources needed by the Innovation Lab (e.g. Technical Architects; Developers; Data Scientists).
- Global Delivery Centres - 110,000 nearshore/offshore colleagues with security-cleared teams (UK-located, if required) who are already delivering for DWP.
- External Ecosystem – IBM and Plexal’s innovation networks from which to curate the best-fit solutions and team for DWP.
 
Use Case: When supporting DWP on Apply for a National Insurance Number (AfN) IBM mobilised a multi-disciplinary team of 12, comparable to the Lab’s requirements, within 3 weeks. Within 8 weeks we delivered an MVP into Private Beta. Over 12 months and 44 iterations, the resource profile varied from 10 to 15, which we tracked, managed and reported financially to DWP over the course of the programme.
 
 
Lead Times
Joint forecasting of resource demand enables us to appoint multi-disciplinary teams in 1-2 weeks. Individual onboarding can occur with a lead time of days. Offboarding can be equally rapid, depending on the requirement to handover to a counterpart.
 
We will continually scan and identify for new resource, on a skills match basis, keeping IBM candidates on standby for future projects. This will ensure DWP has continued access to resource and ability to flex at short notice.
 
Client work takes priority over any internal company commitments. If, by agreement, a role is considered not to require full-time resource we will jointly define the activities, resource requirement and track this as with any other role, as set out below.
 
Financial Information and Accounting 
IBM’s clear financial accounting and reporting fosters trust through transparency at all levels.
 
Use Case: Our team working with DWP on the SRA Programme has grown from five, initially, to a team of twelve. Transparency is provided to DWP through:
- Joint planning – collaborative resource demand forecasting to agree the agile backlog, assign resources and monitor delivery.
- Client reporting – Project Leader provides regular updates to DWP and collects performance feedback, in accordance with contract budgetary requirements and programme governance.
- Timesheets – colleagues complete weekly timesheets accounting for project work.
- Monthly billing (in arrears) - invoices generated by IBM Accounts with an ‘open-book’ approach providing visibility of time spent by allocated resources, activities and outputs.
- Supplier management – Where we work with third parties, IBM Procurement supports project teams to manage third-parties ensuring that costs incurred are consistent with contracts before being submitted.
 
Retention and Development
We continuously incentivise, develop, and upskill our teams to ensure you have access to the best talent. For this project, we will tailor learning paths for each colleague according to their role. We will assign designated innovation mentors within our team to help DWP staff succeed and realise their potential.
 
- Recruitment – our talent pipeline is constantly replenished; IBM Careers targets school leavers, undergraduates, graduates and experienced professionals ensuring IBM attracts, develops and retains the best people.
- Career Platform - curates personalised learning, provides a 24x7 interface supporting individual’s career roadmap, and supports structured career coaching interventions.
- Mentoring - to set goals, schedule ongoing conversations and guide development. At mid-/end-of-year all IBMers are formally assessed with client feedback playing a key role.
- Training - all IBMers complete ‘THINK40’ – 40+ hours of training annually and have access to award-winning learning programmes and tools.
- Incentives - beyond incentivising through learning, IBMers can be rewarded financially with ‘Blue Points’ and through awards for Service Excellence. 
- Retention - has been maintained through well-being programmes, encouraging flexible working, our hybrid remote-working model and access to benefits, e.g. employee assistance programme.
 
Escalation POC
We aim for a ‘one team’ culture with DWP, encouraging continuous dialogue and regular feedback while sharing ideas, and making suggestions for improvements.
 
If concerns over resources need to be raised, our Project Leader is the first point of contact for DWP. In most cases such concerns can be resolved this way. If required, e.g. when additional resources are requested, the Project Leader escalates the matter to our Account Leader, Matthew Pickersgill, who oversees all our work with DWP.
 
Pricing Structure
Our pricing for this contract follows the TS3 structure and rate card.
