Promoting the wellbeing and development of our staff is one of our long-standing commitments supported by a number of programmes and measures in which we will encourage Home Office participation throughout the life of this contract. 
Method Statement 
We tailor our programmes to reflect the latest guidelines in this area. The following table demonstrates the actions we will implement supporting the Mental Health at Work Core and Enhanced Standards and additional criteria in MAC7.1 to improve health and wellbeing on this contract. 
1. Prioritise mental health in the workplace by developing and delivering a systematic programme of activity 
(Enhanced Standard 2: Demonstrate accountability by nominating a health & wellbeing lead at Senior Leadership level, with clear reporting duties & responsibilities) 
We continue to demonstrate accountability in this important area by appointing Health and Wellbeing representatives at our Senior Leadership Level within our organisation. These representatives drive a programme of positive change within our organisation. 
At a local contract level, we will: 
appoint a ‘People Champion’, responsible for developing a programme of activities to encourage a focus on mental health and wellbeing.  
provide access to and signpost existing resources and activities for IBM and Home Office staff, e.g., our active UKI Maternity/Paternity community, providing webinars and ongoing mentoring and support. 
2. Proactively ensure work design & culture drive positive mental health outcomes Our People Champion will: 
continuously create opportunities for employees to feedback through all phases of the engagement to prevent poor work culture and conditions.  
foster an environment where all team members are supported in their growth, offering mentoring opportunities for both IBM and Home Office employees. 
encourage our team to find a healthy work-life balance through embracing flexible working (part time, job share, hybrid) to improve job-satisfaction. 
implement our annual Public-Sector wide Inclusivity and Wellbeing Survey, engaging our workforce and identify issues to address by implementing an agreed action plan. 
3. Promote an open culture around mental health 
(Enhanced Standard 3: Improve the disclosure process to encourage openness during recruitment, ensuring awareness of why information is required) 
We will promote a culture where our team feel empowered to speak about their mental health through: 
utilising IBM’s open and accessible recruitment processes making it clear to candidates that adjustments can be requested and made. We are a Disability Confident Employer. 
encouraging self-identification of any wellbeing issues throughout the contract so any adjustments can be applied.  
access to our established training programme for employees to become Mental Health First Aiders (MHFAs). 2% of IBM’s employee population are now trained as MHFAs, providing colleagues with a broad network of support. 
4. Increase organisational confidence and capability We will ensure all employees working on this contract are provided with access to up-to-date mental health and wellbeing training e.g. 
our Be Equal Campaign (https://www.ibm.com/impact/be-equal/) launched in 2019 and has tens of thousands of employees undertaking an extensive training to be recognised as Be Equal Ambassadors, including personal pledges. 
our Mental Health Ally Badge program, designed to convey basic information about mental health and teaching how to recognize and address stigma, and how to connect peers in need of support with an appropriate service or resource 
5. Provide mental health tools and support 
(Enhanced Standard 4: Ensure provision of tailored in-house mental health support and signposting to clinical help) 
We will provide access to occupational health and mental health support through professional services and trained volunteer MHFA’s e.g. via our 24x7 Employee Assistance Programme (EAP), offering confidential telephone or face to face counselling and access to comprehensive online resources. 
We will provide access to Health and Wellbeing awareness webinars with Specialist Consultants on topics such as Resilience, Menopause, Cancer, Nutrition and we will also run voluntary initiatives to support physical fitness in the FBIS delivery team (e.g. Virgin Pulse GO Challenge; aiming to build resilience and improve health & fitness). 
6. Increase transparency and accountability through internal and external reporting 
(Enhanced standard 1: To Include a leadership commitment and outline of the organisation’s progress on mental health) 
We will: 
monitor wellbeing through fortnightly Wellbeing temperature checks within our delivery team, providing the opportunity for identification of wellbeing concerns that can be immediately addressed within the programme.  
jointly develop and publish a Project Charter to agree commitments and goals with regards to behaviours and events/learning activities surrounding wellbeing.  
measure team engagement – running quarterly surveys and events to promote engagement and identify actions to help us improve overall satisfaction of the team. Our People Champion will be responsible for collating and reporting feedback/insights to the Home Office team and IBM to further strengthen our strategy, approach and action plans.  
As an organisation we encourage self-identification of diverse abilities or disabilities, so we can offer adaptations for colleagues with visible/ non-visible/ neuro disabilities. We publicly publish our commitments and progress in our annual Environmental, Social and Governance report (IBM 2022 ESG Report).
