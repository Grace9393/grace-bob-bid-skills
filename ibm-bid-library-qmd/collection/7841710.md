UKHSA has a new, enduring remit which depends on building a permanent, market leading data, digital, and technology capability with a reduced reliance on the contracting/consulting market.  A new Academy and Centre of Excellence (CoE) will be a key enabler to meet your ambition of creating: “a long-term, permanent, diverse, sustainable workforce with succession planning that meet0s the needs of DDaT business groups to deliver key programmes/capabilities and services to protect citizens; and creates a workforce ecosystem that is as attractive to new joiners as it is to existing civil servants”.
You have a moment in time now to build a compelling employee value proposition to attract, retain, and develop DDaT talent both for UKHSA and to be a trailblazer across UK Government.
By bringing together IBM and EY, we have formed a partnership which has worked shoulder-to-shoulder with your DDaT teams over the last two years. We are specialists in Digital, Data and Technology (DDaT), Academies/CoEs and Employee Value Propositions in support of technology transformations, skills modernisation, shift to cloud, API services and platforms. Our team will bring specific and applicable knowledge of both the UKHSA context and these skills (BR4A114R).  Brief examples include:
- Partnering with Government Digital Services (GDS) to develop the DDaT career framework and taxonomy consulting with departments across government to gain agreement on the relevant job families and proficiency levels
- In 2022, working with the Cabinet Office to create a digital fluency academy and Centre of Excellence across the whole civil service aligned to the DDaT framework (BR4A103Q)
- Running the Government Learning Campus for bespoke learning (Academy across the Civil Service) which has included over 3,000 coaching sessions, with provision delivered both digitally and face to face (in 2022, SRO fundamentals training, PFI Centre of Excellence and PHE transition coaching) (BR4A105Q)
- What we have learned from these experiences and bring to UKHSA (BR4A102Q) is an understanding of the challenges around how to grow, reskill, and upskill a complex technical workforce eco-system (BR4A1204Q), the lessons learned, how to build skills, learning and career paths dynamically, and how to run academies effectively delivering graduates able to ‘hit the ground running’ (BR4A101Q)
Collectively we have over 1000 people consultants in the UK focusing on this type of work. You will have access to a research division investing nearly £5billion annually in innovation and future technology.  We bring an IBM Academy of Technology, its own distinguished engineers, and partnerships with major technology industry vendors to provide technical expertise, technology transformation insights, briefings, and practice events to UKHSA. We will use this network to support UKHSA to develop independent links with industry and academia (BR4A118R).
