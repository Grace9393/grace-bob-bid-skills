We have resources immediately ready to support you from 150+ UK IBM Interactive(iX) staff  skilled in quantitative and qualitative research.  IBM and Scottish Government won the MCA Change and Transformation Award 2020 using quantitative and qualitative evidence to iteratively build Social Security benefit services. We engaged 1,400+ citizens across 40 locations, identified and evaluated creative solutions such as designing pregnancy benefit services to utilise existing data sources, removing the need for midwife validation.  We prioritised product development decisions by iterating and validating with users, including feedback from live for improvement of the experience for 100,000 households, averaging 93% customer satisfaction.
