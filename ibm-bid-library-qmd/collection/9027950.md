IBM and/or Tenable users would be client users of the VPN provided by SPEN.  We are not proposing a site-to-site VPN connection. Client machines are subject to a wide range of mandatory protections, including but not limited to automated and enforced patch level requirements, endpoint detection and response anti-malware technology, and only permitted use of approved operating systems.
