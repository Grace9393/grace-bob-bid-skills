IBM draws on our Global Client Innovation Centres (IBM CIC) to access lower cost offshore resource. IBM India CIC has 110,000+ resources. Our CICs enable us to deliver a cost effective mix of expertise & experience across a broad spectrum of services including:
-Build and transition services for NHS ESR and University Hospital Leicester, leveraging offshore and landed resources;
-End to end application development for DWP;
-Digital definition, DevOps, Build & Transition services for Nationwide Building Society and Data Management for US healthcare clients e.g. Anthem. Multi-site projects are business as normal for IBM with most of our projects leveraging a balance of onsite/offsite resources. Our methods & tools are the same in all geographies to ensure consistency in delivery. Our 8 sub-contractors have delivery capability in offshore centres that we will leverage where appropriate.
