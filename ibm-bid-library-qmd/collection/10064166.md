Ensuring our technical delivery complements and integrates with your current organisation
 
Maximising Knowledge, Experience, and Reuse
As a key part of the Agency Live Service and your successful delivery partner for the Low-Income Benefits (LIB) and Disability Benefits (DB) programmes - we have hands-on experience in all areas specified within your Enhancements, Notifications and Live Running (ENLR) requirements, we understand your users’ needs, and are uniquely placed to leverage knowledge of LIB, DB, Notifications and SPM, as well as the surrounding technical landscape.
This knowledge covers all functional areas including interfaces, notifications, organisation and security, redeterminations and appeals, straight through processing, upgrades, case transfer and live running. We have previous experience in implementing data purge and full record print. We are already an integral part of both the Disability Services and Enhancements teams and the Notifications Centre of Excellence (CoE) team, providing technical guidance and lessons learned based on our extensive experience across the programme.
During the Discovery and Alpha phases, we will rapidly analyse, identify, and plan which components are:
- Cross-cutting/Common to encompass current and future needs.
- Development extensions, building on existing frameworks and patterns.
- Benefit specific configurations and rules. 
 
Working in collaboration with your technical teams we will determine the appropriate designs and the right team and implementation path for each area. 

 
 
Our approach to enable parallel configuration and development across your teams
From our existing deliveries we have proven experience of:
- Ensuring effective cross-collaboration and communication between all parties, including your technical teams and other suppliers.
- Working very closely with your SPM Design Team (SDT) and helping shape and promote their Ways of Working.
- Successfully managing parallel teams to implement and release multiple benefits and continued improvements.
For ENLR we will build on these principles of collaboration, agreed processes, and joint team management to support the co-operative working needed; aligning with the standards, processes, and governance procedures that we supported you in creating. 
How the ENLR team collaborates effectively with existing delivery teams across the wider programme will be key. We will achieve this by continuing to follow the methods proven from previous deliveries:
- Transparent Communication, early and often (e.g., weekly reports).
- Scaled Scrum Stand-ups to discuss progress, cross-team dependencies, and joint approaches.
- Regular architectural reviews with project SMEs to ensure any proposed solutions for new features align with existing work.
- Being fully embedded with SDT, sharing technical highlights and concerns, and agreeing approaches.
- Utilising tools to share information across teams (e.g., Slack, Jira, Confluence).
 
This collaborative approach will be underpinned by an appropriate GIT strategy that supports parallel development across the programme strands.
We have been central to the creation of the existing strategy and have a process in place based on the below diagram of how we can easily incorporate ENLR features and releases into the current approach.

We will be agile in our approach to this strategy and adapt to the changing programme demands.
For example, we have continuously demonstrated flexibility to accommodate re-planning, resulting in the smooth transition to additional branches for post go-live releases, simplifying downstream merging to reduce impact on development teams and promote earlier testing.
 
Delivering changes into the test and production environments
The foundations of successful delivery of change across environments are clear communication on upcoming releases across all teams, and a highly automated approach which enables rapid deployment into test and production environments.
To communicate across all teams what components are being deployed and the support required for different testing demands, our Scrum Master and DevOps resources will continue to follow the proven practice of running a clear release schedule managing the flow of change into each environment. This is regularly reviewed and flexed, accommodating changing demands, including urgent releases required to keep testing progressing.

For deployment, we have an efficient well-proven, documented, and largely automated process that takes completed sprint deliveries through the appropriate stages into test. DevOps play a critical role in supporting these delivery activities by provisioning environments, automating processes, and managing deployments.

Using these processes, our teams develop, build, and test the SPM deployable components with a self-service process allowing Developers and QAs to deploy Development and Test environments on demand.
The artefacts and environment manifests are promoted into non-production environments in a controlled manner to enable wider testing. Following successful completion of assurance, we use a well-rehearsed, proven automated process to deliver the same artefacts into production.
We have over the course of our delivery on SSP, addressed several challenges through innovative approaches and solutions. 

 
 
We have worked with you in transitioning to your shared tooling which enables a consistent toolset for all suppliers, making cross strand working more efficient. We also moved AEM and associated components to use your strategic Terraform pipelines and worked directly with IBM SPM Product Development Cloud team to build the first stages of the strategic OpenShift platform. 
During the ENLR programme we will make further improvements, moving to a consistent deployment model throughout all four environment stages utilising a container-based packaging and runtime model to improve scaling and service availability.
 

 
 
Ensuring faults are not introduced to existing benefits
From day one of delivery, we will focus on code quality, assurance reviews, collaborative branch and merge strategies, and shared regression packs to ensure we do not impact existing services or services being developed by other teams.
We have continuously improved our testing and assurance processes throughout our deliveries, working across all your teams to identify and apply continual improvements - we see this approach as critical to bringing the best ideas to reduce the risk of faults impacting critical services.
We focus on the following key areas when imposing stringent assurance and testing standards in our joint delivery:
- Technical Designs
We will continue to deliver technical designs for key components. We now have an extensive catalogue of designs based on the enhanced process and template that we recommended to the SDT for all suppliers.
- JUnit coverage and pass reporting
We will continue to report on JUnit coverage which provides assurance that functional areas work as expected. We have worked directly with the IBM SPM Product Development team and successfully delivered an enhanced, container-based, highly scalable, Junit infrastructure to allow the end-to-end suite to run in a much shorter time, providing developers with rapid feedback after any code change.
- Code Reviews (manual and automated)
We will continue with regular reviews and developer sessions where code standards are reinforced, safeguarding the quality of new and existing code. These internal processes and learning, coupled with close collaboration with SDT in evolving the guidance, has meant that code quality is measurably improving.

- Code Merge Guidelines
Following a documented merge process with controls to ensure appropriate review and approval by peers provides additional manual verification changes won’t cause issues to existing functionality.
- Early involvement of QAs in the delivery cycle
Testing from the outset with a focus on rules processing and integration scenarios to identify key areas, test data, and dependencies enabling them to be managed effectively and corrected early.
- Maximise use of automated regression testing
We have established automated regression packs, covering LIB, DB, and Cross-cutting features. We will continue to enhance these, preventing any adverse impact from delivered changes being merged into code branches.
