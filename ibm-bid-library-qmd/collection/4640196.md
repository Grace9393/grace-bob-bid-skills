IBM maintains a large elastic pool (over 500 resources plus an SME ecosystem) of existing SC resources available to onboard, supported by our own Security Department; managing clearance and aftercare processes including BPSS and NPPV3, via Warwick for National clearance.  We work closely with HO for SC processes and have DV cleared individuals for specific engagements. 
For HO’s Shared Application Service we demonstrated our scale and speed; we were awarded the contract on 16th October, signed on 27th October then located, selected and built a 40 strong team, to meet the expectations of the HO PM by 12th November 2020.
