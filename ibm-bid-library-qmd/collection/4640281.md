BP needed to rapidly develop a mobile service for their customers, to reduce the time spent refuelling and increase the customer retention. To accelerate this, we made use of our existing CI/CD assets to build the underpinning DevOps capability needed to support mobile app development and automated testing from the very first sprint. Closing the feedback loop at such an early stage was crucial in accelerating the development lifecycle and enabled quality of the mobile development to be monitored from the outset. The resulting BPme app has high adoption rates across all deployed countries and an AppStore rating of 4.6.
