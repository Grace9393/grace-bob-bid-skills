IBM have developed the Garage Method to co-create minimum viable products (PcCs) using agile processes: 
•	Design Thinking to support ambiguous requirement development and the concept of product journeys;
•	Backlog driven prioritisation for flexible iterative development; and
•	Failing Fast to pivot quickly as requirements or other factors necessitate change throughout the PoC.
Recently for the cloud migration of a Home Office Border system, we led a PoC to select the appropriate technology for a component.  Selection was between an AWS commodity or custom-coded option but we demonstrated flexibility and pivoted to assess and ultimately select a newly available HO developed service.
