We believe that one critical consideration for the UK government to future proof its AI approach is a fit-for-purpose reference architecture. Working with Cabinet Office, we have started to explore common architecture models for the UK government to adopt. The approach we have explored includes:
 
- key capabilities to enable departments to realise the full potential of Generative AI
- full end to end model consumption, tuning and training, deployment and governance, and
- using open source and proprietary models from major vendors.
 
Departments will want individual solutions to their problems, but especially for conversational type solutions citizens will want one view of government. We have seen this in the past with One Govt Login and Tell Us Once. This needs to be solutioned into the overall approach for the UK government, not as a ‘one platform for all’ but by a reference architecture approach that will allow this to happen over time when required.
 
We have explored four key capabilities to enable UK government to be future proofed that we would like to continue to explore with you. These are:
 
- conversational interface – ability to interact conversationally with open source and proprietary models via a single chat interface
- fine-tune and train – how to set approaches to fine tune and train custom models using organisation data but then share those approaches with other departments
- retrieve and deploy on every cloud – how to share and deploy across multiple clouds and across organisations, and
- monitor and govern – monitoring and governance across the AI lifecycle, including how to manage risk and compliance across all the AI solutions.
