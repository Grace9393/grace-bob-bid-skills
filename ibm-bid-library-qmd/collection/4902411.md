From day one, we’ll identify, deliver, and evaluate platform changes by operating a continuous improvement backlog according to the governance process below. This is our proposed starting point; we’ll work to adapt these to your processes:
- The support team, and your wider technical community, can add items to the backlog.
- Items will be triaged at regular technical design authorities (TDA) with all parties present, prioritised and elaborated.
- System context, container and component diagrams are produced in Confluence and agreed at subsequent TDAs.
- Prior to each sprint, engineering teams select the highest priority items, that are suitably elaborated, for implementation.
We operated this mechanism at SG, which allowed us to protect the resilience of the live platform while implementing important upgrades, such as the ability to recover the OU structure into a fresh Active Directory. The benefits included complete visibility of all changes, from their earliest iteration onwards, to all technical stakeholders. Like we did at SG, your platform changes will be designed iteratively, with artefacts easily accessible on Confluence, meaning their implementation is fully transparent.
