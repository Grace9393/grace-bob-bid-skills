We are partnering with BP on their transformation toward an integrated energy company. Regulation is driving significant change demanding the need to be open, collaborative and connected.  BP’s response is a shift to a portfolio & product approach. Working as their global digital-transformation-partner over the past four years, we have set the foundation for change through establishing trailblazing digital product teams across their business (20+B2B/B2C/internal products, 30+markets), and are enabling acceleration through connected innovation to enhance products and offers; training teams in product mindset, product management skills, agility and design thinking; extending this operating model to new ecosystem-based value pools.
