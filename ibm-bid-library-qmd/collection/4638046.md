London commuter operator c2c partnered with IBM to build a new mobile app for their customers, completing the roll-out of the Pico4UK ticketing system.
This provides a single customer record across all channels, allowing both c2c customers and staff to review and manage ticket purchases from any device. Since PICO4UK was first introduced, it has sold nearly 5M tickets in the UK (generating £130m in revenue).
Smart ticketing is particularly important as c2c and other rail operators are driving customers towards digital tickets as the industry’s preferred approach, as part of the strategy for recovery from the impact of Coronavirus.
