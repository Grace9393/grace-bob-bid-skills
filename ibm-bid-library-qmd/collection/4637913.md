IBM works closely with scientific SMEs across the globe, including with our own Science and Research departments which gain more yearly patents than any other organisation.
Our MoD practice works alongside scientific colleagues at DSTL in joint projects and larger collaborative partnerships including the International Technology Alliance in Distributed Analytics and Information Sciences.
https://research.ibm.com/labs/uk/dais.html
 
Further to this we are part of the Science and Technology Facilities Council (STFC), supporting many SMEs at the Hartree Centre. In a recent press announcement, we have formed a joint partnership with STFC to invest in further development in this area.
 
https://newsroom.ibm.com/2021-06-03-UK-STFC-Hartree-Centre-and-IBM-Begin-Five-Year,-210-Million-Partnership-to-Accelerate-Discovery-and-Innovation-with-AI-and-Quantum-Computing
