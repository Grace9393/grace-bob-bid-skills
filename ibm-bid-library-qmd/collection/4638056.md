IBM Application Managed Services (AMS) is a dedicated global service organisation providing a focused 24/7 support service with 20,000+ practitioners. This IBM function is process and tool driven providing service excellence and continual improvement for value and service across hundreds of engagements supporting critical government and business systems. 
ITIL and ISO27001 are fundamental best practices within this function and for both traditional delivery models as well as our advanced use of DevOps.                                            An example of this is a UK TOC where we provide 24/7/365 support for a business critical mobile and analytics platform applying ITIL and ISO27001 principles.
