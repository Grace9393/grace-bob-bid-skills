This relies on an investment of time early to create a holistic migration strategy. Over the longer term, cost efficiencies could be realised by creating packages of work that can be delivered by offshore teams. IBM has experience of working with the Department for Work and Pensions as well as several US government agencies to provide secure offshore delivery in data-related programmes.
