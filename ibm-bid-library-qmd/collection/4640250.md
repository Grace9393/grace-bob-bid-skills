Experienced IBM user researchers planned and executed research in developing Social Security benefits in Scotland, incorporating existing research and regional data, then undertaking comprehensive user-needs analysis and user-based testing for 24,000 geographically-dispersed users. Holding 483 sessions in 40locations across Scotland, researchers brought empathy and respect meeting vulnerable groups such as parents that have suffered neonatal death, young parents (including under 16) and users with disabilities. Extensive user-research coverage meant front-end services were focussed on personalised user-journeys, improving user engagement and making it easier for vulnerable citizens to access key information, resulting in 90% digital uptake and extremely high user-satisfaction.
