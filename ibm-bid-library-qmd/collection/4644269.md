At DWP, the IBM team deployed and configured IBM’s OPAL process library as the basis of our service. These processes integrate the key elements of CMMI, ITILv3 and ISO27001 compliance projects. They have been central to our track record of high quality delivery for over 5 years. Our processes integrate into DWP’s own ITILv3 processes and ServiceNow toolset to provide a comprehensive Service Management framework. We provide both Level 3 application support and Level 4 Infrastructure and software provider support. We cover all aspects of service management including Service Level, Incident, Problem, Change, Capacity and Availability Management.
