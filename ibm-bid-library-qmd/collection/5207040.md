Our SIM process provides a robust security incident management service based on a tiered response system. This allows an incident to progress from initial identification and triage all the way to technical security expertise and incident management. 
 
We will always have technical security analysts available 24/7 to support investigation and client/other supplier conversations. Analysts will be supported by the Shift Lead, who is an experienced analyst themselves.  Once a security incident has been identified and confirmed as not being false-positive it will be escalated following the pre-agreed SIM process. During Major Incidents our Service Manager will establish a bridge call, to co-ordinate the security incident response and invite all required participants. We will jointly investigate the problem, pulling in further expertise from our team as required, and staying on the bridge until the situation is resolved.
 
You will always have reassurance that the correct skillset is available throughout. As well as our smart hands and eyes resources available 24/7, our daylight team will also be on-call out of hours. Under normal office hours the daylight team performs tuning and service improvement, but in times of sustained attack the daylight team bolsters the duty shift team to ensure service levels are maintained.
 
For novel attacks our team can query our Threat Intelligence Platform, X-Force Exchange to identify global trends and compromise indicators and we have access to our Global SOC Teams who can provide global threat response intelligence to support our team to a rapid resolution.
