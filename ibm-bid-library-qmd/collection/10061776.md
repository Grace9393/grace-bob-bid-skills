Ensuring effective knowledge transfer and upskilling
We’ve focused on building your team’s skills and experience throughout our time working with you; it’s in our teams’ nature to co-create with you so your teams grow and develop as we deliver together.
Through this, we’ve viewed knowledge transfer (KT) as a way to continuously share our experience across all aspects of the programme and the teams we engage with.

Core to our approach is how we work with you at every step, from supporting training at the outset of someone’s learning, developing their skills through our joint delivery and then mentoring and guiding them in their new role. 
We underpin this with key foundations of a clear common documentation library, knowledge of the existing services and established cross team support in role specific communities of practice.
This approach has been built on a combination of lessons learnt with other clients and through the 4-year journey we have had together delivering the current services.
 
Skill development and knowledge transfer
We’ve already been working together in building your team’s capability and whilst we have made progress towards this goal, we recognise that we need to do more to support you.
We still advocate the benefits of working together in joint teams where our people can share skills, experience and support your staff to grow through on-the-job learning but we will bolster this with additional specific activities.
Developing your SPM Capability through a funded programme
Our goal is to leave a legacy of fully capable Scottish Government staff who can take on the roles that we provide when we leave. 
Key to that is focussing our efforts on helping to develop SPM Business Analysts and Developers with a strong understanding of the solutions that we’ve implemented for you.
One key lesson learnt is that access to formal SPM training has hindered your progress in developing capability as fast as you would like. 
To resolve that we are proposing to fund SPM training and certification exams for up to three Business Analysts and five Developers per year of the contract.
We’ve kept the numbers limited so that we can provide dedicated 1:1 support as your staff work through their training. 
The diagram shows our first-year intake being guided through their learning plan with their mentor.

 
In the second year of this programme your newly trained staff will start shadowing our mentors as we repeat the process again with your next trainee intake. 
Through this process we can then enable your staff to continue the training when our contract ends.
We believe that through this approach, we can help you build a team of up to 40 SPM certified people and give you the tools to develop your own SPM staff in the future, allowing you to achieve your goal of self-sufficiency in SPM.  
Clear Learning Plans
We’ve developed the following structured learning plans that we will work through with your nominated candidates in the funded programme, ensuring the training is followed through and tracked with active use in delivery.

 

Supporting foundations for knowledge transfer
Ensuring understanding of the existing Live Services
It’s important to not only have SPM knowledge but also knowledge of your developed solution that so many of our team possess.
To ensure we cover this aspect of your team’s development we have included on-the-job learning of your existing services as part of our learning plan. 
We know it’s helpful to have something to refer to, so we’ll produce a series of videos that cover the core flows of every service that has been delivered to date and add them to the document library for future reference.
We’ll ensure we continue to share this learning as we develop the new Carers and Winter Benefit services.  
 

 
Common documentation library
We’ve made real progress in improving the sharing of knowledge, leading the way in how JIRA and Confluence are used to capture information. We worked with you to create the common document library containing comprehensive document for each benefit release which we’ve maintained throughout our delivery and will continue to build on in the future.
 
 

 
Communities of Practice
We now have six Communities of Practice (CoPs) designed specifically for efficient knowledge-sharing across teams within role-types. 
These networks are connected through slack channels and are ready to welcome new members to their community.
 
Overall KT Plan
We’ve outlined a clear plan with key activities in each strand of our approach with regular governance points so we can jointly track handover progress and your team’s growth.
