We will partner with VISION, your existing cultural and operational transformation provider, for the mobilisation, implementation and continuous support of Enterprise Asset Management.
IBM will be the Systems Integrator, responsible for programme management, design, build, test, deploy and support of the solution.  VISION will be the Business Change Partner, identifying opportunities for performance improvements, prioritising the business benefits case and promising to deliver those benefits as measured by specific key performance indicators.  VISION will implement the new operating practices and transformed behaviours required by the new business processes, promote the new solution and mobilise the changes in working practices with the teams, building a positive mood around adoption and resolving resistance to change.
We mobilise for implementation using our Maximo method (“Maximize”) and we perform the following activities:
- Staff appropriately skilled individuals for each role (we use our Professional Market Place tool to map open seats to candidates, who are then interviewed by the project leadership team and accepted or rejected for the role)
- Create an onboarding pack so that new team members can quickly understand the project context and commitments
- Conduct a method adoption workshop to agree the delivery method (e.g. Maximo “Maximize”), project management tools (e.g. risk & issues log, requirements management), project procedures (e.g. deliverable acceptance, project change control) and project governance (e.g. daily stand-ups, weekly project status, weekly design authority and monthly board meetings).
- Install and configure the necessary project management tools (such as Jira for requirements management, Trello for actions, Mural for whiteboarding, and so on)
- Detail the schedule and project work breakdown structure
- Establish the technical environment (e.g. sandbox system to show the screens during the subsequent design phase)
- Review the statement of work with the Client and agree a RACI matrix so that everyone is aware of their responsibilities
- Conduct “conditions of satisfaction” interviews with senior client stakeholders to understand and manage their expectations
- Conduct a series of one-to-one interviews, using VISION’s diagnostic interviewing technique, across a selection of key senior stakeholders, management, and end users.  This will give us insights into the concerns, anxieties, ambitions, and potential for resistance in the communities who will be impacted by the programme, which will help accelerate the realisation of the benefits.
At the end of mobilisation, the following outcomes are achieved: 
- Delivery method agreed
- Tools installed
- Plan and work breakdown structure updated
- Organisation chart with named individuals per role defined
- Project management procedures, reporting and governance established
- Statement of work and conditions of satisfaction reviewed with Client
- Financial plan established
- Staff onboarding conducted
- Project kick-off event conducted
We ensure continuous support for the project by way of:
- Monthly reviews with our risk department to address issues and mitigate risks
- Regular liaison with our HR and resource & capacity management teams to backfill any roles if required or to hire new professionals in the market if the project requires a new niche skillset
- Regular industry and technology-specific training courses for our consultants
- Quarterly reviews with our Quality Partner to monitor project health
- Specialist water expertise from our IBM Global Centre of Competency for Energy & Utilities
- Advice on technology trends from our IBM Client Technical Advisers
- IBM conferences to champion Scottish Water in front of your peers
- Access to our partnership ecosystem to cover the full solution delivery lifecycle
- Access to our IBM Energy & Utilities Incubation Hub to build proofs of concept
The key mobilisation challenges are:
- Client resources not being fully committed to the project, resulting in lack of ownership and poor attendance at workshops.  This is mitigated by assigning specific deliverables to client team members, creating a clear workshop plan and working closely with the client project manager to dedicate client resources to the project.
- Client expectations not being aligned with the statement of work, resulting in confusion and delay.  This is mitigated through “conditions of satisfaction” interviews with key client stakeholders and by agreeing a RACI matrix, so that is clear what each party is responsible for delivering.
- Staffing the most appropriately skilled IBMers at the right time.  This is mitigated by our demand forecasting of open seats in our Professional Market Place tool, which gives early visibility to our resource & capacity management department to find appropriate candidates as per the project timescales.  We have a global delivery model, which means that we have access to a wide pool of skilled resources from multiple onshore, nearshore and offshore locations.
The typical timeline for mobilisation is 4 weeks.
