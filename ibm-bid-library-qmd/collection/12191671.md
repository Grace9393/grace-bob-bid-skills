3.4 How we will deliver apprenticeships, traineeships and T Level industry placement opportunities (Level 2, 3 and 4+) in relation to ESN
Since 2010, our talent teams have been working with school leavers to build a programme that recognised IBM as a registered Apprenticeship Employer Provider in 2017. The program has since expanded to more than 25 registered apprenticeship roles including cybersecurity, software development, data science, and design. In Dec 2021 this scheme was recognised as ‘Outstanding’ by Ofsted. The UK school systems still primarily encourage more traditional routes into higher education via university excluding many young people from professional roles and emerging opportunities due to a lack of qualifications and employability skills. As individuals progress through their apprenticeship, they receive academic education as well as practical work skills. At completion of their scheme, they become part of our broader workforce with the same opportunities as a graduate. 
In addition to supporting our own apprentices, we have an extensive track record of proactively supporting our clients’ apprentices in their career development, to achieve their apprentice certification. On a recent engagement with the Home Office, our project managers acted as task managers and ensured all the HO apprentices became fully productive team members. One was nominated by an IBM project manager for the ‘Rising Star’ award at the National Apprentice Awards 2020
We are also tackling the skills gap issue head on with different pathways and a focus on skills beyond the conventional three-year degree for individuals from a diverse range of educational, cultural and social backgrounds. In 2019 we decided to expand our award-winning apprentice programme and launching the new Business Partner Apprenticeship Programme
Starting in 2020, the new 23-month programme will give apprentices the opportunity to work within the sales team at our business partners across the UK. These companies range from traditional resellers to new, born-on-the-cloud companies, from solutions architects to deployment experts. Apprentices will experience working within IBM and our Partner Ecosystem and jointly selling creative IBM solutions to our joint customers.
The full-time IBM apprentices will complete the programme with a Level 3 – IT Technical Sales Diploma, equipping them with qualifications and practical experience needed to excel within a technology sales environment. The new programme provides a pathway for school leavers to start a career in the industry and work with some of our Business Partners, providing maximum exposure for the individual and some of the most exciting talent for our Business Partners.
We also offer paid internships to help people gain work experience and expose them to the many different career opportunities that exist within the IT sector. We run several placements a year including 12-month University Placement opportunities in Business & Finance, HR, Technology, Marketing, Software Development and Design, and a 12-week ‘Extreme Blue’ Summer Internship where interns work on high profile projects for our clients.
In sections 3 and 5 of our response, we clearly outline our commitment to creating 126 new apprenticeship roles over the term of the contract. Recruitment for these roles will be regionally targeted. Offering a twofold benefit for ESN – driving new skills opportunity in England, Scotland and Wales, but also a digital skilled workforce that will support the delivery of the programme and roll out into UOs. In addition, we will support the growth of cyber skills through investment in the NCSC CyberFirst bursary scheme that supports the training and accreditation of early professional.
