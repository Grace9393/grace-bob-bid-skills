We will mobilise our team drawing on our experience from working across Public Sector and Healthcare engagements, such as NHSD, Manchester Genomics, and UK HSA. We recognise the value of rotating specialists across our client engagements, bringing domain specialism to new programmes. We operate a 'Factory Services' model with a flexible resource pool, repeatable processes and tools, enabled by artificial intelligence to match skills and experience against demand. We will work with you to select the right team for your requirements, drawing on the depth and breadth of our talent pools to enable rapid matching of individuals to roles to satisfy the demands and priorities of the Spine Clinicals Programme. We will create an onboarding process to facilitate a smooth mobilisation by focusing at a role-by-role level, which will minimise disruption to in-flight delivery. Resources will be onboarded and introduced to their incumbent counterpart where they will agree their knowledge transfer plan, to begin handover. 
Day one resources will be confirmed in stage 0 to expedite onboarding to VDI and NHS tools, and as additional resources are jointly agreed these will follow the same onboarding process. From previous mobilisations on NHS T&T and DToS, we know that success is highly dependent on the type of role and the level of demand in the marketplace. For example, on DTOS, the mobilisation and ramp-up of Delivery Management, Architecture, Test and Business Analysis roles was successful and mobilised in good time along our planned timeline. However, Development resources, specifically those with Full Stack and front-end skills are in high demand and were more challenging to source. As a consequence, we have factored this into our mobilisation plan to mitigate the risk, providing a role-by-role mobilisation plan. IBM has a large ecosystem of suppliers which we will call upon to ramp-up quickly to source required skills. These do come with added lead times, which is included into our plan. We will specifically be looking to utilise this ecosystem for the core RIAK and Apigee skills which are in demand and will use our dedicated resourcing teams to drive the search and source before onboarding. As an example, Scottish Government and IBM co-created a scalable, integrated, cloud-based benefits solution, enabling citizens to apply via a portal for benefits. In response to the requirement to rapidly scale, we mobilised 7 agile teams in 6 weeks supporting the full delivery life cycle from service design, analysis, and architecture to solution development
