The tools we put in place to allow people to continue to work and collaborate remotely
Over the past few years the IBM CIO’s office has been investing heavily in the tools we use to deliver value for our clients. 
Long before COVID-19 struck IBM implemented tooling across three key areas that, along with some additional training, enabled 98% of our consulting businessto be operate remotely in a matter of weeks. 
Having the right tools in place empowers our people to adapt to their new working environment quickly and continue serving the needs of our clients. 
 
 
Messaging 
Slack was rolled out globally over the last few years enabling asynchronous communication across the organisation and connecting our development tooling directly to our people.
 
Meetings
Webex enables thousands of daily standups everyday. User research and design reviews have all moved online. Use is up 200% and we now use ~150 million meeting minutes a month.
 
Management 
Tools that allow us to visualise work (Trello, Jira) help team quickly identify what is blocking progress Collaboration tools like Box enable people to work seamlessly together on a user research.
Finally workshop tools like Mural power all our Design Thinking workshops, co-creating with clients across the globe to deliver to the needs of their customers.
