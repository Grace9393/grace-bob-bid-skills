As one of the first partners to operate the cloud adoption frameworks of the hyperscalers, we have years of experience of leveraging the method in the design of our plans. Below is an example of an effective, tailored CAF approach for multi-cloud consumers such as UKHSA, built on best practice frameworks encompassing both AWS and Azure methodologies and continuous lifecycle of maturing capability and execution for cloud sustainability.

Our experience with CAFs is demonstrable through a recent engagement with BMW Group. Their ambition, like UKHSA, was to have a Public Cloud Platform that would be a central gateway to the cloud through a Cloud-first transformation strategy, leveraging both Azure and AWS to develop a truly global PaaS and IaaS platform. However, they had limited expertise and operational maturity across multi-cloud environments, which increased cost and evidenced low maturity of governance, meaning significant backlog of projects and low operational capability. Through our Multi-Cloud hybrid CAF approach, we enabled BMW Group to increase their agility, operational excellence, control and automation.
