IBM works with Disclosure and Barring, providing DevOps Squads to deliver their roadmap of digital services.   Using modern iterative engineering approaches and lean methodologies, based on Six Sigma, including innovative processes; Design Thinking, User Personas  Journey Maps and Feedback loops, the AWS services were evolved in a product-centric continuous-improvement manner.   Further optimisation, such as removing overheads in the DevOps CI/CD pipelines and unnecessary release governance.  FinOps is a key pillar of our service, aligned to AWS Well Architected Framework, leading to reduced Home Office consumption. DBS won ‘Best Use of Customer Insight’ award at the 2020 UK Customer Satisfaction Awards.
