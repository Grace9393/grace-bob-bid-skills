A major UK defence contractor started a considerable process of Merger and Acquisition across Europe. IBM, in particular our Security Business Unit, worked with the customer to support their acquisitions and to create a digital transformation plan to move the companies acquired to the Azure cloud. 
 
With 300 users being moved to Azure, IBM are providing the strategic plan, low-level designs, and delivery of all Azure environments; including security policies and information event management (SIEM), anti-virus/malware, PKI, networking and security groups. 
 
IBM assessed the existing environments in 60 days and produced the low level and deployment designs in 120 days.
