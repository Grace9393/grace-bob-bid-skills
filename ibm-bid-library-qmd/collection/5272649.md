IBM's delivery model is a loop of "observe, reflect and make" an iterative process to which user groups are an essential component. Enhancements that surface in user group meetings are prioritized based on user value and included in the Product Backlog E.g. For Scottish Government, we took a user-centred design approach from the outset, performing research with diverse groups of people, designing from real user needs, rapidly prototyping concepts and then incorporating enhancements based on user feedback into agile sprints. We launched the first benefit 6 months early, with a digital uptake of 90%, average completion times less than 5 minutes and incredibly high satisfaction ratings from users. IBM has also delivered user-centred services at Manchester Foundation Trust for genomic patient records where we use inclusive design principles to concentrate user research on those who are most vulnerable or have difficulty interacting with a service.
