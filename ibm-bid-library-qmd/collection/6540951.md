Through IBM Decision-Making Framework for Innovation, Demographics Data Transformation team can refine and optimize its solutions, to address data issues, by using feedback from business, technical, and strategy teams. The data issues will be mapped to solutions ideas after proper root cause analysis and feasibility. The accepted ideas will be prioritised and fed into the Backlog.  
IBM Garage Methodology, Cognitive Data Classification, tagging and metadata generation will be utilised to build-in innovation into the NHS Digital DevOps processes.  
As an example, at a North American Automotive Insurer we were able to achieve 2X Delivery velocity, 99.99% availability and a cost reduction of 20% through DevOps observability, AIOps, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) and ChatOps.
