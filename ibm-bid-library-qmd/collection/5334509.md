Creation of Reusable Materials
The production of high-quality materials, stored in an accessible platform, is essential to nurturing your cloud expertise.
From our experience, the timeliness of knowledge creation is extremely important when developing skills; as those learning may need to reference materials more frequently. Therefore, we recommend taking the following actions to embed the creation of re-usable materials into ways of working.
