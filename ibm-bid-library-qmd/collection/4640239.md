Our ITIL qualified teams have extensive Service Management experience. For DWP we designed a Service Management approach, deploying and configuring IBM’s OPAL process library integrated with DWP’s ITILv3 processes and ServiceNow toolset. With process integration of industry-standard CMMI, ITILv3 and ISO27001 compliance projects, this has underpinned 5 years of vastly improved service management. Moreover, we’ve designed and transitioned major services to GDS Digital Service Standards. For DWP’s Customer Information System we designed, delivered and smoothly transitioned the service into Live without business disruption and minimising training needs, enabled by working in joint IBM/DWP scrum teams and upskilling DWP resources throughout.
