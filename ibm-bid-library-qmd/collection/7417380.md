In this section, we set out: how we identify individual requirements, build the proposed curriculum, our delivery modes including a digital platform, and the administration of the Academy:
- Our recommended approach is for people to drive their own career pathway: through using our competency self-assessment tool, Spotmentor (described further in Q2.2.2), UKHSA will be able to empower their staff to use a self-serve approach to build their own learner journeys, allowing them to focus on the key skills most relevant to them currently and in their aspirational roles (BR4A125R)
- We will design a curriculum (covered in more depth in Q2.4.2) drawing on our long history as a technology leader. We will bring the best of this content to you to drive the skills that we identify during the discovery process. We will create curate or source new content for you which is relevant to the UKHSA context

Our Per4mance Learning Journey architecture is the basis for designing all our learning and development programmes which change behaviour, shift performance and drive organisational impact.​ Underpinned by a robust, evidence-based understanding of how people learn, behave and improve their performance it has three key phases – engage, activate and accelerate learning. This will be the basis for the design of any learning journeys for UKHSA to ensure you deliver the outcomes you want. We deliver both virtually and in situ depending on two key factors – the preference of UKHSA and the type of content required. We will recommend which learning is digital, virtual and in situ through our discovery process (BR4A123R).
We will establish the recommended skills framework, career paths and learning journeys in the discovery phase. Once we have this data, we will finalise the curricula which is shared more in Q2.4.2.  As an example, we can draw on pre-existing content including (BR4A102R):
- Through the IBM Skills Academy – Artificial Intelligence Analyst, Big Data Engineer and Cloud Native App Developer
- Through IBM prebuilt Technical academies including Cloud, AI, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Design Thinking, Blockchain, Quantum
We align to your beliefs around the 70/20/10 model of learning* and we recommend that the delivery mechanisms for the Academy and CoE are fully blended and multi-platform including: 
Academy
Centre of Excellence (CoE)
- Digital learning journeys 
- Facilitator-led interventions (virtual and face-to-face) 
- Coaching, mentoring and shadowing 
- Learning Labs ((hands on experience)
- Projects and presentations 
- Assessments 
- Social learning
- Communities of practice 
- Industry insight
- Innovation hub
- Action learning
- Alumni network
 
*70% of learning is experience based, 20% comes from interactions and 10% comes from formal learning.
Building the digital platform to support the Academy and CoE
We work with our clients on all leading platforms including Oracle, Workday, SAP Cornerstone, Avature, Saba, Edcast, Docebo, Degreed as well as our own platforms. Our team includes learning experience designers, multi-media designers, developers, data and integration experts and subject matter resources with deep expertise in technical development. They will work collaboratively with you to:
- Build a branded digital home which enables a seamless user experience across several platforms to enable people to manage their career within UKHSA.  We will enable these platforms to have single sign on and have data reporting protocols which enable you to track who is doing what (allowing personalised nudges) and create the interventions through self-service for learners (BR4A125R). 
- Build on our pre-existing content to develop your learning journeys, either by creation of content or tailoring our existing content (BR4A109R).
- Determine any content or 3rd party integrations required to enable a seamless UKHSA experience
We will stringently assure all content provided to UKHSA, working collaboratively with you in order to agree the assessment criteria, to ensure that it is high-quality and directly relevant to UKHSA’s role (BR4A107R) and advise on the Badge process to help you achieve recognised industry certifications.  Third party costs are per person per annum for unlimited badges and typically less than £7 per annum per person. A typical journey through the academy will be a motivational experience focussing on developing targeted skills.  Each candidate will go through a mixture of training (digital and in person), coaching, on the job experience through projects or placements, shadowing, and ongoing assessment to validate progression. For example, one of our pre-existing learning journeys on Data Science has a duration of 20 hours (BR4A119R) and involves interactive case studies, lecture labs and knowledge checks leading to a certified badge (BR4A125R).

We can deliver a smooth user experience for UKHSA staff through our managed service team by taking ownership of administration, design, and delivery. This can include: 
- A learning administration team who will oversee the curation and administrative management of the end-to-end learning service for UKHSA  
- Oversight of the digital platform to ensure smooth access for users, including issue resolution 
- Preparing for, academy cohorts and facilitating and evaluating face-to-face and virtual learning events
- Communications & engagement team to co-ordinate campaigns and success stories being shared
