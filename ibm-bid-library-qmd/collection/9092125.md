IBM is proud of our culture of innovation, continuous re-invention/improvement and associated patents.  We look forward to bringing this value, ethos, and expertise to PCDP and explain below how we will explore where we can enhance your existing systems to drive further resilience, cost reductions and performance improvements, minimising risks to delivery without additional support from the Authority.
 
During our engagement with Scottish Government, with whom we built their benefit processing platform on AWS, we embraced leading-edge best practice including cloud-native and CI/CD patterns to create their architecture, enabling delivery/support of a fully automated and resilient infrastructure service, with auto-healing capability.   Following a complete AWS Availability-Zone failure, our SRE capability ensured that teams were operational, and citizens were unaware of the outage. Our work on this platform secured IBM the MCA Award in 2020 for ‘Best Change and Transformation Project’ in the Public Sector.
 
We’ll bring the same drive and expertise to PCDP, understanding its importance within the Government’s wider MBTP data strategy.  HO articulated that resilience, security and automation are of paramount importance to enable your future vision.  We will achieve excellence across your estate by engaging stakeholders in critical design reviews such as ‘AWS Well-Architected’, ensuring your services adhere to best practice and enhancing the platform to comprehensively support the scale/agility the Authority require to evolve to a futureproof, person-centric solution. 
 
We understand the multiple components and inherent complexity of your data platform, requiring management of data structures, APIs and applications for a multitude of data producers/consumers, scaling up to billions of records.  We would like to utilise several of our performance optimisation techniques and procedures, including analytics, AI and SRE which focus on effective data management and platform resilience.  We can drive improved data access and ingest with better observability of data across your estate.  Through improved governance and maturity of APIs, we will decouple you from data consumers and provide a more reliable, performant capability.
 
Our delivery approach for innovation would be to leverage our ‘innovation incubator’, an investment we make into the HO by ringfencing a standing team of 12 engineers we can use to perform technology-independent Proof-Of-Value exercises at no cost to HO and regardless of vendor (AWS, Google, etc).  This includes developing a mature SRE capability/toolchain and improving traceability of data.  We would engage this team upon contract commencement, producing an initial Proof-Of-Value prototype within 8 weeks.
