We have many years’ experience partnering with the Home Office and other Public Sector clients to deliver agreed outcomes in blended teams with clients and other suppliers. We think that uniting teams around common goals, irrespective of team members organisation, is key to delivering outcomes and during transition we will facilitate an orientation session to bring us together as one team. 
 
For Semaphore and IABS support we follow Home Office operating models and processes including SIM, working in a badges-off way with the Home Office and other suppliers to ensure any security incidents are successfully resolved within the SLA’s. Our service lead will join you and other suppliers in regular service governance meetings to drive service quality improvements across the supplier ecosystem.
 
We will create and agree with you, the HODDaT Service Desk and your suppliers a documented SIM procedure that defines the activities, processes and RACI through the SIM lifecycle from initial recording and identification through investigation, escalation and ultimately resolution and closure.
 
Our SIM process interlinks into our problem management, root cause analysis (RCA) and continual service improvement (CISP) processes.  Problem management and RCA will ensure we not just resolve incidents, but also prevent them from happening in the future; and CSIP applies lessons learnt and automations to improve our support processes and application security.
