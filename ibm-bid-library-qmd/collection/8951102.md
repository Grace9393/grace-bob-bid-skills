IBM delivers a wide range of technical support services to our customers, from legacy on-premise platforms through to a fully remote managed services for Public, Private and Hybrid Cloud platforms, available on a 24/7/365 basis. We can meet all of the support requirements stated in the Specification document, Lot 3, and provide examples below:
 
Requirement 1.1 / 1.2 Realtime Support Channels:
- We deliver services using multiple different engagement channels such as telephone, ChatOps, email and online ticketing, enabling the appropriate support channel to be selected by the Approved Organisation to receive the correct level of response to their issue (e.g., ChatOps/email for P3/P4, or telephone for P1).
- We streamline the management of support incidents using automated alerts (including proactive AIOps-based solutions), AI models and provide self-help, automation and self-healing capabilities, reducing response and resolution times. Ticketing systems allow for real-time updates to tickets, with integration between our ITSM tooling and that of the Approved Organisations.
Requirement 1.3 Reporting Tools:
- We have experience with a wide range of reporting and monitoring tools, along with the cloud-native tooling, and integration of these platforms with either our own ITSM tools, or that of our customers to allow for a single enterprise-wide dashboard and remove silos of knowledge. For example, at Lloyds Banking Group, we integrate our monitoring platform with theirs to ensure that they see issues at the same time we do.
Requirement 1.5 Cloud Platform Support:
- IBM provides support services across the major cloud vendors (AWS, Azure, GCP, as well as IBM Cloud) supporting hybrid, cloud, and on-premise solutions across the public sector. For NHS ESR, we provide an application managed service for an on-premise system, and we provide a managed service for the NHS App, which is cloud-native, that covers applications and platform management. This service contains a full end-to-end application and infrastructure planning, design, build, test, deploy, and support of this critical platform for patient interaction.
Requirements 1.4, 1.6-1.12:
- Our teams operate in a flexible manner, allowing Approved Organisations to select the skills needed at a time that suits them, be that consultancy for application deployment, DevOps, infrastructure, business change, security, or assistance with troubleshooting a platform issue. An example of where we deliver this skills model is within NHS England, providing engineering support to their Cloud Centre of Excellence, the development of Directory of Services (DOS), and flexibly scaling the teams within the NHS App as and when required.
Requirement 1.13 – Training:
- IBM is a recognised and award-winning training provider (Brandon Hall Gold Excellence Award for Best Innovative Learning Programme) and certified training partner for the major cloud providers. IBM is able to create tailored learning journeys for Approved Organisations (to meet drawing on our catalogue of training material, whilst also providing softer training through mentoring and coaching. Through our digital badges, IBM is able to offer formal certifications to staff of Approved Organisations in a broad range of digital and cloud skills.
