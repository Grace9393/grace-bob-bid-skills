IBM’s XFTM portfolio is based upon a fixed price and fixed outcome solutioning model, rather than costing for individual resources for each solution. IBM’s XFTM solution architects have designed the model in this way in order to leverage the significant efficiency (both in cost and delivery of outcomes), productivity and increased analytics that this methodology facilitates. 
 
IBM would be happy to provide more details to ENWL with a more detailed understanding of the type of requirements/baselines that we can solution to. From there we would be happy to provide a cost estimate from our SKU based deployment services.
