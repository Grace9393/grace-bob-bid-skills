IBM is experienced in supporting security needs of public sector bodies; from BPSS-DV. We have a dedicated public sector clearance team, based in Hursley, who have established long standing relationships with key clearance departments such as Home Office Cluster 2. 
 
New Clearances:  
 
Within the IBM Public Sector business unit, as mentioned we ensure all employees are put through BPSS checks as standard. This is a pre-requisite mandated by the Cabinet Office for application for higher clearances, ensuring our resources have already passed this step helps to expedite the higher clearance process.  
 
Processing & Transferring:  
 
Key to the smooth processing of clearances within our public sector clearance department is this team’s integration and close working with our account management and resourcing teams. This provides a forward-look of demand and helps to align capacity within the team in-case of a surge in resource and clearance requests.  
 
Key to the efficient running of this process will be the early identification of clearance sponsors within the Home Office for each requirement, we recommend this is jointly mapped out upfront and the information is maintained to avoid unnecessary delays to initiating the clearance process.  
 
As new clearance applications and transfers progress, our government clearance team take ownership in ensuring there are no blockers both within IBM, ensuring all the correct paperwork is completed and supplied by the individual in a timely manner, and within the Home Office, by having regular contact with the Home Office Cluster 2 team to check on the application’s progress.  
 
Our team also performs rigorous aftercare activities for our cleared staff; tracking status, providing ongoing training and validation of this being completed.
