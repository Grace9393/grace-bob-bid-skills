IBM brings deep experience and expertise in shaping and leading overall technical architecture for a range of portfolios, programmes and projects.  
For EU-LISA, IBM are leading the shaping and creation of the technical architecture for the Schengen border crossing solution to securely share information between member states.  IBM has created and shared design artefacts, using open standard formats aligned with TOGAF.  All IBM certified Architects are Open Group certified.  
The architecture and solution is designed with open standard technology; making use of common data formats to enable interoperability. The underpinning container platform provides scalability, portability and supports modular design approaches.
