Our approach to benefits identification and realisation during migration 
Using BlueCAT, we will develop a detailed benefits case for you that we will track through the programme. One of the most important benefits that we will track is overall cost savings realised for SLC. We will create and maintain a cost model for you based on your operating costs today, adjusted over time in alignment with the migration roadmap and the savings we expect to achieve based on benchmarks in IBM Garage Method for Cloud. This will provide you with a detailed projected cost savings model that we will track as the programme is executed. Examples of these benefits are improved system performance/response time, improved system availability, reduction in number of tickets raised for faults and outages, increased customer satisfaction, increased employee satisfaction, improved time to market for new offerings and services and reduction in licensing requirements through system rationalisation. We will agree with you which benefits are important to capture and how to do so, and will regularly report on them as KPIs in a benefits realisation dashboard. We will automate this and make it available online or via mobile app for your leadership team. We would also be happy to discuss commercialising these KPIs under an SoW, providing you with even greater confidence that the outcomes will be achieved.
