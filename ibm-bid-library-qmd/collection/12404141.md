The strength and breadth of this relationship will be built through experience, partnership, success achieved and more. As such the supplier needs to include answers to, at least,  the following within their response:

•	Are you a summit or platinum partner? 
•	For how long has the supplier been delivering Salesforce based solutions?

•	What else can you tell us about your relationship with Salesforce that would add value to your work/solution for ACRO?"

IBM Consulting maintains a global strategic partnership with Salesforce spanning over 23 years, holding Platinum Partner status and consistently achieving industry recognition, including 
 
•          2 x Salesforce Partner Innovation Awards in 2021 (HSE and FirstGroup)
•          Salesforce UK&I Consulting Partner of the Year 2022
•          Outstanding Connected Ecosystem Partner 2023
 
Our Salesforce practice has delivered over 12,000 successful customer engagements while maintaining an exceptional customer satisfaction (CSAT) rating of 4.8 out of 5.
 
Our extensive certified expertise forms the foundation of our Salesforce partnership. Our global team includes 5,900 Salesforce experts holding over 22,700 certifications. In the UK and Ireland, we maintain 220+ certified experts with 1,000+ certifications. This excludes our partner ecosystem from which we can draw specific skills and expertise, including Salesforce's professional services team. This breadth of expertise ensures ACRO has access to specialists across all relevant Salesforce technologies, including Experience Cloud, Public Sector Solutions, Service Cloud, and MuleSoft.
 
We deliver data-driven experiences and outcomes for our customers by combining the power of the Salesforce platform with the strength of IBM’s capabilities, technology and insights.
 
 
 
As a Platinum Partner, we maintain privileged access to Salesforce resources that will directly benefit ACRO's implementation. Our status provides priority access to Salesforce product teams and technical resources, while our early insight into Salesforce roadmaps enables forward-looking solution designs. IBM sits on over ten global partner advisory boards, helping to shape the Salesforce roadmap via client and partner feedback. This relationship provides the ability to escalate any platform issues through established senior relationships quickly and offers a direct channel to influence product development based on client needs.
 
Our proven public sector experience demonstrates our capability to deliver for ACRO. We deeply understand public sector requirements and compliance needs, supported by our track record of delivering secure, scalable Salesforce solutions for government organisations such as NHS Test and Trace, Health Service Executive Ireland, and HMRC. This experience extends to implementing solutions that handle sensitive data with appropriate security controls that are directly relevant to ACRO's requirements.
 
IBM brings significant value-added capabilities to complement our Salesforce expertise. We provide access to IBM's extensive suite of pre-built Salesforce assets and accelerators, such as IBM Consulting Advantage, our AI platform that combines large language models with all of Salesforce's documentation. Our expertise spans IBM's technology portfolio, including AI and automation, featuring industry-leading AI capabilities that complement Salesforce Einstein, such as watsonx.ai/data/governance and Watson Orchestrate. Our focus on responsible AI implementation aligns perfectly with public sector requirements.
 
This comprehensive partnership enables us to rapidly resolve technical challenges through established escalation paths and design optimal solutions leveraging the latest platform capabilities. We can ensure security and compliance requirements are met while accelerating delivery through proven assets and methodologies. Importantly, we can future-proof implementations through roadmap alignment.
 
Our long-standing relationship with Salesforce, combined with our public sector expertise and technical capabilities, position us ideally to support ACRO's transformation journey while minimising risk and maximising value from the Salesforce platform. The depth and breadth of our partnership ensures ACRO will benefit from industry-leading expertise, proven delivery capabilities, and innovative solutions throughout its transformation journey.
 
This response formed part of a winning bid with an overall score of 76.67%, however individual scores/feedback for the responses were not provided.
