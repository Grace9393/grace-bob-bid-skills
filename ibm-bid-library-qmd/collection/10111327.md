Service Management is a foundation of IBM’s Integrated Process Framework, see Figure TC5.1. We approach it by seamlessly applying ITIL4-aligned policy, process, and procedures and using industry-standard ITSM tools (eg ServiceNow) for management and tracking the delivery of application-managed services.
 
Each service includes a service-level management component, using Service Levels Agreements (SLAs) and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to monitor, measure, and report progress against objectives. We review SLAs and KPIs at regular service reviews to monitor our adherence to them and discuss, agree, and action on service improvement initiatives which we track to completion When engaging third parties to provide components of our services, we flow down relevant SLAs, credits, and thresholds establishing back-to-back service obligations.
 
Our approach has been proven successful across many UK government departments of similar size, scale, complexity, and criticality, eg Home Office, Defra, and DWP. Our approach will minimise delivery risks for your new service by building on our existing service delivery and extending our proven approach across your L2/L3 portfolio.
 
We’ll exceed your requirements by continuing to supply our ITIL-certified Service Delivery Management team, who are familiar with your working methods and deliver service excellence for UKHSA, as evidenced by the excellent NPS results (below).
 
Situation:
UKHSA required a trusted partner to set up and run a 24x7 L2 and L3 team to support critical Test and Trace (T&T) applications during the COVID-19 pandemic.
 
Task:
UKHSA appointed IBM to manage its production environment and support T&T applications. IBM was made responsible for providing L2 and L3 support, ensuring a reliable and transparent service, and quickly resolving any issues. The management team was responsible for overseeing incident and change management.
 
Actions:
We established a support organisation that was fully operational within the first month. The organisation provided governance structures based on monthly service reviews, supported by our Integrated Process Framework, tailored to UKHSA to monitor, measure and report on service performance initially using Jira and, latterly, ServiceNow.
 
We’re involved in early design to understand what we’re taking on and then manage knowledge transfer as we transition into operation. We control service acceptance of development work, enabling continuous service improvement with minimal disruption.
 
SLAs were introduced to measure and assess response and restoration times for events disrupting and degrading the service, and the response times for implementing changes. Eg, one SLA pertains to Change Management, specifically the "Time to Execute Changes". As part of our commitment to continuous improvement, we developed with MuleSoft and implemented a script to automate the monthly application of MuleSoft patches in the lower environments to accelerate the time to execute this change.
 
Other service improvements included introducing supporting capabilities such as Slack and Teams to allow IBM to engage, guide and direct the triage and fix incidents across the multi-supplier environment, ensuring consistent service level achievement.
 
Results:
We delivered a service management capability to monitor, measure and report on the T&T service within days of operations commencing. The framework ensured new applications were fully reviewed and assessed, so they could be managed appropriately without impacting the environment's resourcing, performance, or status.
 
Our service has resulted in:
• Zero P1s in the past 2-years (with only two P1s prior to this)
• Improvement in the time to execute monthly MuleSoft patches from 0.5 days a month to 30 minutes
• Minimal rollbacks resulting from a change, evidencing quality of governance, controls and delivery, and
• Excellent customer satisfaction score; a maximum NPS of 10 awarded by UKHSA for 2022 and 2023.
