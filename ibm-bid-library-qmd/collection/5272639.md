IBM runs rigorous processes & systems to forecast resource demand and track recruitment, skills development and retention. We supplement demand forecasts with insights from our strategic clients. Areas forecasting high demand are supported with recruitment, targeted re-skilling, partnerships and acquisition. We also maintain a very active presence in the technical sub-contractor market. IBM is currently investing in targeted upskilling in Cloud/DevOps, Full Stack (end to end) development, AI/Data Science and Data Management. In 2019, IBM invested $622 million in global employee education programs with a focus on external certification in Cloud and Data skills.
