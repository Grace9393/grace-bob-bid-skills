One of the core reasons as to why IBM have selected SAP4HANA Public Cloud as the solution for SZC’s ERP system is to ensure scalability. As the construction of the new nuclear power station grows, the system needs to absorb the growth, to adapt to the increased requirements and volume without disruption. IBM view SZC’s requirement as needing a simple, effective, ERP system that runs silently. with no disruption to the components integrated to the solution: Finance, HR & Supply Chain and potentially more systems.
 
By adopting and embracing an off the shelf product, the processes which are defined by SAP simplifies this further. 
 
With the SAP4HANA Public Cloud product that IBM have recommended, we do not perceive that SZC will have any constraints as a result of scaling up, in tandem with the growth with SZC construction programme.
