As a delivery organisation, IBM has spent decades creating and refining project methodologies that meet the needs of our client.  Over recent years, we have modernised the methods we use to deliver services to our clients, building on our ever-changing environment:  Digital, Employee Centric, Integrated and Agile for example. IBM are happy to adopt and use Sims Metal Management preferred project management methodology. Alternatively, IBM has a highly developed and proven programme delivery methodology for deploying large complex global deployments. Our methods cover the skills to successfully manage and deliver a project, implement Cloud technology, and deploy successfully to your people.  We are able to tailor our approach to SIMS unique needs whilst also using IBM’s tried and tested assets and accelerators such as Blueworks Live or our Migration Toolkit.
IBM propose to combine the following methodologies in our engagement with you – IBM’s Programme Management Method, Ascend to Cloud Method (Oracle or SAP variations) and our Change Method.  These are described in more detail within this document, as well as how we integrate our methods with your own existing ways of working in a Method Adoption Workshop.   Strong governance and an effective PMO function are essential to drive programme success. 
 
 
IBM Programme Management Method
Overarching all our Methods is our Worldwide Programme Management Method (WWPMM). WWPMM is a result of IBM's drive to provide a "consistent approach" to managing programmes and embedded within WWPMM is our Seven Keys to Success framework for assessing, managing and reporting on the health of the whole programme. 
IBM has evolved this framework based on experience gained from   the thousands of programmes we run every year. It has been proven to deliver tangible improvements in successful delivery and is now mandated as the way we report programme health internally within IBM. As an output of the Method Adoption Workshop, IBM will work with Sims Me to incorporate components of WWPMM and our Seven Keys to Success into your programme dashboard and reporting process. 
The “Seven Keys to Success™”, allows IBM programme managers to utilise the seven keys as the framework and window for all progress reporting and issue resolution from Steering Group to the work stream meetings.
The management method and approach encourage our staff to look at the leading indicators of success (based on empirical evidence wherever possible) to determine the true status of progress and whether the conditions have been established for future success. It extends the traditional outlook of managing assignments to considering not only the ultimate beneficiaries of, and stakeholders for, the work but also the assignment contributors. The sense of this leading indicator is that if all parties are benefitting at the corporate and personal level then positive conditions have been set for a motivated team.   
 
Our Programme Manager will utilise IBM Programme Work Centre (IPWC), IBM’s strategic Project and Programme Management tool throughout this implementation.  It is a licensed product that we provide free of charge for the period of the project. It is a cloud-based Project and Programme management tool which offers a collaborative environment to the entire project team for managing all related work products.  This tool supports tasks and milestones, issues and risks, decisions, reporting (with our Seven Keys to Success framework), change control, document management and business benefits. More information is available at http://ibm.com/ipwc
