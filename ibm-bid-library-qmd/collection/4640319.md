IBM has worked closely with Defence Business Services (DBS) on systems, data, architecture and content for 20 years.
The nature of the work is both live support and implementation of change. IBM has been directly involved in the design, implementation and on-going support of most of the corporate services solutions provided by DBS to the wider MOD user base. This includes defining strategies through to design, implementation and support of the underlying infrastructure, databases, interfaces and applications. 
IBM has also developed the Corporate Business Intelligence solution, including the underlying Data Warehouse, integrating multiple data sources for users across the MOD.
