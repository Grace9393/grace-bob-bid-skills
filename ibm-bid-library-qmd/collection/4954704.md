Our Experience & Skills
 
We are excited for the opportunity to build on our 20+ years of experience working in collaboration with security teams across the UK government. We specialise in empowering clients to be adaptive to ever-changing security threats through secure-by-design architectures, adoption of DevSecOps methodologies and implementation of robust governance frameworks to help understand, quantify and mitigate their risks. Led by our dedicated Security Services, we are proud to be listed as a Strong Performer in the recent report ‘The Forrester Wave™: European Cybersecurity Consulting Providers, Q3 2021’, as well as a Worldwide Leader in the IDC ‘Marketscape: Worldwide Managed Security Services 2020 Vendor Assessment’.
 
IBM is delighted to bring our leading knowledge and strategy to work with the Home Office to develop a culture of ‘secure by default’ working and a thriving community of security experts. We understand that to build an enduring legacy and self-sustaining DDaT Security capability it is not enough to mandate security standards from an ivory tower. Instead, our Security specialists will share their thought leadership and innovation to empower DDaT Professionals across teams and specialisms to build their security expertise. We will provide Industry experts who will lead the delivery of effective and secure services through sharing of best practice, collaborative strategy building, and standardising approaches to Security across products through robust, future-proof governance. At the same time as leading the secure delivery of your portfolio, we will be supporting the profession to take ownership, working towards a self-reliant community of experts that future-proofs the capability after the contract finishes. 
 
To achieve this vision, we will provide leading experts in the Security field. We have a mature and vibrant Architecture Profession with over 2,500 architects, who work very closely to share our thought leadership with The OpenGroup and therefore our professionals achieve accreditation to The OpenGroup architectural career across Certified, Master and Distinguished levels as part of their architect training. Our architects use methods such as TOGAF or SABSA and have certifications including CISSP, CISM, and CCP. Our continuous learning journey means architects are well versed in modern concepts, such as Zero Trust, Hybrid Cloud, Micro-segmentation, Micro-Services, Containers and Serverless solutions and follow the Security architecture guidance from international organisations such as NIST and NCSC. 
 
We are equally proud of the other partnerships we have developed across the technology landscape, having built expertise in a wide array of products and services:
- IBM are a Premier Partner with AWS, earning the status of Distinction for the AWS Partner Network for the number of certifications achieved.  Further to this, our IBM Security unit are endorsed as an AWS Premier Consulting Security Competency Partner, an AWS Advanced Technology Competency Partner, an AWS ISV Accelerate Partner and an AWS Level 1 MSSP Competency Partner; 
- We are also a Microsoft Gold Partner and a member of the President’s Club and Inner Circle, which is awarded to the world’s top 1% of Microsoft partners along with numerous Azure certifications;
- Furthermore, we have Google Cloud certified professionals as well as RedHat and IBM Cloud, both of which are very familiar to our team being part of IBM;
- Outside of the Cloud hyper-scalers we have strong partnerships with ISVs / SaaS vendors such as ServiceNow, Salesforce and Adobe along with more traditional vendors such as Oracle and Open-Source technologies; and
- Through Red Hat, an IBM company, our consultants also have access to the expertise and though leadership behind RHEL OS, security products such as KeyCloak, automation technologies such as Ansible Tower as well as the Openshift Container Platform and Kubernetes container orchestration tools – including the security aspects required to support this contract.
 
This broad range of relationships enables us to provide resources up to date with the rapidly changing technologies and critically provide the Home Office with the skills they need. We will bring these relationships to our work within the Profession as well as broader Home Office projects; creating wider networks through our staff, the partners and Home Office teams whilst leveraging the partnerships to benefit the inflight and future work being carried out, including training opportunities for Home Office staff to further support the development of the Security Architecture Community.
 
Below is a sample of the work we do across the UK that showcases the skills across all those identified in Section 3.2 of Appendix B of the tender documentation:
 
Example
Our Technical Leaders thrive when working across rainbow teams with multiple clients / vendors, as shown through our work with HMRC where we manage a multi-vendor architecture service reporting directly to the HMRC chief architect. Similarly, as part of our work with DWP we have been working in the rainbow Architecture Team to drive strategy regarding future technical direction, adoption of new technology and consolidation of existing disparate teams and products.
 
IBM has already been a part of shaping the security strategy and architecture within SEMAPHORE, where we manage a Security Operations Centre monitoring and resolving alerts for critical infrastructure contributing towards national security. Our team also assisted with System Migrations through comprehensive penetration testing of both the Authority’s AWS environment and the Application Container Platform. We then worked with your Security Teams to advise on the results, incorporating necessary changes into the security strategy to mitigate container & public cloud vulnerabilities. 
 
We have a proven track record leading work to drive a culture of shifting security left and enabling security communities through the production of robust, adaptive governance across delivery lifecycles. For the NHS-Digital Data Security Centre (DSC), we developed a Security Operating Model which enabled centralised management of security strategy which enforced policies out to individual NHS Trusts. This allowed scaled adoption of a unified security approach. 
 
IBM collaborated with a large UK Retail client to develop a centralised collection of Security Assets, accessible through a web portal that drove best practice throughout the organisation. Through use of collaboration tools, we have empowered staff across organisations / departments to be part of the security conversation and drive a culture of continuous improvement and sharing of best practice. 
 
We have also been delighting our clients within Defence & Intelligence, where security is paramount, and the highest standards are adhered to. We have boosted the client’s engineering capabilities through the production and curation of reusable assets that enable rapid, secure development. This includes vetted container images, UI Component libraries, and secure, scalable DevOps deployment pipelines making use of vulnerability scanning tools to verify code at each stage of the application lifecycle. These tools standardise the design and development approach and accelerate adoption of security best practice whilst minimising any impact on delivery velocity.  As part of our client’s work to adopt robotic process automation (RPA) technologies into their high-security environment, we also worked closely with their security and legal teams to design an implementation that adheres to existing security standards, legislature and auditability requirements. We also co-authored their automation strategy that mandated secure implementation whilst still allowing them to realise the benefits of the technology. 
 
 
We are proud that all the work we do with our clients demonstrates the requirement in section 3.3.2 with regards demonstrating the right behaviours and engaging with the community.  Our team will integrate and deliver, as part of your wider success structure, aligned to the DDaT strategy and building the Home Office capability.  We do this already with you, notably at Semaphore, DBS and SAS and will continue with the same positive attitudes for this service.

Through these events we work with clients to provide practical experience dealing with security tools and scenarios. We propose to agree a programme of support and training events like these as part of bolstering your security profession, giving DDaT staff access to the very latest learning and innovation within the Security Industry.
