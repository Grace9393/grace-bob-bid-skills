We have identified the following challenges:  
 
Making sure that the support team has sufficient knowledge about the app and back-end. We will mitigate this by adopting a Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) approach. Our 3rd line support function will consist of both on-call application developers and platform DevOps engineers. As part of a rotating on-call function, they will spend 50% of their time on call and the 50% in new feature delivery. Using the same team and set of engineers to develop and deploy new app features, will minimise organisational silos, creating a smooth running and efficient service. Down time and other risks will be minimised, whilst optimising the flow of new features into production in this continuous delivery model. 
 
The demand for high availability of the application to citizens. IBM is the trusted partner for companies looking for high availability of their systems. We develop and run the global FX platform for CLS Group, managing over $5 trillion/day of transactions, and used by over 90% of the world’s financial institutions. We will achieve this high availability by building automation and self-healing into the service using a DevOps ops approach so that the app is available when needed for end users. 
 
App Security. Taking a DevSecOps approach, our teams will develop and release according to NHS Standards. Our security architect will regularly review and feedback on development activities, we will constantly monitor for external threats and probes, and actively work with partners to provide additional  
insights into app security. We have helped TSB to transform their app, from one that was rated as one of the worst performing banking apps with respect to security, to one of the strongest, independently verified by Which.  
 
Ensuring 3rd party services are available 24x7x365. This will be mitigated through regular governance and including 3rd parties as part of the wider team with agreed escalation processes. Providing automated monitoring and managing change windows with regular maintenance will all support consistent availability of the app.
