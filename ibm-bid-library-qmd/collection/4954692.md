IBM has a dedicated UK Public Sector security practice which will deliver the security requirements for this contract.  We have specialists in Information Assurance for our Government clients and are well versed in NCSC guidance and all Government security policies and frameworks. We are used to working at SC and DV levels with staff submitting appropriate vetting, as required by the role. IBM’s Security Aftercare process maintains these clearances and allows resources to be identified in a controlled way. Our resourcing will be drawn from this group who are already cleared, many also with experience of successful delivery with the Home Office. We would then look for the right people from our UK resources who are capable of clearance, additionally we have a series of individual and third-party contractors that have the right skills and clearances in place. Drawing form this large pool of resources enables us to quickly identify the right resources and onboard them to the relevant SoW.
