When we started the partnership with our client BP they had an outdated digital presence which had 30 different sites globally, with 74 different designs. We created one responsive solution for multiple devices, 29 languages and 43 countries. Our delivery was anchored in automated QA and regression testing. The test strategy covered Static, Unit, System, Integration and Performance testing, using products including Selenium Webdriver and BrowserStack, Mocha, Jasmine, Jmeter, Taurus and Sonarqube. The automated wide-coverage enables confidence in pushing direct to users in the fortnightly sprint cycle. Manual testing of specific assistive technologies was performed on a slower cycle.
