IBM has been a world leader in data and data architecture since the early days of computing in the 50’s and 60’s by leading the development of network and hierarchical databases.  In June 1970 the pioneer of relational databases, Edgar F Codd, was a researcher at IBM and he described the RDBMS theory and published a model for data manipulation.  By 1974 the IBM San Jose Research centre had developed a relational DBMS, System R, to implement Codd's concepts and a key development of the System R project was the Structured Query Language (SQL) which is still at the forefront of data management today.
 
Today IBM continues to shape future data architecture thinking and future data technologies through thought leadership, service offerings and our products.  We continue to build on our deep experience of passenger data products such as Biometrics and aviation data gained on Home Office projects such as IAB and Semaphore.  We will bring this approach to our work with the Data & Data Architecture Profession, learning from best practice, creating the data architecture patterns and methods of the future together and sharing appropriate success to the benefit of all - establishing the Data Architecture Profession, as part of our partner services, as a Thought Leading organisation, enriching the Data Architect ecosystem that we build with you, helping your professionals to grow.
