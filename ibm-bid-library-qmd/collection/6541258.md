These technologies, when delivered correctly, bring simplicity, meaning that NHS Digital will spend less time managing technology and more time support clinicians at work, help patients get the best care, and use data to improve health and care. Our approach to establishing the NHS CCOE includes three critical elements:  
 
- Building a movement across NHS – we will help you build Guilds and provide the training to establish a culture where everyone thrives, develops, and grows, which will be critical to achieving the right behaviour for your journey to cloud. Embedded in this will be technical tooling such as the portal to help the Guilds grow and mature. 
- Automating to accelerate – IBM cloud assets will accelerate the work NHS Digital has started, for example our Klarity Cloud Management Tool brings automation of best practice capabilities and creation of landing zones and shared services. The user-friendly dashboards give you full control of your cloud estates and costs across Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud Platform and on-prem VMware. 
- Thinking big, starting small – we will build a holistic business case through discovery of the existing estate and use this insight to create roadmaps that bring ‘quick wins’ such as building the portfolio of policy as code and incrementally growing and mature the services to transform the way in which NHS develops Cloud based Digital Services.
