How we will ensure effective onboarding that reduces customer impact 
Continuity of service, customer and user experience underpin our approach to all transition projects. To minimise impact to your customers, we will: 
- start by further understanding your key customer touchpoints for the in-scope services and apply this knowledge to measure and prioritise risk mitigations during transition 
- review factors such as live service issues, peak customer demand periods and external forces such as COVID-19 to understand other factors impacting you and your customers within the transition period 
- automate data collection activities using the Discovery tooling described above, limiting demand for time from your SMEs and support teams so they can focus on service, and 
- develop our plans to avoid peak times and align with those of other partners so we don’t duplicate disruption. 
To enable this, our team will 
- record KT sessions and produce comprehensive documentation, which means we will ‘only need to be told once’, and 
- start to perform basic delivery tasks from the start of transition, allowing SMEs to focus on complex tasks. 
The SCRs measure, against the defined criteria, the readiness of the component to be transitioned. This process provides SLC and IBM the confidence that the service is ready to be transitioned and that customers will not be impacted.
