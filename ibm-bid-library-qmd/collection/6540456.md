We absolutely agree that this philosophy is highly effective, drives the right outcomes and increases the whole team engagement. The skilled professionals we will bring will embody these principles in the way we set up our teams and how we operate on a day-to-day basis. This approach features in all IBM delivery methods which put agile and DevOps at the centre and drives greater engagement from support and development teams. We have extensive experience with this model with clients such as the Eire Health Service Executive (a vaccine management service), the Scottish Government, HMRC, DWP, Virgin Money and TSB Bank. We have seen an average productivity gain of 10% per year when clients adopt this approach. 
Enablement of an effective DevOps culture is a whole team activity and within the rainbow teams foster a culture of continuous curiosity in which knowledge sharing is the norm, successes will be celebrated as much as continual learning from issues and challenges. Team charters will be the deliverables here that will be brought to life by our passionate and dedicated professionals whilst following a “badges at the door” culture – we understand this approach is a requirement within 2.7 section of schedule 20 however we embed this philosophy in all our DevOps engagements. Our goal is to achieve a comparably successful blended and “badgeless” blend team environment for Spine Clinicals as we did on NHS Test & Trace, facilitating trusted relationships and collaboration across the NHS T&T ecosystem, regardless of employer. This led Simon Bolton (at the time, NHS T&T CIO) to comment: “If you can create that environment where you have got a team working together, with a single purpose and a passion, it is truly astounding what you can deliver.”  
From a technical perspective, it is of upmost importance that there is effective Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment in place to ensure that delivery through the route to live is efficient, predictable and repeatable. We will work with you to leverage existing pipelines and identify opportunities for enhancements as appropriate, to continually test the quality of deliverables with any failures being prioritised to be resolved before moving forwards. This is critical to maintaining both delivery momentum and system integrity. 
Within the appropriate governance, our developers are typically empowered to run pipelines and automation in a self-serve manner, with the ability to deploy their code and run unit tests prior to submission. Effective pipelines as part of a standardised process gives individuals absolute understanding, reducing defects and incidents due to inability interpret and follow complex process. A Developer Pledge should be defined and implemented during the transition phase outlining best practice for Developers to follow – where there is uncertainty Developers will highlight issues for discussion rather than implementing obscure non-standard processes or work-arounds. This combined with effective User Access Management substantially reduces the risk of “Manual Intervention” (non-Build manual changes by developers) and resulting “Environment Drift” (environments becoming different from one another over time another reducing validity of test).  
Knowledge sharing can be highly effective when designed and executed correctly. A L3 support rota involving a vertical set of the team will both further harden knowledge from transition of the solutions and further increase ownership that delivers a good and stable experience for the user base through a larger set of the team having knowledge of the products and the applications that underpin them. Knowledge sharing and Thought Leadership would not just be for the IBM team, we would include the whole team to drive the team together and increase interactions, build relationships and have meaningful discussions to drive quality, safety and continuous improvement. 
Within the team, we will give opportunities to work across the Clinical Services Programmes by supporting the structuring of the teams to deliver a product centric approach delivering across the current and future platform pillars.
