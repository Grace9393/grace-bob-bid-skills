IBM - Quality Engineering – Digital Testing Services

Our offering Framework

The Digital Testing service is part of the IBM QE (Quality Engineering) suite of offerings.

IBM QE offerings cover the full breadth of testing needs.

The purpose of these services is to help you deliver quality government services at scale and to get increasingly complicated applications to market faster, with fewer defects and at a lower cost.

IBM's Digital Testing Services is a comprehensive suite of solutions designed to help businesses ensure the quality and performance of their digital assets. The services include functional, performance, security, and test automation. These services enable companies to validate that their digital products and services meet functional, performance and security requirements and are delivered on time and within budget. The services also help businesses optimise their testing processes, reduce costs and improve the overall quality of their digital assets.
