Introducing the right day 1 skills and providing skills continuity throughout the contract duration will be monitored using a skills supply and demand model. The model will be fed by existing support metrics, skills requirements and product roadmap combining to provide a holistic view of ‘demand’.  The ‘supply side’ comes from a dataset detailing each team member’s skills. We will marry these together for a holistic view of upcoming workload, skills needed and options to fulfil. The model will be used to identify skills coverage, where investment is needed in cross-training / upskilling, and helping you to plan future resourcing and building in-house capability.
