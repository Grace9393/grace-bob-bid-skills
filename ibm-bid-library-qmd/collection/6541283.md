Response to capability and capacity requirements, fitting with the requested services:We will closely monitor the backlog and jointly work with NHS on a resource demand management forecasting model. During Operational Planning Events, we will commit IBM or subcontractor resources to meet your CCOE requirements. Our approach to resource planninghas been effectively used with several healthcare clients to manage ramp ups/downs in delivery teams, for example at University Hospital Leicester.There will of course be changes to demand as a result of the bi-weekly sprint planning process, whilst this is not likely to make a major variance to the number of resources, it is important that we are able to respond quickly,in order to maintain the momentum of the programme. In these instances, we will engage our ecosystem of subcontractors and out Platinum/Gold partnerships, with Cloud Hyperscalers, to offer the required capability and capacity that is appropriate to CCOE services.
