Rapid mobilisation
 
Our core team will comprise many of the resources that you already know and trust to deliver the COP service. Throughout the term of the future service, we commit to fulfilling demand and rapidly mobilising team members as follows:
 
- Existing Expertise: From day one, we will provide a team with expertise across the COP technology stack, complemented by extensive knowledge of the business context and its deployment to support end-users.
- Alongside our core team, we will leverage our trusted supplier network, such as Lucid, to access a vast pool of specialised technical resources. This network, with over 5,000 SC Cleared Tech Professionals, enables us to swiftly respond to changing demands and maintain an acceptable level of service during transitions or shifts in priorities. 
- IBM's Internal Resource Pool: With a pool of 4,300+ SC-Cleared resources skilled in COP-relevant areas, we can support your broader needs. 
- IBM Incubator: Our Incubator program enables rapid resource deployment, allowing us to quickly adapt to new requirements and maintain service quality during transitions. This concept will be discussed in more detail below. 
- Knowledge Transfer and Handover: A well-defined process will ensure minimal disruption and seamless integration of new team members, regardless of changes in priorities or personnel. 
- Forward Planning and Regular Leadership Engagement: Meetings with stakeholders and Programme leadership, will enable us to stay ahead of shifting priorities, forecast demand and proactively adapt our resourcing plans. 
 
By combining our expert core team, broad network, and strategic resource management practices, we will deliver a swift and efficient mobilisation process. This approach ensures our team is productive in maintaining and further developing the COP platform, even in the face of changing priorities or contract transitions.
 
Our flexibility and capacity to meet changing demands
 
A dedicated IBM Account Lead will manage resources, capacity, and planning, liaising daily with key stakeholders including the COP Delivery Lead, Product Owner, and Delivery Managers to adjust priorities and resourcing plans.
 
We have an IBM Resource Management team managing SC cleared resources working on Home Office projects. This team will engage with the COP Account Lead, Home Office, and delivery team on activities including capacity analysis and forward planning. We will maintain a skills demand projection alongside IBM staff availability; the Resource Management team will enable us to reconcile the two effectively. This flexible and responsive resource pool is shown above.
 
Together with instructor led training, we have a range of online training, including access to Udemy, an online learning platform. Our IBM Learning and Development team aligns with our Industry Leaders, forecasting skill trends and requirements. We have specific learning pathways for professions with 6 stages for each; Associate, Entry, Foundation, Experienced, Expert and Thought Leader. Accreditation is awarded at each level and completion criteria involves mandatory training and evidencing relevant experience.
This approach enables a steady pipeline of qualified resources ready to step into new roles. To enable smoother transition, we will document standard operating procedures and best practices on COP, facilitating robust knowledge transfer, minimising disruption and ensuring seamless integration of new team members.
 
Facilitating Skills & Knowledge Transfer across Support Levels
 
To deliver comprehensive 2nd, 3rd, and 4th line support we will facilitate regular training sessions, workshops, and knowledge-sharing forums to transfer essential skills and expertise among team members. This practice ensures that critical knowledge is retained and disseminated. An approach we successfully took with the Scottish Government, was to create a central document library. We defined user guides and training plans by role. Team members paired for on-the-job learning with monthly progress reviews and content updates. Communities of practice were created to enhance skills, fostering cross-team learning and coaching, resulting in cross-skilled resources across all teams.
 
Our team will deliver 2nd line support, resolving technical issues in partnership with the Live Service team via the ticketing system. Adopting a collective ‘fix first’ approach, we will prioritise swift resolution of P1 incidents, regardless of source or supplier responsibility.
 
Evidence: IBM multi-vendor Incident support at DWP 
At DWP we join multi-vendor incident response calls, operating 24x7 until resolved. Supporting in resolving a P1 failure of a critical employment provider referral service, where the underlying issue on platforms was outside our application scope, we engaged in incident resolution, bringing additional specialists from IBM’s global team. Through our partnership with Oracle, SMEs restored the platform, ensuring vulnerable individuals continued to receive critical employment and financial support. 
 
For complex issues, senior specialists will provide 3rd line support, analysing problems and working with 2nd line teams for efficient resolution. Where required, 4th line support will involve architects or subject matter experts to offer strategic guidance, enabling the COP programme to align with best practices and emerging technologies.
 
To facilitate a smooth handover and ongoing support, we will create comprehensive documentation, including; Technical specifications and architecture diagrams, operational procedures and guidelines, troubleshooting guides and frequently asked questions (FAQs), training materials and user manuals and lastly, configuration management records and change logs. These supporting materials will be maintained in a centralised, easily accessible repository, ensuring that all team members have access to the most up-to-date information.
 
Enhancing Workforce Skills and Stability through Assessment and Training
We foster a culture of open feedback and celebrating success, ensuring all team members have clear, personalised goals and performance targets. As part of our quarterly appraisal cycle, managers will gather formal feedback from the COP project, contributing to performance reviews and skills assessments. Strong performance is recognised through our HR reward and recognition programmes, including annual compensation reviews. We actively address retention risks through strategies such as role rotation for growth opportunities, promotions where appropriate, compensation reviews, and tailored training and development.
 
Within the contract we will manage and support the team’s career goals, motivate them to improve their skills in areas relevant to COP, and plan growth opportunities within the team to retain knowledge, succession plan and maintain a stable workforce. Our Account Lead will be key to facilitating these processes and providing constructive feedback on performance and reward as input to their internal HR processes. This model is being successfully used across our active Home Office projects.
Managing hundreds of resources across 100+ statements of work for Central Contracts
In 2022 IBM was appointed as the prime supplier to provide highly skilled resources across two professions in the Home Office: ‘Security Architecture’ and ‘Data & Data Architecture’. In addition, we were awarded the ‘Multi-Disciplinary’ contract. The dedicated IBM Contract Director for Central Contracts works closely with senior Home Office stakeholders to understand, plan and forecast capacity and demand. Once specific demand has been identified, reviewing Home Office’s forecast at regular interlocks, they engage with our internal Capacity and Workforce Management teams, Practice Leads and Talent Acquisition teams to ensure requirements are fully understood and met. Since 2022, we have successfully delivered 100+ SOWs.
Benefits we bring through our networks and affiliations
IBM has 15,000 global alliance partners, software vendors, service integrators, and SME suppliers (over 200 of which reside in the UK). Our supply chain can leverage our elite status across numerous strategic partners, including AWS Premier Tier Partner and Microsoft Gold Partner.
Within IBM UK, over 1000 of our professionals hold an AWS certification. At our own investment, we are currently collaborating with the Home Office and AWS on a Deep Racer Event for Women in Tech; a learning event to enable more women in the Home Office to hone their machine learning skills which will be open to COP team members.  
 
Our vast network of connections and partnerships will be leveraged to bring benefits to COP, such as access to AWS / ACP Service expertise, highly skilled resources with knowledge of the COP service, industry expertise, and collaborative learning events. 
 
Business planning and capacity management process. 
IBM has a dedicated Resource Management team for our Home Office projects. They administer systems set up to ensure we can resource all work we do for you with the best resources. The resource team raise each job role /skill set in our system to signal demand. This feeds into our business planning processes, mapping and committing IBM resources with the required skills and availability. Any skill gaps will drive upskilling / cross-skilling plans, as well as third-party hiring from our ecosystem partners and permanent headcount hiring decisions. 
 
Supporting Changes of Priority
Utilising an agreed prioritisation method e.g. Weighted, Shortest Job First (WSJF), with which we are well experienced, we will work in coordination with Product Leadership through refinement sessions to review and prioritise the backlog, building and informing the longer-term Product Roadmap.
We will ensure close communication and collaboration with the business, such that Border Force are informed and updated regarding any change of priority or impact to the planned delivery roadmap. We understand that changes of priority can be required for several reasons:
- Change of Policy
- To deliver critical platform / Product stability
- To deliver new functionality in response to the geopolitical or operational environment (e.g. the Ukrainian War or Small Boats Operational risk).
Early communication with clear rationale for re-prioritisation is crucial, enabling stakeholders to understand and discuss changes. A Change Board and Business Change liaisons will be central to our approach, with operational impact and risk remaining key considerations in all re-prioritisation activities.
We are experienced in this area as evidenced recently in our Enterprise Services team where we were asked to re-prioritise tasks to accommodate a critical incident, receiving the below feedback from Trevor Smith, MBTP Delivery Director:
“For some time, we have been working with IBM and the Merged Identities team in the Home Office’s quest to resolve a specific issue we have in our data platform, one that attracted adverse publicity earlier this year. Over the past week and a bit, we have also had to deal with a critical incident on one of our services that deals with facial images. As a result, there was a need for the team to assist the Home Office in understanding the scale of this problem and ultimately systematically sense checking a sizeable pool of records. Without any hesitation, IBM accepted this challenge and initially took on the task of checking some nearly 1000 records over an 8-hour period, putting every possible resource that had access to the systems. That, and all the hard work since has resulted in us being able to positively message Ministers on this matter, reduce the risk to our customers and maintain the integrity of the systems. I therefore wanted to place on record our sincere thanks for the dedication, innovation and flexibility shown by the team”
 
Out of Hours Service
 
Providing an out of hours support service for COP, reflects the critical nature of the Platform and its significance within the MBTP portfolio.  We are excited to offer this capability through our Shared Service model, which is already providing service to other applications in the MBTP portfolio in addition to those of other mission critical clients.   
 
Managed by a dedicated Service Manager, our team operates onsite in a List X certified facility 24/7. We provide a single contact point through various channels (Service Now, Jira, Telephone, MS Teams, Email, and Slack) to collaborate with the Product team. The service includes proactive platform monitoring to identify issues before they impact service, as well as incident management to mobilise a fix team and restore service.
 
Given the 24/7 shared service nature, we can meet the response time SLAs in Appendix B and work closely with the COP Team to resolve issues based on the service/ticket priority.
 
We offer this service to multiple projects and have a proven fixed-price calculator that accounts for SLAs, incident volumes, and application complexity, rather than dedicated headcount. Our team is familiar with COP's service requirements and ready to mobilise.
