For NHS-Digital Data Security Centre (DSC) we led the development of a new Security Operating Model and governance structure.  We  supported Security teams operating both centrally, allowing the DSC to act as the Hub, and at multiple spokes within each NHS Trust to deal with local issues. Security Information is actively shared from the central hub, enabling the local teams to tailor against specific policies and processes. Our approach has enabled the spokes to benefit from the Hub's economies of scale and request advice on particular issues from other spokes within the Trusts all under a common governance structure
