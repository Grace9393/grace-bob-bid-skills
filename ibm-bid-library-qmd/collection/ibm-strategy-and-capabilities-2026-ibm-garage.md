# IBM Garage

IBM Garage is IBM Consulting's proven, collaborative engagement model — now powered by AI — that helps clients fast-track innovation and realise **5–7× the value** over traditional approaches.

## The Problem IBM Garage Solves

- **75%** of AI initiatives fail to meet their expected ROI
- Only **16%** of digital transformation initiatives scale enterprise-wide
- By 2030, AI could contribute up to $15.7 trillion to the global economy — but only organisations that embed AI into core business will capture it

IBM Garage is designed to close the gap between AI experimentation and enterprise-wide scaled value.

## Three Phases

### Co-Create
A combined team of diverse subject matter experts is immersed in intensive research and value orchestration activities to expose the true nature of a client's opportunity.

- Enterprise design thinking workshops
- Business opportunity assessment and value discovery
- User research (Lean UX, Golden Threads)
- Problem definition, prioritisation, and solution framing
- Identifies and transparently tracks value drivers from the start

### Co-Execute
A solution development cycle that uses agile and DevSecOps practices to quickly launch and test an MVP, whilst capitalising on IBM's full business, technology, and partner portfolio.

- Agile and adaptive development (not traditional waterfall)
- DevSecOps practices embedded throughout
- MVP development with iterative testing
- Integration of IBM hybrid cloud, AI, and ecosystem partner technology
- Capitalises on IBM Consulting Advantage assets and agents

### Co-Operate
Hardens and scales the robust solution — and the new culture of innovation — across the enterprise.

- Enterprise culture transformation and change management
- Solution scaling strategies
- New ways of working embedded in the organisation
- Continuous improvement and optimisation

## Methodology Foundations

IBM Garage combines six practices into a single integrated engagement model:

| Practice | Application |
|----------|-------------|
| **Lean Startup** | Entrepreneurial approach: test, learn, iterate quickly |
| **Value Orchestration** | Outcomes focus: discover, prioritise, and track value at every stage |
| **Lean UX / Golden Threads** | User focus throughout design and build |
| **Enterprise Design Thinking** | Human-centred co-creation with clients |
| **Agile Development** | Iterative delivery over exhaustive upfront planning |
| **DevSecOps** | Security and operational excellence built into delivery |

All practices are now **accelerated by generative AI tools** via IBM Consulting Advantage.

## IBM Garage for Generative AI

IBM Garage rapidly applies generative AI to new and existing use cases through a value-measured engagement model:

- Value-tracked from discovery through to scaled deployment
- Integrates IBM watsonx with partner AI capabilities (AWS, Azure, Salesforce, SAP, Adobe)
- Uses AI to improve the Garage process itself — not just the outputs
- **Productivity Measurement Lab**: scientifically measures GenAI asset performance improvements (throughput, quality, maintainability, cost reduction)
  - Recent experiment result: **41% lower costs AND higher quality and maintainability**

## IBM Consulting Advantage Integration

IBM Garage is embedded into **IBM Consulting Advantage**, IBM's AI-powered delivery platform:

- Garage methods pre-loaded as reusable assets and agents
- Value tracking and measurement built into the platform
- **30–50% productivity gains** on projects for clients
- Enables a **Composable Engagement Model** where human consultants work alongside 1.6 million digital workers

## The 4P Framework (People, Process, Platform, Partners)

IBM Garage is the **Process** element of IBM Consulting's 4P delivery model:

- **People**: 160,000+ consultants with AI certifications, supercharged by IBM Consulting Advantage assistants
- **Process**: IBM Garage — collaborative, value-tracked, AI-accelerated engagement model
- **Platform**: IBM Consulting Advantage — AI-powered delivery platform
- **Partners**: Open ecosystem integrating Microsoft, SAP, AWS, Salesforce, Adobe, Palo Alto Networks
