IBM is investing in targeted upskilling in Cloud/DevOps, Full Stack (end-to-end) development, AI/Data Science and Data Management. In 2019, IBM invested $622 million in global employee education programs with a focus on external certification in Cloud (Azure & AWS) and data skills. 
IBM has a strong values-driven culture which enables us to recruit and retain a diverse workforce from apprentices and graduates to deep technical specialists. We retain talent by offering challenging projects, investing in leading-edge training and offering competitive pay and benefits. Our approach to staff development focuses on growing skills within the organisation, using a wide range of programs, initiatives, tools and partnerships. This includes bringing:   
Expertise acquired through our award-winning internal learning programmes. 
A panel of coaches and SMEs to help foster collaboration and success. For example, to provide coaching in areas such as Agile. 
We will work with you to agree appropriate flexible working opportunities for all staff, as exemplified by our ConnectingWomen@IBM and Be Equal Ally Campaign. 
Training will be shared across the rainbow team on key tools and agile methodology. 
If requested, we are able to create a learning portal for the programme which provides free IBM content curated for and relevant to the whole programme community.  
We also invest in upskilling client teams. For example, at the DWP we coached the client team on the Customer Information System programme to build skills in agile techniques through joint agile teams and pairing our resources with DWP counterparts. Similarly, at NHS Test & Trace the training IBM created specifically for the front-line team was very highly regarded. 
“Whoever put this training together actually put a lot of thought into it. Not just what has to be learned but how people learn. My background is 30 years NHS finishing as a clinical manager then into public health now a senior health protection practitioner/manager. This is one of the best training sessions I have seen put together.” – Client, NHS Test & Trace
