we recognise the importance of ensuring that Urgent and Emergency Care (UEC) and NHS.UK provide a consistent Mental Health (MH) service. Well-being and Mental Health are incorporated in every aspect of IBM’s global business, please see https://www.ibm.com/ibm/responsibility/employee_well_being.shtml. We have successfully delivered web UI for many health customers e.g. Ohio State, ManGen, NHS D and Charite Health and would like to use the same Design Thinking & Agile approach in this SOW, including the approach as outlined in Fig. 9.2.1. 
 
Onboard teams (milestone MS01):  Correct onboarding is key to getting the joint team working effectively, in addition to those areas covered in SOW1 we would also include: 
Agreement of alignment dependencies between SOW1 & 2. 
Current MH processes for maintenance and use of the current separate directories of service. 
 
Discovery outputs and user req. (milestone DoS001):  Our business analyst will work through personas using the mental health services and together with your team will determine key user groups and user needs. Our data engineers will work with you and your NHSD team to assess the existing datasets and taxonomy and whether there is an external dataset that would provide a better list of providers within the sector and then together we can form the rationalisation approach required to meet the user needs. Initial KPIs and measurement framework will be designed for data metrics. 
Proposed solution (alpha) with user research (milestone DoS002): the new distributed data model will require convergence of current datasets and standards and across current UEC and NHS.UK data stores. We are experienced in delivering HL7 and FHIR e.g. through IBM FHIR server. Changes to support the data model will be required and we will work with you to update the .Net Model and Data logic to support the new metadata for mental health. Prototype user research will provide additional backlog for the next iterations. We will align data maintenance processes across UEC and NHS.UK to the new data set. Where additional metadata is required e.g. private or NHS, this will be collected from the health providers. The measurement framework will be based on assessments using Adobe Analytics. 
Proposed solution (beta) (milestone DoS003): From the backlog we will use agile cycles to update .Net (Data, Views & Controllers) or Netcall Liberty Create (where used for MH) and support functional and acceptance testing of the .Net solution and Adobe Analytics.  As you use standard Azure DevOps we can support this DevOps process where required.  A key step will be in transitioning UEC and NHS.UK to the new data model as outlined in Fig. 9.2.2.  It is recommended that this is done separately, with a possible transition of NHS.UK first to ensure that any learnings can be taken into the transition of UEC. Assuming current data stores are maintained, data migration will be minimal. We can discuss the best approaches to conflict resolution e.g. Last write wins, custom field merge and other resolution approaches. Compliance with data regulations e.g. GDPR is also key. 
Health guide & training (milestone DoS004): IBM has successfully delivered training and knowledge transfer for many companies, for example NHS Test & Trace: “This is one of the best training sessions I have seen put together. Bottomline. I like it. I can see our contact tracers doing well with it.”. We will work with you to understand & deliver the training requirements across call centre, admin and mental health professionals (see also Q10) 
Recommended data accessibility attributes (milestone DoS005): We recognise the importance of delivering a solution which supports all user groups and will embed the needs of disabled users within personas to ensure these are considered within all stages of use cases. This goes further than technically ensuring alt tags are accurate, succinct and supportive for screen readers but to all aspects of the design, for example, support for tabbing, mouse-overs for partially sighted, high contrast. 
New user management module for the profile editor. (milestone DoS006):  The solution will need to integrate the new data model within the UEC and NHS.UK websites and must also provide the facility for mental health providers to be able to update and check their data. We anticipate that this will require new data maintenance for profiles, change user permissions, add/delete users and assign profile permissions.  This would require a new view and controller and link to the Model and Data access logic, which would be designed to allow reuse for future data collection requirements.
