IBM designed an online criminal record check service with the Disclosure Barring Service which has now entered Beta. We successfully took the project through GDS Alpha assessment against 18 Digital Service Standard criteria scoring an 'exemplar rating'. From Discovery outputs, we iteratively prototyped and tested solutions with users, informing key design decisions (for example, users who are uncomfortable verifying identity online can use a Post Office service option). We designed UX in line with the GDS Service Manual. We built shadow GDS assessments into our sprint cycles, continuously checking that the service design linked back to specific user needs.
