Requirement 10.1
The proposed solution will integrate DLP and provide support in line with SGN Microsoft Purview.
 IBM security will provide SGN with requirements to integrate the DLP solution with MS Sentinel platform. This will cover instructions on how to integrate device telemetry, the secure protocols to use for event collection and the event types to collect from the devices in scope.
 Device on-boarding is driven by use-case new threats as they continuously emerge, IBM security will as part of its deliveries monitor the threat landscape holistically and develop new rules within our Use case library. During transition a use case prioritization workshop will be held to select and review use cases for deployment.
 IBM Security will establish clear communication matrixes so that change control can be followed appropriately when any changes are to be incorporated into setup in future.
Requirement 10.2
The Service shall recommend best practice Data Leakage Protection (DLP) policies, maintenance, and optimisation for the MS DLP solution as it is deployed. The scope of the services inlcude:
- Management and Maintenance - The service includes running the toolset and tuning the DLP rules.
- Administration and Policy Management for DLP infrastructure.
- Upgrading DLP Management server.
- Troubleshooting and resolving issues related to DLP Management server.
- Provide Troubleshooting and Resolution steps to Deskside team for the issues related to DLP on endpoints.
- Reporting - Providing Standard reports on Compliance and Data Leakage incidents handled
- Align support to Current Mode of Operation (CMO) services.
- Provide validation and classification support to:
- Detect and validate alerts reported by monitoring tools.
- Creation of tickets, capturing details of user and data exposure.
- Triage of false positives events and resolution of alerts.
- Calculate priority/severity based on clients priority and severity levels and escalate or resolve.
- Collection & Analysis support to:
- Triage of false positive events and resolution of alerts.
- Perform reviews of assessed tickets.
- Invoke Incident response plans and notify stakeholders
- Notify clients threat intel team based on assessment
- Create conference bridge to involve all relevant stakeholders for data breaches dependent on the type of breach
- Analyse and determine the nature of identified threats using the clients investigation process
- Resolve tickets and update closure notes and close the alert, or escalate if further analysis is required
- Provide Containment & Eradication up to requirement for Forensics.  L3 will be made aware of containment for collaborative response
- Supply a monthly report consisting of:
- Events being handled per month.
- Number of alerts, including a breakdown of incidents and false positives
- Details of incidents that required L3 support and actions taken
- Policy recommendations to enhance accuracy of product data security policy
