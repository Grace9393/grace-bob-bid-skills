Ease of use, operation and maintenance requires complementary initiatives. 
Successfully incorporating the needs of end users of all types into the DToS Programme products via user centred design and data-driven methods, e.g., incorporating insights from analytics in live services and providing easy means for service users and operators to give feedback. 
Providing usable interfaces for screening services to manage their own maintenance and improvements under careful governance, to promote agility and reduce demands on build teams. 
Validating that the technology products we develop for the DToS Programme have maintenance considerations built in from the outset, following coding standards and best practice while considering overall maintenance use cases and end users as part of the process. 
Providing analytics and monitoring tools that give insights across screening operations, so that product managers and service owners understand the pain points and can prioritise improvements. 
We increase the chances of success by conducting appropriate user research at each stage, including users with assisted digital needs, empowering the team to change product direction and to select the right technology products and services to deliver maximum usable value.
