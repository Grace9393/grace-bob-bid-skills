Yes. It is IBM’s statutory obligation to have such a record and to provide it to the Supervisory Authority upon request. As a processor, IBM is required in Art. 30 para 2 GDPR to maintain a list of all Controllers, all categories of processing activities carried out on behalf of a controller including contact details of their DPO.
