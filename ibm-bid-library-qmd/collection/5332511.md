Our solution and the environments supporting it is entirely cloud-based, hosted in the UK. 
The setup allows us to rapidly develop, test and implement, with the highest levels of security and rigour, and the ability to scale rapidly from the hundreds to over 35,000 agents as specified. Salesforce is used for large-scale deployments globally. The Salesforce platform, in any 24-hours, helps contact centre agents to handle 17.5m inbound customer service calls; gives 9.1bn Artificial Intelligence predictions; sends 2.2bn customised messages; runs 59.5m reports and dashboards; logs 10.9m new cases; and handles 71.8m case interactions. 
The environments we’ve proposed to be provided include the following instances of Salesforce clouds. We utilise AWS Connect for outbound phone calls, with the AWS Connect interface and instance also based out of the UK. 
 
 
 
Tools supporting development: 
- Confluence Cloud: Document management of specifications, linked to JIRA 
- Jira: Requirements management and test case management 
- Bit Bucket: Salesforce configuration and Code management 
- Selenium: Execution of automated test cases (including regression test) 
- SonarQube: Automated code quality and security checks 
- Cucumber: Creation of acceptance tests 
- Zeyphr: Scaled test management, allowing tracking of testing against user stories 
- SauceLabs: Testing of delivered functionality across Mobile, Desktop and Tablet 
- JMeter: Perfomance testing 
- Splunk & BitBucket Plugin: Monitoring the DevOps process 
- Splunk & Salesforce Plugin: Monitoring of all operational activities, analyse trends, security and user behaviour 
 
As each of the elements is cloud-based, we will not require additional specialised support from NHS digital over environments. We will require data in the form of reference data and test data. We will also require support from the NHS Digital personnel in any integrations that are required such as with Gov.Uk 
IBM confirms that the solution provided will meet the NHS Digital Guidelines, as outlined in the ITT. 
To deliver the flexibility of scale required for the solution, each of the solution elements, from the usage of Salesforce through to partnership with Amazon allows dynamic scaling of system resources in response to demand, within the licence parameters. 
As part of our ongoing governance, we will track the licences that are requested, deployed and utilised every month. We will review the tooling licence requirements on an ongoing basis and forecast any increase or reduction as part of the monthly and quarterly governance to ensure that licences are not idle. This approach provides maximum flexibility and no exposure to unnecessary cost.
