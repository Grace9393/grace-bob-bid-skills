With a proven track record supporting critical national infrastructure platforms including EBSA and PCDP, we excel in managing Level 2 support and service transitions for wider MBTP services, including AccessUK, ATLAS, and Status. This enables effective incident triage, prioritisation, impact assessment, and seamless management, ensuring SLAs and objectives are consistently achieved.

We achieve this through clear governance structures, joint escalation procedures, and defined roles. Incident triage and prioritisation 
 
To deliver stable, responsive and efficient service delivery across a multi-supplier ecosystem, we will implement a robust and efficient incident triage and prioritisation framework, building on our proven methodologies, including those successfully utilised by our HO Merged Identities team. We understand the critical importance of swift incident identification, notification, triage, and prioritisation in minimising impact and achieving timely resolutions. Building on our deep experience, we have developed and refined processes to effectively manage incidents, including high-pressure scenarios, as outlined below.
 
Our approach includes the establishment of a dedicated Slack communication channel where suppliers can report potential merged case incidents after raising them in ServiceNow, initiating the triage process. Once notified, our team conducts an initial technical triage by reviewing the ServiceNow ticket. If the case is accepted as an incident, it is immediately prioritised using mutually agreed criteria, including impact and urgency, and assigned to the appropriate resolver group. Incidents are prioritised based on a structured risk matrix, which considers factors such as data breaches, public-facing issues, and service outages. Each incident will be categorised as P1 (critical), P2 (high), P3 (medium), or P4 (low) priority, ensuring a consistent and transparent approach to incident management. Communications are tailored to the priority level, ensuring clear and timely updates. Linked JIRA tickets are added to the ServiceNow record, to ensure transparency and efficient tracking through to resolution and closure.
 
We work closely with MBTP supplier partners, for example CACI, Fujitsu etc to resolve issues swiftly and efficiently. Utilising our expertise in ITNow/ServiceNow, we manage problem records with precision to ensure timely resolution. For example, a recent issue affecting biometric identities was identified and addressed quickly; Our team collaborated with key stakeholders and suppliers, including Leidos, Accenture, and Deloitte, while engaging the High Priority Incident (HPI) team and Service Management. To streamline communication with external resources, we established dedicated Slack channels and Teams chats.
 
The issue was escalated to P1 priority, and our team displayed flexibility, commitment, and professionalism, often working evenings and weekends to mitigate the problem until a strategic solution was implemented. Throughout, we provided real-time data to the SRO and Ministers, keeping them informed.
 
Whilst resolving the root cause was outside our remit, we played a pivotal role in identifying affected cases and delivering vital information to the supplier, enabling them to implement tactical resolutions. Our team’s proactive approach meant we were recognised as a standout supplier, exemplifying our ability to collaborate and deliver under pressure.
 
We provide monthly incident packs to key HO stakeholders, offering insights into volumes, trends, and outcomes. Prioritisation strategies are reviewed with Service Management during monthly service reviews, ensuring alignment with service design and effective processes throughout the contract lifecycle.
 
We are committed to innovation and improvement, advocating for ServiceNow incident tagging to enhance reporting and analytics. This initiative improved our ability to generate detailed insights into incident types, enabling informed decision-making and strategic improvements. For instance, it enabled the organisation of 42,000 Aslyum Claim records into 220 distinct problem areas, allowing resources to be prioritised for addressing higher-impact issues.
 
Critical Product and Application Management 
 
Ensuring the continuity of critical products and applications in line with target Service Level Agreements (SLAs) is at the core of what we do. We achieve this through a proactive, collaborative approach, leveraging our HO technology expertise and supplier relationships. Below, we outline how we act between suppliers to maintain operational excellence and provide an example that demonstrates our commitment.
 
Clear Communication and Defined Expectations
 
From day 1, we prioritise clarity and alignment across the supplier ecosystem to ensure seamless collaboration. This involves:
- Defining Roles and Responsibilities: Establishing a clear understanding of each supplier’s responsibilities, emphasising interdependencies within the ecosystem. On Merged Identities this has enabled prompt issue resolution with clear ownership and support functions.
- Regular Communication: Regular meetings, such as daily stand ups, weekly status meetings and monthly performance reviews. On the COP programme this has built a strong team identity and culture. 
- Documenting SLAs: Drafting comprehensive SLA agreements that outline key performance metrics, response times, resolution times, and escalation protocols. For example, on Merged Identities, this ensures that all parties involved enter into incident management with a shared understanding of expectations, enabling each to perform effectively and collaboratively.
 
We act as the central contact point for stakeholder communication, acting as the glue that brings all suppliers and stakeholders together by ensuring everyone has the right information at the right time. This effective coordination fosters transparency, accountability, and alignment among all parties, emphasising the critical importance of maintaining system uptime and ensuring the uninterrupted operation of essential products and applications in line with target SLAs. We achieve this by maintaining clear and consistent communication, providing regular updates to suppliers on incident status, and coordinating responses to enable swift resolutions. Additionally, we ensure suppliers have seamless access to up-to-date ServiceNow records, Confluence documentation, and communication platforms such as Slack and Teams, further supporting collaboration and efficient issue resolution.
 
Our team uses a variety of real-time monitoring tools, including Cloudwatch, Grafana, Prometheus, Dynatrace, Sysdig, Zabbix, and Kibana, to ensure comprehensive oversight of services. With years of expertise in utilising these tools, our engineers effectively manage service delivery across complex multi-supplier ecosystems.
 
Collaborative Problem Resolution 
 
Our approach to collaborating with multiple suppliers to analyse and resolve problems efficiently is built on transparency, effective coordination, and a shared commitment to service excellence. Recognising the value of strong relationships, we prioritise building connections with suppliers and stakeholders in advance, ensuring a foundation of trust and mutual understanding before engagement is required to resolve an incident. With significant experience operating in multi-supplier ecosystems, we have established robust processes and promote a culture of open communication and accountability, enabling seamless collaboration and timely problem resolution.
 
We are experienced in collaborative problem resolution as evidenced in our Merged identities team where we were asked to re-prioritise tasks to accommodate a critical incident, receiving the following feedback from Trevor Smith, MBTP Delivery Director: 
 
“For some time, we have been working with the Merged Identities team in the Home Office’s quest to resolve a specific issue we have in our data platform, one that attracted adverse publicity earlier this year. We have also had to deal with a critical incident on one of our services that deals with facial images. As a result, there was a need for the team to assist the Home Office in understanding the scale of this problem and ultimately systematically sense checking a sizeable pool of records. Without any hesitation, they accepted this challenge and initially took on the task of checking some nearly 1000 records over an 8-hour period, putting every possible resource that had access to the systems. That, and all the hard work since has resulted in us being able to positively message Ministers on this matter, reduce the risk to our customers and maintain the integrity of the systems. I therefore wanted to place on record our sincere thanks for the dedication, innovation and flexibility shown by the team"
 
We adopt a "one team" approach, establishing clear roles and responsibilities through a project charter that aligns all suppliers with shared objectives. Regular collaboration is facilitated via shared communication platforms, such as Slack, ensuring real-time visibility of incidents and swift responses. This is complemented by regular checkpoints during critical periods, enabling effective coordination and rapid problem resolution.
 
When incidents occur, our Service Manager and Incident Manager act as central points of contact, ensuring consistent communication and engagement with relevant stakeholders. They draw on our pool of over 300 dedicated technical experts and a network of over 200 Security Cleared SMEs within our partner ecosystem. This extensive resource base allows us to deploy the right expertise at the right time, ensuring timely and effective resolutions.
 
We take a structured approach to problem analysis by working collaboratively with suppliers to identify root causes and implement preventive measures. High-priority incidents are documented through detailed High Priority Incident Reports (HPIRs), which capture insights and lessons learned to inform future processes. This continuous improvement mindset ensures that each incident strengthens our collective capabilities and enhances the service delivered.
 
At DWP we join multi-supplier incident response calls, operating 24x7 until resolved. Supporting in resolving a P1 failure of a critical employment provider referral service, where the underlying issue on platforms was outside our application scope, we engaged in incident resolution, bringing suppliers together and additional specialists from our global team. Through our partnership with Oracle, SMEs restored the platform, ensuring vulnerable individuals continued to receive critical employment and financial support. 
 
We are a dedicated team, deeply committed to delivering a stable and dependable service that supports UK Security. We approach incidents not merely as technical challenges but with empathy and compassion, considering the perspective of the caseworker or applicant. This philosophy is ingrained throughout our teams, where new team members are paired with experienced colleagues to quickly adopt our values and methods. Our incident triage and prioritisation process is supported by a centralised management system, a clear prioritisation framework, and a collaborative, team-based approach. By building strong relationships and working collectively across all suppliers, we ensure seamless coordination, effective incident management, and consistent delivery that meets or exceeds SLAs.
