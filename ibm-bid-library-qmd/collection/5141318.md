IBM have 4300 consultants with existing SC clearance, and a significant number with clearance higher than SC. IBM do a large amount of work in the UK on defence and intelligence projects. IBM have an internal security office that manage clearances for employees. Approximately 1/3 of the existing IBM team working on DWP projects have existing SC clearance. Some of the resources identified for this opportunity already have SC in place. IBM would be comfortable that resources could still be provided in the timescales for this engagement if SC became a mandatory requirement instead of a nice-to-have.
