Delivery Model 
Our approach to assuring quality recognises that testing has a part to play across all phases of the agile delivery lifecycle. Our Quality Analysts (QAs) have continuously improved this approach throughout the delivery of LIB, establishing strong relationships with key stakeholders and acquiring an in depth understanding of your solution. 
We have led the way in developing an automated test framework, enhancing its functionality with each release, delivering a regression pack covering the full scope of your live service. Working alongside our DevOps team, we have introduced features such as self-service environments, automated deployments, ‘time-travel’ and parallel test execution, improving the capability available to testers across the Social Security programme. 
Confidence Through Testing 
Discovery 
During Discovery we will agree our Operational Readiness and Continuous Improvement (ORCI) Test Approach. We will ensure it is aligned to our delivery method and reflect any updates into the Programme Test Strategy. A Master Test Plan will be produced which details our testing stages, objectives and dependencies. A review of the user needs identified will be conducted as they come in, allowing us to make an early start on updating our regression pack for areas of focus to ORCI. 
Alpha 
As regular users of the system our QAs will be able to contribute ideas for improvements to the solution during Alpha. Our in-depth knowledge of your solution will enable the early identification of test data, impacts to system architecture and environmental requirements. The highly integrated nature of your system increases the importance of Integration testing. We will be engaging early with your delivery partners through systematic walkthroughs of interface specifications, communication of dependencies and provision of test data requirements. By engaging early, we hope to de-risk later integration phases whilst enabling the in-sprint testing of integrations to real components as opposed to stubs. 
As the new backlog develops, our QAs will contribute to initial effort assessments to support backlog prioritisation. We will enhance our Test Automation Framework to include new functionality that improves regression test coverage and reduces ongoing maintenance of our regression pack once we get into Beta. 
Beta 
Our goal during Beta is to test the new enhancements being delivered, whilst ensuring no new issues are introduced to the service. We will adopt a risk-based approach, using our existing knowledge of the solution to focus effort on scenarios with the highest business impact of failure. Both manual and automated tests will be executed against Acceptance Criteria, in line with the In-Sprint Test Plan. 
The types of testing we will conduct are detailed in the diagram below. We will record defects as well as opportunities for improvement in Jira for prioritisation by the Product Owner (PO). As with our current delivery we will use QTest to document test coverage and evidence to support PO sign off of user stories. To improve their efficiency using this product our QAs have taken the QTest Application Specialist course. 
Each sprint we will update our regression pack for the release, so that on the completion of Beta there is an automated regression pack that can be used to provide confidence no new issues are introduced. 
We will carry out in-sprint testing in as fully integrated an environment as possible, minimising the requirement for post-sprint testing. Other teams can continue to leverage outputs from our testing, for example letters generated will be shared with CHF. 
As in our cross-cutting delivery, we’ll be embedding the Release Test team in our scrum team. This early engagement will help define, scope and prioritise scenarios, reducing duplicate effort and promoting knowledge sharing across the programme. 
Our support to Release Testing continues post-beta, working closely with the non-functional test teams to support the completion of Operational Acceptance Testing, Performance Testing and Penetration Test accreditation (ITHC). Once new functionality is deployed our QAs will provide post go-live support to the Agency and Live Running Operations. 
Bringing this all together 
We are invested in building quality into the services we deliver, while thinking long term about how testing can be improved across Social Security. We’ve delivered seven new services and proud of our track record of avoiding the introduction of new issues. 
The ORCI team will be making changes to your live service and delivering value to your end-users more quickly than ever before.
