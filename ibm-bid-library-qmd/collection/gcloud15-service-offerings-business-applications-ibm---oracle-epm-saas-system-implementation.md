Service Description
IBM delivers transformation and implementation services for Oracle Cloud EPM SaaS Solutions Planning, Budgeting & Forecasting (PBCS, Financial, Capital, Projects, Workforce), Financial Consolidation and Close (FCCS), Profitability and Cost Management (PCMCS), Account Reconciliation (ARCS), Tax Reporting (TRCS), Master Data Management (MDM) and Reporting.

Our solutions are configured to maximise business performance, provide a simple, modern user experience, enable faster decision making with real time data inline with statutory, legislative compliance and move to a service driven solution for the Organisation. Validated against your transformation and business requirements, IBM can deliver your optimal target state from day one via a ready off-the-shelf modules that is fully working and optimised for Oracle Cloud Applications.
These include including IBM’s Oracle SaaS solutions for HCM; Payroll; CX; Supply Chain Management; ERP; EPM; Analytics; PaaS & IaaS. We also provide Managed Service and Cloud Infrastructure services.
