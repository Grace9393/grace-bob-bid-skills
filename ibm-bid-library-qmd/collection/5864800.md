Our approach to resourcing the APM support service is based on our track record of delivering resilient, high-performing services across the Public Sector including the Home Office. We routinely take on support and enhancement for Critical National Infrastructure and understand how to provide a high quality service offering excellent value to our clients. Our approach for APM is guided by this expertise, as well as leveraging economies-of-scale by combining the new APM team with our existing support teams within the HO, offering resiliency and avoiding single point of failures whilst minimising cost and maximising flexibility for you. We understand and support your strategy to bring product management back in-house, and we will field a team of technical experts that will complement your own team, seamlessly integrated with your leaders to support APM under your direction. 
 
Team Structure
Our solution incorporates APM support into the wider IBM Home Office Service Support Centre, supporting Enterprise Services and COP. We will bolster our existing team, consisting of support, development, cloud and test experts and share APM support across all resources. This approach allows us to offer an efficient solution with optimised additional FTEs, while still providing you the security/resilience of a larger team, avoiding single points-of-failure and benefiting from established experts with in-depth knowledge of the Authority. The team will have a dedicated APM Service manager and ring-fenced capacity for APM as per effort estimations based on ticket volumes and development work. This allows adoption of APM into our mature support model, fulfilling ticket triage/resolution, and onboarding of new air-carriers through multiple 'part-FTEs'.  Out-of-Hours support will be delivered through the same team, offering comprehensive coverage of your service at a price that offers great value for APM. This team can be scaled up/down in response to direction from the HO architects/leadership team in response to change in demand. 
 
Mobilising at Pace
Aided by the our pre-existing team structure and knowledge of APM, we are confident in a rapid, seamless adoption of the service into support. We will take on support of APM through our established team of HO experts, providing immediate continuity of service. To ensure the team can scale up for the additional effort required, we will resource additional staff through our Home Office 'Incubator', where we ring-fence public sector talent, providing them training in Home Office technologies/skills while arranging their clearance. This provides a flexible pool of talent, available to mobilise immediately, and flexible to changing demand.
 
Supporting Top Performance
IBM proposes following existing performance management processes as agreed within the Home Office. This involves daily interaction with service stakeholders, monthly service reviews and regular reporting containing incident management statistics/narrative. We will regularly review with you the future-view of APM, understanding that as the Home Office and aviation landscapes evolve, maintaining a top-quality service requires proactive identification of tech-debt/enhancements to achieve a continually-improving service. IBM also holds a quarterly Supplier Performance forum with Senior Authority Stakeholders and will include the APM service as part of the reporting pack within that meeting.
