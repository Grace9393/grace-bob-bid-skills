# Pricing Examples

Worked examples of common pricing calculations.

## Example 1: Fixed Price Project

**Project:** 9-month Salesforce implementation

**Team:** 8.5 FTE × 9 months = £1.21m labor
**Expenses:** £40k
**Overheads (1.5×):** £1.87m
**Contingency (15%):** £280k
**Total Cost:** £2.15m

**Pricing:**
- Aggressive (25% margin): £2.69m
- Competitive (35% margin): £2.90m
- Premium (45% margin): £3.12m

## Example 2: Blended Rate Calculation

**Team:** 3 onshore + 6 offshore = 9 FTE

**Costs:**
- Onshore: £57k/month
- Offshore: £40k/month
- Total: £97k/month

**Blended Rate:** £97k / 180 days = £539/day

**Savings vs 100% Onshore:** 36%

## Example 3: Gain-Share Payment

**Agreement:**
- Base: £600k
- Share: 40% of savings
- Cap: £1.08m (180% of base)

**Results:**
- Year 1 savings: £1.2m → £480k gain-share
- Year 2 savings: £1.4m → £560k gain-share
- Total: £1.64m (capped at £1.08m)

**Client retains:** £1.52m (59% of total savings)
