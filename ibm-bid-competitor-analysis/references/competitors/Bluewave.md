### Bluewave

**Profile**: UK-based Salesforce specialist with strong central government and NHS presence, known for technical depth and complex integration capability.

**Market Position**:
- Salesforce Gold Partner with 150+ employees
- Established presence in UK central government and NHS since 2012
- Strong focus on health, justice, and central government departments
- Known for technical excellence and complex system integration

**Positioning & Messaging**:
- Positions as "Technical Salesforce experts for complex government systems"
- Emphasizes security clearance and government experience
- Strong narrative around integration with legacy government systems
- Highlights technical architecture and data migration expertise
- Focuses on compliance, security, and governance capabilities

**Commercial Approach**:
- Premium pricing compared to other UK specialists (but below Big 4)
- Typically proposes time & materials for discovery, fixed-price for build
- Strong on framework call-offs (G-Cloud, DOS, CCS agreements)
- Rate cards typically 10-20% below Big 4, 20-30% above offshore competitors
- Willing to negotiate on commercial terms but holds firm on technical scope
- Hybrid delivery model balances cost and quality

**Delivery Strategy**:
- UK-based technical leads and architects (London, Bristol, Edinburgh)
- Some offshore capability for development work (India, Eastern Europe)
- Security-cleared resources for sensitive government work
- Strong emphasis on technical governance and architecture reviews
- Typically proposes 6-12 month implementation timelines for complex projects
- Resource model: 40-50% onsite/onshore, 30-40% nearshore, 10-20% offshore

**Deal Behavior**:
- Active on G-Cloud, DOS, CCS, and departmental frameworks
- Responds to central government and NHS Salesforce opportunities in £500K-£5M range
- Will bid on large-scale transformations as prime or as technical partner
- Strong at winning through technical credibility and security clearance
- Typically proposes 5-10 FTE teams for standard implementations

**Strengths**:
- Deep technical Salesforce expertise and architecture capability
- Strong security clearance base (SC, DV cleared resources)
- Proven track record in complex government integrations
- Good balance of technical depth and delivery pragmatism
- Strong relationships with government digital teams
- Experience with legacy system integration and data migration
- Credible technical governance and assurance capability

**Vulnerabilities**:
- Limited scale for very large enterprise transformations (£10M+)
- Offshore capability less mature than Indian heritage competitors
- Narrow technology stack (Salesforce-focused, limited multi-cloud)
- Less experience with business transformation and change management
- May struggle with large-scale programme management
- Limited presence outside UK government sector
- Higher cost base than offshore-heavy competitors

**Likely Attack Lines Against IBM**:
- "IBM brings unnecessary complexity and overhead"
- "We're Salesforce technical specialists, not generalists"
- "Our security-cleared teams understand government systems"
- "We deliver technical excellence without the corporate bureaucracy"
- "Better value through focused technical expertise"
- "We understand the GDS Service Manual and agile delivery"

**IBM Counter-Positioning**:
- Emphasize enterprise programme governance and delivery assurance
- Highlight IBM's broader technology stack and integration capability
- Reference Client Zero as proof of enterprise-scale Salesforce delivery
- Position business transformation and change management as differentiators
- Demonstrate IBM's security clearance base and government experience
- Show multi-cloud and AI capability for future-proofing
- Emphasize IBM's financial stability and long-term partnership capability
