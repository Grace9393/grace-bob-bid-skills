- Strong on NHS frameworks and healthcare procurement routes

- Strong emphasis on clinical safety and regulatory compliance

- Typically proposes 6-12 month implementation timelines for regulated projects

**Deal Behavior**:

- Will bid on large-scale NHS transformations as prime or specialist partner
- Strong at winning through healthcare domain expertise and compliance credibility
- Typically proposes 4-8 FTE teams for standard implementations
- Often partners with clinical transformation consultancies

**Strengths**:
- Deep healthcare domain knowledge and clinical expertise
- Strong regulatory compliance capability (MHRA, clinical governance)
- Proven track record in NHS and life sciences
- Good relationships with NHS digital teams and clinical stakeholders
- Healthcare-specific accelerators and pre-built solutions
