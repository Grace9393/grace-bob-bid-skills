# IBM Bid Qualification Criteria

Complete reference for all 20 qualification criteria with scoring standards.

## A: Client Maturity

### 1. Incumbent Status
**Question:** Is there an existing incumbent?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: No incumbent exists
- **2**: Incumbent exists and is the preferred supplier
- **3**: Incumbent exists but project success is uncertain
- **4**: Incumbent exists but client is unhappy, interested in IBM bidding
- **5**: IBM is the incumbent

### 2. Organisation Size
**Question:** Is the organisation > 100 FTEs? Or Finance > 150 FTEs?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: No, <50 FTEs
- **2**: No, >50 FTEs
- **3**: Yes, >100 FTEs
- **4**: Yes, >150 FTEs
- **5**: Yes, >200 FTEs

## B: Client Justification

### 3. Operating Model Review
**Question:** Has an operating model review been done/in progress/planned?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: No operating model exists
- **2**: Plan to create an operating model
- **3**: Operating model in progress
- **4**: Operating model exists
- **5**: Operating model completed and IBM built it

### 4. Technology Upgrade
**Question:** Is a technology upgrade planned/in-flight?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: No technology upgrade planned or in-flight
- **2**: Plan to upgrade technology in future
- **3**: Technology upgrades in progress
- **4**: Technology roadmap exists but not yet reviewed by IBM
- **5**: Full technology roadmap that IBM helped build

### 5. Leadership Appointment
**Question:** Have they appointed a leader?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: No appointed leader or unknown
- **2**: Plan to appoint a leader
- **3**: Leader exists but IBM doesn't know them
- **4**: Leader exists with occasional IBM contact
- **5**: Leader exists with good IBM relationship and intel-gathering capability

## C: Emotional & Buying Momentum

### 6. Case for Change
**Question:** Is there a compelling and recognised case for change?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: Business need unknown or not understood
- **2**: Business need known but not pressing or compelling
- **3**: Compelling business need but alternative approaches being considered
- **4**: Pressing business need with no viable alternatives to submitted offers
- **5**: Pressing business need plus potential for additional business opportunities

## D: Relationship Connections

### 7. Decision-Maker Relationships
**Question:** Do we have strong relationships? Do we know who makes the selection decision and how they will decide?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: Don't know decision-makers, strong incumbent present
- **2**: Know decision-makers but little to no relationship
- **3**: Know decision-makers and process, have some supporters (competitors do too)
- **4**: Know decision-makers with good relationships, strongly placed
- **5**: Excellent relationship with decision-makers who are coaching/supporting IBM

### 8. Competitive & Political Landscape
**Question:** Do we understand the competitive and political landscape?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: No understanding
- **2**: Understand only political OR competitive landscape
- **3**: Partial understanding of both
- **4**: Good understanding of both
- **5**: Deep understanding of both competitive and political landscape

## E: IBM Reputation

### 9. Delivery Track Record
**Question:** Do we have a record of strong delivery within this area of the client?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: No record of delivery with client
- **2**: Unsuccessful delivery record with client
- **3**: Mixed record (some strong, some poor delivery)
- **4**: Strong record with some slightly troubled delivery
- **5**: Strong record of delivery

### 10. Recent Transformation Work
**Question:** Has IBM been involved in transformation work in last 2 years with this area of client?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: No transformation work
- **2**: Transformation work was unsuccessful
- **3**: Transformation work completed with some delivery issues
- **4**: Successful transformation work with good reputation
- **5**: Currently involved in transformation work with good reputation

### 11. Industry Reputation
**Question:** Does IBM have a good reputation in this industry?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: Poor reputation in industry
- **2**: No reputation in industry
- **3**: Reasonably good reputation with some past issues
- **4**: Good reputation with no major issues
- **5**: Excellent reputation, trusted adviser status

### 12. Specialist/Regulatory Expertise
**Question:** Do we have specialism/regulatory expertise and/or relevant product in the industry (e.g., Promontory, Pharma, drug development)?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: No relevant specialism, expertise, or product
- **2**: Relevant capability but client doesn't want to utilise it
- **3**: Relevant capability but client currently unaware
- **4**: Relevant capability and client will consider it
- **5**: Relevant capability and IBM is the only provider

## F: Solution Differentiators

### 13. Clear Differentiators
**Question:** Do we have clear differentiators relevant to the industry or client?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: No unique propositions relevant to opportunity
- **2**: Believe have differentiators but unconfirmed with client, competitors' differentiators unknown
- **3**: Know IBM differentiators and competitors' differentiators
- **4**: Know differentiators and competitors', confirmed with customer
- **5**: Customer incorporated IBM USPs into requirements

### 14. Solution-Requirement Fit
**Question:** Do we have an existing solution/product that closely matches customer's requirement?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: No solution meets requirements
- **2**: Solution meets some requirements with significant gaps
- **3**: Solution meets most requirements but needs significant customisation
- **4**: Solution meets most requirements with little change, all requirements with some customisation
- **5**: Solution meets all requirements with no significant change

### 15. Competitive Positioning
**Question:** How do we stack up against the competition?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: Don't know other bidders
- **2**: Know bidders, some capable and credible, one is incumbent
- **3**: Know bidders, believe IBM is strongest, one is incumbent
- **4**: IBM is incumbent or customer wants to replace incumbent, IBM is strongest
- **5**: IBM is only bidder or all others weak/uncompetitive, IBM is incumbent

## G: Decision Making Process Knowledge

### 16. Bid Timeline
**Question:** Are the timescales for the bid manageable?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: Customer timescales unknown
- **2**: Timescales known but don't fit sales cycle, insufficient time for QA/review
- **3**: Timescales don't fit sales cycle, must reassign resource from other bids
- **4**: Timescales realistic, allow review and QA, can resource without disruption
- **5**: Timescales realistic for IBM, competition will struggle to meet them

### 17. Opportunity Size
**Question:** Is the opportunity the right size?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: Too small for profitability or >10x nearest comparable win
- **2**: Too small for profitability or >5x nearest comparable win
- **3**: Small but opens door for larger opportunities, or >3x nearest comparable win
- **4**: Within range of comparable wins
- **5**: Within range of comparable wins and strategically important for new business lines

### 18. Budget Alignment
**Question:** Can we price our solution within customer's budget?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: Customer budget unknown
- **2**: Budget known but unrealistic, IBM cannot meet it
- **3**: Budget known, unlikely IBM will be within it
- **4**: Budget known, IBM will be close (above or below)
- **5**: Budget known and confident IBM will be within it

### 19. Commercial Framework
**Question:** Do we have the relevant commercial framework/rate card in place compared to competition?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: Not on customer's commercial framework
- **2**: On framework but rate card not competitive
- **3**: (Not defined in original)
- **4**: On framework, won work with this customer using rate card
- **5**: On framework, won work, rate card more competitive than competition

### 20. TUPE Involvement
**Question:** Does this deal involve Transfer of Acquired Rights (TUPE)?

**Scoring:**
- **1**: TUPE involved and not capped
- **2**: TUPE involved with >10 people in scope
- **3**: TUPE involved with 5-10 people in scope
- **4**: TUPE involved with <5 people in scope
- **5**: No TUPE
