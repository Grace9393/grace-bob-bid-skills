## Client Pain Points  

- **Content Supply Chain**  
  - Challenges in managing the rapidly expanding content needed to deliver personalization  
  - Distributed, duplicated and unstructured assets increases costs and hinders creation  
  - Addressing requirements to deliver content faster, to more channels at a lower cost  
  - Inability to measure the impact of content investments  

- **Personalization @ Scale**  
  - Distributed and incomplete customer data makes the 360 view impossible  
  - Data access and quality does not provide insights needed for targeting and messaging  
  - Inability to manage the creation and deployment of highly personalized communications  

- **Campaign & Journey Optimization**  
  - Manual and disjointed processes lead to increased costs and long lead times  
  - Inability to apply automation and insights to campaigns reduces timeliness and relevance of messages  
  - Lack of rigorous measurement of performance at the customer and campaign level
