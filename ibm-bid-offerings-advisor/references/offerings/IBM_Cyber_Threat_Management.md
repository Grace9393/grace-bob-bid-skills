## Offering Description
Cyber Threat Management offerings help clients increase resiliency, stay ahead of evolving threats, and minimize the impact of attacks to lower business risk. Our decades of expertise are now amplified by AI agents, assistants, and automation that uncover vulnerabilities, detect attacks, and accelerate response and remediation. Clients gain visibility and control across complex hybrid environments.

We strengthen existing security investments with mature threat intelligence, hunting, and AI-powered automation combined with human-led analysis. This enables 24/7 threat detection and response across hybrid multi-cloud environments. Our X-Force services provide proactive security through offensive testing, vulnerability management, and incident response, while our Threat Management Advisory team consults with clients every – shaping strategy, aligning investments, and preparing them for a smooth transition into steady-state services.

## Value Proposition
Proactively reduce risk of ransomware and cyber disruption by testing defenses, practicing response, and actively managing threat detection and response. Our Cybersecurity Services Platform is vendor-agnostic and built on an open ecosystem, supporting dozens of security tools to protect client assets across on-prem and cloud environments.

With Cyber Threat Management Asset Packs, we deliver patented agents and AI assistants (including ATOM) to accelerate threat detection and response.

Because our services are fully integrated into the platform, we ensure global resiliency, regulatory compliance, and consistent operations across industries.

## Client Pain Points
- **Fragmented tools and inefficiency**: Siloed security tools create blind spots and slow response. CTT unifies technologies in a vendor-agnostic, AI-powered platform, enhancing existing investments with automation and expertise.
- **Rising ransomware and disruption**: Ransomware and cyberattacks pressure security teams. CTT uses AI agents and ATOM to accelerate detection, response, and remediation – improving resilience.
- **Limited visibility in hybrid environments**: Organizations lack unified visibility across hybrid multi-cloud. CTT delivers 24/7 monitoring, AI-driven threat intelligence, and advisory support for proactive control.
- **Unprepared for real-world attacks**: Without practice, defenses falter. CTT’s X-Force testing, vulnerability management, and IR exercises validate defenses and prepare teams before attacks occur.
