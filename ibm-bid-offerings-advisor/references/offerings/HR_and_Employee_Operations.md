## Offering Description  

HR and Employee Operations partner with clients to combine AI, market-leading technologies, and HR experts at scale to deliver a seamless employee experience across the entire HR life cycle – empowering employees, managers and HR with access to information and insights on demand. The services we provide include, but are not limited to, the following:  

### Employee Services  
- Employee anchored service delivery model  
- Employee Business Process Outsourcing  
- Proven product-first digital access channels  
- Employee Contact Center  

### HR Operations  
- Workforce Administration and Analytics  
- Learning Administration  
- Benefits and Compensation  
- Payroll  

---

## Value Proposition  

Partnering with clients, we will unlock the effectiveness and efficiency of the HR service delivery organization as the same time of providing an unparalleled employee experience. Enabled by embedding AI and automation throughout, we will deliver:  
- A strong employee experience  
- Strong self-service adoption  
- Reduced cycle time  
- Maximized investment in client’s cloud HCM  
- Reduced cost to serve  
- Agile practices for speed and responsiveness  
- Consistent transparency to preserve trust and reduce reputational risk.

## Client Pain Points  
- Challenge shifting from a holding company to an operating company business model  
- Lack of HR organization ability to anticipate and respond to business dynamics  
- HR seen as lacking relevance to the business as a result of a state HR function  
- Lack of capacity and/or ability transform HR operating model  
- Need for reduced HR operating costs  
- Subpar HR employee experience  
- Low employee self service capabilities and low adoption of self service  
- Challenges with data timeliness and accuracy
