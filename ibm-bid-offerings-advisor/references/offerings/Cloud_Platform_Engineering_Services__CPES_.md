## Offering Description  

Cloud Platform Engineering Services (CPES) combines the principles of Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) with a "Hybrid-by-Design" strategy to deliver exceptional performance, scalability, and reliability across complex hybrid clouds and architectures enabled by Hybrid Cloud Console (HCC), powered by Redhat and HashiCorp technologies.  

- We provide comprehensive Infrastructure Operations across all major hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, IBM, OCI, GCP) and on-premises (MIS) for the physical layer.  
- We enhance system resilience and minimize downtime through leveraging proactive AI enabled monitoring and automated ticketing and resolution (AIOps) with data-driven insights.  

This allows organizations to focus on innovation while ensuring reliability, security, and compliance of their cloud environments, enabling faster time-to-market adding to client’s top line while improving the bottom-line.  

We run the world’s most challenging infrastructure setups for clients such as the Masters, Wimbledon (through IBM Sports), as well as the world’s largest enterprise solutions for SAP and high-performance computing solutions.

## Client Pain Points  

- **Complexity and Fragmentation:** Disjointed cloud environments lead to increased costs and reduced agility.  
- **Uncontrolled Cloud Costs:** Lack of governance and visibility results in escalating expenses in hybrid and multi-cloud settings.  
- **Security and Compliance Risks:** Challenges in meeting regulatory requirements can cause inefficiencies and potential security breaches.  
- **Scalability and Reliability Issues:** Cloud infrastructure struggles to keep pace with growing business demands.  
- **Resource Gaps:** Limited expertise hinders the design, build, and management of cloud-based solutions.  
- **Operational Disruption:** Downtime during cloud transformations negatively impacts business operations and revenue.  
- **Technical Debt:** Clearing it is essential to enable AI-driven optimization and modern services.  
- **Funding constraints:** Organizations often need a stepwise transformation, slowing down cloud benefits.
