## Offering Description  

We simplify the complexities of sales and commerce to deliver trusted, relevant, and convenient buying experiences for both consumer and B2B buyers which drive strategic and profitable growth for our clients across all industries.  

Our deep domain expertise coupled with our innovative, asset-based consulting services enable our clients to transform their business processes and workflows.  

This includes reimagining complex sales processes like CPO, optimizing product catalogs by transitioning from keyword to natural language search, reinventing digital and physical channel experiences, and by effectively using AI and automation throughout the buying experience.  

Our platform agnostic offering harnesses proven assets and methods, such as our open-source IBM I X Experience Orchestrator, Generative AI portfolio, and IBM Garage. These tools enable us to modernize and transform our clients’ sales and commerce operating models, platforms, and processes, delivering data-driven commerce journeys at scale.
