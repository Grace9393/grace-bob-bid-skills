## Offering Description
By 2030, HFS forecasts that consulting firms will deliver Service-as-Software. IBM leads this trend with an asset-based, intelligence-driven approach to Business Process Operations. Our Industry Operations offering facilitates zero-touch operations and transformation for industries including Banking, Financial Markets, Insurance, Consumer Goods, Retail, Government, Life Sciences, Healthcare, and Industrial Manufacturing, using an asset-based strategy.  

We transition conventional labor arbitrage projects into comprehensive digital transformations, integrating data and AI for automated, efficient industry operations. This drives business value and ROI. Examples include Trade Finance, KYC, Claims, Citizen Services, Billing, and other industry-specific operations and front-office processes.  

Our method leverages data-driven Process Intelligence, AI-powered automated workflows, and Agentic AI for digitization, enabling higher-value tasks. For instance, a digital worker in Trade Finance can enhance transparency, speed, and risk management.  

## Value Proposition
Transform your industry operations with IBM’s Industry Operations. This offering boosts revenue, enhances customer experience, and raises satisfaction by eradicating errors, fortifying security, ensuring compliance, and optimizing efficiency and productivity.  

With Industry Operations offering, you’ll be able to shift from human-centric to asset-based solutions to allocate resources for strategic tasks, scaling your transformation journey for competitive success.  

With Industry Operations offering, you’ll be able to scale your transformation journey with Automation as a guardrail, integrating data and AI for comprehensive end-to-end transformation.  

## Client Pain Points
- Slowed automation and AI initiatives inhibit enterprise-wide transformation and efficiency.  
- Inadequate ROI and business value from technology investments result in people arbitrage.  
- Manual processes lacking integrated technology and industry-specific solutions pose risks and compliance issues.  
- Operational costs escalate, exerting financial pressure to cut labor-intensive expenses and optimize OPEX/CAPEX.  
- Labor-heavy processes cause skill gaps and talent retention challenges.
