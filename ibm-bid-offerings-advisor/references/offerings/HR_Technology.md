## Offering Description  
The HR technology offering enables clients to leverage automation, AI/Gen AI, predictive analytics, and modern HR platforms to deliver exceptional employee and candidate experiences, while reducing the administrative work for HR professionals.  

- **Transform HR Technology** – leverage our depth of HR Product capabilities to create an AI first technology vision and roadmap to maximize HR investments and minimize HR systems  
- **Digitize with HR Technology** – leverage our genAI configuration and testing capabilities to rapidly implement new modules, reconfigure existing modules, and extend data & reporting capabilities on: Workday, Oracle HCM, SAP SuccessFactors  
- **Innovate with AI** – deliver 24x7 self-service capability for common HR queries and transactions as well as AI guided assistance to complete complex HR transactions
