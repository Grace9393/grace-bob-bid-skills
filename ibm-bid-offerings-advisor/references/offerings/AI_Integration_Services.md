## Client Pain Points
- **Struggling to scale AI beyond pilots**  
  Many organizations are able to launch small AI pilots but lack the enterprise capabilities needed to scale—such as a robust architecture, solid AI governance, and an accelerated development lifecycle. Without these, AI remains siloed and delivers limited impact.  
- **Lack of unified context, orchestration, and control**  
  AI solutions are often deployed in isolation—copilots, assistants, or automation tools—without a common business context, governance, or orchestration layer. This results in inconsistent outputs, operational risk, and limited transparency across systems and functions.  
- **Process debt embedded in legacy applications**  
  Core business and IT processes remain locked in aging, highly customized systems that slow innovation, limit automation potential, and make it difficult to integrate AI seamlessly into end-to-end workflows.
