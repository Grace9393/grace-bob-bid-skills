## Offering Description  
This offering delivers right-fit transformation of the mainframe application estate based on functional, non-functional and TCO considerations while de-risking execution and change management, embracing hybrid multi-cloud destinations.  

By combining deep expertise from the Modern Systems acquisitions, ICA Gen AI Assets and Products such as WCA4Z, we can strategically retain and right-size mainframe workloads, refactor functionality to distributed clouds and / or securely integrate applications and data across the mainframe-distributed cloud estate, boosting agility, lowering costs and greatly accelerating speed of modernization and digital transformation.  

## Value Proposition  
The IBM Mainframe has exceptional modern engineering Hybrid Cloud and AI capabilities, and this offering helps clients maximizes and unlock the full potential of their deep expertise across industries. IBM offers customized mainframe modernization solutions, integrating AI-powered tools and connecting mainframes to public cloud environments.  

- **Accelerated Transformation with risk mitigation**: Leverage the Hybrid by Design Framework, IBM ICA Assets and deep mainframe expertise to determine which workloads should be retained on mainframes versus run on distributed clouds and leverage the highly automated journey-based execution model to drive accelerated speed of modernization and ROI.  
- **Co-existence Framework**: Most enterprises run mission core business processes on mainframes, therefore any disruption in functionality or availability can result in significant revenue loss. IBM Consulting’s coexistence framework, enables continuous modernization allowing both old and new systems to operate simultaneously with seamless transitions to avoid business disruptions.  
- **"Build on" Mainframe for modern workloads**: IBM Consulting provides capabilities to run modern workloads such as high-density containers, quantum safe transactions and data stores, Data Analytics and AI workloads based on client requirements.  
- **WCA42**: Accelerates modernization through AI-driven code refactoring, optimizing workloads for hybrid cloud environments.  
- **Strengthen Security**: Integrated “security as code” best practices, as well as threat monitoring and testing services.  

## Client Pain Points  
- **High Operational Costs**: Rising MIPS and software costs limit profitability and hinder innovation.  
- **Talent Shortages & Legacy Processes**: Skill gaps and outdated DevOps slow time-to-market and reduce agility.  
- **Knowledge Gaps**: Limited documentation and system expertise risk operational stability and scalability.  
- **Modernization Failures**: Previous attempts to meet SLAs and performance benchmarks in new environments resulted in disruption.  
- **Inflexible Systems**: Monolithic, batch-oriented mainframes hinder business agility and responsiveness.  
- **Innovation Barriers**: Proprietary architectures restrict the adoption of modern technologies like containers, limiting transformation.  
- **AI/ML Latency**: Delays in AI/ML processing reduce the effectiveness of data-driven decisions.
