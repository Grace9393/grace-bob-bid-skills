## Client Pain Points
- **Limited Cloud Adoption**: 80% of mission-critical workloads still remain on-premises, hindering digital transformation.  
- **High IT Spend**: 75% of IT budgets are spent on maintaining legacy systems, limiting investment in innovation.  
- **Security Risks**: 30% of applications face significant security vulnerabilities.  
- **High Legacy System Costs**: Maintaining outdated systems is expensive, and migration costs may be prohibitive.  
- **Technical Debt**: Years of patchwork fixes and outdated architectures slow modernization, creating operational inefficiencies.  
- **Lack of Expertise**: Limited in-house skills make it difficult to manage complex migrations and modernization.  
- **Slow Time-to-Value**: Legacy systems and inefficient processes delay the realization of business value.
