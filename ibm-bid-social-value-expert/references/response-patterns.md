# Response Structure Patterns

## Pattern 1: Commitment-Led Structure (Most Common)

Use when the question asks "What social value will you deliver?" or "Describe your social value commitments."

```markdown
## Social Value Commitments

### Commitment 1: [Specific, measurable commitment]

**Activities:**
- [Specific activity 1 with timeline]
- [Specific activity 2 with timeline]

**Measures:**
| Measure | Target | Reporting |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| [TOMs measure or custom metric] | [Quantified target] | [Frequency and method] |

**Accountability:**
- **Owner**: [Named role or team]
- **Governance**: [Forum or mechanism]
- **Reporting**: [To whom, how often]

### Commitment 2: [Next commitment]
[Repeat structure]

### Integration with Service Delivery
[How social value activities connect to core service — not bolt-on]

### Governance and Assurance
[Overall governance structure, reporting cadence, escalation process]
```

## Pattern 2: TOMs-Led Structure

Use when the buyer explicitly requests National TOMs Framework responses or provides a TOMs template.

```markdown
## Social Value Response: National TOMs Framework

### Theme 1: Jobs

**Outcome**: [Relevant outcome from TOMs]

**Measures and Commitments:**
| Measure | Description | Target | Proxy Value | Total Value |
|---------|-------------|--------|-------------|-------------|
| NT1 | Local people employed | 15 FTE | £18,500 | £277,500 |
| NT2 | Apprenticeships | 5 | £25,000 | £125,000 |

**Delivery Approach:**
[Named IBM programmes and evidence]

**Activities:**
- [Specific activity 1]

**Governance:**
[Accountability and reporting]

### Theme 2: Growth
[Repeat for each theme]

### Total Social Value Commitment
[Summary table — total proxy value across all themes]

### Reporting and Assurance
[Tracking, reporting, assurance approach]
```

## Pattern 3: Question-Led Structure

Use when the buyer asks multiple specific questions (e.g., "How will you support local employment?", "What environmental initiatives?").

```markdown
### 1. Supporting Local Employment

**Commitment**: [Specific commitment]

**How we will deliver**: [Named programme or mechanism]

**Activities**:
- [Activity 1 with timeline]

**Measures**: [Quantified targets and reporting]

**Evidence**: [IBM proof point with quantified outcome]

### 2. Environmental Sustainability
[Repeat for each question]
```

## Governance Block Template

Include in every response. Customise roles and cadences to match the contract context.

```markdown
### Governance and Assurance

**Accountability:**
- **Social Value Lead**: [Named role], reporting to [Senior role]
- **Delivery teams**: [Roles responsible for specific commitments]

**Governance:**
- Monthly review at Service Delivery Board
- Quarterly Social Value Steering Group with client representation
- Annual independent audit of outcomes

**Reporting:**
- Quarterly reports via Social Value Portal (National TOMs)
- Monthly dashboard showing progress against targets
- Annual social value impact report

**Escalation:**
- Targets at risk flagged at monthly review
- Mitigation plans developed within 2 weeks
- Client notified of material variances within 5 working days
```

## Common Question Types

### "Describe your social value commitments"
→ Pattern 1 (Commitment-Led). 3–5 commitments, each with activities, measures, governance. Add integration + overall governance sections.

### "Complete the National TOMs Framework template"
→ Pattern 2 (TOMs-Led). 8–12 measures across themes. Quantify targets and proxy values. Show total social value commitment.

### "How will you support [specific outcome]?" (local employment, environmental sustainability, etc.)
→ Pattern 3 (Question-Led). Mirror buyer's numbering. Deep detail on that area. Named IBM programmes + strong evidence.

### "What added value will you bring beyond the core service?"
→ Integration-focused. Show social value enhancing service delivery. Local economic benefit, knowledge transfer, community engagement integral to the service.

### "How will you measure and report social value?"
→ Governance-focused. Measurement framework, data collection, verification, reporting cadence, governance structure, example dashboard format.

## Troubleshooting

**Response feels generic or marketing-focused:**
Replace adjectives with named programmes. Add quantified targets and IBM precedent. Name the people who will deliver. Show the governance mechanism.

**Commitments seem undeliverable or risky:**
Scale back to proven IBM capability. Add governance controls and escalation. Show how IBM will track and report. Reference similar delivered commitments.

**Social value feels disconnected from service delivery:**
Explain how social value activities use service delivery resources. Link supplier diversity to service procurement. Connect volunteering to service-relevant skills.

**Weak on local context:**
Reference buyer's geography and community priorities. Name local partners, colleges, or organisations. Adapt national programmes to local needs.

**Scoring below 3 on evaluator:**
Add more specific quantified targets. Strengthen evidence with IBM precedent. Clarify accountability and governance. Make commitments more concrete and measurable.
