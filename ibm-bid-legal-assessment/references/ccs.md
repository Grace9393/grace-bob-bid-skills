# Crown Commercial Service (CCS) Framework Assessment Criteria

Generic CCS framework legal risk assessment questions for frameworks not otherwise specified (e.g., Technology Products 2, Professional Services, Wider Public Sector frameworks).

## Framework Overview

CCS operates multiple category-specific frameworks with varying terms and conditions. This reference covers common elements and directs to Model Services Contract provisions where applicable.

## Common CCS Elements

- **Model Services Contract**: CCS template forming basis of many frameworks
- **Call-off flexibility**: Framework terms plus call-off schedules
- **Aggregation**: Multiple buyers, varying requirements
- **Direct awards**: vs further competition within framework
- **Volume commitments**: Often none, may see minimum commitments in call-offs

### 1. Mandatory Eligibility and Exclusion Grounds

**CCS Context**: Framework admission requires full SQ assessment. Call-offs may impose additional checks (particularly for higher-value or sensitive contracts). Look for Cabinet Office PPN incorporation.

**IBM Position**: Standard CCS eligibility requirements typically satisfied by IBM UK. Call-off specific additions require individual assessment.

### 2. Liability Caps, Indemnification, IP Ownership

**CCS Context**: Model Services Contract provides 125% total contract value cap as baseline. Many frameworks adopt this. Others specify 100% annual charges. Call-off contracts vary significantly.

**IBM Position**: CCS baseline terms generally acceptable. Material deviations in call-offs require legal review before bid submission.

## Risk Classification - CCS Generic

**RED FLAGS for CCS**
- Call-off liability terms materially worse than framework baseline
- Parent company guarantee requirement not disclosed in framework
- IP assignment of supplier background IP
- Unlimited TUPE liability with workforce unknown at bid stage
- Security clearance requirements impossible to deliver in timescale
- Payment terms >60 days (government commitment is 30 days)

**AMBER for CCS**
- Framework terms acceptable but call-off adds onerous provisions
- Volume commitments without exclusivity
- Benchmarking without defined methodology
- Key Personnel requirements for >50% of delivery team
- Exit assistance >90 days without payment
- Insurance requirements above IBM standard cover
